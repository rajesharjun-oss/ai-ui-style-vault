# Facebook / Public Directory Source Playbook

Use this playbook for Facebook business pages and third-party directory/review/listing sources such as local directories, Yelp/Tripadvisor-style listings, Foursquare-style venue records and similar public pages.

## Objective

Use these sources as corroborating evidence without assuming third-party records are current or owner-controlled.

## Extract carefully

Capture exactly when visible:

- business/display name;
- category;
- address/location;
- phone;
- website;
- opening hours;
- rating/review count;
- listed services/attributes;
- source-specific description;
- official social/external links only when the listing explicitly associates them with the business.

Record which source supplied every material claim. Do not merge conflicting values into a new hybrid value.

## Freshness and authority

A third-party listing can be stale. Prefer owner-controlled sources for current operational facts when they conflict, but retain the conflict in evidence and research gaps. Do not silently overwrite one source with another.

A directory category or autogenerated description should not be treated as owner-authored positioning. Keep it as listing metadata unless corroborated.

## Reviews

When reviews are used, preserve them verbatim and retain source attribution. Do not combine sentences from multiple reviews, normalize grammar, invent relative times or convert aggregate sentiment into a direct quote.

## Media

Directory/Facebook media may have uncertain rights or may be uploaded by customers. Record provenance/ownership status. Do not treat all listing photos as official business photography. If ownership cannot be established, keep the media as research evidence rather than automatically adding it to the build asset allowlist.

## Facebook-specific cautions

If the page is blocked behind login, do not claim inspection. Visible About details, contact buttons and owner posts may be stronger evidence than comments or tagged/customer media. Do not infer current availability from an old post without date/context.

## Directory-specific cautions

Do not turn category tags, amenity checkboxes or reviewer statements into guaranteed business capabilities without appropriate evidence. Do not use directory navigation/map URLs as the business website unless that exact URL is the stated business website field.

## Blocked/partial access

Set `accessStatus` accurately and use other public or user-supplied sources as separate evidence. A partial snippet/search result is not equivalent to inspecting the underlying listing.

## Default asset stance

Usually prefer `verified-only` for owner-controlled media or `allowlist-only` when the user explicitly approves exact listing images. When media provenance is unclear, exclude it from the build asset allowlist.
