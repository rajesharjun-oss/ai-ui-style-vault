# Instagram Business Source Playbook

Use this playbook when an Instagram profile is a primary or supporting source for a real business.

## Objective

Extract business identity, offer evidence, brand voice, visual direction and conversion signals without treating captions/social behavior as stronger evidence than they are.

## Extract first

Capture exactly when visible:

- profile display name and handle;
- bio text;
- profile category;
- contact buttons/details;
- website/link-in-bio destination when directly visible;
- location text when explicitly stated;
- stated order/enquiry channel such as DM, WhatsApp, booking link or website;
- named products/services that are repeatedly or explicitly presented;
- operational statements such as `available 24/7`, delivery coverage, appointment-only, made-to-order or turnaround only when the account itself states them.

Do not infer legal business name, physical address, delivery area, prices, gender/audience scope or availability from imagery alone.

## Posts, captions and highlights

Use posts/highlights as evidence only when they can be inspected. Record the exact post/reference used for important facts. Separate recurring brand claims from one-off promotional language.

Product/service labels in captions may be stored as source facts. Style descriptors such as `luxury`, `premium`, `minimal`, `bold` or `cultural-modern` should normally remain inference unless the business explicitly uses them as positioning.

## Media

Treat official profile/post media as business media only when provenance is clear and use is permitted. Preserve the source URL/reference and do not present downloaded/generated replacements as the original.

Infer palette, typography mood, photography style, garment/product categories, material cues and composition patterns only under `inference.brandSignals`.

## Comments and social proof

Do not convert comments, follower counts, likes or engagement into customer testimonials or business claims unless the user explicitly asks and the evidence is appropriate. Never invent testimonial text from engagement.

## Conversion

Instagram often provides strong conversion evidence: DM, WhatsApp, booking link, call or external website. Record the exact visible channel. Do not invent checkout, payment, delivery or booking capabilities simply because the business accepts enquiries.

## Blocked/partial access

If Instagram requires login, blocks browsing or exposes only limited metadata, mark the source `partial` or `blocked`. Ask for screenshots/recordings or use other public sources. Do not fill the gap with generic assumptions based on the handle/category.

## Default asset stance

Use `verified-only` or `allowlist-only` for official account media when the build is expected to stay faithful. Use generated concept imagery only when explicitly authorized and keep it visibly distinct from official work/products.
