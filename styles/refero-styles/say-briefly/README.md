Light creative-agency sketchbook style system captured from Refero Styles.

- Source: https://styles.refero.design/style/8b91f4c9-74e5-4925-90a3-3dd31fd5725e
- Reference site: https://saybriefly.com
- Captured: 2026-07-28
- Theme: light
- Mood: creative agency sketchbook on cream paper

## Use This When

Use this style for creative agencies, brand studios, pitch tools, briefing products, idea boards, playful AI products, collaboration tools, and startup sites that should feel handmade, smart, friendly, and slightly rebellious.

## Core Idea

Say Briefly is a cream-paper studio moodboard. Forest Ink provides the structural backbone for text, borders, and CTAs. Bricolage Grotesque creates chunky sticker-like display headlines, Highlighter Yellow acts like marker wash behind keywords, and pastel sticky-note colors appear as scattered accents rather than large surfaces.
