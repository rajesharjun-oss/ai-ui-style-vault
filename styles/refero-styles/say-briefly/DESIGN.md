# Say Briefly - Style Reference

## Positioning

Say Briefly feels like a creative studio sketchbook: warm paper, chunky display type, tiny utility labels, sticker-like color surfaces, and a sense of handmade looseness. It should feel friendly and expressive, but not chaotic. Forest Ink keeps the system grounded while yellow and pastels create playful punctuation.

## Theme

- Theme: light
- Mood: creative agency sketchbook on cream paper
- Best fit: creative agencies, brand studios, briefing tools, idea boards, playful AI tools, collaboration products, pitch decks, startup studio pages

## Visual Principles

1. Use Cream Paper as the canvas.
2. Use Forest Ink as the primary structural color.
3. Use Highlighter Yellow as a marker behind words.
4. Use Bricolage only for large display headlines.
5. Use Inter for functional copy and UI.
6. Use pastel accents as sticky notes, not full section themes.
7. Separate surfaces with fills and borders, not heavy shadows.

## Typography

Use Bricolage Grotesque for display headlines only. Its 800 weight and positive tracking create the chunky sticker-book voice. Do not use it below 40px or for body, nav, or buttons.

Use Inter for body, subheads, buttons, navigation, cards, and most UI. Use Roboto Mono for tiny labels, nav utility text, small tags, and technical metadata.

Recommended fallbacks:

- Display: `Bricolage Grotesque`, `Archivo Black`, `Mulish`, `ui-sans-serif`, `system-ui`
- UI: `Inter`, `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `Segoe UI`, `sans-serif`
- Mono: `Roboto Mono`, `JetBrains Mono`, `IBM Plex Mono`, `ui-monospace`, `monospace`

## Spacing And Shape

- Base unit: 8px
- Density: comfortable
- Page max width: 1200px
- Section gap: 64px
- Card padding: 24px
- Element gap: 16px
- Buttons: 6px radius
- Cards: 12px radius
- Nav: 16px radius
- Tags: 9999px radius
- Subtle button shadow allowed
- Large yellow nav glow is a signature atmospheric shadow

## Layout

Use a warm Cream Paper page with centered 1200px content. Hero sections should feel like a poster or moodboard: a tagline badge above, chunky display headline with highlighted words, a supporting paragraph, CTA group, reassurance caption, and optional backed-by logo row. Cards can feel like sticky notes or pasted objects, but keep the layout orderly enough to scan.

## Imagery And Illustration

Use hand-drawn sketch elements sparingly in Forest Ink at low opacity. They should feel like atmosphere, not iconography. Avoid polished 3D, glossy gradients, and generic stock images.

## Components

### Primary CTA Button

Forest Ink fill, Cream Paper text, 6px radius, Inter 16px weight 500, 19px by 40px padding for large, 12px by 20px for compact. Optional inline arrow before the label. Use only subtle shadow.

### Outline Nav Button

Transparent fill, 1px Forest Ink border, 6px radius, Inter 14px weight 500, Forest Ink text, 8px by 16px padding.

### Highlighted Word

Highlighter Yellow rectangular wash behind individual words or short phrases in a Bricolage headline. This should read as marker pen, not a badge.

### Logo Mark

Highlighter Yellow square with hand-drawn monogram in Forest Ink, paired with an Inter 700 wordmark at 20px.

### Sticky Note Card

12px to 16px radius, 24px to 28px padding, pastel accent or Cream Paper fill, Forest Ink text, optional 1px Forest Ink border, no heavy shadow.

### Top Navigation Bar

Cream Paper rounded 16px container with 1px Pencil Gray border, logo left, centered Inter 14px links, auth buttons right, and the yellow glow around the nav.

### Tagline Badge

Small pill or rounded rectangle with tiny icon, Highlighter Yellow fill, Forest Ink text, Inter 12px to 14px weight 500. Center above the display headline.

### Backed-By Logo Strip

Small horizontal row of muted partner logos, preceded by a 12px Inter label. Keep logos gray and quiet.

### Display Hero Headline

Bricolage Grotesque 800 at 66px to 90px, Forest Ink, line-height 1, positive 0.04em to 0.05em tracking, with one or two yellow highlighted words.

### Subhead Paragraph

Inter 18px to 20px, weight 400, Forest Ink, line-height 1.5, centered, max-width around 600px.

### Reassurance Caption

Inter 12px to 14px in Whisper Gray or Pencil Gray, placed 8px to 12px below the primary button.

### Decorative Sketch Element

Hand-drawn Forest Ink line at about 30% opacity, 1px to 2px stroke, slightly imperfect. Use as background atmosphere, not a UI icon.

### Pastel Accent Button

Sticky Note Blush, Teal, or Mint fill, Forest Ink text, Inter 14px to 16px weight 500, 6px radius. Use for playful secondary paths.

## Rules

- Use Forest Ink for primary text, links, borders, and CTA buttons.
- Use Bricolage Grotesque only for display headings at 40px and above.
- Use positive tracking on Bricolage display type.
- Use Highlighter Yellow only as a word highlight, badge, or accent.
- Keep the page mostly Cream Paper and Forest Ink.
- Use pastels only as individual cards or secondary buttons.
- Use 6px radius for buttons and 12px to 16px for cards.
- Separate layers with color fills and 1px borders.
- Do not introduce a second primary brand color.
- Do not use heavy drop shadows.
- Do not use Bricolage for body, nav, buttons, or small UI.
