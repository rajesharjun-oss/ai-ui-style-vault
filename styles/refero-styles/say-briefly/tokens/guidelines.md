# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Forest Ink for primary text, links, CTA buttons, and borders.
- Set display headlines in Bricolage Grotesque 800 with 0.04em to 0.05em tracking.
- Use Highlighter Yellow as a background wash behind individual headline words.
- Use 6px radius for buttons and 12px to 16px for cards.
- Set body copy at 18px to 20px Inter with 1.5 line height.
- Separate layers with fills and 1px borders.
- Keep the page mostly Cream Paper and Forest Ink.
- Use Sticky Note Mint, Blush, and Teal as individual card or button fills.
- Use sketch elements lightly as atmosphere.

## Do Not

- Do not use Bricolage for body, nav, buttons, or anything below 40px.
- Do not introduce a second primary brand color.
- Do not apply heavy drop shadows.
- Do not use Highlighter Yellow as the primary CTA fill.
- Do not make large pastel section backgrounds.
- Do not mix sharp 0px corners into the rounded system.
- Do not make the design feel glossy, corporate, or over-polished.
