# Components

## Primary CTA Button

- Background: Forest Ink
- Text: Cream Paper
- Radius: 6px
- Padding: 19px vertical, 40px horizontal for large; 12px vertical, 20px horizontal for compact
- Font: Inter 16px weight 500
- Optional icon: inline arrow before label
- Shadow: subtle only

## Outline Nav Button

- Background: transparent
- Border: 1px solid Forest Ink
- Text: Forest Ink
- Radius: 6px
- Padding: 8px vertical, 16px horizontal
- Font: Inter 14px weight 500

## Highlighted Word

- Background: Highlighter Yellow
- Shape: rectangular wash behind text
- Text: Forest Ink
- Usage: one or two words inside a display headline
- Rule: not a CTA and not a full surface

## Logo Mark

- Mark: 40px by 40px Highlighter Yellow square
- Drawing: hand-drawn monogram in Forest Ink
- Wordmark: SayBriefly in Inter 700 at 20px, Forest Ink

## Sticky Note Card

- Background: Sticky Note Mint, Blush, Teal, Terracotta, or Cream Paper
- Text: Forest Ink
- Radius: 12px to 16px
- Padding: 24px to 28px
- Border: optional 1px Forest Ink
- Shadow: none

## Top Navigation Bar

- Background: Cream Paper
- Radius: 16px
- Border: 1px solid Pencil Gray
- Logo: left
- Links: centered Inter 14px weight 500
- Auth controls: right
- Padding: 8px to 12px vertical
- Shadow: yellow nav glow

## Tagline Badge

- Background: Highlighter Yellow
- Text: Forest Ink
- Radius: pill or rounded rectangle
- Font: Inter 12px to 14px weight 500
- Optional icon: tiny lightbulb or star
- Placement: centered above display headline

## Backed-By Logo Strip

- Label: Inter 12px weight 400
- Logos: muted gray, 16px to 20px high
- Layout: horizontal row
- Placement: below CTA group

## Display Hero Headline

- Font: Bricolage Grotesque
- Size: 66px to 90px
- Weight: 800
- Line height: 1
- Tracking: 0.04em to 0.05em
- Color: Forest Ink
- Highlights: one or two Highlighter Yellow word washes

## Subhead Paragraph

- Font: Inter
- Size: 18px to 20px
- Weight: 400
- Line height: 1.5
- Color: Forest Ink
- Max width: about 600px
- Alignment: centered

## Reassurance Caption

- Font: Inter
- Size: 12px to 14px
- Color: Whisper Gray or Pencil Gray
- Placement: 8px to 12px below primary button

## Decorative Sketch Element

- Stroke: Forest Ink at about 30% opacity
- Stroke width: 1px to 2px
- Fill: none
- Usage: background atmosphere only

## Pastel Accent Button

- Background: Sticky Note Blush, Teal, or Mint
- Text: Forest Ink
- Radius: 6px
- Font: Inter 14px to 16px weight 500
- Usage: playful secondary path
