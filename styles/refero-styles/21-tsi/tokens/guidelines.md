# Guidelines

## Do

- Use full-bleed layout instead of a max-width container.
- Keep UI chrome to black, white, and one muted gray border.
- Use Saans or a precise geometric sans everywhere.
- Apply positive tracking to uppercase labels from 12px to 20px.
- Use 70px pill radius for every interactive control.
- Treat the photograph as the only emotional color source.
- Leave large black breathing space for display headings.

## Do Not

- Do not apply Crimson Heat as a CSS fill, button, badge, or background.
- Do not use filled colored CTAs.
- Do not place body text over editorial photography.
- Do not add drop shadows, glows, blur, glass, or elevation.
- Do not introduce icon fonts, second typefaces, or decorative marks.
- Do not use intermediate headings between 20px and 47px.
- Do not round the full-bleed hero image.

## Accessibility Notes

- White on black has strong contrast, but 12px uppercase labels need adequate tracking and spacing.
- Do not rely on tiny active dots as the only indicator of state.
- Keep focus states visible with white outlines or underline treatments.
- Provide alt text for editorial photography when implementing real pages.
