# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Full-bleed by default.
- No max-width content container.
- Top hairline navigation is the only persistent chrome.
- Hero image fills 100vw and near viewport height.
- Oversized headlines sit in separate black sections.
- Text should be sparse and given its own breathing room.
- Components sit flat, as if printed on the canvas.

## Hero Recipe

1. True-black page canvas.
2. 1px white hairline across the top.
3. Minimal 12px uppercase nav and utility labels.
4. Full-bleed crimson editorial photograph.
5. No overlay, no scrim, no headline over the image.
6. Oversized Saans headline before or after the photograph in black space.

## Photography Direction

- Single human subject.
- Profile or three-quarter pose.
- Gaze downcast or off-frame.
- Off-center placement, often rule-of-thirds left.
- Warm crimson side lighting.
- Deep crushed-black shadow falloff.
- Bronzed, slightly desaturated skin tones.
- Motion in hair, fabric, or pose.
- Cinematic grain is acceptable.

## Avoid

- Multiple subjects.
- Busy compositions.
- Direct eye contact as the main hero frame.
- Clean white studio backgrounds.
- Lifestyle stock photography.
- Product mockup hero sections.
- Icon-heavy layouts.
