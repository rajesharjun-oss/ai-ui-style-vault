# Components

## Top Hairline Navigation

- 1px Paper White rule across the full viewport.
- Transparent background.
- Saans 12px, weight 380, uppercase.
- Tracking: 0.6px or 0.05em.
- Color: Paper White.
- Gap between groups: about 38px.
- Right utility cluster may include CONTACT, FR, and SOUND-style controls.

## 21|TSI Wordmark

- Render as text.
- Use Saans 12px, weight 380, uppercase.
- Draw the vertical separator as a 1px white bar around 12px tall.
- Do not add a separate logo mark.

## Ghost Pill Button

- Fill: transparent.
- Border: 1px solid Paper White.
- Radius: 70px.
- Padding: 10px 20px.
- Label: Saans 12px, weight 380, uppercase, 0.6px tracking.
- Optional leading active indicator: 4px white dot.

## Text Utility Control

- No border.
- No fill.
- Saans 12px uppercase white label.
- Hover: small opacity change only.

## Full-Bleed Editorial Hero

- Width: 100vw.
- Height: about 90vh or full viewport height.
- Image should be unrounded, unpadded, and unscreened.
- Do not add overlay text, gradient, or scrim.

## Display Headline

- Saans 79px to 245px.
- Weight: 300 for whisper-light display or 570 for stronger moments.
- Line height: 1.0 to 1.14.
- Tracking: -0.025em.
- Place in its own black section, not over image content.

## Circular Portrait Frame

- Square crop.
- Border radius: 594px.
- Use sparingly as a geometric counterpoint to full-bleed photography.

## Body Card

- Radius: 8px.
- Border: 1px solid Smoke.
- Fill: transparent or Black Void.
- Padding: 20px.
- Text: Saans 16px, weight 380, line-height 1.43.
- No shadow.
