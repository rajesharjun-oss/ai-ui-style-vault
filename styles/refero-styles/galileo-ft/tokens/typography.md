## Rules

- Use weight 100 for all headings at 42px or larger.
- Do not use 600 or 700 headline weights.
- Body copy should remain at weight 300 or above for readability.
- Use 10px uppercase labels with 0.25em tracking.
- Keep large-heading tracking slightly negative.
