# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Max width around 1200px.
- Outer gutters around 32-64px.
- Hero is a full-bleed deep navy band.
- Split hero: ultrathin heading on the left, 3D glass visual on the right.
- Product sections use 2-column text-plus-dashboard layouts.
- Section gap is around 64px.
- Floating chat trigger stays fixed at bottom center.

## Imagery

- Primary hero motif: translucent cobalt glass chain, link, or liquid sculpture.
- Product imagery: dashboard screenshots or mockups in rounded cards.
- Use dark dashboards with cobalt and teal data accents.
- Avoid lifestyle photography, people, environmental photos, and generic stock.

## Iconography

- Outlined icons with 1.5-2px strokes.
- Use lavender, cobalt, or teal sparingly.
- Do not create a multi-color icon system.

## Composition Checks

- Is the headline ultrathin?
- Is cobalt the single primary action color?
- Are card boundaries hairlines instead of shadows?
- Are cards generously rounded?
- Is the gradient limited to full-bleed banners?
