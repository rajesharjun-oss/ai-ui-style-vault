# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Plain Ultrathin weight 100 for hero and section-level headlines at 42px or larger.
- Use 48px radius for buttons, nav pills, and floating action triggers.
- Use 35px radius for cards, panels, and content containers.
- Use `#3d50fc` exclusively for primary filled actions and active states.
- Use 0.25em tracking on all 10px uppercase eyebrow labels.
- Separate dark surfaces with 1px `#292f66` hairlines.
- Use teal-to-cobalt gradients only on full-bleed banner surfaces.

## Don't

- Do not use weight 600 or 700 for headlines.
- Do not add drop shadows to cards or buttons.
- Do not use more than one vivid accent color per surface.
- Do not use solid underlines on links.
- Do not set body copy below weight 300.
- Do not use sharp corners on interactive elements.
- Do not introduce another dark canvas color outside `#03081a` and `#020626`.

## Accessibility Notes

- Weight-100 headlines can become fragile on small screens; preserve contrast and increase size/line-height thoughtfully.
- Do not rely on cobalt alone for active state; add shape, border, or aria state.
- Body text should stay at weight 300 or stronger.
- Lavender borders need sufficient thickness/contrast on dark surfaces.
