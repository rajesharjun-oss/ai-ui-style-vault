# Components

## Primary Filled Button

Pulse Cobalt fill, Pure White text, 48px radius, 22px by 14px padding, Plain 16px weight 300, no shadow, no border.

## Ghost Outline Button

Transparent background, 1px Pure White border, Pure White text, 48px radius, and the same 22px by 14px padding.

## Pill Navigation Link

Transparent pill with Pure White or Quartz Lavender 1px border, 48px radius, and 13-14px Plain text.

## Dark Card

Deep Indigo background, 1px Inkline Violet border, 35px radius, 32px padding, and no drop shadow.

## Light Card

White or Glacier White surface, 1px Mist Lilac/Dusk Iris border, 35px radius, 32px padding, and dark text.

## Tab Pill

Active tab uses Pulse Cobalt fill and white text. Inactive tab is transparent with a faint violet border. Radius is 17px.

## Eyebrow Label

10px uppercase Plain label with 0.25em tracking. Use Quartz Lavender or Signal Teal.

## Outlined Link

Quartz Lavender text with a 1px Quartz Lavender bottom border. Do not use a standard solid underline.

## Floating Chat Trigger

Glacier White bottom-center pill, 48px radius, light border, avatar or glyph at left, and 14-16px Plain text.

## Announcement Bar

Full-bleed teal-to-cobalt gradient strip with white 14px text, ghost button, and dismiss control.

## Hero Headline

Plain Ultrathin, weight 100, 56-83px, Pure White, left-aligned, paired with a 3D glass visual.

## Dashboard Product Screenshot

Dashboard image or mockup inside a 35px-radius bordered card with cobalt/teal data accents.

## Navigation Menu

Right-aligned vertical list of 14-16px thin Plain links. Login can be a ghost pill.

## Brand Logo Lockup

Minimal white wordmark/glyph in top-left position with slim Plain text.
