# Colors

## Brand

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pulse Cobalt | `#3d50fc` | `--color-pulse-cobalt` | Primary filled actions, active states, key icons, and brand signal. |

## Accents

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Signal Teal | `#05e0e0` | `--color-signal-teal` | Supporting icon, tertiary link, and data-viz signal. |
| Cyan Teal | `#05cee0` | `--color-cyan-teal` | Decorative gradient origin and atmospheric accent. |
| Gradient Teal Blue | `linear-gradient(90deg, #05a1c9 0%, #3d50fc 100%)` | `--gradient-teal-blue` | Full-bleed brand banner and announcement surfaces. |

## Neutrals

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Void Navy | `#03081a` | `--color-void-navy` | Page canvas and primary dark surface. |
| Deep Indigo | `#020626` | `--color-deep-indigo` | Dark cards, panels, and structural fills. |
| Inkline Violet | `#292f66` | `--color-inkline-violet` | Hairline borders, dividers, and structural strokes. |
| Dusk Iris | `#4d5499` | `--color-dusk-iris` | Muted borders, disabled states, and low-priority outlines. |
| Mist Lilac | `#7a83cc` | `--color-mist-lilac` | Tertiary text and low-emphasis supporting UI. |
| Quartz Lavender | `#aab1f2` | `--color-quartz-lavender` | Secondary text, outlined links, and inactive navigation. |
| Glacier White | `#f5f6ff` | `--color-glacier-white` | Light card surface and high-contrast text. |
| Pure White | `#ffffff` | `--color-pure-white` | Primary text and high-contrast button borders. |

## Usage

- Cobalt is the only primary filled action color.
- Teal supports icons, data, and gradients, but should not compete with cobalt on the same surface.
- Dark surfaces should be only Void Navy and Deep Indigo.
- Use violet/lavender hairlines for structure instead of shadows.
