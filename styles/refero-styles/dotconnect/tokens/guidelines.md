# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Ember for exactly one high-intent CTA per viewport.
- Use charcoal arrow-pill buttons for normal inline movement.
- Use Signal Blue only for outlined secondary actions.
- Enable `dlig`, `ss02`, and `ss08` OpenType features.
- Use 20px radius for cards and images.
- Use 24px radius for CTA buttons.
- Use 44px to 48px for pill controls.
- Use 96px vertical spacing between sections.
- Use 1px Mist Border lines instead of shadows.
- Keep body paragraphs left aligned.
- Use abstract 3D renders as sparse interludes.

## Do Not

- Do not use shadows or drop-shadow effects.
- Do not place multiple Ember CTAs in one viewport.
- Do not use Signal Blue as a filled background.
- Do not add decorative gradients, glows, or blur.
- Do not right-align body paragraphs.
- Do not set body text below 16px or above 21px.
- Do not use photography, people, or lifestyle imagery.
- Do not use icon strokes below 1.5px or above 2px.
- Do not turn the hero into an image-led composition.
