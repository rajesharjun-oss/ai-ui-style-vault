# AI Implementation Prompt

Build a Compound-inspired wealth-product interface.

Use an almost fully achromatic palette: Ink `#171717`, Paper `#ffffff`, Graphite Hairline `#e5e7eb`, Vellum `#f3f3f3`, Slate `#6f6f6f`, Pewter `#5e5e5e`, Ash `#a0a0a0`, Carbon `#222222`, Stone `#c7c7c7`, and Cream Notice `#ffe9bf`. The cream color may appear only in a top announcement bar.

Use one typeface only: Monument Grotesk or a close fallback such as Inter, General Sans, or Sohne. Use weight 400 everywhere. Do not use bold, italic, light, serif, mono, or a second typeface. Make hierarchy through scale: 14px body, 18px subheads, 36px small headings, 60px to 72px display headlines.

Core layout:

- Full-width cream announcement bar at the top.
- Clean top nav with wordmark left, centered links, sign-in link, and a dark pill action on the right.
- Centered hero headline at 60px to 72px with a gray translucent 3D ribbon behind it.
- Centered product preview card below the hero with 20px radius, pale border, and soft four-layer shadow.
- Four-column service card row.
- Three-column publication logo row.
- Section dividers with centered labels.
- 80px vertical gaps between sections.

Core components:

- Filled pill button with Ink fill, white 14px text, and 9999px radius.
- Ghost text link with underlined Ink text.
- Product preview card with Paper surface, Graphite Hairline border, 20px radius, and pale shadow.
- Vellum service card with 20px radius and no border.
- Vellum publication logo card with centered grayscale logo.
- Circular icon containers at 32px to 40px.
- Stats metric row with Slate label and large Ink value.

Avoid colored UI accents, heavy shadows, extra font weights, sharp corners, dense layouts, photography, lifestyle imagery, and background colors beyond Paper, Vellum, and Cream Notice.
