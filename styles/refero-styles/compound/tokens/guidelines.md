# Guidelines

## Do

- Use one typeface at one weight.
- Use full-radius buttons, tags, and icon containers.
- Use 20px radius for cards.
- Use Graphite Hairline for all borders and dividers.
- Keep section spacing generous.
- Keep the palette achromatic except the cream announcement bar.
- Use four-layer soft shadows with very low opacity.
- Let large type and whitespace carry hierarchy.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce new chromatic accents.
- Do not introduce bold, italic, light, or secondary fonts.
- Do not use sharp corners.
- Do not use heavy shadows.
- Do not use strong colored surfaces.
- Do not reduce section spacing below 64px.
- Do not add decorative colored strokes to headings.
- Do not use photography or lifestyle imagery.
