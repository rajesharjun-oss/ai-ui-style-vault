# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a centered 1200px max-width page rail.
- Put the announcement bar flush at the top.
- Use a clean horizontal nav beneath it.
- Center the hero display headline.
- Place a translucent gray abstract 3D ribbon behind the hero text.
- Put the product preview card centered below the hero.
- Use a 4-column service card row.
- Use a 3-column publication logo row.
- Separate sections with 80px gaps or labeled hairline dividers.
- Avoid sidebars and asymmetric overlapping compositions.

## Imagery

Use:

- Abstract translucent gray 3D ribbon or helix forms.
- Product dashboard screenshot inside a preview card.
- Grayscale publication logos.

Avoid:

- Photography.
- Lifestyle imagery.
- Human figures.
- Colorful illustration.
- Dense screenshot collages.
