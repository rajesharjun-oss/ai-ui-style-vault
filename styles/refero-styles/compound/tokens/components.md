# Components

## Announcement Bar

Full-bleed Cream Notice strip with centered Ink text and a small filled pill button. Sits flush at the top.

## Top Navigation

White navigation with wordmark left, centered links, sign-in text link, and filled pill action on the right.

## Filled Pill Button

Ink fill, white 14px text, full radius, compact padding, no border, and no shadow.

## Ghost Text Link

Ink text, no background, no border, and a 1px underline.

## Product Preview Card

Paper surface, 20px radius, Graphite Hairline border, and pale four-layer shadow. Contains product dashboard UI.

## Feature Service Card

Vellum or transparent surface, 20px radius, 24px padding, Ink label, and Slate description.

## Publication Logo Card

Vellum surface, 20px radius, no border, no shadow, and centered grayscale publication logo.

## Section Divider

1px Graphite Hairline rule with centered Slate label interrupting the line.

## Hero Display Headline

Centered 60px to 72px headline, weight 400, Ink color, and tight line-height.

## Circular Icon Container

32px to 40px full-radius container with Vellum or Paper fill and monochrome icon.

## Stats Metric Row

Small Slate label above or beside a larger Ink value.
