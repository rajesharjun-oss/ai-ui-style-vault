## Use This Style When

Use Compound when an interface should feel like a quiet wealth-management journal: achromatic, spacious, typographic, and high-trust. It fits wealth tools, private banking, investment dashboards, advisory products, personal finance portals, and editorial financial landing pages where restraint should feel premium.

## Quick DNA

- White canvas with almost no chromatic color.
- Monument Grotesk-style typography at one weight only.
- Hierarchy comes from large type jumps, not font weight.
- Cream notice bar is the only warm accent.
- Pill buttons and icon containers use full radius.
- Cards use 20px radius and pale hairline borders.
- Product preview card is the only strongly object-like surface.
- Shadows are soft, pale, multi-layer, and under 0.10 opacity.
