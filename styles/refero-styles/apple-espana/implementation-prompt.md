# Implementation Prompt

Build a premium product-retail page inspired by Apple Espana.

Use `#ffffff`, `#fbfbfd`, and `#f5f5f7` as the core surfaces. Keep the page bright and product-led. Use `#1d1d1f` for main headings, `#424245` for strong body text, `#6e6e73` for captions and secondary copy, and `#d2d2d7` for hairline borders.

Use `#0071e3` for the main filled buy button and `#0066cc` for inline links. Keep blue sparse and commerce-oriented. Do not create secondary filled accent colors.

Use SF Pro Display for hero and section headings, and SF Pro Text for nav, body, captions, labels, and buttons. Use system Apple fonts as fallback. Set display headlines around 56px to 80px, body around 17px, subheads around 21px, and nav around 12px to 14px.

Build sections as centered product stages: eyebrow, headline, subhead, CTA row, and large product image. Use 28px to 32px rounded product tiles with white or frost backgrounds. Use pill buy buttons with very large radius, around `980px`. Keep product imagery sharp, full, bright, and inspection-friendly.

Avoid decorative gradients, heavy card shadows, unrelated colors, body text over images, custom fonts, dark sections unless the product campaign specifically requires one, and conventional SaaS feature-card clutter.
