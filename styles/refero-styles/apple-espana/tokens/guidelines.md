# Guidelines

## Do

- Use white and frost surfaces.
- Use SF Pro or Apple system fonts.
- Use `#0071e3` for buy actions and important commerce CTAs.
- Use large centered product imagery.
- Use generous negative space.
- Use pill controls for buy actions.
- Use `1px #d2d2d7` hairline borders.
- Keep legal and footer content dense but muted.
- Keep product hierarchy strict and obvious.

## Don't

- Do not add decorative gradients unless they are tied to product lighting.
- Do not use custom display fonts.
- Do not add heavy shadows around product tiles.
- Do not use non-blue CTA colors.
- Do not use card radius above `32px` except for pills.
- Do not place body text over product images.
- Do not introduce multiple chromatic accents.
- Do not use dark mode unless the product launch page specifically calls for it.
