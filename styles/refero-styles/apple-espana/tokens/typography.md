# Typography Tokens

## Fonts

| Font | Token | Role | Substitute |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SF Pro Display | `--font-sf-pro-display` | Hero headlines, product names, large display hierarchy. | -apple-system, Inter |
| SF Pro Text | `--font-sf-pro-text` | Body, nav, captions, buttons, product UI. | -apple-system, Inter |
| SF Pro Icons | `--font-sf-pro-icons` | Inline Apple-style icon glyphs and chevrons. | system UI icons |

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Line Height | Weight | Token |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| caption | `12px` | `1.33` | `400` | `--text-caption` |
| body-sm | `14px` | `1.43` | `400` | `--text-body-sm` |
| body | `17px` | `1.47` | `400` | `--text-body` |
| body-lg | `21px` | `1.38` | `400` | `--text-body-lg` |
| subheading | `24px` | `1.17` | `600` | `--text-subheading` |
| heading-sm | `28px` | `1.14` | `600` | `--text-heading-sm` |
| heading | `40px` | `1.1` | `600` | `--text-heading` |
| heading-lg | `56px` | `1.07` | `600` | `--text-heading-lg` |
| display | `80px` | `1.05` | `600` | `--text-display` |

## Font Rules

- Use SF Pro Display for product and hero headings.
- Use SF Pro Text for body, nav, and CTAs.
- Keep text tracking normal or subtly tight.
- Avoid serif, monospace, or decorative fonts.
- Do not over-bold body text.
