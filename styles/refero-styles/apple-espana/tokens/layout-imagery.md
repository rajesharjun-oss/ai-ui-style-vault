# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Grammar

Apple Espana uses a retail-theater model: each section stages one product promise with very few competing elements. The page alternates full-width product stages with rounded product tiles, comparison cards, and accessory panels.

## Common Layouts

- Centered hero with eyebrow, product name, large headline, subhead, CTA row, and dominant product image.
- Frosted top nav with compact links and bag/search icons.
- Product tile grids on frost backgrounds.
- Comparison cards with product image, specs, and small buy pill.
- Accessory cards with large product image and minimal text.
- Ribbon banners for trade-in, financing, delivery, and support messages.
- Dense legal footer with muted links and notes.

## Imagery

- Use real product renders or inspection-friendly product photography.
- Keep products bright, crisp, and unoccluded.
- Let product lighting create depth.
- Avoid lifestyle stock imagery unless the product story requires it.
- Avoid decorative icons as primary imagery.

## Motion

- Use subtle transitions around `200ms`.
- Product transitions can be smooth and cinematic but should remain restrained.
- Avoid bouncy UI motion and heavy parallax.
