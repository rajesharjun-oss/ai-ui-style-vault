# Implementation Prompt

Create an original light healthtech landing page or care-platform interface inspired by the Ease Health Refero style.

Use a warm cream canvas `#fffefc`, forest-green ink `#0f3e17`, soft botanical panels `#e1f4df`, `#cfe7d3`, and `#b1dbb8`, plus a cool product showcase panel `#b6ced5`. Do not add shadows. Do not introduce additional accent colors.

Use a weight-300 display serif such as Faire Octave, Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, or Canela for hero and section headings. Use a humanist sans such as Suisse Intl, Inter, Soehne, or Helvetica Neue for body copy, buttons, navigation, labels, and product UI.

Build these elements:

- Minimal nav with left logo, centered links, and a forest-green "Book a Demo" pill.
- Two-column hero: left botanical panel with serif headline, sans body copy, and filled CTA; right slate panel with three cream product cards.
- Product preview cards for CRM, EHR, and RCM style modules.
- Pill badges with cream fill and forest-green text.
- Sage and keylime feature cards with 14px radius and generous 28px to 42px padding.
- FAQ rows where the question is a 40px weight-300 serif and the answer is 14px sans.
- Monochrome investor or partner logo strip on the cream canvas.

Do not copy Ease Health brand assets, exact screenshots, page copy, or proprietary layouts. Generate a new healthcare/product screen that borrows only the mood, token relationships, and component language.
