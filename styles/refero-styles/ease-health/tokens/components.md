# Components

## Primary Forest Button

- Role: Filled CTA for demos, explores, and form submits.
- Background: `#0f3e17`
- Text: `#fffefc`
- Radius: `14px`
- Padding: `14px 21px`
- Font: Suisse Intl `14px`, weight `400`
- Icon: optional trailing white arrow
- Hover: `#0c2f10`

## Large Forest Button

- Role: Hero-scale CTA.
- Background: `#0f3e17`
- Text: `#fffefc`
- Radius: `14px`
- Padding: `42px`
- Use sparingly for maximum-weight hero action.

## Pill Badge / Tag

- Role: Category labels, module tags, status indicators.
- Background: `#fffefc`
- Text: `#0f3e17`
- Radius: `999px`
- Padding: `9px 14px`
- Font: Suisse Intl `14px`, weight `400`

## Sage Feature Card

- Role: Heavier-tinted content panel.
- Background: `#b1dbb8`
- Radius: `14px`
- Padding: `42px`
- Shadow: none.
- Good for feature highlights and illustration/product content.

## Keylime Soft Card

- Role: Light secondary feature panel.
- Background: `#e1f4df`
- Radius: `14px`
- Padding: `28px`
- Shadow: none.

## Slate Product Panel

- Role: Cool-toned container for product screenshots and demos.
- Background: `#b6ced5`
- Radius: `14px`
- Padding: `42px`
- Shadow: none.
- This should be the exclusive home for product screenshots.

## Product Preview Card

- Role: Inner product mockup card for CRM, EHR, RCM, patient views, or revenue graphs.
- Background: `#fffefc`
- Radius: `14px`
- Padding: `28px`
- Border: none.
- Shadow: none.
- Place inside Slate Product Panel.

## Navigation Bar

- Background: transparent on cream canvas.
- Text: `#222222`
- Font: Suisse Intl `14px`, weight `400`
- Interactive radius: `7px`
- Right CTA: Forest Ink filled pill.
- No sticky behavior or mega menu by default.

## Eyebrow Label

- Role: Section category marker.
- Font: Suisse Intl `11px`, weight `600`
- Letter spacing: `0.08em`
- Transform: uppercase
- Color: `#0f3e17`

## FAQ Row

- Layout: two-column grid.
- Left column: Faire Octave `40px`, weight `300`, Forest Ink.
- Right column: Suisse Intl `14px`, weight `400`, Charcoal.
- Divider: `1px solid #efeeeb`

## Investor Logo Strip

- Role: social proof row.
- Treatment: monochrome logos in Charcoal on Cream Paper.
- Padding: `42px` or more vertically.
- No enclosing border or decorative background.
