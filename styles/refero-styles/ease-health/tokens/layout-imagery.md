# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Patterns

- Max-width 1200px centered container.
- Vertical section gaps of 64px to 96px.
- Two-column hero: editorial message panel plus slate product showcase panel.
- Sections alternate between cream canvas and tinted botanical panels.
- FAQ uses a two-column text grid.
- Investor or partner logo strip is full-width on cream.
- Navigation is minimal: left logo, centered links, right CTA.

## Imagery

- Use product-native imagery: CRM cards, EHR patient views, RCM charts, intake forms, care-plan modules, and patient/provider workflow cards.
- Product preview cards should be crisp cream/white cards inside Slate Hush panels.
- Avoid lifestyle photography and generic stock medical imagery.
- Iconography should be minimal, stroke-based, and rendered in Forest Ink.
- Illustrations can use sage/keylime/mint only.

## Responsive Notes

- On mobile, stack the two-column hero with the message panel above the slate product panel.
- Keep 14px radii and generous padding even when stacked.
- Convert the three product preview cards into a horizontal scroll row or vertical stack.
- Keep FAQ rows readable by stacking question above answer below tablet widths.
