# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Forest Ink for every CTA, heading, and primary link.
- Set all display headings in a weight-300 serif with tight tracking.
- Pair serif questions with sans-serif answers in FAQ and feature blocks.
- Layer surfaces in this order: cream, keylime, mint, sage, slate.
- Use 14px radius on cards and buttons.
- Use 999px radius on badges and tags.
- Apply 0.08em tracking only to uppercase 11px/600 eyebrow labels.
- Keep the Slate Hush panel as the exclusive home for product screenshots.

## Don't

- Do not add box shadows to cards.
- Do not use Forest Ink as a background tint.
- Do not mix multiple heading weights.
- Do not place colored borders on cards.
- Do not use more than one serif at a time.
- Do not introduce new accent hues.
- Do not use padding under 21px on cards.
- Do not use sage, keylime, or mint tones for text.
