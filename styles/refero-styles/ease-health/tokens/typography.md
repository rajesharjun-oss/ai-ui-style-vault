# Typography

## Families

| Family | Token | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Faire Octave | `--font-faire-octave` | Display serif for hero copy, section headings, and FAQ questions |
| Suisse Intl | `--font-suisse-intl` | Body copy, nav, UI labels, buttons, captions, and product chrome |

## Substitutes

- Display substitutes: Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, Canela, or a light editorial serif.
- Body substitutes: Inter, Soehne, Helvetica Neue, or another humanist sans.

## Weights

| Family | Weights | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Faire Octave | `300` | Use weight 300 for all display headings. Never bold the serif. |
| Suisse Intl | `300`, `400`, `600` | Use 300 for quiet feature copy, 400 for body/buttons, 600 only for eyebrow labels. |

## Eyebrow Labels

- Font: Suisse Intl
- Size: `11px`
- Weight: `600`
- Transform: uppercase
- Letter spacing: `0.08em`
- Color: `#0f3e17`
