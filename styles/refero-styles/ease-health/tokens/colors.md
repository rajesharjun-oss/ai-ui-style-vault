# Colors

## Brand

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Forest Ink | `#0f3e17` | `--color-forest-ink` | Primary action buttons, headings, links, and icon strokes |
| Sage Mist | `#b1dbb8` | `--color-sage-mist` | Mid-tone card backgrounds, feature highlights, and decorative washes |
| Keylime Wash | `#e1f4df` | `--color-keylime-wash` | Hero panel background and light card surfaces |
| Mint Veil | `#cfe7d3` | `--color-mint-veil` | Accent card surfaces and soft section dividers |

## Accent

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Slate Hush | `#b6ced5` | `--color-slate-hush` | Cool product showcase panel and non-green counterbalance |

## Neutrals

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Forest Shadow | `#0c2f10` | `--color-forest-shadow` | Hover state and tiny decorative accents, not primary CTA |
| Charcoal | `#222222` | `--color-charcoal` | Navigation text, body copy, and minor UI strokes |
| Border Mist | `#efeeeb` | `--color-border-mist` | Hairline dividers and subtle borders |
| Cream Paper | `#fffefc` | `--color-cream-paper` | Page background, badge backgrounds, button text, product card fill |

## Surface Order

| Level | Name | Value | Purpose |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 0 | Cream Paper | `#fffefc` | Base page background |
| 1 | Keylime Wash | `#e1f4df` | Primary tinted panel and light feature block |
| 2 | Mint Veil | `#cfe7d3` | Mid-tone section surface |
| 3 | Sage Mist | `#b1dbb8` | Heavier tinted panel and feature highlight |
| 4 | Slate Hush | `#b6ced5` | Cool product showcase panel |

## Rules

- Forest Ink is the only saturated dark.
- Do not introduce new accent hues.
- Do not use sage, keylime, or mint colors for text.
- Do not place colored borders on cards.
- Depth comes from fill contrast, not shadows.
