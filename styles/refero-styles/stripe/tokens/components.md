# Components

## Indigo Primary Button

Indigo Action fill, Paper White text, 4px radius, 8px 16px padding, sohne-var 15px/500. Include a small right arrow for forward motion.

## Navy Secondary Button

Deep Navy fill, Paper White text, 4px radius, same size as the primary. Use for a second high-confidence action.

## Ghost Text Link

Transparent, Deep Navy or Indigo text, no border, 15px/500, small arrow icon. Use for inline links and card actions.

## Top Navigation

Paper White or transparent over Cloud Ledger. Logo left, compact center links, and paired right CTAs. Use 14px to 15px sohne-var.

## Hero Ledger Block

Two-column hero with display heading, short body copy, paired CTA buttons, and right-side product/data mockup.

## Product Mockup Card

Paper White surface, 4px radius, 1px Border Mist border, no shadow, dense internal chart/table/code content.

## Data Table

Rows on Paper White or transparent surface, 1px Border Mist dividers, 13px to 14px text, Blue Gray metadata, Deep Navy values, and tiny status chips.

## Code Panel

Deep Navy or Paper White panel, 4px radius, muted line numbers, and indigo/cyan syntax accents.

## Metric Tile

Paper White card, 4px radius, 1px Border Mist border, 13px caption, 31px value, optional Green Ledger delta.

## Globe Or Map Panel

Cool blue-gray map/grid visualization with hairline grid and tiny Indigo or Cyan nodes.

## Payment Method Strip

Horizontal icon row with Paper White background, Border Mist separators, Deep Navy labels, and Blue Gray captions.

## Footer Infrastructure Grid

Deep Navy footer or Cloud Ledger sitemap with compact link columns, clear headings, and muted link text.
