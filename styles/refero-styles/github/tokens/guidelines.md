# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Terminal Green only for the single primary CTA.
- Use Mona Sans 16px/400/1.5 for body text.
- Use 64px display type at weight 425 with tight tracking.
- Use glass cards with rgba white fills and 1px borders.
- Use 24px radius for major cards.
- Use 6px radius for filled and outlined action buttons.
- Use 60px radius for nav tags and filter pills.
- Use blurred violet radial halos behind hero content.
- Use Mona Sans Mono 12px uppercase for technical micro-labels.

## Don't

- Do not use blue as the main CTA color.
- Do not apply Terminal Green to tags, secondary actions, or decorations.
- Do not use 4px or 8px radius for feature cards.
- Do not use drop shadows for glass cards.
- Do not use solid purple or violet fills for buttons or large surfaces.
- Do not use Cobalt as the general link color.
- Do not replace Mona Sans display weight 425 with conventional bold.
- Do not use lifestyle photography.

## Accessibility Notes

- Ensure glass cards maintain readable contrast over gradient backgrounds.
- Keep focus states visible on green, ghost, and pill controls.
- Do not use color alone to communicate selected or disabled state.
- Product screenshots should include useful alt text when they explain functionality.
