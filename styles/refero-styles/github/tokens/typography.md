# Typography

## Families

| Token | Family | Fallback | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--font-mona-sans` | Mona Sans | Inter | Display, headings, body, nav, buttons |
| `--font-mona-sans-mono` | Mona Sans Mono | JetBrains Mono | Code labels, tags, micro-copy |

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Letter Spacing | Token |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caption | 12px | 500 | 1.5 | 0.18px | `--text-caption` |
| Body small | 14px | 400 | 1.5 | normal | `--text-body-sm` |
| Body | 16px | 400 | 1.5 | normal | `--text-body` |
| Body large | 18px | 400 | 1.5 | 0.18px | `--text-body-lg` |
| Subheading | 22px | 480 | 1.4 | normal | `--text-subheading` |
| Heading small | 24px | 600 | 1.5 | normal | `--text-heading-sm` |
| Heading | 40px | 460 | 1.2 | normal | `--text-heading` |
| Heading large | 48px | 800 | 1.0 | normal | `--text-heading-lg` |
| Display | 64px | 425 | 1.08 | -2.24px | `--text-display` |

## Rules

- Use Mona Sans for almost everything.
- Use display weight 425, not ordinary bold.
- Keep body copy at 16px/400/1.5.
- Use Mona Sans Mono for 12px uppercase technical labels.
- Use 0.015em tracking for mono micro-labels.
- Do not overuse heavy 800 weight; reserve it for specific headline moments.
