## Color Behavior

Terminal Green is the main action beacon. Violet and purple are atmospheric. Sky is for links and icons. Most surfaces remain near-black, glassy, and grayscale.

## Pairings

- Canvas: Deep Void with Snow and Pearl text.
- Glass cards: rgba white fill over Deep Void with Slate Edge or low-opacity white borders.
- Primary CTA: Snow text on Terminal Green.
- Secondary action: Sky text on dark translucent fill.
- Featured card: Ultraviolet border on glass surface.

## Avoid

- Blue primary CTAs.
- Green tags or decoration.
- Solid purple buttons.
- Opaque card blocks.
- Extra semantic colors.
