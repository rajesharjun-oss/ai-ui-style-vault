# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Parchment as the base canvas.
- Use Ink for primary text.
- Use Graphite for secondary labels and inactive nav.
- Reserve Deep Teal for active states, badges, selected chips, and search glow.
- Use 1px Warm Mist borders.
- Keep type weights between 400 and 500.
- Keep the main column narrow at 900px.
- Keep the design flat and compact.

## Don't

- Do not introduce new accent colors.
- Do not use bold 600+ weights.
- Do not add drop shadows beyond the subtle card shadow.
- Do not use pure white as a broad surface.
- Do not apply teal to body text.
- Do not exceed the narrow content column.
- Do not add gradients, blurs, decorative imagery, photography, or screenshots.

## Accessibility Notes

- Check white text on Deep Teal for active nav and badge contrast.
- Use visible focus outlines on search input and buttons.
- Do not rely on teal alone for state; include selected text state when necessary.
