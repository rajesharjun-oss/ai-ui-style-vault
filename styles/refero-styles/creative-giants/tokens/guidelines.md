# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Bone White as the page background everywhere.
- Keep all major headlines at 300 weight.
- Use 64px to 84px type for poster statements when space allows.
- Apply 1440px radius to buttons, menu controls, tags, and pills.
- Keep cards and images sharp with 0px radius.
- Let full-bleed photographs provide the visual weight.
- Use chromatic accents for borders, small fills, single-card highlights, tags, and badges.
- Keep section gaps around 48px to 64px.

## Do Not

- Do not bold headlines.
- Do not use pure white.
- Do not add shadows for elevation.
- Do not round card or image corners.
- Do not use color-filled primary CTAs other than black.
- Do not let display headlines become paragraphs.
- Do not use default browser link blue.

## AI Implementation Notes

- If the page feels empty, increase scale or add stronger photography before adding components.
- If the page feels too plain, add one chromatic accent card or border, not a colorful section.
- If the layout feels too corporate, remove chrome and let the headline and photo do more.
- If the typography feels weak, check that headings are light, large, and tightly tracked.
