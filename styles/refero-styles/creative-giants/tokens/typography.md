# Typography

## Family

- Primary: Switzer.
- Fallback: Inter, Sohne, Arial, sans-serif.
- Use one family across the system.

## Weights

- 300: display, hero, section headlines, oversized editorial text.
- 400: metadata, body text, captions, buttons, controls.
- Avoid 500 and above for anything that reads as a headline.

| Heading | 54px | 300 | 1 | -0.023em |

## Usage

- Keep display type enormous and thin.
- Tighten letter spacing more aggressively as type grows.
- Do not exceed two lines for poster headlines.
- Use all-caps 12px metadata for location, category, and section labels.
- Let the type look carved into the cream canvas rather than loudly printed.
