# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a warm Bone White canvas with minimal chrome.
- Treat the hero as a poster composition.
- Put one giant statement near the top of the page.
- Use full-bleed or full-width imagery with sharp corners.
- Build editorial grids without visible card containers.
- Separate sections with whitespace, not heavy dividers.
- Use hairline dividers only where structure is genuinely needed.

## Section Patterns

- Header: circular mark, small studio descriptor, black Menu pill.
- Poster hero: 64px to 84px headline, no card, no decorative background.
- Image band: full-width photograph, small metadata, lower-left image title.
- Projects grid: image-first cards, short copy, occasional chromatic accent.
- Feature card: one pastel or navy surface in a row of otherwise quiet cards.
- Footer: same cream canvas, hairline divider, compact link columns.

## Imagery

- Use editorial photography, public intervention documentation, gallery installations, event stills, studio process images, and contact-sheet crops.
- Keep image corners sharp.
- Avoid generic stock imagery.
- Avoid rounded previews, image shadows, and decorative filters.
- Let the image crop feel intentional and art-directed.
