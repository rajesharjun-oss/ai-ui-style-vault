# Components

## Pill Menu Button

- Background: `#000000`.
- Text: `#fffef7`.
- Radius: 1440px.
- Padding: 12px vertical, 24px horizontal.
- Font: 16px, 400 weight.
- Border: none.
- Shadow: none.

## Header Lockup

- Background: none.
- Canvas: Bone White.
- Eyebrow: 12px uppercase, 400 weight, Ink Black.
- Mark: 32px circular logo.
- Layout: mark and copy on one side, Menu pill on the opposite side.

## Display Poster Headline

- Size: 84px.
- Weight: 300.
- Line height: 1.
- Tracking: -0.04em.
- Color: Ink Black.
- Width: nearly full viewport width.
- Limit: one or two lines.

## Hero Image Band

- Image: full-width or full-bleed.
- Radius: 0px.
- Shadow: none.
- Metadata: 12px uppercase near the lower-left edge.
- Image headline: 34px, weight 300.

## Section Title Block

- Eyebrow: 12px uppercase, Graphite.
- Statement: 34px to 54px, weight 300, Ink Black.
- Separator: whitespace, not panels.

## Project Or News Card

- Grid: usually 3 columns.
- Image: square or 4:3, sharp corners.
- Card radius: 0px.
- Shadow: none.
- Border: none.
- Title: 18px to 20px, weight 300.
- Body: 14px, Graphite, short clamp.
- Optional accent: 1px Magenta Bloom or Forest Teal border on image edge.

## Chromatic Accent Card

- Background: Candy Pink, Mint Wash, Powder Blue, or Navy Ink.
- Text: Ink Black on pastels, Bone White on Navy Ink.
- Radius: 0px.
- Padding: 24px to 32px.
- Use no more than one per row.

## Carousel Arrow Control

- Size: 32px circle.
- Border: 1px Ash.
- Fill: transparent.
- Icon: 16px Ink Black chevron.
- Hover: darken border only.

## Meta Eyebrow

- Font: 12px Switzer.
- Weight: 400.
- Transform: uppercase.
- Color: Graphite.
- Use as a single line.

## Full-Bleed Footer

- Background: Bone White.
- Divider: 1px hairline.
- Links: 14px, 400 weight, Ink Black.
- Row gap: 8px.
- Dark gradient variant only when a footer needs a rare dark band.

## Tag Or Category Pill

- Padding: 8px vertical, 12px horizontal.
- Radius: 1440px.
- Font: 12px uppercase, 400 weight.
- Default: transparent fill, Ink Black text, 1px Ash border.
- Accent: Signal Yellow fill, Ink Black text, no border.
