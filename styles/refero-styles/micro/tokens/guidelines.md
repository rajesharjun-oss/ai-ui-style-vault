# Guidelines

## Do

- Use the blue-to-teal gradient only on the hero.
- Use perfectlyNineties or a chunky editorial substitute for hero and section display headings.
- Use haffer or a neutral sans for body, nav, product UI, buttons, and labels.
- Use 8px radius for buttons, inputs, and nav.
- Use 14px for product rows and 18px for feature blocks and graph nodes.
- Keep product surfaces `#ffffff` with 1px borders and subtle shadows.
- Use pastel washes for marketing feature blocks while keeping the canvas `#f5f5f5`.
- Color icons categorically with Teal Pulse, Coral Marker, Amber Marker, Orchid Marker, and Azure Action.

## Don't

- Do not apply the gradient sky outside the top hero.
- Do not use the display face for body text, buttons, nav, or product UI.
- Do not skip the 1px border on product cards.
- Do not use 4px radius on feature cards or marketing blocks.
- Do not introduce chromatic accents beyond the named marker palette.
- Do not center body copy inside product UI.
- Do not use pure black `#000000` for text.
