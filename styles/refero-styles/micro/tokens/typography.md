# Typography

## Families

| Family | Token | Role |
|---|---|---|
| perfectlyNineties | `--font-perfectlynineties` | Display and editorial headlines for hero, section H1/H2, and large marketing statements |
| haffer | `--font-haffer` | Primary workhorse for body, UI labels, buttons, nav, product chrome, and card content |
| ui-monospace | `--font-ui-monospace` | Timestamps, email addresses, technical metadata, and AI prompt placeholder text |

## Substitute Fonts

- Display substitute: Druk Wide, Climate Crisis, Recoleta, or Playfair Display 900.
- Primary substitute: Inter, Sohne, General Sans, or Geist Sans.
- Code substitute: JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono, or a system monospace.

## Rules

- Use the display face only for large marketing and editorial statements.
- Use haffer or a neutral sans for everything people must read or operate.
- Use monospace only for data, metadata, and prompts.
- The open tracking on display headlines is part of the signature.
