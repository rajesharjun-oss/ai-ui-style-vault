# Motion And Voice

## Motion

- Default duration: `200ms`.
- Default easing: `ease`.
- Use motion mostly for hover states, nav transitions, and small emphasis.
- Reserve spring or bounce motion for character marks and hero decorations.
- Avoid slow, cinematic, or heavy motion.

## Decorative Marks

- Character marks are circular, `40px` to `48px`.
- Use white fills with `2px` accent borders.
- Accent border colors can rotate through blue, coral, yellow, and sky.
- Marks should feel handcrafted and playful.
- They should not carry required information.

## Voice

- Warm, capable, and product-led.
- Editorial enough to feel thoughtful, but still practical.
- Color and illustration make the interface feel alive without overwhelming the work surface.
