# Component Tokens

## Primary CTA Button

- **Role:** Main conversion action.
- **Background:** `#0075de`.
- **Text:** `#ffffff`, `14px` NotionInter, weight `500`.
- **Radius:** `8px`.
- **Padding:** `6px 15px`.
- **Rule:** Use only one filled primary action per screen.

## Ghost CTA Button

- **Role:** Secondary action.
- **Background:** `#e6f3fe`.
- **Text:** `#0075de`, `14px`, weight `500`.
- **Radius:** `8px`.
- **Padding:** `6px 15px`.

## Ghost Text Button

- **Role:** Tertiary action.
- **Background:** transparent.
- **Text:** `rgba(0,0,0,0.95)`.
- **Radius:** `8px`.
- **Padding:** `6px 15px`.

## Outlined Text Button

- **Role:** Compact mid-priority action.
- **Background:** transparent.
- **Text:** `rgba(0,0,0,0.9)`.
- **Border:** `1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.9)`.
- **Radius:** `4px`.
- **Padding:** `5px 10px`.

## Muted Nav Link

- **Text:** `rgba(0,0,0,0.54)`, `14px` NotionInter, weight `500`.
- **Padding:** `12px 16px`.
- **Radius:** `8px`.
- **Hover:** Darken text toward full black.
- **Avoid:** Underlines and filled active states.

## Pill Tag

- **Role:** Category, status, or inline highlight.
- **Background:** Accent fill.
- **Text:** Black or white depending on contrast.
- **Radius:** `9999px`.
- **Padding:** `4px 12px`.

## Hero Highlight Pill

- **Role:** Inline emphasis around a verb or key phrase.
- **Background:** Accent color such as peach, marigold, or coral.
- **Text:** `#000000`.
- **Radius:** `9999px`.
- **Padding:** `8px 24px`.
- **Placement:** Inline within large display headline.

## White Feature Card

- **Background:** `#ffffff`.
- **Border:** `1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`.
- **Radius:** `12px`.
- **Padding:** `24px`.
- **Shadow:** none.

## Accent Feature Card

- **Background:** One accent color such as `#ffb110`, `#f64932`, `#62aef0`, `#e6f3fe`, or `#02093a`.
- **Radius:** `12px`.
- **Padding:** `24px`.
- **Border:** none.
- **Text:** Choose black or white by contrast.

## Kanban Task Card

- **Background:** `#ffffff`.
- **Border:** `1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`.
- **Radius:** `8px`.
- **Padding:** `8px 12px`.
- **Text:** `14px` NotionInter weight `500`.

## Avatar Character Mark

- **Size:** `40px` to `48px`.
- **Shape:** Circle.
- **Border:** `2px` accent border.
- **Fill:** `#ffffff`.
- **Role:** Decorative punctuation, not content or navigation.
