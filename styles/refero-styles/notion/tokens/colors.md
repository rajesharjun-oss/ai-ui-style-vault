# Color Tokens

## Brand

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Notion Blue | `#0075de` | `--color-notion-blue` | Primary CTA fill, active accent, filled action buttons. |

## Rules

- Page background is `#f6f5f4`, not pure white.
- `#ffffff` is for cards and elevated surfaces.
- `#0075de` is the only filled primary action color.
- Use color variety in cards, pills, and decorative marks, not in competing CTAs.
- Build text hierarchy with black alpha values such as 100%, 95%, 60%, and 40%.
