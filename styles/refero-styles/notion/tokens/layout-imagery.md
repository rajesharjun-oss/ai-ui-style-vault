# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Grammar

Use a centered, contained page with a max width around `1440px`. The rhythm is calm and generous, with approximately `80px` between major sections.

## Hero Structure

- Sticky nav at the top.
- Row or cluster of avatar character marks.
- Large two-line display headline.
- Inline hero highlight pill around a key word.
- Editorial subhead.
- Primary and secondary CTA row.
- Large centered product UI mockup.

## Section Patterns

- White feature-card grids on the warm canvas.
- Colored accent panels for product or AI feature blocks.
- Two-column feature rows with text on one side and colored product panel on the other.
- Logo wall with greyscale partner logos treated like typography.
- Product UI mockups with a controlled shadow.

## Imagery

- Product mockups show real workspace UI patterns such as kanban boards, document views, and AI panels.
- Decorative marks include round character avatars, squiggles, sparkles, arrows, and simple drawn shapes.
- Avoid lifestyle photos, stock photos, glossy 3D renders, and decorative gradients.
