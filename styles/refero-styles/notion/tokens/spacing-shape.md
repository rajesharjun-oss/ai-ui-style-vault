## Borders And Elevation

- Default card border: `1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`.
- Default content card shadow: `none`.
- Sticky nav shadow: subtle low-opacity two-layer shadow.
- Product UI mockup shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`.
