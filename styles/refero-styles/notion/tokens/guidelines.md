# Guidelines

## Do

- Use `#f6f5f4` as the page canvas and `#ffffff` for card surfaces.
- Reserve `#0075de` for the main filled action.
- Use ghost or text styles for secondary actions.
- Apply negative tracking to large display type.
- Keep body text at normal tracking.
- Use `1px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` borders instead of shadows on content cards.
- Use `12px` radius for cards and `8px` for buttons.
- Reserve `9999px` radius for pills and highlight chips.
- Use accent hues as card backgrounds and decorative marks.
- Keep normal hover transitions around `200ms ease`.

## Don't

- Do not use pure white as the page background.
- Do not add shadows to normal content cards.
- Do not use multiple chromatic filled button colors in one view.
- Do not make every text element pure black at full opacity.
- Do not use Lyon Text for nav, labels, buttons, or dense UI.
- Do not use card radius larger than `12px` for rectangular content.
- Do not use gradients as the core surface treatment.
- Do not turn decorative avatar marks into actionable controls.
