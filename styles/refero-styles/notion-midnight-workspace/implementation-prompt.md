# AI Implementation Prompt

Build a Notion-inspired mixed-theme productivity landing page.

Start with a full-bleed Midnight Ink `#02093a` hero. Use large centered white NotionInter display text at 64px, weight 700, line-height 1, and tight tracking. Add a short supporting line at about 20px. Place a filled Signal Blue CTA beside a ghost action. Use a large product screenshot as the hero visual, with 12px radius and soft layered shadow.

After the hero, keep a dark logo band if needed, then transition sharply into warm Paper White `#f6f5f4` sections. Do not use a gradient bridge between dark and light stages. Below the fold, use large editorial section headings, product feature cards, testimonial cards, and clean product screenshots.

Typography:

- Use NotionInter for almost everything.
- Use Inter as fallback.
- Use weight 500 for UI labels, nav links, and button text.
- Use weight 600 for light-section editorial headings.
- Use weight 700 only for the hero display.
- Use Lyon Text only once or sparingly at 32px as an editorial accent.
- Do not use Lyon Text for nav, labels, or small text.

Colors:

- Hero background: `#02093a`.
- Primary CTA: `#0075de`.
- CTA text: `#ffffff`.
- Light section background: `#f6f5f4`.
- Elevated card: `#ffffff`.
- Text on light: `#000000`.
- Muted text: `#615d59`.
- Divider: `#dddddd` or `#c6c6c5`.
- Decorative aura only: `#62aef0`, `#2537b1`, `#455dd3`.

Components:

- Navigation: transparent or midnight bar, logo left, links center, blue CTA and ghost login right, 1px Charcoal divider.
- Filled CTA: Signal Blue background, white text, 8px radius, 14px to 15px NotionInter weight 500, compact padding.
- Ghost button: no fill, surface-matching text, 8px radius.
- Product screenshot card: 12px radius, real UI screenshot, soft shadow in hero, no shadow in light sections.
- Feature card: two-column block with text on one side and product UI on the other, white card surface, 12px radius.
- Testimonial card: white background, 12px radius, 24px padding, company label, 20px quote text, Signal Blue link.
- Badge: 9999px radius, soft blue background, Cerulean text, 12px label.

Rules:

- Do not use pure black as a page background.
- Do not introduce a second filled CTA color.
- Do not mix dark-stage and paper-stage treatments inside one card.
- Do not use tiny radii on cards or screenshots.
- Do not place chromatic buttons on chromatic backgrounds.
- Do not add gradients to text, borders, or small UI elements.

The finished UI should read as a dramatic product workspace followed by clear, warm, editorial product explanation.
