## Style Summary

Notion Midnight Workspace is a two-stage product site style: a deep midnight navy hero with white type, blue CTAs, floating product screenshots, and soft violet-blue auras, followed by warm paper editorial sections with clean cards, testimonials, and product feature blocks. It mixes utilitarian NotionInter with rare Lyon Text editorial moments.

Use this style when an AI needs a polished productivity homepage, workspace SaaS landing page, AI collaboration page, product launch hero, or product-led marketing page that moves from dramatic dark stage to readable warm content.

## What To Borrow

- Midnight Ink `#02093a` for the hero and top navigation stage.
- Paper White `#f6f5f4` for below-the-fold editorial sections.
- Signal Blue `#0075de` as the single filled CTA color.
- Pure White `#ffffff` for elevated cards and reversed text.
- NotionInter for almost all product UI and marketing type.
- Lyon Text only as a rare 32px editorial accent.
- 64px hero display text with tight tracking.
- 12px radius for cards, screenshots, and product mockups.
- 8px radius for buttons and inputs.
- 9999px radius for badges and tags only.
- Product screenshots as the primary imagery.

## Avoid

- Treating pure black as the dark background.
- Mixing dark and light surface treatments inside the same card.
- Adding a second bright CTA color.
- Using Lyon Text for navigation, labels, or small text.
- Gradients on text, borders, badges, or small UI.
- Using tiny radii on cards and screenshots.
- Crowding the midnight hero with too much decoration.
