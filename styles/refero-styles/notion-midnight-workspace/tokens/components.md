# Components

## Filled CTA Button

Primary action.

- Background: Signal Blue.
- Text: Pure White.
- Radius: 8px.
- Font: NotionInter 14px to 15px, weight 500.
- Padding: 16px horizontal, 6px to 10px vertical.
- Use once per major section as the clearest action.

## Ghost Text Button

Secondary action.

- Background: transparent.
- Text: Pure White on dark surfaces or Onyx on light surfaces.
- Radius: 8px.
- Font: NotionInter 14px to 15px, weight 500.
- Keep visual weight close to the filled CTA without competing.

## Hero Display Headline

Opening headline.

- Background: Midnight Ink.
- Text: Pure White.
- Font: NotionInter 64px, weight 700.
- Line height: 1.
- Letter spacing: -2.3px.
- Alignment: centered.

## Editorial Section Heading

Below-the-fold title.

- Font: NotionInter 40px to 54px, weight 600.
- Color: Onyx.
- Background: Paper White.
- Alignment: usually left.
- Use generous margin before cards.

## Product Screenshot Card

Main product visual.

- Image or UI mockup with 12px radius.
- Hero version floats on Midnight Ink with soft shadow.
- Paper-stage version uses Pure White card treatment and little or no shadow.

## Testimonial Quote Card

Customer proof.

- Background: Pure White.
- Radius: 12px.
- Padding: 24px.
- Company name: 16px weight 600.
- Quote: 20px, line-height 1.4.
- Link: Signal Blue, weight 500.

## Logo Grid

Social proof band.

- Place logos directly on Midnight Ink.
- Use Pure White or light gray logo fill.
- Keep generous horizontal spacing.
- No cards or borders.

## Feature Block Card

Product feature showcase.

- Two-column layout.
- Text on one side, screenshot on the other.
- Screenshot container has 12px radius and 24px padding.
- Keep stage consistent: paper card on paper section, dark card on dark section.

## Pill Badge

Status marker.

- Radius: 9999px.
- Background: faint blue.
- Text: Cerulean.
- Font: NotionInter 12px, weight 500.
- Use sparingly.

## Navigation Bar

Top-level site navigation.

- Midnight or transparent background.
- Logo left.
- Links centered in NotionInter 14px weight 500.
- Signal Blue CTA and ghost login on right.
- 1px Charcoal divider.
