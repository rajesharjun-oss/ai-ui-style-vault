# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Pattern

- Full-bleed Midnight Ink hero.
- Centered hero copy and dual actions.
- Large product screenshot near the bottom of the hero.
- Dark logo band.
- Sharp transition to Paper White sections.
- Two-column feature blocks.
- Testimonial section with one featured card and smaller quote cards.
- Minimal top nav with logo, center links, and right actions.

## Content Width

- Page max width: 1200px.
- Hero subtext max width: about 560px.
- Feature blocks: two columns on desktop, stacked on mobile.
- Testimonial grid: featured row plus 3-column cards when there is space.

## Imagery

Use:

- Real product screenshots.
- UI mockups.
- Workspace interface crops.
- Small illustrated mascot or badge accents in the hero.
- Minimal line icons for labels.

Avoid:

- Lifestyle photography.
- Generic abstract stock art.
- Decoration-heavy hero collages.
- Gradients on text or micro UI.
- Screenshots that mix dark and light stage styling inside one card.

## Motion

Use restrained motion:

- Fade or slight lift on product mockups.
- Simple button hover color changes.
- Soft aura movement only if it stays calm.
- No playful bounce or heavy parallax.
