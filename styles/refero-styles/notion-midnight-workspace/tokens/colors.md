# Colors

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Periwinkle | `#455dd3` | `--color-periwinkle` | Secondary dark surface and bridge tone |
| Charcoal | `#31302e` | `--color-charcoal` | Dark nav borders and low-contrast dividers |

## Rules

- Use Midnight Ink for dark surfaces, not pure black.
- Use Paper White for the main light canvas, not pure white.
- Use Signal Blue as the only filled action color.
- Use blue/violet aura colors only at large decorative scale.
- Keep text and border color simple on the paper stage.
