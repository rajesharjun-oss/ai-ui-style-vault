# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Midnight Ink for the hero and top stage.
- Use Paper White for below-the-fold sections.
- Use Signal Blue as the single filled CTA color.
- Set the hero headline at 64px NotionInter weight 700.
- Use NotionInter weight 500 for UI labels and buttons.
- Use NotionInter weight 600 for light-section headings.
- Keep 80px gaps between major page sections.
- Use 12px radius for cards, images, and mockups.
- Use 8px radius for buttons and inputs.
- Use Lyon Text at most once per page as an editorial accent.

## Do Not

- Do not use pure black as the page background.
- Do not use Lyon Text for labels, nav, or small UI.
- Do not add a second CTA color.
- Do not put chromatic buttons on chromatic backgrounds.
- Do not mix dark-stage and paper-stage treatments inside one card.
- Do not use 5px radius on cards or screenshots.
- Do not add gradients to text, borders, badges, or small UI.
- Do not set body text below 14px or above 20px.

## Accessibility Notes

- Pure White on Midnight Ink is high contrast for hero text.
- Signal Blue CTAs work best on Midnight Ink or Paper White.
- Verify Signal Blue contrast when used on Periwinkle or Royal Violet surfaces.
- Use visible focus styles for all buttons, links, and controls.
