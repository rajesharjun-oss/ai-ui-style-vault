## Use This Style When

Use Legora when the interface should feel like an editorial legal journal: warm parchment, whisper-light serif display type, careful humanist body copy, black micro-radius controls, and product surfaces treated like quiet legal documents. It fits legal AI, compliance, enterprise knowledge tools, law firm technology, and premium B2B pages that need authority through restraint.

## Quick DNA

- Warm parchment canvas, never clinical white.
- Rhymes Display 300 at 88px for hero and section-opening headlines.
- Suisse-style humanist sans for legal body copy and UI labels.
- One solid black primary action per viewport.
- Ghost outline secondary actions with 2px radius.
- No saturated brand color; accent washes are pale surfaces only.
- No shadows; use hairline rules and tinted panels.
- One full-bleed cinematic photo only in the hero.
