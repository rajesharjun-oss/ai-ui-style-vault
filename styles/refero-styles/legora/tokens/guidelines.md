# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Rhymes Display 300 at 88px for page and section openings.
- Use Suisse Intl 450 at 14px to 16px for body copy.
- Use one solid black CTA per viewport.
- Pair with a ghost outline button only when a second action is needed.
- Keep the page canvas at `#fefefc`.
- Use 80px section gaps.
- Use Verdigris Wash and Slate Mist as full-card or full-section surfaces.
- Render partner logos in monochrome Graphite at 60 to 70 percent opacity.
- Reserve the full-bleed cinematic photo for hero sections only.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce saturated brand colors.
- Do not use bold or semibold display headlines.
- Do not apply drop shadows or elevated card treatments.
- Do not use 0px or fully rounded button radius.
- Do not use pure white `#ffffff` for page surfaces.
- Do not stack more than one full-bleed photo on a page.
- Do not use Playfair Display as the production display face.
- Do not show partner logos in native brand colors.
