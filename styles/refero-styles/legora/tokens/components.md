# Components

## Solid Black Button

- Role: primary CTA.
- Fill: `#000000`.
- Text: `#fefefc`.
- Radius: 2px.
- Padding: 10px 24px.
- Type: Suisse Intl 450, 14px, letter-spacing -0.14px.
- Use one per viewport maximum.
- Hover: subtle outline or inversion only.

## Ghost Outline Button

- Role: secondary action.
- Fill: transparent.
- Border: `1px solid #0a0a0a`.
- Text: `#0a0a0a`.
- Radius: 2px.
- Padding: 10px 24px.
- Pair with a solid black button when two actions are needed.

## Editorial Headline Block

- Role: hero and section display copy.
- Font: Rhymes Display.
- Weight: 300.
- Size: 88px.
- Line height: 0.95.
- Letter spacing: -1.76px.
- Color: `#0a0a0a` on parchment or `#fefefc` on dark photo.
- Do not break across more than two lines.

## Cinematic Hero Panel

- Role: full-bleed opening section.
- Surface: dark full-bleed photograph.
- Overlay: centered/lower-third display headline in Parchment.
- No gradient veil.
- No repeated photo sections.
- CTAs below headline can use solid and ghost variants.

## Partner Logo Bar

- Role: social proof.
- Layout: single horizontal row.
- Logo color: `#0a0a0a`.
- Opacity: 60 to 70 percent.
- No separators, cards, or chrome.

## Pastel Feature Card

- Role: product capability highlight.
- Surface: `#ebf5ed`, `#bdd4f0`, or a muted gray panel.
- Border: none.
- Shadow: none.
- Padding: none when product screenshot bleeds edge-to-edge.
- Heading below card: 24px to 32px serif.

## Two-Column Text Image Feature

- Role: body content section.
- Split: about 55 percent image, 45 percent text.
- Gap: 48px to 64px.
- Image side: pastel product panel or dark photographic still.
- Text side: 13px uppercase eyebrow, 32px serif heading, 16px body.

## Cookie Banner

- Role: persistent consent card.
- Position: fixed bottom-right.
- Fill: `#fefefc`.
- Border: `1px solid #0a0a0a`.
- Radius: 8px.
- Padding: 16px.
- Body: 13px Suisse Intl 450.
- Action: solid black 2px-radius button.

## Top Navigation Bar

- Role: global header.
- Over hero: dark overlay with white wordmark and nav links.
- On parchment: dark text with black demo action.
- Center cluster: around five primary nav items.
- Right action: solid black demo CTA.

## Input Field

- Role: form field.
- Border: `1px solid #0a0a0a`.
- Radius: 8px.
- Padding: 12px vertical, 14px horizontal.
- Placeholder: `#98a7aa`.
- Value text: Suisse Intl 500 at 13px.
- Focus: border shifts to Verdigris Wash.

## Eyebrow Label

- Role: section identifier.
- Text: uppercase or small caps.
- Size: 11px to 13px.
- Tracking: +0.01em.
- Color: `#6b6b6b`.
- Gap to heading: 8px to 10px.
