# Typography

## Families

- Primary: Suisse Intl.
- Fallback: Inter, Sohne, GT America, Manrope, Arial, sans-serif.
- Secondary/system fallback: system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto, sans-serif.

## Weights

- 400: body, headline variants, standard copy.
- 500: navigation links, emphasis, display headings, button text.

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caption | 16px | 400 | 1.5 | 0.06px |
| Subheading | 20px | 500 | 1.4 | -0.28px |
| Heading Small | 32px | 500 | 1.15 | -0.42px |
| Heading | 42px | 500 | 1.15 | -0.59px |
| Display | 55px | 500 | 1.1 | -0.82px |

## Usage

- Treat typography as the primary visual identity.
- Use display text at 55px/1.10 for editorial statements.
- Keep headline blocks left-aligned.
- Use tight tracking on large type.
- Avoid loose body tracking.
- Do not use system-ui as the primary typeface.
