# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use an implicit left-aligned editorial grid.
- Keep major text blocks around 900px max width.
- Use a 1440px max width for constrained page content.
- Let media sections bleed to viewport edges.
- Alternate: whitespace, editorial headline, full-bleed media, whitespace.
- Keep content density low.
- Prefer single-column reading over dense grids.

## Section Patterns

- Hero: large left-aligned editorial headline and generous top padding.
- Media punctuation: full-bleed video or photograph with no overlay text.
- Content block: Graphite text on Pure White, optional Fog card.
- Feature block: rare Cream Stone card for editorial emphasis.
- Announcement: bottom fixed Electric Yellow strip.
- Footer: long quiet closing area with Graphite links and Muted meta text.

## Imagery

- Use cinematic photography and video.
- Prefer documentary stills, wide environmental shots, and human-centered production content.
- Use media as punctuation between typographic sections.
- Avoid stock imagery, decorative illustration, product-shot grids, and media wallpaper.
- Do not add overlays, filters, or heavy frames.
