# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Suisse Intl at 55px/1.10 with tight tracking for editorial headlines.
- Maintain a monochrome palette.
- Use Electric Yellow as the only chromatic accent.
- Use 8px radius as the default for cards, buttons, inputs, and images.
- Use Hairline for all borders and dividers.
- Let the layout breathe with 80px to 192px section gaps.
- Use ghost links and outlined buttons for most interactions.
- Left-align editorial headlines and body copy.

## Do Not

- Do not add chromatic colors beyond Electric Yellow.
- Do not use shadows, glows, or blur effects for elevation.
- Do not use system-ui as the primary typeface.
- Do not center-align editorial body or headline blocks.
- Do not use random radii outside 4px, 8px, and 9999px.
- Do not fill pages with imagery.
- Do not loosen body letter spacing.

## AI Implementation Notes

- If the UI feels boring, increase type scale and whitespace before adding decoration.
- If the UI feels too colorful, remove every accent except one yellow signal.
- If hierarchy feels flat, use Fog or Cream Stone surface shifts, not shadows.
- If the page feels generic, make the headline more editorial and left-aligned.
