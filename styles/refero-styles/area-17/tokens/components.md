# Components

## Announcement Bar

- Position: fixed bottom.
- Width: full viewport.
- Background: Electric Yellow.
- Text: Graphite.
- Font: Suisse Intl 16px, 1.5 line-height.
- Padding: 12px vertical, 24px horizontal.
- Height: about 40px to 48px.
- Role: the sole chromatic element.

## Top Navigation

- Background: transparent or Pure White.
- Border: none.
- Left: A slash-style mark.
- Right: text links.
- Text: Suisse Intl 16px, 500 weight, Graphite.
- Height: 64px to 84px.
- Padding: 24px to 48px horizontal.

## Editorial Headline

- Font: Suisse Intl.
- Size: 55px or 42px.
- Weight: 400 to 500.
- Line height: 1.10 to 1.15.
- Tracking: tight negative.
- Color: Graphite.
- Alignment: left.
- Max width: about 900px.

## Ghost Text Link

- Background: none.
- Border: none.
