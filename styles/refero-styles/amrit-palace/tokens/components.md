# Components

## Ghost Outlined Button

Transparent fill, 1px context border, Satoshi 14px/500 uppercase, 3px radius, generous padding, no shadow. Use Parchment border on dark photos and Onyx Warm border on parchment.

## Text Link Button

Satoshi 13px to 15px/500 uppercase text-only action. No background or border. Underline on hover.

## Hero Wordmark Display

Satoshi 199px/500 uppercase, line-height 0.8, tight tracking, Parchment text over a dark photographic overlay.

## Editorial Section Heading

TT Ramillas Variable 50px to 115px, weight 300, uppercase, line-height 0.8 to 0.9, tight tracking. Use as the primary section voice.

## Testimonial Card

Parchment background matching the canvas, 0px radius, no border, no shadow. Include Saffron stars, Satoshi body text in Onyx Warm, source label, and Walnut author text.

## Image Overlay Container

Full-viewport restaurant photograph with dark Midnight Roast overlay. Text sits top-left or centered in Parchment.

## Star Rating Display

Five Saffron stars, Satoshi 700 score, Satoshi 500 source label. Can be used as a compact floating widget.

## Menu Item Card

Transparent container, sharp food image, dish name, compact description, and 16px internal spacing.

## Navigation Bar

Transparent over hero. Logo left, nav links in uppercase Satoshi, right action links and 3px square icon buttons. No background bar.

## Section Divider Rule

1px Warm Stone horizontal line across the section, used as an editorial column rule.

## Caption Label

Satoshi 12px to 13px/500 uppercase with tight negative tracking. Use Walnut on parchment or Parchment on dark.
