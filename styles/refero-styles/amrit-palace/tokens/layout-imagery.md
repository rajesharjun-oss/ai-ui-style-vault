# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use full-bleed sections, not app-like containers.
- Hero: dark full-bleed photography with transparent navigation and overlaid type.
- Content: parchment editorial spreads with left-aligned type.
- Section gaps: 120px to 160px.
- Testimonials: horizontal row of sharp cards on same-color canvas.
- Menu: editorial still-life spreads, not dense product cards.
- Navigation: floating transparent bar over the hero.

## Imagery

Use:

- Candlelit restaurant interiors.
- Table-height photography.
- Place settings, candles, food, wood, and warm dim light.
- Shallow depth of field.
- Editorial food still-lifes on dark surfaces.

Avoid:

- Lifestyle photos with people.
- Stock restaurant imagery.
- Abstract graphics.
- CSS gradient atmosphere.
- Rounded image containers.
