## What To Preserve

Amrit Palace is flat, warm, editorial, and sharp. The page should feel printed onto parchment rather than stacked as app cards. Photography gives atmosphere; hairlines give structure; saffron appears rarely. The serif is luxury because it is light, not bold.

The page relies on:

- Parchment as the dominant canvas.
- Onyx Warm for text and dark fills.
- Saffron Glow only for star ratings, active states, and small punctuation.
- TT Ramillas Variable for all display/section headings at weight 300.
- Satoshi for nav, body, labels, buttons, and the hero wordmark.
- 0px radius on cards, sections, and image containers.
- 3px radius only on buttons, tags, and controls.
- 1px Warm Stone dividers as editorial column rules.
- Full-bleed dark restaurant photography in hero and transition sections.
