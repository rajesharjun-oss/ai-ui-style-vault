## Use This Style When

Use Wope when an interface should feel like a dark product cockpit lit from beneath by violet glow. It fits SEO tools, analytics dashboards, AI workspaces, observability products, developer tools, and polished B2B SaaS pages that need drama without losing product clarity.

## Quick DNA

- Nearly black-violet canvas dominates every section.
- Depth comes from purple underlighting, blur, and translucent borders.
- Rebond Grotesque is reserved for hero and section headlines.
- Inter V handles navigation, body, badges, buttons, inputs, and compact UI.
- Buttons and badges are full pills.
- Cards are dark, 16px radius, and embedded rather than elevated.
- Borders use translucent white at low opacity.
- Avoid conventional shadows and bright non-violet accents.
