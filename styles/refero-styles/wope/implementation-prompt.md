# AI Implementation Prompt

Build a Wope-inspired dark violet product interface.

Use Night Violet `#0a0118` as the global canvas and keep most sections in the same dark-violet family. Use Soft Quartz `#ffffff` for primary headings and high-emphasis UI, Ash Lilac `#9b96b0` for body copy, Muted Steel `#d2d0dd` for brighter secondary headings, and Dim Fog `#85808c` for inactive details.

Create depth through illumination, not shadow. Use translucent borders `rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`, glass fills `rgba(255,255,255,0.04)`, blur, and violet gradients with Ultraviolet Core `#713dff`, Lilac Beam `#b7a4fb`, and Horizon Glow `#8562ff`.

Use Rebond Grotesque for hero and major section headlines only: 56px or 72px, weight 700, tight negative tracking. Use Inter V for navigation, body copy, links, buttons, badges, inputs, captions, and smaller headings.

Core components to include:

- Header bar with centered 1248px content, Ash Lilac nav links, and pill actions.
- Primary glass CTA pill with 999px radius, low-opacity white fill, translucent white border, and white text.
- Compact trial badge with glass fill and full-pill radius.
- Large dark product screenshot frame with 16px radius and ultraviolet underglow.
- Feature cards with Night Violet surface, 16px radius, 28px padding, and no drop shadow.
- Frosted info cards with very low-opacity white fill and optional blur.
- Overlay mini cards with 10px radius and thin translucent border.
- Borderless input fields inside dark framed forms.
- Quiet monochrome logo rail.

Use 1248px max width, 108px section gaps, and about 124px vertical padding for major bands. Avoid opaque white cards, conventional black shadows, bright non-violet accents, square buttons, and Rebond Grotesque in dense UI.
