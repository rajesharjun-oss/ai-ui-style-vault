## Radius

| Element | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Inputs | 0px |
| Overlay cards | 10px |
| Cards | 16px |
| Buttons | 999px |
| Badges | 999px |

## Shape Rules

- Controls and badges are full pills.
- Cards are 16px, not pills.
- Floating overlays use 10px.
- Inputs are visually borderless and square inside their container.
