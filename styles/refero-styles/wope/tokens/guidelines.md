# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Night Violet as the dominant canvas.
- Keep most surfaces dark or translucent.
- Use Rebond Grotesque for large hero and section headlines.
- Use Inter V for navigation, body, controls, and compact UI.
- Use Ash Lilac for supporting text and Soft Quartz for primary text.
- Use 999px radius for buttons and badges.
- Use 16px radius for cards and 10px for floating overlays.
- Build depth with violet glow, blur, and translucent borders.
- Keep major section rhythm large and calm.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce bright non-violet accents.
- Do not use square or lightly rounded buttons.
- Do not place opaque white cards on the page.
- Do not add conventional black box shadows.
- Do not use Rebond Grotesque for body, buttons, or labels.
- Do not collapse all text into white.
- Do not use bordered rectangular inputs.
- Do not make the page feel like a generic blue-black SaaS dashboard.
