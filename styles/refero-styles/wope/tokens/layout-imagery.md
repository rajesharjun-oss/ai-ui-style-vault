# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a full-page Night Violet canvas.
- Center page content inside a 1248px max-width rail.
- Use 108px gaps between sections.
- Use about 124px top and bottom padding for major bands.
- Keep hero text centered or strongly focused before showing product visuals.
- Place large product screenshot frames in the purple glow field.
- Keep feature cards dark and embedded.
- Avoid opaque light surfaces.

## Imagery

Use product screenshots as proof. Frame them in dark rounded containers with ultraviolet underlighting. Surround them with atmospheric grid lines, light rays, glow blooms, and dark fade veils.

Use monochrome partner logos for social proof.

Avoid:

- Stock photography.
- Illustration-heavy scenes.
- Bright multicolor graphics.
- Opaque white dashboard cards.
- Generic blue SaaS gradients.
