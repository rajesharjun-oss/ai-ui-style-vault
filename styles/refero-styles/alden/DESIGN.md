# Alden Design Reference

## Essence

Alden makes healthcare feel calm, advisory, and human. The style avoids both sterile hospital blue and loud startup decoration. It pairs a warm white canvas with parchment panels, large serif display type, blue underline highlights, and sage pill actions. The result should feel like a trusted care guide rather than a clinical system.

## Color System

White is the main surface. Parchment softens hero, testimonial, and advisory panels. Ink carries headings and important body text. Stone supports explanatory copy and secondary navigation. Mist Blue creates generous highlight blocks behind key words, while Sky Signal adds underlines, circles, badges, and icon accents. Sage is the primary action color, with Deep Sage for hover states, tags, and grounded controls.

## Typography

The expressive layer is Domaine Display or a refined serif fallback. Use it for hero headlines, large quotes, and selected section statements. Geist or a similar neutral sans handles body, navigation, forms, labels, and UI. Serif type should breathe; sans text should stay crisp, readable, and quietly practical.

## Shape And Space

The geometry is rounded and reassuring. Buttons and badges are pill-shaped. Cards use 24px radius, images use 28px radius, and large panels can reach 32px. Section spacing is generous, usually around 96px. Elevation is almost flat: use borders, color fields, and spacing instead of visible drop shadows.

## Layout Rhythm

Use a centered max-width editorial page with roomy two-column hero sections, advisor cards, care-step grids, testimonial panels, and rounded image frames. Parchment panels should appear as warm relief, not as a full-site beige wash. White remains the clean base.

## Components

- Pill CTA with sage fill, white text, rounded 999px shape, and compact confident padding.
- Text link with Ink text and a Sky Signal underline that reads like an editorial mark.
- Split hero with serif headline, one or two blue-highlighted words, CTA row, and care photo frame.
- Advisor card with white fill, Cloud Border hairline, 24px radius, portrait or icon, and no shadow.
- Care step card with rounded parchment or white fill, numbered marker, title, and Stone body copy.
- Testimonial panel with parchment fill, 32px radius, large serif quote, and small uppercase attribution.
- Healthcare photo frame with rounded corners and optional cream or blue offset patch.
- Form input with white fill, Cloud Border outline, 16px radius, and Sky Signal focus state.
- Badge or pill with Mist Blue or pale Sage fill, uppercase sans label, and generous horizontal padding.

## Implementation Direction

Start with a white canvas and a calm max-width layout. Use the serif display font for the main emotional message, then rely on clean sans text for the rest of the interface. Highlight only a few important words with Mist Blue blocks or Sky Signal underlines. Keep CTAs pill-shaped and sage. Avoid heavy shadows, sharp enterprise rectangles, neon colors, dense dashboard grids, and overly clinical blue surfaces.
