# Components

## Pill CTA Button

- Fill: Sage.
- Text: White.
- Hover fill: Deep Sage.
- Radius: `999px`.
- Padding: `14px 24px`.
- Font: Geist, `16px`, weight `500`.
- Shadow: none.

## Text Link With Blue Underline

- Text: Ink.
- Underline: Sky Signal.
- Underline thickness: about `3px`.
- Underline offset: about `4px`.
- Hover: Sage or Deep Sage text.

## Hero Split Layout

- Max width: `1180px`.
- Layout: two columns on desktop.
- Left: copy, serif headline, highlighted phrase, CTA row.
- Right: rounded medical/advisory photo frame.
- Background: White or Parchment.
- Hero heading: Domaine Display, `56px` to `64px`.

## Serif Highlight Word

- Use inside display text or section headings.
- Background: Mist Blue.
- Accent: Sky Signal underline or circle mark.
- Radius: `32px`.
- Use on one or two phrases only.

## Advisor Card

- Fill: White.
- Border: `1px solid` Cloud Border.
- Radius: `24px`.
- Padding: `24px` to `32px`.
- Optional portrait: small circle or rounded rectangle.
- Shadow: none.

## Care Step Card

- Fill: Parchment or White.
- Radius: `24px`.
- Padding: `24px`.
- Marker: number, icon, or tiny Sky Signal accent.
- Title: `22px`, weight `700`, Ink.
- Body: Stone, `16px`.

## Testimonial Panel

- Fill: Parchment.
- Radius: `32px`.
- Quote: serif, `48px`, weight `400`.
- Attribution: Geist, `14px`, uppercase, Stone.
- Accent: small Sky Signal underline or rule.

## Healthcare Photo Frame

- Image radius: `28px`.
- Optional offset patch: Parchment, Mist Blue, or Sage.
- Subject: clinician, advisor, patient support, or calm care team.
- Avoid cold stock hospital imagery.

## Form Input

- Fill: White.
- Border: `1px solid` Cloud Border.
- Radius: `16px`.
- Padding: `14px 16px`.
- Label: Geist, `14px`, weight `700`.
- Focus: Sky Signal border.

## Badge Pill

- Fill: Mist Blue or pale Sage.
- Text: Ink or Deep Sage.
- Radius: `999px`.
- Padding: `8px 12px`.
- Font: Geist, `13px`, weight `700`, uppercase, tracking `0.08em`.

## Navigation

- Surface: White.
- Height: `72px` to `80px`.
- Brand: Ink.
- Links: Stone.
- Action: Sage pill CTA on the right.
- Keep the bar simple, light, and calm.
