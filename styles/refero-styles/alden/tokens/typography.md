# Typography

## Font Stacks

- Display serif: `Domaine Display`, `Georgia`, `Cormorant Garamond`, `Libre Baskerville`, serif
- Sans/UI: `Geist`, `Inter`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Weights: `400`, `500`, `700`

## Scale

| Purpose | Size | Line height | Weight | Tracking | Token |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Micro label | `13px` | `1.3` | `700` | `0.08em` | `--text-micro-label` |
| Body small | `14px` | `1.5` | `400` | `0` | `--text-body-sm` |
| Body | `16px` | `1.6` | `400` | `0` | `--text-body` |
| Body large | `18px` | `1.55` | `400` | `0` | `--text-body-lg` |
| Card title | `22px` | `1.25` | `700` | `0` | `--text-card-title` |
| Section heading | `40px` | `1.12` | `500` | `-0.01em` | `--text-section-heading` |
| Hero heading | `64px` | `1.08` | `400` | `-0.03em` | `--text-hero-heading` |
| Quote display | `48px` | `1.15` | `400` | `-0.02em` | `--text-quote` |

## Rules

- Use the serif only for emotional, editorial, or advisory moments.
- Use the sans for practical UI, forms, nav, body, and labels.
- Keep hero text generous and readable.
- Use uppercase micro labels sparingly with wide tracking.
- Avoid dense all-caps body copy.
