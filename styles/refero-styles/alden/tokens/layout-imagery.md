# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Model

- Use an editorial max-width container around `1180px`.
- Build a split hero with text on one side and a rounded care image on the other.
- Alternate White content areas with Parchment panels.
- Use card grids for care steps, advisor modules, and benefits.
- Use full-width testimonial panels with generous radius.
- Keep forms simple, rounded, and clearly labeled.
- Let whitespace create trust and calm.

## Imagery

- Use real healthcare, advisor, clinician, care-team, or patient support photography.
- Favor bright, calm, human scenes over sterile hospital stock.
- Crop images into rounded rectangles with optional offset patches.
- Keep faces and gestures natural.
- Avoid dark dramatic lighting, corporate handshake imagery, and generic dashboard screenshots.

## Motion

- Motion should be gentle and clear.
- Use subtle opacity and vertical reveal transitions.
- Avoid bouncy, playful, or overly technical motion.
