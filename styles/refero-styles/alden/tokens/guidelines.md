# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Domaine Display or a similar serif for hero headlines and quotes.
- Use Geist or a neutral sans for body, navigation, labels, and controls.
- Highlight one or two key words with Mist Blue and Sky Signal.
- Use Sage pill CTAs for primary actions.
- Keep surfaces mostly White with Parchment as warm relief.
- Use 96px section gaps and rounded 24px to 32px panels.
- Keep shadows absent or nearly invisible.
- Use real healthcare, advisory, or care-team photography.
- Use blue as a confidence mark and sage as a care/action mark.

## Don't

- Do not use cold hospital blue as the dominant fill.
- Do not use sharp enterprise rectangles.
- Do not add heavy drop shadows.
- Do not highlight every phrase.
- Do not use neon, pure saturated colors, or loud gradients.
- Do not make CTAs rectangular.
- Do not build a stock dashboard grid.
- Do not pair the serif display voice with dense all-caps body copy.
