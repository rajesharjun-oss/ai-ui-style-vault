# AI Implementation Prompt

Build a calm healthcare advisory interface inspired by Alden.

Use a light editorial system with white canvas, warm parchment panels, ink text, muted stone body copy, mist-blue word highlights, sky-blue underline accents, and sage pill CTAs. The experience should feel expert, warm, and human, not like a hospital dashboard.

Requirements:

- Use a refined serif display font for hero headlines and testimonial quotes. Prefer Domaine Display when licensed; otherwise use Georgia, Cormorant Garamond, Libre Baskerville, or a similar editorial serif.
- Use Geist or a neutral sans for body text, navigation, labels, inputs, badges, and buttons.
- Keep the page mostly white, with parchment used for hero side panels, testimonial blocks, care panels, or soft section washes.
- Highlight one or two key words with Mist Blue rounded rectangles or Sky Signal underline marks.
- Use sage pill CTAs with white text and Deep Sage hover states.
- Use rounded advisor cards, care-step cards, and image frames. Cards should use borders and spacing, not heavy shadows.
- Use real healthcare, advisory, clinical, or care-team photography. Keep it bright, calm, and human.
- Add accessible forms with rounded inputs, clear labels, and sky-blue focus borders.
- Avoid cold hospital-blue fills, sharp enterprise boxes, heavy shadows, neon colors, over-highlighted copy, and dense SaaS dashboard grids.

Hero recipe:

Create a two-column hero inside an 1180px max-width container. Left side: large serif headline at 56px to 64px, one Mist Blue highlighted phrase, calm Stone supporting copy, and a sage pill CTA. Right side: rounded healthcare advisor photo with a parchment or pale blue offset frame. Keep the background white or parchment and the nav clean.

Card recipe:

Create rounded 24px advisor cards with white fill, Cloud Border outline, 24px to 32px padding, optional circular portrait, Ink title, Stone body text, and a small Sky Signal mark. Do not add strong shadows.
