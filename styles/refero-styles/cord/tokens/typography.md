# Typography Tokens

## Family

Primary:

- Preferred: Figtree
- Substitutes: Inter, DM Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans

Use one family throughout the product. The contrast comes from weight and scale, not font mixing.

## Weights

- 400 for body and general copy.
- 600 for buttons, chips, links, and emphasis.
- 700 for company names and subheadings.
- 800 for display headlines and the logo wordmark.

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Line Height | Tracking | Weight | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Caption | 10px | 1.5 | 0.1px | 400 | Tiny hints and UI labels |
| Body small | 14px | 1.4 | -0.07px | 400 | Meta rows and footer links |
| Body | 16px | 1.5 | -0.08px | 400 | Inputs, paragraphs, buttons |
| Subheading | 18px | 1.4 | -0.09px | 400 | Hero subtitle and CTA copy |
| Heading small | 20px | 1.3 | -0.1px | 700 | Card and section mini-headings |
| Heading | 24px | 1.3 | -0.12px | 700 | Logo scale and section headings |
| Heading large | 32px | 1.25 | -0.16px | 800 | Large section titles |
| Display | 48px | 1.2 | -0.24px | 800 | CTA banner headline |
| Hero | 100px | 1 | -0.5px | 800 | Main hero headline |

## Rules

- Apply tighter tracking as size grows.
- Keep display headings weight 700-800.
- Avoid 400 or 500 for hero/display copy.
- Use Signal Blue only for small interactive or brand text.
