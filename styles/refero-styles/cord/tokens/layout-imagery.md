# Layout And Imagery

## Page Composition

- Use a centered 1200px container.
- Keep the hero vertically stacked and centered.
- Place search and filter controls directly under the headline.
- Use a 3-column company-card grid on desktop.
- Use one full-bleed dark CTA banner near the end.
- Use a 4-column footer link grid.

## Hero

- Nav at top.
- Toggle pill below nav.
- 100px hero headline in Midnight Harbor.
- Subtitle in Slate Channel.
- Wide pill search field.
- Filter chip row with modest spacing.

## Company Imagery

- Use authentic team or workplace photography.
- Keep imagery natural color.
- Image fills the card width.
- Image radius matches card radius.
- Logos and metadata sit below the photo.

## Avatar Stack

- Circular avatars, 24-32px.
- Border: 2px white on light or 2px Midnight Harbor on dark.
- Overlap: about -8px.
- Pair with compact count text.

## Dark CTA Banner

- Full-bleed `#0b3658`.
- White headline.
- Sea Fog supporting copy.
- Signal Blue CTA.
- Optional avatar stack for social proof.

## Avoid

- Do not use abstract illustrations as primary card visuals.
- Do not desaturate company photos.
- Do not use the dark banner as a repeated card pattern.
