# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Midnight Harbor for all primary text and headings.
- Reserve Signal Blue for the logo, primary CTA, and active states.
- Use Figtree as a single-family system.
- Set display headlines at weight 800.
- Use generous rounding on cards, inputs, and buttons.
- Tint all shadows with Midnight Harbor values.
- Use Sea Fog for borders and dividers.
- Use natural company photography in cards.
- Place the dark CTA banner near the page end.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce a second accent color.
- Do not use pure black for text.
- Do not use sharp corners on cards or buttons.
- Do not use generic gray shadows.
- Do not use weight 400 or 500 for display headlines.
- Do not use Signal Blue for body text or large headings.
- Do not repeat the dark CTA banner in multiple sections.
- Do not use abstract graphics or 3D renders as the core imagery.

## Implementation Heuristic

If the design feels too serious, increase warmth through photography and rounded spacing. Do not add more colors.
