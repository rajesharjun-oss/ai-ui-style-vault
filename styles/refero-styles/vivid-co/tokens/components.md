# Components

## Ghost Nav Button

Transparent fill, Bone White text, uppercase Neue Montreal 14px to 15px, 0px radius, no padding, no underline, no border.

## Outlined Contact Button

Transparent fill, 1px solid Bone White border, 5px radius, 9px by 15px padding, uppercase 14px Bone White text. This is the only explicit header CTA.

## Ghost Service Label

Transparent fill, Fog Blue text, 0px radius, 20px top padding, 30px bottom padding, Neue Montreal 20px. Use under case-study titles.

## Display Headline Block

Neue Montreal 400 at 105px to 136px, line-height 1.00 to 1.01, -0.02em tracking, Bone White. Stack across multiple lines and let scale carry hierarchy.

## Hero Lead Paragraph

Neue Montreal 400 at 18px to 22px, line-height 1.5, Bone White, max-width around 440px.

## Prism Artifact

Four to six glass cubes in a staggered cluster, with Pure Black cores, Bone White specular highlights, and RGB channel-offset red/cyan/lime edges.

## Section Heading

Small 18px to 22px Bone White statement, max-width around 440px, usually placed above a larger display headline.

## Footer Hairline Divider

1px Ash Border rule, full width, with compact top spacing.

## Case Study Title Link

Neue Montreal 400 at 33px, Bone White, letter-spacing -0.01em, line-height 1.2, no underline. Hover may shift to Fog Blue.

## Eyebrow Label

Uppercase Neue Montreal 17px or 32px, weight 400, slight positive tracking at small sizes.

## Rounded Pill Tag

9999px radius metadata chip. Use sparingly; most UI should remain square-edged.
