# Typography

## Families

| Family | Token | Fallback | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neue Montreal | `--font-neue-montreal` | Sohne, Inter, General Sans | Entire interface: headings, body, nav, buttons, links, labels, and footer. |

## Rules

- Use Neue Montreal-style type everywhere.
- Weight 400 is the default.
- Weight 700 is reserved for rare 36px subheadings only.
- Display headings are 105px to 136px with line-height near 1.
- Use -0.02em tracking at display sizes.
- Use slight positive tracking for small uppercase labels.
- Enable stylistic set 1 where available.
