# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a full-bleed Obsidian canvas.
- Center content in a 1440px max-width rail.
- Put the prism artifact near the visual center of the hero.
- Let display text wrap around or overlap the artifact.
- Keep the top nav thin: wordmark left, ghost links right, outlined contact at the end.
- Use 108px section gaps.
- Prefer single-column statements and case-study links over grid systems.
- Keep footer as a simple metadata band with one hairline divider.

## Imagery

- Use a single glass-cube prism artifact as the primary visual.
- Build the artifact with black cores, near-white highlights, and RGB red/cyan/lime channel splitting.
- Avoid photography, people, product screenshots, decorative gradients, multicolor icons, textures, and generic illustration.
- If icons are needed, make them small monoline Bone White glyphs.
