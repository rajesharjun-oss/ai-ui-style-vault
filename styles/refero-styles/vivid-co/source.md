## What To Preserve

Vivid+Co uses a cinematic near-black canvas, one near-white text color, giant weight-400 display typography, and a single chromatic prism artwork. The design should not use conventional CTA colors, filled buttons, gradients, badges, or decorative color systems. The prism carries all color.

The page relies on:

- Obsidian as the full page canvas.
- Graphite Veil as the only meaningful lifted dark surface.
- Bone White for almost every text and interactive element.
- Fog Blue for de-emphasized metadata and service labels.
- Ash Border for rare hairlines and dividers.
- Neue Montreal as the only typeface.
- Weight 400 as the default across display, body, links, nav, and footer.
- Weight 700 only for rare 36px subheadings.
- Display headings at 105px to 136px with tight tracking.
- Prism RGB colors only inside the hero glass-cube artifact.
- Zero shadows and nearly no button chrome.
