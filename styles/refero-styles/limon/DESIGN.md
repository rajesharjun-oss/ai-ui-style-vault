# Limon - Style Reference

Limon is a dark restaurant editorial system. It uses a black-olive canvas, warm cream panels, forest-green typography, lemon-yellow action color, and large food photography. The interface should feel like evening hospitality under warm light: atmospheric, direct, food-first, and deliberately unsoftened.

## Theme

- Theme mode: dark with warm light sections.
- Visual temperature: warm, candlelit, botanical, and editorial.
- Surface logic: dark olive canvas, cream editorial sections, forest text, lemon CTA.
- Energy: confident, restaurant-forward, minimal chrome, image-led.

## Core Principles

1. Keep the palette minimal: dark olive, forest green, lemon yellow, warm cream, sage divider, white.
2. Use lemon yellow as a filled CTA or dark-section display accent only.
3. Use wide positive letter spacing, especially above 46px.
4. Keep corners at 0px or 1px except circular utility controls.
5. Avoid shadows, gradients, glows, and raised cards.
6. Let food photography carry the visual identity.
7. Use cream sections only as breathing room between dark editorial moments.

## Typography

Primary type is VenusCom. Use it across headings, body, nav, buttons, and menu descriptions. Fallbacks: DM Sans, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Arial, sans-serif.

The signature is positive tracking. Display text should feel wide-set and sign-like. Avoid tight tracking and negative tracking.

Recommended scale:

| Token | Size | Weight | Line Height | Letter Spacing |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Caption | 14px | 400 | 1.45 | 0.84px |
| Body Small | 16px | 400 | 1.37 | 0.64px |
| Body Large | 20px | 300 or 400 | 1.25 | 0.4px |
| Subheading | 26px | 300 | 1.2 | 0.52px |
| Heading Small | 30px | 500 or 700 | 1.15 | 0.9px |
| Heading | 36px | 500 or 700 | 1.15 | 1.08px |
| Heading Large | 46px | 300 | 1.1 | 1.84px |
| Display | 68px | 400 | 1.01 | 4.08px |
| Display XL | 75px | 400 | 1.0 | 4.5px |

Rules:

- Use positive tracking at every size.
- Use 0.04em to 0.06em tracking for 46px and larger text.
- Use 0.02em to 0.04em tracking for 14px to 20px text.
- Use uppercase for nav links, CTAs, and order links.
- Do not use negative tracking.

## Spacing And Shape

- Density: comfortable.
- Base unit: 4px.
- Max content width: 1200px.
- Section gap: 60px.
- Card padding: 30px.
- Standard element gap: 15px to 20px.
- Section padding: 30px to 60px vertically.

Radius:

- Cards: 0px.
- Badges: 1px.
- Inputs: 1px.
- Buttons: 1px.
- Circular utility: 40px diameter or 40px radius.
- Avoid 8px to 12px softened corners.

## Elevation

This system is flat.

- No drop shadows.
- No glow effects.
- No gradients.
- No raised cards.
- No framed photography.
- Hierarchy comes from color contrast, type scale, wide tracking, and image scale.

## Layout

- Use a full-bleed Black Olive hero and footer.
- Use Warm Cream for lighter menu and content sections.
- Keep content within a 1200px max-width container.
- Navigation is full-bleed dark, about 80px tall, with centered wordmark and split nav link groups.
- Hero uses large lemon display text over dark canvas and food photography.
- Menu sections use three-column food grids on cream.
- Featured dish sections use oversized food photography offset to one side with text on the other.
- Use 30px to 40px gaps around featured text stacks.

## Imagery

Preferred imagery:

- Editorial food photography.
- Overhead or three-quarter shots of plated dishes.
- Warm surfaces: terracotta, peach, mauve, and dark tabletops.
- Full-bleed food tiles without card chrome.

Avoid:

- People and lifestyle scenes.
- Small thumbnails.
- Thin framed images.
- Rounded image cards.
- Abstract graphics.
- Decorative illustrations.

## Motion

Motion should be minimal.

- Use simple hover underline for ghost order links.
- Use color shifts only when needed.
- Do not animate depth, glow, or image scale heavily.
- Keep the site feeling still, confident, and editorial.
