# Typography

## Primary Family

```css
font-family: VenusCom, "DM Sans", Outfit, "Plus Jakarta Sans", Arial, sans-serif;
```

Use VenusCom across headings, body, nav, buttons, and menu copy.

## Icon Family

```css
font-family: "Font Awesome 6 Free", Arial, sans-serif;
```

Use solid icons only for tiny arrows, chevrons, and social marks.

## Scale

| `subheading` | 26px | 300 | 1.2 | 0.52px |

## Rules

- Use positive tracking, never negative tracking.
- Use 0.04em to 0.06em for display text.
- Use 0.02em to 0.04em at body sizes.
- Use uppercase for nav labels, CTA labels, and order links.
- Keep body copy around 19px on menu descriptions when space allows.
