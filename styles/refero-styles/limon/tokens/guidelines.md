# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Lemon Zest as a filled button background only.
- Use lemon display text on dark sections.
- Keep display text above 46px with 0.04em to 0.06em positive tracking.
- Use 0px or 1px radius on cards, buttons, inputs, and badges.
- Use Black Olive as the default hero and dark section canvas.
- Use Warm Cream for lighter content sections.
- Use large edge-to-edge food photography.
- Pair a filled lemon CTA with a ghost Forest Ink order link when useful.
- Keep section padding between 30px and 60px vertically.
- Use 15px to 20px as the standard element gap.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce new accent hues.
- Do not use 8px to 12px radii.
- Do not use negative tracking.
- Do not put lemon CTA buttons on Warm Cream backgrounds.
- Do not add shadows, glows, or gradients.
- Do not use Forest Ink as a button fill.
- Do not frame food images with borders, rounded corners, or shadows.
- Do not shrink display headlines below 46px.
- Do not use lifestyle photography as the primary image language.
