# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use full-bleed dark sections with `#1d0b0d`.
- Use Warm Cream content sections as visual breathers.
- Keep containers capped at 1200px.
- Navigation is a dark 80px full-width bar with centered wordmark.
- Hero should combine dark canvas, lemon display text, warm cream supporting copy, and food photography.
- Menu grids use three columns on desktop.
- Food cards are photographs first, captions second, with no card chrome.
- Featured sections use large offset food photography and a left text stack.
- Footer can return to the Black Olive canvas.

## Imagery

Use:

- Editorial food photography.
- Overhead or three-quarter plated food images.
- Warm-toned surfaces: terracotta, peach, mauve, and dark tabletops.
- Square or near-square image tiles.
- Large full-bleed dish banners.

Avoid:

- People-first lifestyle photography.
- Small thumbnails.
- Rounded or framed images.
- Abstract graphics.
- Decorative illustrations.
- Product mockups unrelated to food.
