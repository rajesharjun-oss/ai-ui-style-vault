# Components

## Top Navigation Bar

- Role: full-site header.
- Background: `#1d0b0d`.
- Height: about 80px.
- Wordmark: centered, VenusCom around 46px, weight 500, warm cream text.
- Links: uppercase, 16px, weight 400, letter-spacing 0.04em, warm cream.
- Left group: page links.
- Right group: social or follow link.
- Active item: transparent with 1px white border and 1px radius.

## Filled Lemon CTA Button

- Role: ordering or reservation action.
- Background: `#f7ea48`.
- Text: `#1d0b0d`.
- Type: VenusCom 16px, weight 600, uppercase, 0.04em tracking.
- Padding: 12px 16px.
- Radius: 1px.
- Border: none.
- Shadow: none.
- Use sparingly on dark surfaces.

## Ghost Outlined Navigation Item

- Role: active/current nav state.
- Background: transparent.
- Border: `1px solid #ffffff`.
- Radius: 1px.
- Text: `#fcf9f0`.
- Padding: about 5px to 6px horizontally.

## Hero Overlay Display Text

- Role: large editorial brand or hero phrase.
- Color: `#f7ea48`.
- Size: 75px.
- Weight: 400.
- Line height: 1.
- Letter spacing: 4.5px.
- Place over dark canvas and food photography without a plate.

## Hero Sub-Line

- Role: supporting sentence under display text.
- Color: `#fcf9f0`.
- Size: 20px.
- Weight: 400.
- Letter spacing: 0.4px.
- Gap below display: about 20px.

## Food Card Grid

- Role: menu item tiles.
- Layout: three columns on desktop.
- Image: square or near-square food photograph, edge-to-edge.
- Card chrome: none.
- Border: none.
- Radius: 0px.
- Shadow: none.
- Caption gap: about 30px below image.
- Heading: 20px VenusCom in Black Olive or Forest Ink.
- Body: about 19px.

## Ghost Order Link

- Role: tertiary per-item CTA.
- Background: none.
- Border: none.
- Text: uppercase Forest Ink or Black Olive.
- Type: VenusCom 16px, weight 500, 0.04em tracking.
- Icon: small right arrow at 14px.
- Gap: 4px to 15px depending layout.
- Hover: underline only.

## Back-To-Top Circular Button

- Role: fixed scroll utility.
- Size: 40px diameter.
- Background: `#103b15`.
- Icon: `#fcf9f0`.
- Position: fixed bottom-right with about 24px inset.
- Border: none.
- Shadow: none.

## Featured Dish Banner

- Role: full-bleed editorial food section.
- Background: `#fcf9f0`.
- Image: large food photograph offset right, around 55 percent width.
- Text stack: left side, 30px to 40px gaps.
- Heading: 30px to 36px.
- Body: about 19px.

## Section Heading Block

- Heading: 30px to 36px VenusCom, weight 500.
- Tracking: around 1px.
- Color: Forest Ink on cream or Warm Cream on dark.
- Body: 19px, weight 400.
- Gap between heading and body: 20px to 30px.

## Body Paragraph

- Type: VenusCom 19px, weight 400.
- Line height: 1.3.
- Letter spacing: 0.57px.
- Color: Forest Ink on cream, Warm Cream on dark.
- Max width: about 640px.
