## Use This Style When

Use Limon when the interface should feel like a moody restaurant or hospitality editorial: dark olive canvas, warm cream content bands, strong food photography, sharp rectangular controls, and a single lemon-yellow accent. It is a good fit for restaurant sites, food ordering, menus, culinary portfolios, supper clubs, and editorial product pages that need confident atmosphere.

## Quick DNA

- Dark olive-black canvas with warm cream breathing sections.
- One saturated accent: lemon yellow `#f7ea48`.
- Deep forest green for text, dividers, and ghost links on cream sections.
- Wide positive tracking across display and UI text.
- Near-zero radius: 0px to 1px for cards, buttons, badges, and inputs.
- No shadows, glows, or gradients.
- Food photography is full-bleed, large, and edge-to-edge.
