# Guidelines

## Do

- Use gelica 600 at 104px for lowercase display headlines.
- Use Cocoa Ink for headlines.
- Use Marker Orange only for handwritten captions, highlights, underlines, and footer band.
- Use Charcoal for all text, borders, button strokes, and structural edges.
- Round buttons and tags to 20px.
- Round cards to 12px.
- Reserve 56px radius for the footer top edge.
- Keep the canvas at Cream Paper.
- Use Dew Drop as subtle surface tint.
- Let photographed notebooks, pencils, and labels be the hero.
- Scatter stickers at 5 to 15 degree rotations.

## Do Not

- Do not use Sky Sticker, Bubblegum Sticker, or Sprout Sticker for functional UI.
- Do not use filled CTA buttons.
- Do not capitalize headlines.
- Do not add letter spacing.
- Do not use heavy shadows.
- Do not center-align multi-line display stacks after the first line.
- Do not introduce gradients, glassmorphism, or neon accents.
- Do not use Geist for headlines.
