# Typography

## Font Roles

| Font | Role | Weights | Sizes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| gelica | Display, headings, body, annotations | 400, 500, 600 | 16px to 104px |
| Geist | Secondary body, nav, fine labels | 400, 500 | 18px, 20px, 32px |

## Typography Rules

- Use lowercase display headlines.
- Do not apply letter spacing.
- Use gelica for all display and headline moments.
- Keep Geist as a supporting UI typeface.
- Do not center-align multi-line headlines after the first line.
- Keep display stacks under three lines.
