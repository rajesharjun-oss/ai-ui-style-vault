# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use an asymmetric editorial layout.
- Keep the top-left brand mark small.
- Keep the top-right action persistent.
- Hero pattern: left copy and CTA, right product photo.
- Let product imagery break the container when needed.
- Keep most content left-aligned.
- Alternate product photography blocks with typographic moments.
- Use a 1200px max-width as the main guide, but keep the hero spontaneous.

## Imagery

Use:

- Leather notebook photography.
- Colored pencils.
- Sticker-laminated name labels.
- Blue cloud and rose-pink product variants.
- Sticker characters with dark outlines.
- Hand-drawn arrows.
- Marker-orange captions.

Avoid:

- Abstract UI illustrations.
- 3D renders.
- Stock photography.
- Glass or neon effects.
- Product-free hero art.

The product is the imagery.
