# Components

## Pill Action Button

Cream Paper fill, 1.5px Charcoal border, 20px radius, 28px horizontal padding, 10px to 11px vertical padding, gelica 16px weight 500 Charcoal text, subtle button shadow.

## Display Headline

Gelica 104px, weight 600, line-height 1.08, lowercase, Cocoa Ink, normal tracking. Keep it tactile and left-leaning.

## Handwritten Caption

Gelica 20px to 24px weight 400 in Marker Orange. Slight rotation is welcome. Pair with a hand-drawn arrow.

## Marker Highlight

Marker Orange inline word or phrase with rough hand-drawn underline, 2px to 3px stroke.

## Product Notebook

Photographed notebook object with sticker label, colored pencil, and slight 5 to 8 degree tilt. It is the hero visual.

## Name Label Sticker

White card, 1px Charcoal border, 8px radius, Name, Class, and Roll No fields in handwritten-feeling gelica.

## Sticker Illustration

Flat sticker characters with 2px dark outlines and vivid sticker fills. Rotate 5 to 15 degrees and avoid grid alignment.

## Hand-Drawn Arrow

1.5px Charcoal curved arrow with hand wobble, connecting a caption to a product object.

## Pre-Order Info Block

Gelica 16px weight 400, Cocoa Ink, 8px below the primary button, no icon.

## Footer Brand Band

Full-width Marker Orange band with 56px asymmetric top radius. Use gelica text in Charcoal or Cream Paper.

## Top-Left Brand Mark

Small illustrated hand or pointing icon in Charcoal, about 32px, placed at the top-left with 16px margin.

## Top-Right Action

Persistent outlined pill action, same as the main button, top-right with 16px margin.
