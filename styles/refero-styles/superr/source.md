## Capture Notes

The Refero page describes Superr as a warm schoolyard notebook aesthetic. It uses a cream paper canvas, rounded lowercase display type, photographed product objects, dark outlined pill buttons, marker-orange annotations, and decorative sticker illustrations.

The AI-useful pieces to preserve are:

- Cream Paper `#fdfbf9` is the page canvas.
- Dew Drop `#f7efe9` is the secondary warm surface tint.
- Cocoa Ink `#2b1a07` is the headline color.
- Charcoal `#171717` owns text, borders, strokes, and structure.
- Marker Orange `#ff6f1e` is only for annotations, underlines, highlights, and footer band.
- Sky, Bubblegum, and Sprout are sticker illustration colors only, not UI colors.
- Headline type stays lowercase with normal tracking.
- Product photography is the hero, not abstract UI illustration.
- Buttons are outlined cream pills, not filled CTAs.
