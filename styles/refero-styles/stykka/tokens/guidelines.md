# Guidelines

## Do

- Use a white canvas and black text as the complete UI foundation.
- Let product and interior photography create warmth.
- Keep images hard-edged.
- Use regular-weight display typography.
- Use transparent outlined CTAs with black borders.
- Use 1px black rules only when structure is needed.
- Keep spacing generous and alignment exact.
- Make the footer the only large black surface.

## Do Not

- Do not add drop shadows.
- Do not use colored buttons.
- Do not add decorative gradients.
- Do not round image containers.
- Do not use glassmorphism or blurred nav backgrounds.
- Do not create card-heavy layouts.
- Do not make the typography loud or chunky.
- Do not use image overlays as a default.
- Do not add icons unless they serve a clear utility purpose.

## Implementation Priorities

1. Choose strong real photography.
2. Set the page canvas to white.
3. Establish the transparent nav and full-bleed hero.
4. Use Inter with regular-weight display type.
5. Build content sections with white space and precise grids.
6. Add only the minimum borders and interactions needed.

## Common Failure Modes

- The page becomes generic when photography is weak.
- The system loses its quiet premium tone when buttons become colored.
- The design feels too software-like if everything is boxed into cards.
- The hero becomes heavy if a dark overlay is added automatically.
