# Layout And Imagery

## Page Structure

1. Transparent nav over full-bleed hero.
2. Hero with interior/product photograph, label, display headline, and one outlined CTA.
3. White editorial section with short intro copy.
4. Hard-edged feature or product image grid.
5. Product detail or process section using simple columns.
6. Optional mono spec callout.
7. Full-width black footer.

## Grid

- Desktop: use a 1280px max-width rail.
- Large product grids: 4 columns.
- Editorial image grids: 2 or 3 columns.
- Tablet: 2 columns.
- Mobile: 1 column.

Keep grid gutters tight, usually 10-15px, so the page feels precise rather than airy in a decorative way.

## Hero Image Rules

- Use full-bleed real photography.
- No decorative color overlay.
- No blur.
- No device mockup frames.
- No rounded container.
- Use the crop to secure text contrast.

## Product Imagery

Use images that show:

- Kitchens and storage systems.
- Material finishes.
- Rooms with natural light.
- Close details of hardware or surfaces.
- Human-scale interiors when available.

Avoid generic stock office scenes, abstract textures, AI-looking interiors, and isolated products floating on colored backgrounds.

## Composition

The page should feel more like an editorial showroom than a SaaS dashboard. Give images room, keep text compact, and use black hairlines only where they clarify the layout.
