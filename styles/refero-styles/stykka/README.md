## Style Summary

Stykka is a white-light Scandinavian showroom system. It is almost entirely achromatic: white canvas, black ink, thin black rules, transparent outlined buttons, and warm product photography. The brand feeling comes from kitchens, materials, and space, not from UI decoration.

Use this style when an AI needs to build a quiet premium commerce or interior-product interface where images carry the emotion and the interface behaves like careful editorial framing.

## What To Borrow

- Full-bleed hero photography with no dark overlay, blur, or color wash.
- White page canvas with black text, black 1px borders, and generous whitespace.
- Lightweight Inter typography, especially 400-weight display headlines with tight tracking.
- Transparent outlined CTAs that invert only on hover.
- Hard-edged image grids with very small gutters.
- Minimal absolute nav over the hero: left links, centered wordmark, right language plus CTA.
- Black footer band as the only large non-white UI surface.

## Avoid

- Drop shadows, glass panels, tinted cards, or soft elevation.
- Extra accent colors beyond the warmth already inside photography.
- Rounded image containers.
- Heavy headline weights.
- Pill CTAs or colored primary buttons.
- Decorative gradients, illustrations, or abstract background shapes.
