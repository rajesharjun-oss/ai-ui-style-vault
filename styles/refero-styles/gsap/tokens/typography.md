# Typography

## Families

| Family | Token | Fallback | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mori | `--font-mori` | Inter Tight, Sohne, DM Sans | Entire interface: display, body, nav, buttons, labels, cards, and footer. |

## Rules

- Use Mori-style type everywhere.
- Use weight 400 for body, nav, labels, cards, and UI.
- Use weight 600 for display, major headings, and outlined pill buttons.
- Set the hero display to 224px, line-height 0.9, and -0.02em tracking.
- Do not introduce serif or a second display family.
