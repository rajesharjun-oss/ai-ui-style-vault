# Components

## Outlined Cream Pill Button

Transparent fill, Surface Cream text, 1px Surface Cream border, 100px radius, 15px vertical and 24px horizontal padding, Mori 18px/600. Use for navigation entry points, explore links, and secondary actions.

## Gradient-Stroked CTA Pill

Transparent fill, Surface Cream text, 1.5px to 2px green-to-light-green gradient stroke, 100px radius. Reserve for the main get/download action.

## Ghost Nav Link

No background, no border, Surface Cream or Surface 50 text, Mori 16px/400, compact row gaps, hover shift toward Surface Cream.

## Borderless Icon Button

Round icon-only control, no visible background, cream icon, compact hit area. Use for utility controls.

## Category Color Label

Mori 19px to 24px/400, single-word discipline label. Keep the mapping fixed: GSAP green, SVG orange, Scroll pink, Text lilac, UI blue.

## Curly-Bracket Annotation

Mori 16px to 19px/400 Surface Cream text wrapped in literal curly braces. Use before every major section.

## Hero Display Headline

Mori 224px/600, line-height 0.9, -0.02em tracking, Surface Cream, wrapped across large lines and allowed to bleed toward viewport edges.

## Tool Feature Block

Two-column row with organic gradient illustration, discipline color label, cream heading, body copy, outlined pill, and a 1px Surface 25 divider.

## Organic Gradient Illustration

Soft 3D-like pill, dome, liquid blob, or splash using the discipline accent gradient. No frame and no drop shadow.

## Showcase Card

Off Black or near-black surface, 8px radius, 24px padding, cream heading at 24px to 33px, contained preview area, no visible border.

## Announcement Banner

Full-bleed dark band, centered 14px Mori copy in Surface Cream, optional GSAP Green inline link.

## Footer

Off Black footer, 1px Surface 25 top divider, multi-column cream links, muted secondary text, and 60px to 80px vertical padding.
