## What To Preserve

GSAP uses a warm cream-on-black foundation and then maps vivid colors to animation disciplines. Color functions as taxonomy, not general decoration. Buttons stay outlined, typography is the hero, and organic gradient illustrations overlap the layout rather than sitting inside tidy frames.

The page relies on:

- Just Black for the page canvas.
- Surface Cream for headings, body copy, borders, buttons, and nav links.
- Off Black for nested surfaces and footer.
- Surface 25 for dividers and low-contrast outlines.
- Surface 50 for secondary text and disabled states.
- Green for GSAP/core labels.
- Orange for SVG labels and tool visuals.
- Pink for Scroll labels and tool visuals.
- Lilac for Text labels and tool visuals.
- Blue for UI labels and tool visuals.
- Mori as the single-family system.
- 224px display headlines with tight tracking and line-height 0.9.
- 100px radius outlined pill buttons.
- Curly-bracket annotations before major sections.
- No filled CTA buttons.
