# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Parchment as the full page canvas.
- Use Bone for product cards and screenshot frames.
- Keep headings weight 400 and progressively tighter as they get larger.
- Use 4px radius for buttons, cards, inputs, and tiles.
- Use Ember for inline links and tiny emphasis only.
- Use EB Garamond selectively for editorial prose and subheads.
- Use berkeleyMono 12px for code, paths, CLI snippets, and metadata.
- Pair one dark primary action with one light secondary action when needed.
- Let product screenshots and IDE windows carry visual weight.

## Don't

- Do not use pure white or pure black as foundational colors.
- Do not use weight 600 or 700 for display headings.
- Do not use pill buttons or round cards.
- Do not introduce gradients, glows, bokeh, or large color washes.
- Do not tint shadows blue or make the style glassy.
- Do not use Ember as a background or large surface color.
- Do not use system-ui for prominent headings.
- Do not stack three filled buttons in one action group.
