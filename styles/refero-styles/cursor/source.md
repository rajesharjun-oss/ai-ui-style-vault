## What To Preserve

Cursor uses a warm cream page canvas, warm near-black type, compact spacing, and very sharp 4px surface geometry. The interface should feel printed, restrained, and technical. Headings stay light at weight 400 and become more tightly tracked as they grow. Ember orange is punctuation for inline links and emphasis, not a button fill or background color.

The page relies on:

- Parchment as the full page background.
- Bone as the product-card surface.
- Ink as primary text and primary action fill.
- Stone hairlines instead of heavy borders.
- Ember as a text-only accent.
- Amber and Forest as small in-product action colors.
- CursorGothic for UI, navigation, headings, and body.
- EB Garamond only for editorial prose and subheads.
- berkeleyMono for CLI snippets, file tabs, code, and metadata.
- 4px radius for buttons, cards, inputs, and tiles.
- Warm soft elevation for product mockup cards.
