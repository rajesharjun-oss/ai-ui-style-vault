# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Anthropic Serif at `20px` for body copy.
- Use Anthropic Sans at `12px` to `16px` for UI chrome.
- Use Ivory Medium as the page canvas and Ivory Light for cards.
- Use Manilla only when a card should feel like a featured editorial spread.
- Use bottom-only `8px` radius on filled ivory buttons.
- Use Clay only for the most consequential CTA on a page.
- Keep inline links persistently underlined.
- Use `24px` radius on card-level surfaces.
- Pair the `61px` sans display system with the `68px` serif editorial display system.

## Don't

- Do not introduce cool grays, blues, or colors outside the warm ivory/oat/clay family.
- Do not use box shadows for elevation.
- Do not use Clay for decoration, icons, hover states, or non-CTA elements.
- Do not set body text in sans-serif when the editorial publication voice matters.
- Do not apply uniform generic border radius to filled buttons.
- Do not use bright pure white as a main surface.
- Do not add gradients, glows, or color washes to backgrounds.
- Do not turn badges into filled chips.
