# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Rhythm

- Use a warm parchment page canvas.
- Keep content centered at about `1280px` max width.
- Use `80px` to `120px` section gaps.
- Hero compositions can be asymmetric two-column layouts.
- Use release grids with three quiet paper cards when needed.
- Use a dark full-bleed footer as the final inversion.
- Keep visual density low: large text blocks, whitespace, and a few editorial cards.

## Imagery

- Use vintage scientific illustration cues, field-journal plates, botanical or naturalist collage, and warm editorial art.
- Keep illustrations harmonized with parchment surfaces.
- Avoid photography, product screenshots, dashboard mockups, abstract gradients, and glossy 3D renders.
- Use minimal iconography: chevrons, sparse line indicators, and warm-neutral strokes.

## Surface Strategy

- Canvas: Ivory Medium.
- Standard cards: Ivory Light.
- Editorial emphasis: Manilla.
- Grouped warm panels: Oat Warm.
- Inversion: Slate Dark footer or consent band.
- Elevation: surface tone shift plus optional 1px Stone border.

## Similar Design Neighborhood

- Research publication sites with serif body copy.
- Warm editorial AI product pages.
- Minimal documentation pages with persistent inline links.
- Paper-based layouts that avoid cold tech-blue UI conventions.
