# Components

## Filled Dark CTA Button

- Role: primary action.
- Fill: `#202020`.
- Text: `#ffffff`.
- Radius: 9999px.
- Padding: 12px 24px.
- Type: Plus Jakarta Sans 14px, weight 700.
- Border: none.
- Shadow: none.

## Ghost Outline Button

- Role: secondary or alternate action.
- Fill: transparent.
- Border: `1px solid #e8e8e8` or `1px solid #0091ff`.
- Text: `#202020` or `#0091ff`.
- Radius: 9999px.
- Padding: 10px 20px.
- Type: Plus Jakarta Sans 14px, weight 700.

## Nav Pill Button

- Role: compact header item.
- Fill: transparent, with `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` on hover.
- Text: `#2a2a2a`.
- Radius: 9999px.
- Padding: 4px 12px.
- Type: 14px to 16px.

## Feature Tag Pill

- Role: feature category, active filter, or product label.
- Fill: transparent or `#f8f9fa`.
- Text: `#202020` or `#0091ff`.
- Border: optional `1px solid #e8e8e8`.
- Radius: 9999px.
- Padding: 8px 16px.
- Type: 14px, weight 700.

## Product Screenshot Card

- Role: hero product proof.
- Fill: none.
- Radius: 12px on container, 16px to 32px on screenshot if needed.
- Padding: 0.
- Shadow: optional soft directional lift only.
- Content: dense dashboard UI, status pills, task rows, sidebar, avatars.

## Stat Callout Card

- Role: social proof metric.
- Fill: `#ffffff`.
- Border: none.
- Shadow: none.
- Padding: 28px.
- Number: 60px to 80px Plus Jakarta Sans 700, `#090c1d`, tracking around -0.04em.
- Caption: 16px to 18px Inter, `#646464`.

## Dark Feature Card

- Role: contrast section or premium feature block.
- Fill: dark fade from `#111111` to `#000000`.
- Radius: 12px to 16px.
- Padding: 40px to 80px vertical.
- Heading: white, 48px to 60px Plus Jakarta Sans 650.
- Body: `#b3b3b3`, 16px Inter.

## Trust Badge Strip

- Role: trusted-by logo row.
- Layout: horizontal row.
- Logos: grayscale from `#838383` to `#202020`.
- Label: Sometype Mono 10px uppercase, 0.08em tracking, `#838383`.
- No cards, borders, or backgrounds.

## Rounded Avatar Cluster

- Role: users inside product UI.
- Shape: circular, 9999px radius.
- Size: 24px to 32px.
- Overlap: about 8px.
- Separator: 1px to 2px white stroke.

## Award Badge

- Role: G2 or award/social proof card.
- Fill: white.
- Border: `1px solid #d4d4d4`.
- Radius: 8px.
- Padding: 16px.
- Arrange in 3-column grid.

## Conic Gradient Border Element

- Role: signature expressive CTA or premium element.
- Use an animated conic rainbow border around a pill or card.
- Border: about 1.5px to 2px.
- Inner fill: white.
- Radius: 9999px for pills, 12px to 20px for cards.
- Use once per section at most.

## Checkmark List Item

- Role: benefit bullet.
- Check icon: `#0091ff`, around 18px.
- Lead text: `#202020`, 14px to 16px, weight 600 to 700.
- Descriptor: `#646464`, regular weight.
- Row gap: 8px to 12px.

## Status Pill

- Role: in-product status indicator.
- Radius: 9999px.
- Text: 10px to 12px, weight 600.
- Padding: 3px 9px.
- Done: Mint surface with dark text.
- In progress: Signal Blue fill with white text.
- Overdue: pink or magenta fill.
