# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Plus Jakarta Sans weight 650 to 800 for display text 34px and larger.
- Use `#202020` instead of pure black for primary action fills and main headlines.
- Use 9999px radius for buttons, tags, and badges.
- Use `#6647f0` only for brand badges and identity moments.
- Use `#0091ff` for links, outlined actions, checkmarks, and active tag text.
- Keep spacing on a 4px base.
- Use `1px solid #e8e8e8` as the default structural border.
- Add one rotating conic-gradient border to one hero or premium element per section.
- Use real product screenshots as the main visual proof.

## Do Not

- Do not use pure black for large text fills or large backgrounds.
- Do not use brand violet as a primary CTA fill.
- Do not use Inter for display headings.
- Do not use Plus Jakarta Sans below 14px.
- Do not use positive body tracking.
- Do not stack multiple shadow or elevation levels in one section.
- Do not add decorative gradients to normal cards or section backgrounds.
- Do not use lifestyle photography or stock people imagery.
- Do not animate every component.
