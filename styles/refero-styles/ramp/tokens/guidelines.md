# Guidelines

## Do

- Use `lausanne` at weight `400` only.
- Enable `font-feature-settings: "ss01";`.
- Use `#e4f222` as the only chromatic accent.
- Reserve the accent for primary actions, active states, counters, and money signals.
- Use `#f4f2f0` for the canvas and `#ffffff` for cards.
- Use 1px hairline borders for structure.
- Use 64px, 96px, or 128px section spacing.
- Use radii from the 6/10/12/16px scale.
- Use product screenshots and browser-frame UI visuals.
- Keep text left aligned inside centered containers.

## Do Not

- Do not add blue, green, purple, orange, or red accent colors.
- Do not use bold, semibold, or heavy type weights.
- Do not add shadows to cards, modals, or panels.
- Do not use `#0c0a08` as a large background. Use `#1a1919` for inverted sections.
- Do not center body copy or long headings.
- Do not use decorative gradients.
- Do not use stock photography or lifestyle images.
- Do not create fully rounded pills.
- Do not use 4px corner radius.
