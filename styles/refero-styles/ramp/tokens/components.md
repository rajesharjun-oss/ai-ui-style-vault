- Full-width strip with `#1a1919` background.
- Labels: uppercase 10px, `#6d6c6b`.
- Values: `#ffffff`, around 14px.
- Use subtle opacity transitions only.

## Sticky Navigation

- Paper surface.
- 1px bottom border.
- Announcement strip above, around 40px tall.
- Main nav plus announcement total height around 102px.
- May use subtle inset white highlight.

## Logo Grid Cell

- Transparent fill.
- Hairline grid borders.
- Grayscale logos only.
