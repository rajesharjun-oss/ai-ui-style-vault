## Use This Style When

Use Ramp when the interface should feel like a sharp financial publication: calm, editorial, trustworthy, and operational. The system is nearly monochrome, with one vivid yellow-green signal reserved for money movement, primary actions, live counters, and active states.

## Quick DNA

- Warm off-white canvas with white cards and near-black text.
- One chromatic accent: `#e4f222`.
- Flat components with hairline borders and almost no shadow.
- Lausanne-style neo-grotesque typography at weight `400` only.
- Hierarchy comes from scale, spacing, and tracking instead of boldness.
- Editorial layout: left aligned text, wide centered containers, large product screenshots, and one dark counter strip.
