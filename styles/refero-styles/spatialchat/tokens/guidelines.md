# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Brand Violet only for filled CTAs, announcement bars, New badges, active states, and bullet markers.
- Keep buttons at 12px radius.
- Keep cards at 16px radius.
- Use Satoshi 700 at 40px to 60px for major headings.
- Pair filled violet and ghost bordered CTAs in hero sections.
- Keep all shadows at 6 percent black.
- Use Fog for borders and Mist for input borders.
- Use Cloud for canvas and Paper for cards.
- Use product screenshots as visual proof.

## Do Not

- Do not add a second accent color.
- Do not use violet for body text or generic borders.
- Do not set button radius outside roughly 10px to 14px.
- Do not use colored shadows or darker shadows.
- Do not use Satoshi weights below 400 or above 700.
- Do not center-align long body copy.
- Do not place violet controls on Tinted Highlight.
- Do not use dark full-bleed sections.
- Do not use lifestyle stock photography.
