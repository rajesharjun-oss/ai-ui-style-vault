# Components

## Announcement Bar

Full-width Brand Violet band with centered Paper text, Satoshi 16px/500, and 12px vertical padding. This is the only full-bleed color band.

## Primary CTA Button

Brand Violet fill, Paper text, 12px radius, Satoshi 16px/500, 12px 20px padding, and subtle 0 1px 2px shadow.

## Ghost CTA Button

Transparent fill, 1px Ink Black border, Ink Black text, 12px radius, same size as primary. May include a trailing arrow icon.

## Navigation Link

Satoshi 16px/500, Ink Black, no underline, 8px horizontal padding, optional small chevron.

## New Badge

Tinted Highlight fill, Brand Violet text, Satoshi 12px/500, 8px radius, 4px 10px padding.

## Login Link

Quiet transparent nav action, Satoshi 15px/500, Ink Black, 12px radius, 12px 20px padding.

## Hero Headline

Centered Satoshi 60px/700, Ink Black, line-height 1.30. Pair with 18px Slate subhead and CTA pair.

## Social Proof Row

White trust strip with review badges, star rows, short proof labels, and 16px gutters.

## Logo Strip Card

Full-width Paper row with 40px vertical padding. Keep logos in native colors and constrain content to 1200px.

## Section Heading

Centered Satoshi 40px/700 headline, 18px Slate paragraph below, 64px rhythm around the section.

## Feature Section

Two-column block with 48px gap. Left side: tag pill, 32px heading, feature list, stat grid, and CTA. Right side: screenshot card.

## Feature List Item

8px Brand Violet bullet, 12px gap, 16px/600 title, 14px/400 description.

## Stat Block

No card shell. 24px/700 number in Ink Black, 14px/400 label in Slate.

## Product Screenshot Card

Paper surface, 16px radius, `0 4px 28px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)` shadow. Image fills the card within the radius.
