# Typography

## Font

- Primary: Satoshi
- Fallback: Inter or General Sans
- Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Letter Spacing | Token |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caption | 14px | 400 | 1.43 | normal | `--text-caption` |
| Body Small | 16px | 400 | 1.5 | normal | `--text-body-sm` |
| Body | 18px | 400 | 1.56 | normal | `--text-body` |
| Subheading | 20px | 600 | 1.5 | normal | `--text-subheading` |
| Heading Small | 24px | 600 | 1.4 | normal | `--text-heading-sm` |
| Heading | 32px | 600 | 1.38 | normal | `--text-heading` |
| Heading Large | 40px | 700 | 1.3 | normal | `--text-heading-lg` |
| Display | 60px | 700 | 1.3 | normal | `--text-display` |

## Typography Rules

- Use Satoshi across the whole interface.
- Use 700 for hero and section headlines.
- Use 600 for subheadings and feature titles.
- Use 500 for buttons, badges, and nav labels.
- Use 400 for paragraphs and labels.
- Do not use weights below 400 or above 700.
- Avoid centered body paragraphs longer than two lines.
