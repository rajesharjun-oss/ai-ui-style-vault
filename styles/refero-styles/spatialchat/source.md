## Capture Notes

The Refero page describes SpatialChat as a light SaaS marketing surface with violet used as a single disciplined accent. The design uses a 1200px container, centered hero stack, paired CTA buttons, social proof, logo strip, and two-column feature sections with product screenshot cards.

The AI-useful pieces to preserve are:

- Brand Violet appears only on filled CTAs, announcement bars, New badges, active states, and bullet markers.
- Satoshi is the single-family type system, with 700 for 40px to 60px display moments.
- Cloud `#f9fafb` is the page canvas, while Paper `#ffffff` is used for cards and nav.
- Buttons use 12px radius; cards use 16px radius.
- Shadows are always 6 percent black and very soft.
- Hero CTA pattern is filled violet button plus ghost bordered button.
- Product screenshots are the main imagery.
