# Implementation Prompt

Build in the SpatialChat style: an airy light SaaS marketing page with Cloud off-white canvas, Paper white cards, Satoshi typography, soft 6 percent black shadows, and one disciplined violet accent.

Use these rules:

- Page canvas: `#f9fafb`.
- Card/nav/input surface: `#ffffff`.
- Accent: `#5727e7`.
- Highlight chip surface: `#f2f2ff`.
- Heading text: `#030712`.
- Body text: `#4b5563`.
- Borders: `#e5e7eb`.
- Input borders: `#d1d5db`.
- Font: Satoshi, with Inter or General Sans fallback.
- Display: 60px, weight 700, line-height 1.3.
- Section heading: 40px, weight 700, line-height 1.3.
- Feature heading: 32px, weight 600, line-height 1.38.
- Body: 18px, weight 400, line-height 1.56.
- Buttons: 12px radius, Satoshi 16px/500, 12px 20px padding.
- Cards: 16px radius, 24px padding.
- Badges: 8px radius.
- Max width: 1200px.
- Section gap: 64px.
- Element gap: 12px.
- Feature column gap: 48px.
- Shadows: only `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.

Design structure:

1. Full-width violet announcement bar.
2. White top nav with logo, menu links, Login, ghost CTA, and filled violet CTA.
3. Centered hero with 60px headline, 18px subhead, filled violet CTA plus ghost CTA.
4. Social proof row.
5. Full-width logo strip.
6. Centered section headings.
7. Alternating two-column feature sections with text on one side and product screenshot cards on the other.

Use product screenshots as imagery. Show video rooms, chat panels, avatar bubbles, polls, and collaboration UI. Native logo colors are allowed only in the client logo strip.

Avoid:

- Second accent colors.
- Dark full-bleed sections.
- Violet text for body copy.
- Colored shadows.
- Shadows darker than 6 percent black.
- Overly rounded pill buttons.
- Centered paragraphs longer than two lines.
- Stock photography or abstract illustrations.

The result should feel spacious, modern, product-led, and approachable.
