# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Lavender for the primary action button only.
- Set display headlines at 60px/600 with -3px tracking.
- Use 9999px radius for every button, chip, and tag.
- Apply 1px Fog borders to cards and tables.
- Pair Mint text with Mint Wash backgrounds for positive metric callouts.
- Use OpenRunde weight 500 for headings and buttons.
- Use OpenRunde weight 400 for body and captions.
- Center the hero headline and CTA stack.
- Keep feature cards visually light and mostly background-free.

## Do Not

- Do not use Iris for body CTAs.
- Do not use large dramatic shadows.
- Do not mix multiple accent colors in one component.
- Do not use pure black for headings.
- Do not use Ember for UI chrome.
- Do not break the pill convention with rectangular buttons or sharp tags.
- Do not add background fills to feature card containers.
- Do not use lifestyle imagery or people-focused marketing photos.
