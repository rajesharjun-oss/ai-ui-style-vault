# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Obsidian and Deep Space as the dominant background.
- Use Charcoal Glass for cards and panels.
- Use one violet-to-teal gradient action path.
- Use Inter for UI and JetBrains Mono for metadata.
- Use waveform and transcript visuals as product proof.
- Use pill shapes for voice and command controls.
- Use status colors sparingly.
- Keep the interface dark, private, and focused.

## Do Not

- Do not use bright white page backgrounds.
- Do not introduce many unrelated neon colors.
- Do not make every card glow.
- Do not use generic AI stock imagery.
- Do not use heavy gradients as full section backgrounds.
- Do not make the UI feel like a generic music player unless voice recording is the main mode.
