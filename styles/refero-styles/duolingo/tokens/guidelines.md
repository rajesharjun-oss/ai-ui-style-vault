# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Eager Green for progress, green display headings, primary CTA fills, and footer band.
- Use Spark Blue for links and outlined secondary CTAs.
- Keep body text Pencil Gray at 17px/500.
- Use Feather-style display at 48-64px only.
- Use 12px radius for all buttons, pills, tags, and nav items.
- Use 2px borders for outlined controls.
- Pair one text block with one large illustration per section.
- Keep the page mostly white.
- Use uppercase labels only for nav, language pills, and small actions.

## Don't

- Do not put colored text in body paragraphs.
- Do not use sharp corners.
- Do not use gradients, shadows, glows, or glass effects.
- Do not use display type below 48px.
- Do not apply illustration-only colors to UI chrome.
- Do not use green for dense small text.
- Do not place green or blue controls on green backgrounds without contrast checks.

## Accessibility Notes

- Test white text on Eager Green for CTA and footer contrast.
- Use visible focus states on outlined controls; a thicker blue or dark outline is acceptable.
- Avoid green-on-green and blue-on-green action states.
- Keep body copy width near 480px for comfortable reading.
