# Colors

## Brand

| Name | Value | Token | Use |
|------|-------|-------|-----|
| Eager Green | `#58cc02` | `--color-eager-green` | Progress, display headings, primary CTA fills, and footer band. |
| Storybook Green | `#d7ffb8` | `--color-storybook-green` | Soft green wash and highlighted footer labels. |

## Accent

| Name | Value | Token | Use |
|------|-------|-------|-----|
| Spark Blue | `#1cb0f6` | `--color-spark-blue` | Interactive links and outlined secondary CTAs. |
| Fresh Leaf | `#a5ed6e` | `--color-fresh-leaf` | Footer links and green-band supporting text. |
| Night Ink | `#000437` | `--color-night-ink` | Deep accent text and dark emphasis surfaces. |

## Neutrals

| Name | Value | Token | Use |
|------|-------|-------|-----|
| Paper White | `#ffffff` | `--color-paper-white` | Page canvas, card surfaces, and text on green or dark fills. |
| Charcoal | `#4b4b4b` | `--color-charcoal` | Hero headings and primary dark copy. |
| Pencil Gray | `#777777` | `--color-pencil-gray` | Body paragraphs and secondary descriptive text. |
| Faded Gray | `#afafaf` | `--color-faded-gray` | Button borders, disabled states, and low-emphasis nav labels. |

## Rules

- Use green for progress, headings, CTA fills, and footer band.
- Use blue for links and secondary actions.
- Keep body copy gray.
- Keep secondary illustration colors inside the artwork.
- Do not use green for dense small text or body paragraphs.
