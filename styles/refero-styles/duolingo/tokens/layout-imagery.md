# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use pure white full-width sections.
- Center content in a 1200px max-width track.
- Build sections as text-left and illustration-right.
- Keep each major section roomy, often 800-900px tall.
- Use 80-120px vertical breathing room between sections.
- Avoid dense cards and multi-column feature grids.
- Use a full-bleed green footer band.
- Repeat the rhythm: headline, paragraph, CTA, illustration.

## Imagery

- Use flat 2D mascot or character-style illustration.
- Use rounded geometric shapes and bold outlines.
- Let illustration contain secondary colors like pink, purple, yellow, orange, and blue.
- Do not use those secondary colors for UI controls.
- Avoid photography.
- Avoid image containers, cards, or background panels around illustrations.

## Composition Rules

- Keep white space generous and uncomplicated.
- Let green and blue UI accents do only specific jobs.
- Use one large illustration per section.
- Keep CTAs chunky and sticker-like.
- Use a strong green footer as the page anchor.
