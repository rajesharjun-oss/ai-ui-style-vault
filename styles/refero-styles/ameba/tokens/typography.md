# Typography

## Font Stacks

- Display: `F37 Bolton`, `Sohne`, `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif
- Body/UI: `Open Sauce Sans`, `Inter`, `Arial`, `Helvetica`, sans-serif
- Mono/Data: `IBM Plex Mono`, `JetBrains Mono`, ui-monospace, monospace

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Line height | Weight | Tracking | Token |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Data micro | `11px` | `1.2` | `500` | `0.091em` | `--text-data-micro` |
| Caption | `12px` | `1.35` | `500` | `0.077em` | `--text-caption` |
| Body small | `14px` | `1.5` | `400` | `0` | `--text-body-sm` |
| Body | `16px` | `1.55` | `400` | `0` | `--text-body` |
| Body large | `18px` | `1.45` | `400` | `0` | `--text-body-lg` |
| Card title | `24px` | `1.15` | `300` | `-0.012em` | `--text-card-title` |
| Section heading | `54px` | `1.02` | `300` | `-0.019em` | `--text-section-heading` |
| Hero heading | `86px` | `0.95` | `300` | `-0.022em` | `--text-hero-heading` |

## Rules

- Use F37 Bolton weight 300 for display and section headings.
- Use negative tracking for headings at 54px and above.
- Use Open Sauce Sans for readable body and practical UI.
- Use IBM Plex Mono only for metadata, system values, and data badges.
- Keep body text at 14px or larger.
- Keep mono text at 11px or larger.
