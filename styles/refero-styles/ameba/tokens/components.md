# Components

## Dark Radial Hero

- Surface: Midnight Ink.
- Background: radial glow from Radial Blue to Midnight Ink.
- Accent: Arc Cyan particle or network glow.
- Headline: F37 Bolton weight 300, negative tracking.
- CTA: single Signal Blue filled button.
- Avoid extra CTAs competing with Signal Blue.

## Signal Blue CTA

- Fill: Signal Blue.
- Text: White.
- Radius: `8px`.
- Padding: `12px 18px`.
- Font: Open Sauce Sans, `14px` to `16px`, weight `600`.
- Shadow: none.
- Gradient: none.

## Midnight Feature Card

- Fill: Midnight Ink or solid near-dark surface.
- Border: low-opacity blue or white hairline.
- Radius: `8px`.
- Padding: `24px`.
- Text: White and Mist.
- Do not use semi-transparent white glass.

## White Product Surface

- Fill: White.
- Border: `1px solid` Silver Border.
- Radius: `8px` or less.
- Text: Carbon, Slate Body, Mist.
- Use for the actual product UI, tables, browser chrome, and app views.

## IBM Plex Mono Data Badge

- Font: IBM Plex Mono.
- Size: `11px` to `12px`.
- Weight: `500` or `600`.
- Tracking: `0.077em` to `0.091em`.
- Radius: `4px`.
- Border: Silver Border on light or subtle blue hairline on dark.

## Active Timeline Bar

- Active color: Signal Blue.
- Inactive: Silver Border, Mist, or low-opacity dark line.
- Shape: straight, no pill ends unless tiny.
- Use for system progress, process state, or AI workflow stages.

## Chat Input Bar

- Fill: Midnight Ink or White depending on surface.
- Border: Silver Border on product UI or subtle dark hairline on hero.
- Radius: `8px`.
- Action: Signal Blue button or icon.
- Text: Open Sauce Sans.

## Metric Card

- Surface: dark solid card or White product card.
- Heading: F37 Bolton, `24px` to `54px`.
- Description: Open Sauce Sans.
- Value: IBM Plex Mono or F37 Bolton depending on importance.
- Border: hairline, no shadow.

## Product Screenshot Frame

- Outer frame: dark browser chrome.
- Content: White product surface.
- Chrome: traffic-light dots or URL bar.
- URL example: app.ameba.ai.
- Interior: sidebar, table rows, forms, and data labels.

## Dark Footer Links

- Surface: Midnight Ink.
- Links: Open Sauce Sans.
- Text: White and Mist.
- Active: Signal Blue sparingly.
- No decorative warm accents.
