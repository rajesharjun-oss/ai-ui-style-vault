# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Midnight Ink as the canvas for hero, feature, and atmospheric sections.
- Reserve Signal Blue for the single filled CTA, active timeline bars, and key visualization state.
- Set display headlines in F37 Bolton weight 300 with negative tracking.
- Use 8px radius for cards and buttons.
- Use 4px radius for tags and images.
- Use IBM Plex Mono with tracked spacing for data, status, and system metadata.
- Keep product surfaces White with 1px Silver Border hairlines.
- Pair the dark hero with one radial glow from Radial Blue into Midnight Ink.

## Don't

- Do not add drop shadows to cards or panels.
- Do not use Arc Cyan for text, buttons, or borders.
- Do not set body text below 14px in Open Sauce Sans.
- Do not set IBM Plex Mono below 11px.
- Do not use warm hues for brand identity.
- Do not round corners above 8px.
- Do not fill cards with semi-transparent white on dark backgrounds.
- Do not use gradient fills on UI elements.
