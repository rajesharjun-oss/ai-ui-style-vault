# Guidelines

## Do

- Use near-black backgrounds and white alpha borders.
- Keep cards and grid panels square-edged.
- Use pill radius only for buttons and inputs.
- Use Compass Gold only for icons and tiny marks.
- Use Pulse Green only for status dots.
- Keep typography weight mostly 400.
- Use hairline dividers and grid lines for section structure.
- Keep partner logos monochrome.

## Don't

- Do not add gradients, glow effects, glassmorphism, or colored blobs.
- Do not round cards.
- Do not use gold as a CTA fill.
- Do not turn green into a broad accent color.
- Do not introduce extra accent colors.
- Do not make the layout image-heavy.
- Do not use thick borders or heavy drop shadows.

## Accessibility Notes

- Paper on Obsidian is high contrast.
- Check Muted White and Soft White text sizes for contrast on Deep Navy.
- Use visible focus outlines with Paper or Translucent White.
- Do not rely only on Pulse Green for state; include labels where meaning matters.
