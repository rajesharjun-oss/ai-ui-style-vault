# Lattice Style Reference

## North Star

Lattice feels like mist over a people-ops dashboard. It is soft and human, but still structured enough for HR data, review workflows, survey results, goals, and employee insights.

The system avoids harsh SaaS chrome. It uses warm off-white backgrounds, airy panel cards, muted blue-green blocks, friendly rounded corners, and abstract people/team illustrations. The result should feel supportive, composed, and trust-building.

## Color

The palette is warm neutral with cool mist panels and small amber/teal action colors.

- `Charcoal` `#282a2a`: primary text, headings, nav labels, logo text.
- `Warm Paper` `#f4f3ef`: main page canvas and soft off-white background.
- `Paper` `#ffffff`: cards, forms, and clean content panels.
- `Mist Blue` `#c6d7db`: hero panels, large image backgrounds, calm blue surfaces.
- `Muted Green` `#749f99`: secondary panels, people-card fills, trust-oriented dashboard accents.
- `Slate Teal` `#477d78`: stronger teal for buttons, labels, and active secondary states.
- `Amber Glow` `#e7ae3c`: single primary CTA fill and warm conversion accent.
- `Soft Coral` `#e78876`: gentle people/illustration accent and warning-adjacent warmth.
- `Lavender Haze` `#a78fb2`: diversity-oriented illustration tint and soft contrast accent.
- `Pale Sky` `#d7e7ea`: alternate blue-gray wash, empty states, light chart fills.
- `Line Mist` `#d7d7d2`: borders and subtle dividers on warm surfaces.
- `Body Gray` `#5c6060`: secondary copy and muted labels.

Use accent colors as punctuation. Do not flood whole screens with amber, coral, or lavender.

## Typography

Use Inter Tight as the main family, falling back to Inter and system-ui. The type should feel compact but friendly.

Recommended roles:

- Micro label: 12px, weight 500, line-height 1.3.
- UI: 14px, weight 500, line-height 1.4.
- Body: 16px, weight 400, line-height 1.6.
- Card title: 18px, weight 600, line-height 1.3.
- Section heading: 28px, weight 600, line-height 1.2, slight negative tracking.
- Hero heading: 56px, weight 600 or 700, line-height 1.05, negative tracking.

Keep type confident but not heavy. Avoid condensed black weights and dramatic editorial contrast.

## Layout

The layout balances marketing warmth with product-dashboard clarity:

- Warm Paper page canvas.
- Centered content max width around 1180px.
- Hero section with a soft mist panel or dashboard collage.
- Product cards with rounded corners and quiet borders.
- People/insight sections using calm grids and pill filters.
- Dashboard views arranged in tidy rows and cards.

Desktop should use spacious two-column or asymmetric product storytelling. Mobile should stack clearly and preserve the soft canvas and rounded cards.

## Shape And Elevation

The shape language is friendly and controlled:

- Cards: 16px.
- Panels: 20px to 24px.
- Inputs: 10px to 12px.
- Buttons: 9999px or 12px depending on context.
- Small pills: 9999px.

Use soft shadows only when needed, with very low contrast. Borders and color contrast should do most of the work.

## Components

### Primary CTA

Amber Glow fill, Charcoal text, pill or softly rounded form. Use it for the most important action only, such as demo, start, or contact sales.

### Secondary CTA

White or transparent background with Slate Teal text and a subtle border. Use for lower-pressure actions.

### Insight Card

White or Soft Linen-like surface, 16px radius, thin Line Mist border, compact chart or metric area, and quiet text hierarchy.

### People Card

Muted Green or Paper surface with rounded avatar shapes, compact labels, and small status chips.

### Pill Filter

Small 9999px control for segments, survey filters, lifecycle states, or team groupings.

### Hero Dashboard Panel

Large rounded panel using Mist Blue or Pale Sky. It can contain cards, charts, people tiles, and abstract illustrations, but avoid dense enterprise clutter.

### Navigation

Use compact text links, Charcoal labels, subtle hover states, and a warm primary CTA on the right.

## Imagery

Use abstract people systems, soft geometric diagrams, small dashboards, employee cards, survey bars, org/team shapes, and calm product screenshots. Avoid aggressive neon, dark tech, glossy 3D, or generic stock-office photos.

## Do

- Use Warm Paper as the dominant canvas.
- Use Mist Blue and Muted Green for large calm surfaces.
- Use Amber Glow for one primary action.
- Use Slate Teal for secondary action and active states.
- Keep cards rounded, quiet, and readable.
- Use people-ops metaphors: goals, teams, feedback, surveys, growth.

## Do Not

- Do not use dark, high-contrast SaaS chrome.
- Do not overuse amber as a page background.
- Do not introduce saturated blues or purples outside the palette.
- Do not make the UI too playful or cartoonish.
- Do not use tiny dense table layouts as the first impression.
- Do not use sharp square cards.
