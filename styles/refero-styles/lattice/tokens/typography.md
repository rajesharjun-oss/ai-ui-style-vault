# Typography

Primary family: `"Inter Tight", "Inter", system-ui, sans-serif`

## Roles

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Micro | 12px | 500 | 1.3 | 0 | Tiny labels, metric captions |
| UI | 14px | 500 | 1.4 | 0 | Nav, buttons, filters |
| Body | 16px | 400 | 1.6 | 0 | Main body copy |
| Card Title | 18px | 600 | 1.3 | -0.01em | Insight and people cards |
| Section Heading | 28px | 600 | 1.2 | -0.02em | Product sections |
| Hero | 56px | 700 | 1.05 | -0.035em | First-screen headline |

## Guidance

- Keep the voice compact and clear.
- Use heavier weights for headings, but do not make the page shout.
- Use body copy with enough line-height for warmth.
- Avoid dramatic editorial serif pairings.
- Avoid ultra-condensed or black display text.
