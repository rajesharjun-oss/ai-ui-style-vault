# Components

## Primary CTA

- Background: `#e7ae3c`
- Text: `#282a2a`
- Radius: 9999px or 12px
- Font: Inter Tight 14px to 16px, 600
- Use once per page for the main action.

## Secondary CTA

- Background: `#ffffff` or transparent
- Text: `#477d78`
- Border: 1px solid `#d7d7d2`
- Radius: 9999px or 12px
- Use for lower-pressure actions.

## Insight Card

- Background: `#ffffff`
- Border: 1px solid `#d7d7d2`
- Radius: 16px
- Padding: 20px to 28px
- Includes metric, chart, goal, survey, or feedback content.

## People Card

- Background: `#ffffff`, `#749f99`, or pale surface variant
- Radius: 16px
- Uses avatars, team labels, small status chips, and people-focused metadata.

## Pill Filter

- Radius: 9999px
- Background: Paper, Pale Sky, or selected Slate Teal
- Use for teams, lifecycle states, survey groups, and view segments.

## Hero Dashboard Panel

- Background: `#c6d7db` or `#d7e7ea`
- Radius: 20px to 24px
- Padding: 32px to 48px
- Contains cards, bars, employee tiles, and product UI snippets.

## Survey Bar

- Track: `#d7d7d2`
- Fill: `#477d78`, `#749f99`, or `#e7ae3c`
- Radius: 9999px
- Use compact labels and readable percentages.

## Top Navigation

- Background: transparent or Warm Paper
- Links: Charcoal or Body Gray
- CTA: Amber Glow
- Keep it calm and compact.
