# Colors

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Charcoal | `#282a2a` | `--color-charcoal` | Primary text, headings, nav labels |
| Warm Paper | `#f4f3ef` | `--color-warm-paper` | Main page canvas and warm background |
| Paper | `#ffffff` | `--color-paper` | Cards, forms, clean content panels |
| Mist Blue | `#c6d7db` | `--color-mist-blue` | Hero panels, dashboard blocks, calm blue surfaces |
| Muted Green | `#749f99` | `--color-muted-green` | Secondary panels, people cards, trust accents |
| Slate Teal | `#477d78` | `--color-slate-teal` | Secondary actions, active states, strong teal labels |
| Amber Glow | `#e7ae3c` | `--color-amber-glow` | Single primary CTA and warm conversion accent |
| Soft Coral | `#e78876` | `--color-soft-coral` | Gentle illustration accent and warm emphasis |
| Lavender Haze | `#a78fb2` | `--color-lavender-haze` | Soft contrast accent and illustration tint |
| Pale Sky | `#d7e7ea` | `--color-pale-sky` | Alternate blue-gray wash, empty states, chart fills |
| Line Mist | `#d7d7d2` | `--color-line-mist` | Borders and subtle dividers |
| Body Gray | `#5c6060` | `--color-body-gray` | Secondary copy and muted labels |

## Guidance

- Use Warm Paper as the dominant canvas.
- Use Mist Blue and Muted Green for the large calm panels.
- Use Amber Glow for one primary CTA only.
- Use Slate Teal for secondary and active states.
- Keep Coral and Lavender small and illustrative.
- Do not introduce saturated unrelated accents.
