# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Warm Paper as the dominant background.
- Use Mist Blue and Muted Green for dashboard and hero panels.
- Use Amber Glow for the primary CTA.
- Use Slate Teal for active states and secondary actions.
- Keep cards soft, rounded, and readable.
- Use people, feedback, goals, surveys, and team metaphors.
- Keep data views airy and supportive.

## Do Not

- Do not use dark or neon technology styling.
- Do not overuse amber as a large background.
- Do not introduce saturated extra accent colors.
- Do not make the UI cartoonish.
- Do not use sharp square cards.
- Do not make the first impression a dense table.
- Do not use glossy 3D or generic office stock imagery.
