# Guidelines

## Do

- Use display headlines between 72px and 155px.
- Keep display line-height between 0.9 and 1.0.
- Use Signal Orange as the dominant accent.
- Reserve blue, green, and coral for category cards.
- Use Ink Roast for body text and headings.
- Keep white as the default canvas.
- Use 16px radius for cards and inputs.
- Use pill geometry only where the component is clearly a pill.
- Separate sections with generous whitespace.
- Use sparse, documented elevation only.

## Don't

- Do not add another typeface.
- Do not use 600+ font weights.
- Do not use pure black for body text.
- Do not add heavy shadows to buttons or text.
- Do not use all category accents together in one component.
- Do not make the layout dense or dashboard-heavy.
- Do not use sharp corners on interactive elements.
- Do not turn the orange tint into a full-page background.

## Accessibility Notes

- Use Ink Roast on white or pale surfaces for readable body copy.
- Verify white text over Signal Orange for contrast in production.
- Keep focus states visible, preferably using Signal Orange plus outline or offset ring.
- Preserve readable mobile line lengths; do not simply shrink huge headings until they become cramped.
