# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use full-width sections with centered max-width content at 1200px.
- Lead with a left-aligned editorial hero headline.
- Keep vertical breathing room large, especially around the hero.
- Use two or three column feature grids with wide gutters.
- Prefer short copy blocks beside large typographic or geometric moments.
- Use 80px or larger section gaps.
- Keep most surfaces white; introduce pale tints only for contrast or emphasis.

## Imagery

- Typography and geometric UI are the primary visuals.
- Use abstract fintech shapes, product-adjacent UI, or particle/3D geometric forms.
- Avoid generic lifestyle photography.
- If product imagery is included, keep crops tight and warm.
- Suggested warm filter for product visuals: `sepia(0.4) hue-rotate(329deg) contrast(1.1) saturate(2.2)`.

## Composition Rules

- Let one orange CTA or text link punctuate large white space.
- Use cards as content units, not as nested decorative wrappers.
- Avoid crowded matrices and repeated small controls.
- Balance massive headings with brief supporting copy.
- Keep category accent cards visually distinct but not louder than the core orange brand.
