# Components

## Primary Pill Button

- Fill: `#f73b20`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 12px
- Padding: 8px vertical, 16px horizontal
- Type: 14px, weight 450, 0.03em tracking
- Use: principal screen action

## Ghost Text Link

- Fill: none
- Text: `#f73b20`
- Radius: 8px
- Padding: 8px
- Use: inline navigation, "learn more", and secondary actions

## White Ghost Button

- Fill: transparent
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Border: 1px solid `#ffffff`
- Radius: 84px
- Padding: 16px horizontal
- Use: nav or light/glass overlay context

## Outline Brand Button

- Fill: transparent
- Text: `#f73b20`
- Border: 1px solid `#f73b20`
- Radius: 0 or text-link treatment
- Use: inline brand CTA, not primary action

## Utility Icon Button

- Fill: transparent
- Text/icon: `#000000`
- Radius: 16px
- Padding: 12px
- Use: compact labels, currency controls, toggles

## Frosted Glass Card

- Fill: `rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`
- Radius: 16px
- Padding: 16px
- Blur: 20px-40px
- Shadow: `--shadow-lg`
- Use: floating panel above hero or product visual

## Feature Category Card

- Fill: `rgba(247,59,32,0.05)` or semantic category tint
- Radius: 16px
- Padding: 24px or roomier
- Include: large geometric icon, concise title, one accent color
- Use: Exchange, Send, Add, funding, and category blocks

## Floating Input Field

- Fill: `rgba(247,59,32,0.05)`
- Text: `#360802`
- Radius: 16px
- Padding: 17.6px top, 6.4px bottom, 48px left
- Use: warm form fields with icon affordance

## Content Card

- Fill: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 16px
- Padding: 16px
- Shadow: `--shadow-md`
- Use: flat editorial cards with subtle upward lift

## Top Navigation

- Layout: logo left, nav center, toggle and CTA right
- Links: 14-16px, weight 400-450
- Controls: pill-shaped where grouped
- Surface: white or lightly frosted
