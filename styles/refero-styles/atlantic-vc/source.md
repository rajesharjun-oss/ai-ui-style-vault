## What To Preserve

Atlantic.vc should feel stark, technical, and speculative. It is not a generic dark SaaS palette. The system uses Monument-style display type for command presence and monospace copy for precision. CTAs are mostly text or outlined forms, keeping attention on content and data rather than colorful controls.

The page relies on:

- Deep Midnight as a full-bleed dark foundation.
- Ghost Ink for primary text, borders, links, and nav.
- Electric Blue for strategic highlight terms and small interaction states.
- Ignition Orange for focused edges, tags, dividers, and action text.
- Monument as the primary display/nav typeface.
- Mono for body text, labels, and technical copy.
- 0px button radius and 16px to 24px card radii.
- Full-bleed layout and generous internal padding instead of a strict page max width.
