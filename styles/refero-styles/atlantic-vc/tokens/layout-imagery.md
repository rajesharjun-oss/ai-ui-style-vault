# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use full-bleed dark sections.
- Avoid a strict page-level max-width; use generous section padding and implied columns.
- Hero: centered Monument display headline over abstract digital/noise atmosphere.
- Section rhythm: 50px to 100px vertical gaps.
- Use centered stacks, alternating content blocks, and horizontal company grids.
- Navigation: sticky top bar with sparse links and outlined or orange text action.

## Imagery

Use:

- Abstract digital noise in blue tones.
- Product or frontier-tech visuals.
- Company logo grids.
- Structured, desaturated headshots if people are needed.
- Minimal outline icons in Electric Blue or Ignition Orange.

Avoid:

- Lifestyle stock imagery.
- Light-card galleries.
- Generic colorful gradients.
- Heavy illustration.
- 3D decorative objects.
