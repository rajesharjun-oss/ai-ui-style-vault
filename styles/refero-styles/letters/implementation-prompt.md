# Implementation Prompt

Create an original light medical letterwriting or clinical transcription interface inspired by the Letters Refero style.

Use a full-bleed hero with `linear-gradient(180deg, #779bc1 0%, #9abfda 58%, #cbdcec 100%)`. Center an 80px Open Runde headline, weight 600, line-height 0.90, letter-spacing `-0.04em`, in white. Add short centered subtext in 16px to 18px white at 90% opacity. Below it, use a near-black `#070709` filled pill CTA with 100px radius and white text.

Below the hero, switch to a pure white or `#f5f5f5` workspace. Use large feature cards with 18px to 32px radius, 40px to 48px padding, white surfaces, and the blue-tinted shadow stack. Use Surgical Blue `#2597d0` only for envelope icons, microphone icons, waveform lines, checkmarks, and tiny inline numeric accents.

Build these elements:

- Sticky white navigation with centered links and right-side login/sign-up actions.
- Hero headline, subtext, and near-black pill CTA.
- Two-column feature-card grid for Letters and Transcribe.
- Upload drop zone with dashed `#bebecc` border, 12px radius, and blue icon.
- Waveform audio player with blue waveform on white.
- Before/After document cards with a decorative handwritten before card and clean Open Runde after card.
- Pill tags with 100px radius, black border, small blue icon, and Open Runde label.

Do not copy Letters brand assets, exact screenshots, page copy, or proprietary layouts. Generate a new healthtech/documentation screen that borrows only the mood, token relationships, and component language.
