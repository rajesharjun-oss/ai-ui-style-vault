# Typography

## Families

| Family | Token | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Open Runde | `--font-open-runde` | Primary display and heading face; use for headings, nav, buttons, and large text |
| Inter | `--font-inter` | Secondary body/UI text for small labels, nav items, and inline links |
| The Doctor FreeVersion | `--font-the-doctor-freeversion` | Decorative handwritten note face for before-card illustrations only |

## Substitutes

- Open Runde substitute: Inter Tight, General Sans, or Manrope.
- Inter substitute: system-ui or -apple-system.
- The Doctor substitute: Caveat or Kalam.

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Line Height | Letter Spacing | Token |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Caption | `12px` | `1.2` | `-0.01px` | `--text-caption` |
| Body Small | `14px` | `1.4` | `-0.01px` | `--text-body-sm` |
| Body | `16px` | `1.49` | `-0.01px` | `--text-body` |
| Body Large | `18px` | `1.4` | `-0.01px` | `--text-body-lg` |
| Subheading | `20px` | `1.4` | `-0.04px` | `--text-subheading` |
| Heading Small | `28px` | `1.2` | `-0.04px` | `--text-heading-sm` |
| Heading | `44px` | `1.1` | `-0.04px` | `--text-heading` |
| Display | `80px` | `0.9` | `-0.04em` | `--text-display` |

## Rules

- Use Open Runde at weight 600 for display and section headings.
- Keep display tracking tight at `-0.04em`.
- Use Inter only where a more neutral small-text rhythm is needed.
- The Doctor font is decorative only, never functional UI text.
