# Guidelines

## Do

- Use 100px radius for all buttons, tags, and pill-shaped elements.
- Set display headings at 80px Open Runde weight 600 with `-0.04em` tracking and `0.90` line-height.
- Use Obsidian for all filled CTAs; never use a chromatic button background.
- Apply the sky gradient only to the hero band.
- Reserve Surgical Blue for icons, waveform players, and small functional accents under 24px.
- Use blue-tinted low-opacity shadows for feature cards.
- Center-align section headings and hero text.
- Use 40px to 48px internal padding on feature cards.

## Don't

- Do not use a chromatic color as a CTA button background.
- Do not flatten the letter spacing on large headings.
- Do not apply the sky gradient below the hero.
- Do not use shadows with opacity above 0.1 on cards.
- Do not pair Open Runde with generic geometric sans-serif fonts like Helvetica or Roboto.
- Do not use The Doctor font for functional UI text.
- Do not place white cards directly on the sky gradient without padding.
- Do not use Surgical Blue for text longer than two words.
