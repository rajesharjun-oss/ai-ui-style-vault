# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Patterns

- Full-bleed sky gradient hero.
- Centered hero headline, centered subtext, and centered CTA pill.
- Seamless transition from sky hero to white content.
- Sticky white nav with center links and right auth actions.
- 2-column feature grid with large rounded cards.
- Centered section headings above every feature grid.
- Alternating white and Cloud Gray sections.
- No sidebar, mega-menu, or asymmetric layout.

## Imagery

- Visuals are mostly product UI: document cards, upload zones, waveform players, and before/after letter transformations.
- Use handwritten decorative typography only inside before-card illustrations.
- Icons are flat, line-based, and Surgical Blue.
- Avoid lifestyle photography, abstract 3D renders, and heavy illustration.
- Keep mockups slightly rotated at most; no dramatic perspective.

## Responsive Notes

- On mobile, reduce the 80px hero display size while preserving tight tracking.
- Stack feature cards vertically.
- Keep card padding generous, but clamp it from 48px to about 28px on small screens.
- Keep hero and section headings centered.
- Preserve pill CTAs and 100px tag radii.
