# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Phosphor Green for CTAs, emphasized keywords, logo, active nav, and input focus.
- Ration green to one element per visual region.
- Use 9999px radius for all buttons and tags.
- Use 16px radius for cards.
- Use 8px radius for inputs.
- Use Circular 400 for most text.
- Use Circular 500 for emphasis and button labels.
- Separate surfaces with 1px Charcoal borders.
- Keep letter spacing at about `-0.007em`.
- Keep the page canvas at Obsidian.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce chromatic colors outside the green family.
- Do not use Circular 600 or 700.
- Do not add box shadows to cards, popovers, or modals.
- Do not use radius values outside the strict pill, card, and input scale.
- Do not place Phosphor Green on large background areas.
- Do not use a non-Obsidian canvas.
- Do not add gradients.
- Do not use photography or lifestyle imagery.
