# Components

## Pill Primary Button

Phosphor Green fill, Snow text, 1px Phosphor Green border, 9999px radius, 8px 16px padding, Circular 500 at 14px. On hover, use Forest Depth at the border or pressed edge.

## Pill Ghost Button

Transparent fill, 1px Slate border, Snow text, 9999px radius, 8px 16px padding, Circular 400 at 14px. Hover may use Graphite border and very subtle white-alpha fill.

## GitHub Stars Pill

Ash fill, 1px Charcoal border, Snow text, GitHub icon, 9999px radius, 6px 10px padding, Circular 400 at 12px.

## Feature Card

Obsidian fill, 1px Charcoal border, 16px radius, 24px padding. Include a Phosphor Green icon, Snow 18px Circular 500 heading, Silver Mist 14px body, and optional Void illustration well.

## Product Showcase Card

Larger technical card with dark product UI, wireframe geometry, database grids, or schematic visuals. Keep the same 16px card radius and Charcoal border.

## Testimonial Card

Obsidian fill, 1px Charcoal border, 16px radius, 20px padding. Use 40px avatar, Snow 14px Circular 500 handle, and Silver Mist quote.

## Announcement Pill

Ash fill, 1px Charcoal border, 9999px radius, 6px 16px padding, Silver Mist 12px Circular 400, optional chevron.

## Logo Cloud Item

Monochrome logo in Smoke, about 24px high, no border or background.

## Top Navigation Bar

64px fixed top bar on transparent or Obsidian background, 1px Charcoal bottom border. Use muted nav links and green only in the brand mark or active item.

## Input Field

Obsidian fill, 1px Slate border, 8px radius, 8px 12px padding. Text Snow, placeholder Smoke. Focus border becomes Phosphor Green, with no glow ring.
