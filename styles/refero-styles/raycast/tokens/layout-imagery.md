# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Full-bleed dark hero.
- Floating glass nav over the hero.
- Centered 1200px content after the hero.
- Section gaps around 80-120px.
- Feature grids can be 3-column for compact tiles or 2-column for larger cards.
- Avoid alternating light/dark bands; keep the page dark throughout.
- Prefer one strong statement per section instead of dense dashboard composition.

## Imagery

- Hero uses abstract red/blue gradient geometry and atmospheric blur.
- Product visuals below the hero should be app-window mockups, command bars, result lists, and extension tiles.
- No photography, lifestyle imagery, or stock visuals.
- Use product UI as the hero artifact.

## Iconography

- Use system-style glyphs and product icons.
- Icon containers are circular, dark, and dock-like.
- Icons should usually be Mist or white on dark.
- Coral icon accents should be rare.

## Composition Checks

- Is the page still dark after the hero?
- Are CTAs neutral instead of coral?
- Is coral limited to brand and hero moments?
- Are cards tactile through inset treatment rather than floating shadows?
- Does the layout stay on the 8px grid?
