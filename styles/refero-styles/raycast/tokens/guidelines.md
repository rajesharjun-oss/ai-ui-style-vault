# Guidelines

## Do

- Use `#040506` as the page canvas.
- Keep all body sections dark.
- Use Mist filled buttons with Iron text for primary actions.
- Reserve Coral Pulse for the brand mark, hero artwork, AI badge, and tiny selected states.
- Use Inter 56px regular for the hero headline.
- Use key-shadow inset treatment for card depth.
- Use Geist Mono for technical metadata.
- Keep layout spacing on the 8px grid.

## Don't

- Do not use coral for body text, normal links, broad icon accents, or CTA fill.
- Do not use chromatic action buttons.
- Do not add conventional drop shadows to cards or panels.
- Do not introduce white or light theme sections.
- Do not use many accent colors on the same surface.
- Do not use SF Pro Text for body copy.
- Do not break the 8px spacing grid.

## Accessibility Notes

- Neutral Mist buttons need Iron text for contrast and a visible focus state.
- Coral should not be the only cue for selection or errors.
- Dark-on-dark cards need visible borders or the key-shadow ring.
- Technical metadata in mono should not drop below 12px for important content.
