# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a centered 1200px max-width container.
- Use 24px to 48px horizontal page padding.
- Use a floating centered nav pill, not a full-width navigation bar.
- Hero is full-bleed Void Canvas with centered headline, subtitle, CTA, and wide product screenshot.
- Keep 96px to 120px gaps between sections.
- Do not alternate background colors; keep the page as one continuous dark field.
- Feature grids often use 4 columns and 2 rows.
- Testimonial grids often use 3 columns and 2 rows.

## Section Patterns

- Hero: badge, 72px AeonikPro headline, subtitle, CTA, product screenshot, star field.
- Feature grid: outlined icons, compact titles, short descriptions, no card surfaces.
- Testimonial grid: Midnight Surface cards with avatar, handle, and quote.
- Section header: centered headline, subtitle, optional badge.
- Divider: subtle Aurora stroke.

## Imagery

- Use product screenshots of note editors, sidebars, daily note panels, calendars, and AI writing surfaces.
- Use tiny stars on the hero canvas.
- Use small testimonial avatars only where social proof needs them.
- Use gradient text for one highlighted phrase.
- Avoid lifestyle photos, 3D renders, decorative illustrations, and busy dashboard collages.
