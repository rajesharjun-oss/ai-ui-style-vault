# Components

## Hero

- Full or near-full viewport height.
- Centered headline, subhead, and two-button row.
- Twilight Indigo base with violet, coral, and orange atmospheric blending.
- Floating product cards below or around the central copy.
- Optional 3D mascot or playful object near a lower corner.

## Navigation

- Floating or sticky top bar.
- Transparent over the hero, warm parchment or paper over content.
- Compact labels and subtle active state.
- Keep nav visually lighter than the hero headline.

## Buttons

- 12px radius.
- Prefer neutral fills: Paper White on dark, Ink Black on light.
- Use violet or pastel accents as outlines, icon details, or nearby marks.
- Avoid large flat solid-violet CTAs.

## Chips And Tags

- 9999px radius.
- Compact 12px to 14px type.
- Useful for categories, suggestions, and filter-like controls.

## Cards

- 12px radius for normal cards.
- 16px or 24px for large product panels.
- Use thin warm borders and subtle warm shadows.
- Feature cards can rotate pastel backgrounds.

## Tabs

- Simple text tabs.
- Active state can be an underline or a quiet contrast shift.
- Avoid heavy segmented-control styling unless the whole surface is a prompt panel.

## Prompt Panels

- Place Paper White panels on top of illustrated or twilight sections.
- Use 16px to 24px radius.
- Keep inner controls compact and readable.
