## Color Behavior

- Use parchment as the ambient base.
- Use pure white only for elevated panels and inputs.
- Use `Ink Black`, not pure black, for normal foreground.
- Rotate accent colors in small UI and illustration zones.
- Keep CTA surfaces neutral; let accents live around them.
