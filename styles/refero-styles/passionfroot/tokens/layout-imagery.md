# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Max content width: 1200px.
- Start with a centered hero composition.
- Alternate dark atmospheric sections with parchment and white card sections.
- Keep most layouts single-column or paired-card rather than dense grids.
- Use horizontal logo rows with wide gaps.
- Let major cards sit visibly on top of illustrated or gradient backgrounds.

## Imagery

- Twilight gradients and soft sky transitions.
- Floating product UI cards.
- Cream paper panels over dark atmosphere.
- Pastel illustrations and simple 3D mascots.
- Small colorful icon strokes and decorative details.

## Direction

This style should feel like a creative SaaS product staged in a warm twilight studio. The viewer should see useful product cards and readable controls, but the atmosphere should carry a soft editorial personality.
