# Guidelines

## Do

- Use a light editorial serif for large display headlines.
- Use `Parchment Cream` as the default page background.
- Apply 12px radius to cards, inputs, and buttons.
- Reserve 9999px radius for chips and tags.
- Build elevation with warm tinted shadows and hairlines.
- Rotate pastel accents across feature cards and illustration details.
- Use `Ink Black` for high contrast text and structural outlines.

## Do Not

- Do not use weight 700 for display headlines.
- Do not use pure white as the full page background.
- Do not use pure black for normal text.
- Do not add heavy black or gray shadows.
- Do not make the primary CTA a generic solid violet rectangle.
- Do not introduce random radii beyond 12px, 16px, 24px, and 9999px.
- Do not use the serif font for small body or UI text.

## AI Builder Checks

- Does the page move from twilight atmosphere into parchment product clarity?
- Are colorful accents distributed as small moments instead of one dominant wash?
- Are the main CTA surfaces neutral and confident?
- Are all small text and controls set in `Nunito Sans`?
- Does the design feel warm and tactile instead of glossy or sterile?
