# Typography

## Primary UI Text

- Family: Helvetica Now Text
- Substitute: Inter, Helvetica Neue, Arial, or system sans
- Weights: 400 and 500
- Sizes: 16px and 20px
- Line-height: 1.5, 1.75, 1.86
- Use for body copy, nav links, product names, card titles, footer links, and button labels.

## Medium UI Text

- Family: Helvetica Now Text Medium
- Substitute: Inter Medium or Helvetica Neue Medium
- Weight: 500
- Sizes: 12px, 14px, 16px
- Line-height: 1.5, 1.75, 1.86
- Use for compact labels, product names, button labels, captions, and nav items.

## Display Medium

- Family: Helvetica Now Display Medium
- Substitute: Inter Medium or Helvetica Neue Medium
- Weight: 500
- Sizes: 20px and 24px
- Line-height: 1.2 and 1.5
- Use for category labels, card headlines, footer headers, and medium-scale section titles.

## Hero Display

- Family: Nike Futura ND
- Substitute: Futura PT Medium
- Weight: 500
- Size: 76px
- Line-height: 1
- Use only for full hero headlines.

## Type Scale

| Token | Size | Weight | Line Height | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `caption` | 12px | 500 | 1.5 | tiny labels and product pills |
| `body-sm` | 14px | 500 | 1.75 | compact helper text and labels |
| `body` | 16px | 400 | 1.75 | running copy and footer links |
| `subheading` | 20px | 500 | 1.5 | category names and intro text |
| `heading-sm` | 24px | 500 | 1.2 | medium section headings |
| `display` | 76px | 500 | 1 | hero headlines |

## Rules

- Use only weights 400 and 500 in normal UI.
- Do not use bold or semibold for headings.
- Keep most text between 12px and 20px.
- Use the Futura-style face only at hero scale.
