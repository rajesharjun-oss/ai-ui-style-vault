# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Start with a full-viewport editorial hero.
- Follow with a 2-by-2 full-bleed category grid.
- Use a 3-column product row or horizontally scrolling product grid.
- Keep product cards compact with 12px padding.
- Use 48px section gaps.
- Let large content blocks span the viewport.
- Use the 1440px max width mainly for nav and footer structure.
- Keep transitions between dark hero and white grids stark.

## Imagery

- Product photography is the dominant design asset.
- Use high-resolution product shots with crisp materials and strong contrast.
- Crop shoes and apparel tightly enough to make product form the composition.
- Use category photography with motion or athlete detail when needed.
- Keep all image containers sharp: no radius and no frames.
- Avoid illustration, abstract graphics, and decorative 3D renders.

## Surfaces

| Level | Name | Value | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Paper White | `#ffffff` | Product grids, page canvas, footer |
| 2 | Soft Mist | `#f5f5f5` | Search fields, subtle tint, tags |
| 3 | Concrete Gray | `#e5e5e5` | Dividers and grid seams |
| 4 | Obsidian | `#111111` | Dark editorial sections and text |

## Motion

- Keep interaction motion minimal.
- Use opacity changes for hover states.
- Avoid bounce, spring, parallax, and theatrical page effects.
