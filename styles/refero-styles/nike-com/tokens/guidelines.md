# Guidelines

## Do

- Use only the monochrome UI palette.
- Let product photography provide all saturation.
- Set every interactive CTA as a 30px pill.
- Keep product and category images sharp-edged with 0 radius.
- Use Helvetica Now-style medium text at 16px for nav, button labels, and product names.
- Use Futura-style display type only for 76px hero headlines.
- Separate sections with Concrete Gray hairlines and whitespace.
- Keep the nav visually quiet on white with no shadow or blur.

## Do Not

- Do not add blue, red, green, yellow, or other accent colors to UI.
- Do not use bold or semibold weights in normal UI.
- Do not add box shadows to cards, nav, tiles, or buttons.
- Do not round product cards or image containers.
- Do not create more than two CTA color treatments: dark pill on light, or light pill on dark.
- Do not place decorative gradients, patterns, or background art behind text.
- Do not use display typography below 20px or above 76px.
