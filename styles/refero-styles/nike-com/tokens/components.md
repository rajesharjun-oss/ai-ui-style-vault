# Components

## Pill Button

- Role: all CTA moments
- Fill: Obsidian on light surfaces or Paper White on dark surfaces
- Text: inverse of fill
- Radius: 30px
- Padding: 6px vertical, 8px horizontal
- Font: Helvetica Now Text Medium, 16px, 500
- Border: none
- Shadow: none
- Hover: subtle opacity shift only.

## Category Hero Card

- Role: large full-bleed category tile
- Container: no border, no radius, no shadow
- Image: edge-to-edge product or athlete photography
- Text: bottom-left overlay in white
- Label: Helvetica Now Text, 16px
- Headline: Helvetica Now Display Medium, 20px to 24px
- CTA: white pill with Obsidian text.

## Product Card

- Background: Paper White
- Border: none
- Radius: 0
- Shadow: none
- Image: centered product shot with sharp edges
- Product label: small Obsidian pill with white text
- Avoid price, description, and extra CTAs inside the card.

## Top Navigation Bar

- Two-tier structure.
- Utility row: small search, help, and login text at 12px.
- Main row: logo left, category links centered, search and icons right.
- Category links: Helvetica Now Text Medium, 16px.
- Search field: Soft Mist background, 24px radius, no border.
- Nav surface: white, no shadow, no sticky blur effect.

## Editorial Hero Section

- Full-bleed dark or near-black background.
- Large product photo centered or angled.
- Headline: Futura-style display, 76px, white, line-height 1.
- Subcopy: Helvetica Now Text, 16px, white.
- CTA: single white pill with Obsidian text.
- Do not add decorative patterns or extra UI controls.

## Footer Link Grid

- Background: Paper White
- Four columns
- Headers: Helvetica Now Display Medium, 16px to 20px, Obsidian
- Links: Helvetica Now Text, 16px, Steel
- Row gap: 12px
- No dividers or filled blocks.

## Icon System

- Style: outlined stroke icons
- Stroke: 1.5px to 2px
- Size: 16px to 20px
- Default: Obsidian
- Secondary: Steel
- No filled or multicolor variants.
