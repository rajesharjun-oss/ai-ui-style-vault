# Nike.com - Style Reference

Nike.com behaves like a monochrome performance gallery. The interface gets out of the way so product photography, silhouettes, materials, and campaign imagery can carry the color and emotion.

## Visual Thesis

- Use black, white, and gray only for UI.
- Let product photography be the only chromatic layer.
- Keep commerce cards flat, sharp, and unframed.
- Use rounded pills for actions, search, and tags.
- Use one large sport-display headline only in hero moments.
- Separate sections with whitespace and hairline gray borders, not shadow.

## Core Ingredients

- Canvas: mostly Paper White, with dark full-bleed editorial hero sections.
- Text: Obsidian for primary copy, Steel for secondary text.
- Typography: compact Helvetica Now-style text with Nike Futura-style hero display.
- Shape: 30px pill buttons, 24px search/nav radius, 0px card and image radius.
- Elevation: none. No shadows on cards, buttons, nav, or tiles.
- Imagery: full-bleed product photography with sharp edges.

## Page Composition

Use a dramatic dark hero first, then move into product or category grids on white. Category tiles can form a 2-by-2 full-bleed grid. Product cards are flat and white with centered product images. Footer sections return to simple white link grids.

## Color Behavior

The UI should not introduce a brand accent color. No blue links, red CTAs, green badges, yellow highlights, or colorful icons. If the page needs color, it should come from shoes, apparel, campaign images, or product art.

## Type Behavior

Most interface text stays between 12px and 20px. Use Helvetica Now-style medium text for nav links, buttons, product names, and card labels. Use a Futura-style display face at 76px only for hero headlines, with line-height locked to 1.
