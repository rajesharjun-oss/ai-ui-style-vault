# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Max width: 1078px centered.
- Hero: full viewport, full bleed, dark/iridescent media, centered 225px headline.
- Navigation: fixed transparent 66px bar, wordmark left, locale center, menu stack right.
- Body: spacious editorial rhythm with 46px section gaps.
- Arrangement: asymmetric text-image pairings after the hero.
- Project list: single-column, full-width image and simple title, no card chrome.
- Footer: compact three-column address block in muted gray.

## Imagery

Use:

- One full-viewport liquid iridescent hero texture or video.
- Editorial project photography and campaign stills.
- Sharp image edges.
- Minimal circular typographic ornament.

Avoid:

- Repeated gradients in regular sections.
- Rounded image containers.
- Shadows, frames, and card wrappers.
- Illustrations, icons, and decorative shapes.
- Generic stock imagery.
- Chromatic UI controls.
