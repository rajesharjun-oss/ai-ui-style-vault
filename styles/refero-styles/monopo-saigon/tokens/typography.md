# Typography

## Roobert

- Token: `--font-roobert`
- Fallback: Inter or Sohne
- Weights: 300, 400, 600
- Sizes: 11px, 12px, 16px, 18px, 29px, 30px, 39px, 45px, 54px, 78px, 94px, 225px
- Line height: 0.70 to 2.34
- Role: primary typeface for navigation, hero, body, lists, project titles, and footer text.

## Raleway

- Token: `--font-raleway`
- Fallback: Montserrat or Jost
- Weight: 400
- Size: 54px
- Line height: 1.39
- Role: rare heading accent only.

## system-ui

- Token: `--font-system-ui`
- Weights: 400
- Sizes: 9px and 16px
- Line height: 1.15 to 1.32
- Role: micro labels, cookie banner body, and fine print.

## Rules

- Use 225px/400 for the full-viewport hero headline.
- Use 78px/300 for quiet atmospheric statements.
- Use 94px/400 with 0.76 line-height for dense editorial statement blocks.
- Do not use weights above 600.
- Do not use 600 above 45px.
