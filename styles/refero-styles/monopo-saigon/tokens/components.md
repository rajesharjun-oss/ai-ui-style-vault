# Components

## Ghost Pill Button - Dark Surface

Transparent fill, 1px translucent white border, Paper text, 75px radius, Roobert 16px/400, 11px vertical padding, and 33px horizontal padding. Use on the hero or dark inverse sections.

## Ghost Pill Button - Light Surface

Transparent fill, 1px Obsidian border, Obsidian text, 75px radius, Roobert 16px/400, 11px vertical padding, and 33px horizontal padding.

## Filled Neutral Pill

Slate Pill fill, Paper text, 1px Paper border, 75px radius. Use for consent or utility confirmations only.

## Underline-Free Text Link

No background, no border, no underline, 0px radius. Use Roobert 12px to 16px. Use Paper on dark surfaces and Obsidian on light surfaces.

## Hero Display Headline

Roobert 225px/400, Paper text, centered over full-viewport iridescent media. The headline is the hero content.

## Section Heading - Whisper

Roobert 78px/300, line-height 1.10, Obsidian or Paper depending on surface. Use for atmospheric manifesto copy.

## Section Heading - Anchor

Roobert 94px/400, line-height 0.76. Use for dense editorial divider statements.

## Project List Row

Transparent background, 0px radius, no shadow, sharp full-width image, and project title below in Roobert 16px to 18px.

## Top Navigation Bar

Transparent fixed 66px bar with wordmark left, locale center, and menu right. No background fill and no shadow.

## Rotating Scroll Indicator

Circular typographic SVG badge with slow continuous rotation. Use it as a subtle hero ornament, not a CTA.
