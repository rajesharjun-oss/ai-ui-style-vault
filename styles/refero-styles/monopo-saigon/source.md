## What To Preserve

monopo saigon depends on restraint. The interface is not colorful. It is black, white, and gray, with color appearing only as a media treatment in the hero. Cards and images are sharp. Text links are sharp. Buttons and tags are full pills. Typography is allowed to become enormous and quiet at the same time.

The page relies on:

- Pure black primary foreground.
- Pure white page canvas.
- One hero-sized iridescent media gesture.
- Roobert as the main typeface across almost everything.
- Raleway only as a rare heading accent.
- system-ui only for micro labels and fine print.
- 0px radius for content surfaces.
- 75px radius for buttons and tags.
- No shadows or elevation.
- Slow, gliding motion.
