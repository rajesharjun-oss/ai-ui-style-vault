# AI Implementation Prompt

Build a polished UI in the Metaview style.

Use a dark AI product control-room visual system: `#000a06` Deep Forest page canvas, `#0a1a14` Pine Bark cards, `#01051b` Abyssal Indigo borders, Paper White text, Ash Gray body copy, and one vivid mint accent, `#7affb4`, used only for the primary CTA, active tabs, and live status.

Typography:

- Use Euclid Circular A-style type throughout the UI.
- Use display headlines at weight 300 with tight negative tracking.
- Use 400 for body, 500 for nav/subheads/buttons, and 700 only for small emphasis.
- Use Onsite SemiMono-style type for candidate counts, numeric metrics, IDs, and technical micro-copy.

Layout:

- Use a 1200px centered max-width.
- Use an announcement bar, full-width dark nav, centered hero, and product showcase sections.
- Hero should use a single-column centered stack with a 68px whisper-weight headline, muted paragraph, and two-button row.
- Product showcase sections should sit on the Bioluminescent Bloom radial gradient and contain flat Pine Bark product cards.

Components:

- Primary CTA: Electric Mint fill, Deep Forest text, 999px radius, 8px by 16px padding, 14px/500.
- Ghost CTA: transparent, 1px Paper White border, Paper White text, 999px radius.
- Feature cards: Pine Bark fill, Abyssal Indigo border, 16px radius, 24px padding.
- Inputs: Pine Bark fill, Abyssal Indigo border, 8px radius, 16px padding.
- Tab pills: 100px radius, Mint Whisper active background, Deep Forest active text.
- Filter chips: 28px tall, 4px radius, Onsite SemiMono counts.

Strict rules:

- Do not use Electric Mint as body text or broad background.
- Do not add extra accent colors.
- Do not use light or white cards on the dark canvas.
- Do not use heavy shadows.
- Do not make display headlines bold.
- Do not use gradients except the product-section bloom and the Pine-to-Abyss atmospheric transition.
