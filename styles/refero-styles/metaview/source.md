## What To Preserve

Metaview is a dark-mode AI product surface with one vivid accent. It should feel like a recruiting agent instrument panel: deep, controlled, precise, and product-first. The page gains depth from green atmospheric bloom behind product mockups, not from decorative shadows or heavy gradients everywhere.

The page relies on:

- Deep Forest as the universal page canvas.
- Pine Bark for cards and product containers.
- Charcoal only for elevated overlays.
- Electric Mint only for the primary CTA, active tabs, and live indicators.
- Euclid Circular A-style type with 300-weight display headings.
- Onsite SemiMono for numeric and technical details.
- 999px pill buttons and tabs.
- 16px cards, 8px inputs, and 4px icons.
- Product screenshots/mockups as the dominant imagery.
