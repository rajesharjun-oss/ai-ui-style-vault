# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Max width: 1200px centered.
- Page rhythm: announcement bar, nav, centered hero, product showcase, alternating dark product sections.
- Nav: Deep Forest background, 80px height, wordmark left, nav center, CTAs right.
- Hero: centered single-column text stack, 120px top padding from nav, two-button CTA row.
- Product showcase: full-bleed bloom backdrop with centered Pine Bark product card.
- Internal product grid: narrow 60px rail and wide chat/product panel.
- Section gaps: 80px.

## Imagery

Use:

- Actual product screenshots or realistic product mockups.
- Chat interface panels and recruiting workflows.
- Bioluminescent green radial bloom behind product sections.
- Monoline icons at 1.5px to 2px stroke.

Avoid:

- Lifestyle photography.
- Stock illustration.
- 3D renders.
- Decorative gradients outside product showcase and footer.
- Light cards on the dark canvas.
