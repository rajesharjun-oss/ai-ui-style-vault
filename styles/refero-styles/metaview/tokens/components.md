# Components

## Pill Primary Button

Electric Mint fill, Deep Forest text, 999px radius, 8px by 16px padding, Euclid 14px/500. This is the only vivid filled button.

## Outlined Ghost Button

Transparent fill, 1px Paper White border, Paper White text, 999px radius, 8px by 16px padding, Euclid 14px/500. Pair with the mint primary button.

## Dark Secondary Button

Pine Bark fill, 1px Abyssal Indigo border, Paper White text, 16px radius, 8px by 16px padding. Use inside cards and mockups.

## Top Navigation Bar

Deep Forest background, full width, about 80px tall. Wordmark left, nav links centered, sign-in and two CTAs right. No drop shadow.

## Announcement Banner

Full-width strip above nav, Deep Forest background, centered Paper White 14px text, 8px vertical padding, no border.

## Hero Section

Deep Forest canvas, centered 1200px content, 68px/300 display headline with -4.08px tracking, Ash Gray support text, and two-button row.

## Product Showcase Card

Bioluminescent Bloom backdrop. Pine Bark card, 1px Abyssal Indigo border, 16px radius, 24px padding, flat elevation.

## Side Icon Nav Rail

Vertical feature selector. Active item uses Mint Whisper background and Paper White text. Inactive items use Ash Gray. Active indicator has 4px radius.

## Chat Interface Panel

Pine Bark panel with subtle inner border, 8px-radius input, 16px padding, circular submit button, and SemiMono candidate counts.

## Tab Pill Group

100px pill tabs. Active tab uses Mint Whisper background and Deep Forest text. Inactive tabs are transparent with Paper White at reduced opacity.

## Filter Bar

28px chips, 4px radius, Abyssal Indigo border, 12px/500 labels, and SemiMono numeric counts.

## Feature Card

Pine Bark background, 1px Abyssal Indigo border, 16px radius, 24px padding. Icon at 24px in Electric Mint or Paper White; heading 20px/500; body 16px/400 in Ash Gray.
