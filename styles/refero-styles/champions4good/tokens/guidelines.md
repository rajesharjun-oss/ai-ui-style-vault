# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Druk Condensed for all display headings at 58px or larger.
- Set display type to line-height `0.78` to `0.85`.
- Use Lavender Shock as the only major chromatic color on Deep Plum sections.
- Apply 9999px radius to every button, tag, and pill element.
- Break dark plum sections with Paper White sections.
- Use Mint Signal and Amber Pulse only as small accents under 20% of the viewport.
- Keep Neue Montreal at 12px to 16px for UI chrome.
- Use left-aligned display headlines that push toward viewport edges.

## Don't

- Do not use non-condensed type for 200px+ display words.
- Do not place Lavender Shock on white or light backgrounds.
- Do not add box shadows to cards or buttons.
- Do not use display line-height above 1.0.
- Do not turn Mint Signal or Amber Pulse into major dark-section surfaces.
- Do not use border radius smaller than 6px on interactive elements.
- Do not center-align display headlines.
- Do not make photography the primary hero visual.
