# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Model

- Start with a full-bleed Deep Plum hero.
- Float transparent navigation over the hero.
- Place pill sport toggles above or near the display headline.
- Use enormous Druk words as visual art.
- Break the page with Paper White sections and black display text.
- Keep display words left aligned and edge-aware.
- Use 1200px max-width for body content, not for the whole hero typography.
- Avoid card grids as the primary page rhythm.

## Imagery

- Iconography is the primary visual language.
- Use simple line-art sport figures in white or lavender.
- Put icons inside sport toggles or small club marks.
- Treat huge condensed words as image-like poster objects.
- Avoid 3D renders, generic illustrations, and stock-photo hero scenes.
- If photography is required, use tightly cropped high-contrast sports imagery with minimal background.

## Motion

- Motion should feel sharp and poster-like.
- Use toggle state swaps, quick color changes, horizontal type reveals, or section snaps.
- Avoid bouncy animation, playful confetti, and soft parallax.
