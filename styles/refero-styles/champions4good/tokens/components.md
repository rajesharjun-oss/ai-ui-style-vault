# Components

## Hero Display Headline

- Role: full-bleed poster-scale word stack.
- Font: Druk Condensed Super Desktop.
- Size: `151px` to `317px`.
- Line height: `0.78` to `0.85`.
- Color: Lavender Shock.
- Background: Deep Plum.
- Layout: words pushed close to viewport edges, usually left aligned.
- Secondary words: Neue Montreal, `58px`, weight `600`.

## Sport Toggle Pill

- Role: two-option selector for sport type.
- Size: `80px x 48px`.
- Radius: `9999px`.
- Active: Lavender Shock fill with dark icon.
- Inactive: transparent fill, `1px` Lavender Shock border.
- Icon: simple single-stroke sport figure.

## Filled Lavender Button

- Fill: Lavender Shock.
- Text: Deep Plum.
- Radius: `9999px`.
- Padding: `12px 20px`.
- Font: Neue Montreal, `14px`, weight `500`.
- Shadow: none.
- Use for compact one-word actions.

## Navigation Bar

- Surface: transparent.
- Height: `64px`.
- Padding: `20px` horizontal.
- Logo: left.
- Links: centered, Neue Montreal `14px`.
- Link color: Paper White on dark, Slate on light.
- Action: Lavender Shock pill on the right.

## Wordmark Logo

- Text: stacked club name in Druk Condensed.
- Size: around `20px`.
- Weight: `400`.
- Color: Lavender Shock.
- Decoration: small sport or club mark.
- No tagline.

## Light Section Display Block

- Surface: Paper White.
- Type: Druk Condensed, `187px`.
- Line height: `0.85`.
- Color: Black or Charcoal.
- Layout: left aligned, occupying about 60% of the viewport.
- Use as the bright poster break after dark sections.

## Card Surface

- Radius: `14px`.
- Padding: `20px`.
- Shadow: none.
- Dark variant: Espresso Brown with Paper White text.
- Light variant: Paper White with Slate text and `1px` light border.

## Badge Or Tag

- Radius: `6px`.
- Padding: `3px to 5px` vertical, `8px to 10px` horizontal.
- Fill: Mint Signal, Amber Pulse, or Lavender Shock.

## Footer Link List

- Text: Neue Montreal, `14px`.
- Color: Paper White on dark or Slate on light.
