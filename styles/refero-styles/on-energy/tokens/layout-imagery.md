# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Full-bleed page model.
- No global max-width constraint on major color sections.
- Dark hero sections use product photography and left-aligned white type.
- Yellow statement sections span edge to edge.
- Industrial White content sections use generous padding and text columns.
- Major section gap is about 120px.
- News and editorial content can use 2-column image grids.

## Section Rhythm

1. Dark industrial hero with hardware image and white headline.
2. Full yellow statement section with large light-weight text.
3. Industrial white content section with black headings and body.
4. Repeat the dark, yellow, white rhythm as needed.

## Imagery

- Use close-up industrial product photography.
- Prefer metal panels, rack hardware, power equipment, and infrastructure.
- Dark moody lighting is appropriate for hero sections.
- Team photography may use yellow backdrop.
- Infrastructure shots should show real deployment.
- Avoid lifestyle office scenes, staged people, abstract illustration, and decorative 3D renders.

## Image Treatment

- Full-bleed atmospheric images for dark sections.
- 16px radius only when images are inside editorial content sections.
- No image shadows or gradient overlays.
