# Colors

## Core Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caution Yellow | `#fff313` | `--color-caution-yellow` | CTA fill, full yellow sections, accent borders, small emphasis |
| Midnight Steel | `#000000` | `--color-midnight-steel` | Dark hero, nav background, text on yellow, black canvas |
| Industrial White | `#eeeeee` | `--color-industrial-white` | Light section backgrounds, text on dark, news surfaces |
| Carbon | `#333333` | `--color-carbon` | Dark nav pills, cards, elevated dark surfaces without shadow |
| Gunmetal | `#4b4b4b` | `--color-gunmetal` | Hairline borders, dividers, dark structural lines |
| Aluminum | `#afafaf` | `--color-aluminum` | Muted body text on dark backgrounds and footer text |

## Application Notes

- Yellow is the signature color moment, not a decorative scatter color.
- Use black text on yellow and white text on dark.
- Use Aluminum for dark-section body copy.
- Use Industrial White as a light section background rather than pure white.
- Do not introduce additional accent hues.
