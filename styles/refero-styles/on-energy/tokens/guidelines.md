# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Univers Next Pro 300 for every display and heading moment.
- Use Univers Next Pro 400 for body and UI.
- Use Caution Yellow for CTAs, full yellow sections, and small accents.
- Alternate full-bleed dark image, yellow, and industrial white sections.
- Use black text on yellow and Industrial White text on dark.
- Keep nav and button radius at 6px.
- Keep cards at 9px radius.
- Maintain 120px vertical gaps between major sections.

## Do Not

- Do not add box shadows.
- Do not add gradients.
- Do not use weights 500, 600, or 700.
- Do not place yellow body text on yellow backgrounds.
- Do not use black text on black backgrounds.
- Do not use large radii above 16px on functional elements.
- Do not constrain full-bleed sections to a global max-width container.
- Do not use lifestyle imagery as the primary visual language.

## Accessibility Notes

- Maintain fixed contrast pairs: black on yellow, white on dark.
- If using muted yellow on yellow, check contrast carefully and keep it decorative only.
- Keep nav controls keyboard-focusable and visibly outlined.
- Do not rely only on product imagery to communicate section meaning.
