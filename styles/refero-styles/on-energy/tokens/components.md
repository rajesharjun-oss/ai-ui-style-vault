# Components

## Yellow CTA Button

- Fill: Caution Yellow.
- Text: Midnight Steel.
- Radius: 6px.
- Padding: 7px 16px.
- Font: Univers Next Pro 400, 14px.
- Shadow: none.
- Gradient: none.

## Dark Navigation Pill

- Fill: Carbon.
- Text: Industrial White.
- Radius: 6px.
- Padding: 5px 14px.
- Font: Univers Next Pro 400, 14px.
- Use as horizontal nav items.

## Logo Mark

- Small Caution Yellow square.
- Black `ON` text.
- Sharp corners.
- Use inside or beside dark nav.

## Hero Headline

- Font: Univers Next Pro.
- Weight: 300.
- Size: 64px.
- Line height: 1.
- Tracking: -2.56px.
- Color: Industrial White.
- Placement: left aligned on dark full-bleed photography.

## Product Highlight Card

- Fill: Carbon or near-black.
- Border: optional Gunmetal or Caution Yellow accent.
- Radius: 9px.
- Padding: 24px.
- Thumbnail on right.
- Yellow label text on left.

## Full-Bleed Yellow Statement Section

- Background: Caution Yellow.
- Width: 100vw.
- Height: often 100vh.
- Text: Univers 300, 36px to 64px.
- Text color: Midnight Steel or carefully muted yellow tone.
- No containers, dividers, shadows, or gradients.

## News Image Card

- Light section background: Industrial White.
- Image radius: 16px.
- Title: Univers 300, 24px, Midnight Steel.
- Layout: 2-column grid with 16px gap.

## Section Heading

- Univers 300.
- Size: 36px.
- Line height: 1.13.
- Tracking: -1.44px.
- Color: Midnight Steel.
- Left aligned.

## Body Text Block

- Univers 400, 16px.
- Line height: 1.2.
- Max width: 400px to 500px.
- Color: Midnight Steel on light and yellow, Aluminum on dark.

## Navigation Bar Container

- Transparent or semi-transparent over hero.
- Solid Midnight Steel or Carbon over yellow or light sections.
- Full width.
- 24px horizontal padding.
- Logo left, nav pills center, yellow CTA right.
