# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Full-bleed dark hero.
- Centered 1200px content after the hero.
- Alternating white and mist-white body sections.
- 50/50 split sections: text left, 3D render right.
- Two-column tinted feature cards for product panels.
- Sticky top navigation can invert from dark to light as the page scrolls.
- No sidebars, mega-menus, or dense dashboard surfaces.

## Imagery

- Use 3D abstract renders instead of photos or illustrations.
- Materials: chrome, liquid metal, dichroic glass, iridescent facets, polished financial artifacts.
- Lighting: dramatic single key light, caustics, lens flare, and reflective highlights.
- Renders should occupy 40-60% of major sections.
- Renders should not be masked, rounded, or framed.

## Composition Checks

- Is the hero dark and gallery-like?
- Are headlines serif?
- Are product panels square and tinted?
- Are controls exactly 2px radius?
- Is depth coming from 3D renders instead of shadows?
- Are action colors used only for action moments?
