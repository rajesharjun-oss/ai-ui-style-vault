# Components

## Navigation Bar Dark

Dark hero navigation on `#191b1f` with white 13px extended-grotesque links. Use flat color, no blur, and no shadow. Join can be a white-outline 2px ghost button.

## Navigation Bar Light

Light-section navigation with white background, vault-ink text, and a dark filled join button. Keep structure identical to the dark nav.

## Teal Filled Button

Primary button using `#186f64`, white 16px medium sans text, 2px radius, 12px vertical padding, 27px horizontal padding, no gradient, no shadow.

## Violet Filled Button

Secondary button using `#536eff`, same typography and geometry as the teal button.

## Blue Filled Button

Tertiary button using `#154ea5`, same typography and geometry as the teal button.

## Ghost Text Link

No background and no border. Use 16px extended-grotesque text. Match the color of the adjacent filled button.

## Tinted Feature Card

Use peach, mint, or lavender background. Card has 0px radius, no border, no shadow, 32px padding, a 22px serif heading, 16px body copy, a filled action, a ghost link, and a large 3D render.

## Dark Hero Stage

Full-bleed `#191b1f` section with centered 80px serif headline, smaller serif support copy, and a large 3D render floating in the lower section.

## Split Content Section

Two-column 1200px section. Text on left, render on right. Background is white or mist white. Maintain generous empty space around the render.

## 3D Abstract Render

Metallic or prismatic artifact with gallery lighting. Do not place it in a card, mask, frame, or rounded container.
