# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Albra Sans-style serif for every headline.
- Use Neufile Grotesk Extended-style sans for UI and body text.
- Keep buttons at 2px radius.
- Use 80px serif display only in the hero.
- Pair every filled button with a matching ghost text link.
- Use tinted wall colors only for product cards.
- Let 3D renders float in raw negative space.
- Keep UI surfaces flat and shadowless.

## Don't

- Do not use sans-serif headlines.
- Do not add rounded cards, pill buttons, or circular avatars.
- Do not use teal, violet, or blue for body text, ordinary borders, or large backgrounds.
- Do not make peach, mint, or lavender full-page backgrounds.
- Do not add gradients to buttons, cards, or sections.
- Do not add drop shadows, box shadows, glass, blur, or glow to UI surfaces.
- Do not mask 3D renders inside containers.

## Accessibility Notes

- Keep button text contrast high against colored fills.
- Do not rely on color alone to distinguish action priority.
- Ensure ghost links have visible hover and keyboard focus states.
- Large serif headlines should wrap cleanly on mobile rather than shrinking indefinitely.
