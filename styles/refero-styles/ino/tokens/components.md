# Components

## Bottom Navigation

- Position: bottom anchored or bottom of section.
- Labels: uppercase Sequel100Wide, `11px` to `12px`.
- Tracking: `0.10em` to `0.12em`.
- Indicators: small circles above or beside labels.
- Color: Ink active, Stone inactive.
- Surface: White.

## Circle Indicator

- Size: `6px` to `10px`.
- Shape: circle.
- Active fill: Ink.
- Inactive: transparent with Ink or Stone border, or Stone fill at low emphasis.
- Use for current page, collection, image, or slide state.

## Product Gallery Card

- Surface: transparent or Chalk image field.
- Padding: `0px`.
- Radius: `0px`.
- Shadow: none.
- Image: centered jewelry/object photography.
- Text: tiny uppercase product name and price below or aligned beside image.
- Border: none unless a very light Fog line is needed.

## Micro Product Label

- Type: Sequel100Wide, `11px` to `13px`, weight `400`.
- Case: uppercase.
- Tracking: `0.08em` to `0.12em`.
- Color: Ink for primary, Stone for secondary.
- Use for product name, metal, size, or collection.

## Object Detail Split

- Layout: large image field plus sparse data column.
- Image surface: Chalk or White.
- Data: product name, material, price, size, and add action in tiny rows.
- Dividers: Fog hairlines.
- Keep the product image visually dominant.

## Utility Link Row

- Type: Sequel100Wide, `11px` to `12px`.
- Links: account, cart, search, collection, material, or price ticker.
- Color: Ink and Stone.
- Layout: evenly spaced or edge-pinned.

## Thin Bordered Button

- Fill: White or transparent.
- Border: `1px solid` Ink.
- Radius: `4px`.
- Padding: `10px 14px`.
- Text: uppercase, `11px` to `12px`, tracking `0.10em`.
- Hover: Ink fill and White text, or Fog field.

## Footer Material Ticker

- Content: material, metal, size, pricing, care, or studio metadata.
- Type: tiny uppercase Sequel100Wide.
- Color: Stone and Ink.
- Dividers: Fog hairlines.
- Keep it informational and quiet.

## Pale Image Field

- Fill: Chalk or Fog.
- Radius: `0px`.
- Padding: minimal or none.
- Image placement: centered and carefully scaled.
- Shadow: none.
