# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Model

- Use White as a continuous gallery canvas.
- Use sparse top utility links and stronger bottom navigation.
- Keep product grids clean, with pale image fields and minimal labels.
- Use large whitespace around small objects so they feel inspected.
- Keep detail pages split between product image and tiny metadata rows.
- Use bottom circle indicators for browsing states.
- Avoid loud marketing sections and dense category cards.

## Imagery

- Use clinical jewelry, metal, gem, watch, accessory, or object photography.
- Prefer isolated products on white, chalk, or fog backgrounds.
- Center objects with careful scale and lots of negative space.
- Avoid lifestyle stock, fashion campaign clutter, colorful backdrops, and illustrated placeholders.
- Keep images crisp and product-first.

## Motion

- Motion should be nearly invisible.
- Use fade, tiny opacity changes, precise slide transitions, and image swaps.
- Avoid playful easing, bouncy hovers, rotating badges, or continuous motion.
