# Guidelines

## Do

- Use a single wide sans typeface at weight 400.
- Keep typography in the 11px to 18px range.
- Use uppercase labels with wide tracking.
- Use only neutral white, black, pale gray, and muted gray.
- Let jewelry or object photography create the luxury signal.
- Use bottom navigation with small circular indicators.
- Keep product cards zero-padded and nearly frameless.
- Use hairline dividers and whitespace instead of decorative UI.

## Don't

- Do not add a colored CTA or accent hue.
- Do not use large hero marketing headlines.
- Do not use shadows, glows, gradients, or glass effects.
- Do not use rounded ecommerce cards or pill buttons.
- Do not use multiple font families or multiple weights.
- Do not add promo stickers, badges, icons, or illustrations.
- Do not make the page feel like a generic Shopify theme.
- Do not crowd product photography with explanatory copy.
