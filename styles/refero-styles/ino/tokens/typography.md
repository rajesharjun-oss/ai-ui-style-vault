# Typography

## Font Stack

- Primary: `Sequel100Wide`
- Fallback: `Eurostile Extended`, `Neue Haas Grotesk Display`, `Inter Tight`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Weights: `400` only

## Scale

| Purpose | Size | Line height | Weight | Tracking | Token |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Micro label | `11px` | `1.2` | `400` | `0.12em` | `--text-micro` |
| Navigation | `12px` | `1.2` | `400` | `0.10em` | `--text-nav` |
| Product label | `13px` | `1.25` | `400` | `0.08em` | `--text-product-label` |
| Body small | `14px` | `1.4` | `400` | `0.04em` | `--text-body-sm` |
| Body | `16px` | `1.45` | `400` | `0.04em` | `--text-body` |
| Quiet heading | `18px` | `1.25` | `400` | `0.06em` | `--text-heading` |

## Rules

- Use a single wide sans throughout.
- Use weight 400 only.
- Keep type tiny, spaced, and controlled.
- Use uppercase for navigation, product labels, material rows, and utility text.
- Avoid large display type, serif contrast, or expressive font pairing.
