# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Max width: 1216px centered.
- Page canvas: continuous Obsidian.
- Hero: split layout with left serif headline and email capture; right product dashboard card.
- Section rhythm: 160px vertical gaps on desktop.
- Later sections: centered serif headline, Inter subtitle, 3-column grids, stat callouts, blog cards, testimonial media cards, and dense tables.
- Navigation: fixed top bar with logo left, center menu, ghost Sign in, and one white Get Started pill.
- Footer: dense multi-column link grid.

## Imagery

Use:

- Dark product UI screenshots.
- Editorial founder/team photography in real environments.
- Slightly desaturated warm portraits.
- Blog thumbnails with editorial finance concepts.
- Monochrome 1px icons.

Avoid:

- Stock-photo polish.
- Bright chromatic UI imagery.
- Copper-filled icons.
- Decorative gradients outside financial charts.
- Heavy image frames and drop shadows.
