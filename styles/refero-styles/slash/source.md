## What To Preserve

Slash depends on a serif/sans collision. Ivy Presto supplies the editorial finance identity, while Inter carries the actual UI. The palette is almost monochrome, with one warm copper note for editorial punctuation and one gilded gradient for financial chart lines.

The page relies on:

- Obsidian as the continuous page canvas.
- Onyx and Carbon as dark product surfaces.
- Bone as the default readable UI text.
- White filled pill used only for the single most important action per viewport.
- Copper for category labels and editorial links.
- Gilded gradient only in data visualization.
- Ivy Presto for headings at 28px and above.
- Inter for body, nav, buttons, forms, labels, and tables.
- 9999px pill geometry for controls.
- 10px cards, no shadows, and 1px borders.
