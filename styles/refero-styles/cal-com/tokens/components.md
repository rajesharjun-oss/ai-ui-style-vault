# Components

## Primary CTA Button

- Shape: pill
- Background: `#101010`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 9999px
- Font: Cal Sans UI, 14px to 16px
- Padding: about 12px 24px

## Secondary Ghost Button

- Shape: pill
- Background: transparent or `#f4f4f4`
- Text: `#242424`
- Border: 1px `#e5e7eb`
- Radius: 9999px
- Padding: about 12px 24px

## Header CTA Button

- Shape: compact rectangle
- Background: `#101010`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 8px
- Font: Cal Sans UI, 14px
- Padding: about 8px 16px

## Tag Button

Small pill for categories, filters, and non-critical controls. Use Paper or Silver fill, Graphite text, 9999px radius, and about 4px 12px padding.

## Scheduling Widget Card

White card, 12px radius, 16px padding, subtle shadow. It should contain a realistic calendar, availability picker, or meeting booking UI.

## Feature Card

White card, 12px radius, 24px padding, subtle diffuse shadow. Include a small tag or number, a 20px Cal Sans heading, and compact supporting text.

## Navigation Link

Graphite text, Cal Sans UI 14px to 16px, no underline by default, compact spacing.

## Information Banner

Use Info Banner BG with Action Blue links or text. Keep the banner functional and restrained.

## Integration Logo Row

Use real integration logos. Logo colors are allowed, but the surrounding UI remains monochrome.
