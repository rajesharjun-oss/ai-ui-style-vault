## Capture Notes

The Refero page describes Cal.com as a pragmatic monochrome utility system. It uses strict black, white, and gray UI color for almost everything, with a rare Action Blue for secondary links and informational highlights.

The AI-useful pieces to preserve are:

- Cal Sans weight 600 for headings only.
- Cal Sans UI Variable Light or Inter Light for body copy.
- Tight negative tracking for body and UI text.
- Pill CTAs with 9999px radius.
- Header CTA may use an 8px rectangular radius.
- 12px radius cards with soft diffuse shadows.
- Light gray Paper page background and White card surfaces.
- Product-centric visuals: scheduling widget, calendar UI, integration logos, and contained screenshots.
