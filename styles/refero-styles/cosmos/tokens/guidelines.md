# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Linen Canvas as the page root background.
- Reserve Paper White for cards, inputs, and outlined buttons.
- Apply 16px radius to containers, buttons, inputs, and video blocks.
- Use 12px radius only for small image tiles.
- Set hero and display copy in weight 350 with tight letter spacing.
- Pair primary filled and secondary outlined buttons at matching sizes.
- Let user imagery provide all strong color.
- Use 24px grid gaps and 16px internal card padding as the default rhythm.
- Keep the navigation as one floating pill.

## Don't

- Do not add red, blue, green, or other accent colors to the UI chrome.
- Do not use font weights above 500.
- Do not add shadows to normal cards, buttons, or nav.
- Do not set body copy outside the 14px to 18px range.
- Do not mix in unrelated radii such as 8px or 24px.
- Do not use pure black `#000000`.
- Do not place colored UI backgrounds behind text.
- Do not build dashboards, analytics tables, or data-heavy screens in this style.
