# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Linen Canvas | `#f7f5f3` | `--color-linen-canvas` | Root page background and warm gallery-wall canvas |
| Ink Black | `#0d0d0d` | `--color-ink-black` | Primary text, icons, filled controls, and low-opacity borders |
| Paper White | `#ffffff` | `--color-paper-white` | Cards, inputs, outlined controls, and light elevated surfaces |
| Stone | `#6e6a69` | `--color-stone` | Body text, secondary icons, inactive UI, and metadata |
| Pebble | `#9a9796` | `--color-pebble` | Helper text, disabled states, placeholders, and tertiary labels |

## Rules

- Do not use pure `#000000`.
- Do not introduce red, blue, green, or other accent colors into the interface.
- Strong color should come from user imagery only.
- Use Ink Black borders at roughly 10% to 15% opacity instead of adding a separate border gray.
- Use Paper White as the only elevated surface above Linen Canvas.
