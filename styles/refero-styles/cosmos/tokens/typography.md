# Typography

## Families

| Family | Token | Role |
|---|---|---|
| cosmosOracle | `--font-cosmosoracle` | Single custom face for display, body, labels, buttons, and UI chrome |
| GT Super / Fraunces / Georgia | fallback | Serif-influenced fallback when the custom face is unavailable |

## Weights

| Weight | Use |
|---:|---|
| `350` | Hero and display copy |
| `400` | Body copy and section labels |
| `500` | Buttons, nav labels, small strong labels |

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Line Height | Letter Spacing | Token |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Caption | `14px` | `1.5` | `0` | `--text-caption` |
| Body | `16px` | `1.5` | `-0.18px` | `--text-body` |
| Subheading | `18px` | `1.29` | `-0.2px` | `--text-subheading` |
| Heading Small | `24px` | `1.2` | `-0.48px` | `--text-heading-sm` |
| Heading | `33px` | `1.1` | `-1.32px` | `--text-heading` |
| Heading Large | `38px` | `1.08` | `-1.52px` | `--text-heading-lg` |
| Display | `58px` | `1.06` | `-2.32px` | `--text-display` |
| Display XL | `74px` | `0.8` | `-3.7px` | `--text-display-xl` |

## Rules

- The soft, light display weight is the brand voice.
- Do not substitute a bold sans for display type.
- Keep large text tight and low, almost like an editorial masthead.
- Keep body copy between 14px and 18px.
- Avoid weights above 500.
