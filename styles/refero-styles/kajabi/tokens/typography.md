# Typography

## Family

- Primary: Haffer.
- Fallback: Inter, Sohne, General Sans, Arial, sans-serif.
- Use one typeface across headings, body, navigation, buttons, and product UI.

## Weights

- 400: body, most UI, and most marketing headlines.
- 500: subheadings, buttons, and emphasis.
- 600: statistics, numbers, and creator names, not marketing display headlines.

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caption | 12px | 400 | 1.5 | 0 |
| Body Small | 14px | 400 | 1.45 | 0 |
| Body | 16px | 400 | 1.5 | 0 |
| Subheading | 20px | 400 | 1.4 | 0 |
| Heading Small | 24px | 500 | 1.3 | -0.4px |
| Heading | 32px | 500 | 1.2 | -0.64px |
| Heading Large | 40px | 600 | 1.1 | -0.8px |
| Poster | 48px | 500 | 1 | -1.2px |
| Display | 60px | 400 | 1 | -1.8px |

## Usage

- Let scale do the emotional work.
- Use display type at 48px to 60px for primary marketing statements.
- Tighten display tracking only at 40px and above.
- Keep body copy at normal tracking.
- Avoid heavy marketing headlines.
