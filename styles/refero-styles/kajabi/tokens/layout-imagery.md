# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a centered 1280px max-width container.
- Hero is centered text first: headline, subtext, email input, and CTA.
- Follow the hero with a full-bleed or wide portrait strip.
- Use a centered stats row for social proof.
- Use Charcoal dark bands for category/video sections.
- Return to Paper White for newsletter and editorial content.
- Keep navigation thin and restrained.

## Section Patterns

- Hero: centered 60px display headline, 20px subtext, email plus CTA row.
- Portrait strip: horizontal sequence of creator portraits with bottom overlays.
- Stats row: three centered columns.
- Dark video band: Charcoal background, centered headline, tab pills, video frame, floating overlay card.
- Newsletter: two-column text and form on white.
- Footer: monochrome lockup and restrained links.

## Imagery

- Use expert portraits, creator photography, coaching scenes, workspace images, and warm lifestyle video.
- Keep natural skin tones and earthy environments.
- Do not filter or duotone imagery.
- Avoid illustrations, 3D renders, abstract graphics, and decorative gradients.
- Use minimal line icons in Ink Black or Driftwood at 1.5px to 2px stroke.
