# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a vertical flow: nav, hero, zone band, grid, zone band, grid, footer.
- Keep card grids and text blocks inside a 1280px max-width container.
- Let the hero and section header bands stretch edge-to-edge.
- Use 4-column artwork grids on desktop.
- Keep dense catalog rhythm without making the UI utilitarian.
- Avoid sidebars and heavy breadcrumb flows in the main page.

## Section Patterns

- Navigation: flat white bar with black bottom border.
- Hero: full-bleed dark image with centered display text.
- Zone header: gray or white band with label, description, and underlined link.
- Catalog grid: artwork cards in a consistent 4-column rhythm.
- Footer: white background, black top border, compact link lists.

## Imagery

- Use fine-art photography: paintings, sculptures, works on paper, and mixed-media objects.
- Crop artwork as straight product images on white.
- Keep artwork edge-to-edge inside card frames.
- Use one dark full-bleed atmospheric hero image as an editorial mood-setter.
- Avoid lifestyle room scenes, human models, decorative graphics, icons beyond utility glyphs, and colorful UI overlays.
