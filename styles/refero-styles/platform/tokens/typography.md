# Typography

## Family

- Primary: MediumLLWeb.
- Fallback: Sohne, Inter, Neue Haas Grotesk, Arial, sans-serif.
- Use one typeface for UI, body, metadata, and display.

## Weights

- 400: default for body, navigation, descriptions, and display.
- 500: labels, artist names, prices, and compact emphasis.

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caption | 12px | 500 | 1.2 | 0.01em |
| Body Small | 15px | 400 | 1.2 | 0.01em |
| Subheading | 20px | 500 | 1.15 | 0.01em |
| Heading | 48px | 400 | 1.15 | 0.02em |
| Display | 72px | 400 | 1 | 0.02em |

## Usage

- Keep the scale to exactly five sizes.
- Use 15px as the main body size.
- Use 12px 500 uppercase for compact labels and artist names.
- Use 48px and 72px for editorial hero and spotlight type.
- Enable tabular numerals for prices and numeric metadata.
- Do not use 13px to 17px body sizes.
