# Components

## Pill Navigation Bar

Floating Pure White horizontal bar, 10px radius, 11px to 30px horizontal padding, positioned near the top center. Contains a small star or compass icon plus inline text links.

## Ghost Link Button

No fill and no border. Off-Black text at 16px weight 350. Underline on hover only.

## Signal Blue Text Link

Signal Blue text, no fill, no underline at rest, optional 0.5px Signal Blue bottom border on hover.

## Pill Tag Chip

9999px radius, 0.5px Signal Blue border, Signal Blue text, no fill, 2px 10px padding, 10px to 12px weight 350.

## Rounded Card

Pure White surface, 54px radius, 69px padding, 0.5px semi-transparent black border, no shadow.

## Floating CTA Pill

26px radius, near-transparent charcoal wash, Pure Black 13px weight 400 text, and a small Signal Blue icon. Keep it restrained.

## Dark Band Footer

Full-width Off-Black footer with centered Pure White text, 50px top padding, and 69px horizontal padding.

## Section Label Pair

Small 10px to 12px Ash Gray label above 27px Off-Black heading. Both use weight 350.

## Inline Icon Link

Signal Blue icon and Signal Blue text, small and inline. Use for research links or update links.

## Update Banner

Small floating banner with transparent charcoal wash and a Signal Blue outlined NEW badge.
