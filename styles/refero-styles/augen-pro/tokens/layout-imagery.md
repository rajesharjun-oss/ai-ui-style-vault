# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use full-bleed sections with centered 1200px content.
- Place a floating pill nav at the top center.
- Build the hero as a full-viewport composition.
- Put sparse text bottom-left and the 3D render centered-right.
- Separate editorial sections with 90px to 100px gaps.
- Use asymmetric grids for content, especially label, heading, and paragraph columns.
- Keep long body content left-aligned.
- End with a full-width dark footer band.

## Imagery

Use one high-fidelity 3D clinical render:

- Human or device portrait.
- Cool white surroundings.
- Soft fade into white.
- Warm organic detail against clinical background.
- No dense image gallery.

Avoid:

- Photography.
- Stock imagery.
- Decorative illustration.
- Product screenshot grids.
- Gradients.
- Patterns.
- Background texture.

The render carries depth. The interface stays quiet.
