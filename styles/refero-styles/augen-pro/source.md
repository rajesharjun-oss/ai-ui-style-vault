## Capture Notes

The Refero page describes Augen Pro as a surgical-white, premium consumer-tech style with deep charcoal text and one precise Signal Blue accent. The system avoids shadows and decorative chrome, relying on white-tone shifts, hairline borders, pill controls, and a single high-fidelity 3D hero render.

The AI-useful pieces to preserve are:

- PP Neue Montreal or equivalent whisper-weight geometric sans.
- Weight 350 for nearly all text; 400 only for tiny button or icon labels.
- Uniform `-0.0200em` letter spacing.
- Signal Blue used only as text, border, icon, or focus edge, never as fill.
- Off-White canvas with Pure White elevated panels.
- 54px rounded cards and 9999px tag pills.
- 90px to 100px section gaps.
- No drop shadows; use 0.5px hairline borders.
- Floating pill nav instead of full-width header.
- One 3D clinical hero render carries the visual identity.
