# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a 1200px centered content width.
- Keep the hero, announcement bar, and dark navigation full-bleed.
- Use full-height hero photography with overlaid serif headline.
- Alternate cream and white bands with 80px vertical rhythm.
- Use two-column feature splits.
- Use horizontal 4-card rows for solution categories.
- Use grayscale logo strips directly on the cream canvas.
- Keep the page spacious, with few elements per row.

## Imagery

- Use warm-lit human portrait photography.
- Favor close crops, natural expressions, golden-hour tones, and gentle lens warmth.
- Blend hero imagery into Canvas Cream with a diagonal gradient overlay.
- Use outlined icons in Ink Black or Highlighter Orange.
- Avoid abstract hero graphics, 3D renders, generic corporate stock, cool-toned backgrounds, and multicolor icons.
