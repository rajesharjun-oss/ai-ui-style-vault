# Components

## Top Announcement Bar

Espresso full-bleed bar, Geist 14px copy, Highlighter Orange headline/link accents, white supporting copy, and compact 8px vertical padding.

## Primary Navigation

Ink Black or Espresso full-width dark nav. Logo left, centered white Geist links, search/language/demo controls right, compact padding, and no shadow.

## Hero With Image Overlay

Full-height warm-lit portrait photograph with a diagonal cream gradient overlay. Set Exposure30 82px to 96px headline in the bottom-left. No card around the headline.

## Logo Trust Bar

Horizontal grayscale partner logo row on Canvas Cream. No card backgrounds, no heavy labels.

## Feature Split Section

Two-column layout. Left side has Exposure30 42px heading, Geist body, and dark CTA. Right side has a flat Paper White preview card with Hairline border.

## Industry Solution Card

Paper White, 0px radius, 1px Hairline border, 24px padding, outlined icon, Exposure30 24px title, Geist 14px Stone body. Add one small orange dot to the featured card only.

## Primary CTA Button

Ink Black fill, Paper White text, Geist 14px/500, 12px vertical padding, 11px horizontal padding, 2px radius, no shadow.

## Ghost Text Button

Transparent, Ink Black text, Geist 14px/400, 2px radius, minimal padding, and an orange arrow or underline.

## Navigation CTA Button

Outlined white button on dark nav, 1px white border, white text, 2px radius, 8px by 12px padding.

## Outlined Accent Element

Highlighter Orange border or stroke, transparent fill. Use for links, icon badges, accents, and callouts. Never fill it.

## Section Heading

Exposure30 42px/400, Ink Black, line-height 1.10, slight negative tracking, no decorative accompaniment.

## Product Preview Card

Paper White, 0px radius, 1px Hairline border, 24px padding, small abstract/product graphic, and Geist body copy.
