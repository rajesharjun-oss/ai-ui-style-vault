## What To Preserve

Parloa uses a warm cream page, dark ink typography, dark navigation bars, flat white content cards, and a single vivid orange accent. The orange should behave like a marker stroke: links, outlines, icon strokes, dots, and callouts only. Primary actions are dark ink filled buttons with white text.

The page relies on:

- Canvas Cream as the dominant page background.
- Paper White cards pinned to the canvas with 1px Hairline borders.
- Ink Black for body text, headings, buttons, and dark surfaces.
- Espresso for announcement and secondary dark bars.
- Highlighter Orange for accent outlines and link marks.
- Exposure30 for display headings only.
- Geist for navigation, body, buttons, labels, and metadata.
- 0px to 2px radii for the printed/editorial feel.
- Flat surfaces without box shadows.
- Warm-lit photography in the hero with a cream gradient overlay.
