# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Model

- Center content in a 1200px max-width rail.
- Use full-bleed Chalk Canvas as the page background.
- Hero is a centered text stack with filled pill and ghost link.
- Place a horizontal feature-card carousel after the hero.
- Alternate white and Lilac Wash sections for product explanations.
- Use two-column sections with product mockups beside copy.
- Use a 5-column footer link grid on Chalk Canvas.
- Keep density compact, with 80px between major sections and 8px between small elements.

## Imagery

- Prefer product-first visuals over lifestyle photography.
- Use flashcard interfaces, study guide tables, diagrams, practice-test screens, score cards, and illustrated UI cards.
- Use flat white product panels inside pastel feature-card containers.
- Let the card carousel provide the main color splash.

## Avoid

- Lifestyle-photo hero treatments.
- Abstract decorative gradients.
- Heavy 3D mockups.
- Extra saturated accent colors.
- Full pages of lilac or pastel fill.
