# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Iris Bolt only for filled interactive elements, links, and active states.
- Apply 200px radius to all primary filled buttons.
- Set body text in Ink Charcoal at weight 400.
- Use 8px radius on content cards.
- Use 24px radius only on feature category cards.
- Build Paper White card surfaces over Chalk Canvas.
- Use 80px section gaps, 24px large card padding, and 8px element gaps.
- Apply the single subtle shadow token only to nav bars and elevated cards.

## Don't

- Do not introduce additional saturated colors beyond Iris Bolt.
- Do not use 4px or 8px radius on primary filled buttons.
- Do not set headings to weight 700 by default.
- Do not use Pure Black for normal body text.
- Do not place cards directly on Lilac Wash without an inner white panel.
- Do not apply heavy or multi-layer shadows.
- Do not use Mist Border as a fill.
- Do not make feature pastels the whole palette; they are controlled category surfaces.
