# Typography

## Family

| Role | Preferred Font | Fallbacks | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whole system | hurme_no2-webfont | Hurme Geometric Sans, Inter, Arial | Geometric sans for body, nav, cards, buttons, and headings |

## Scale

| Token | Size | Line Height | Weight | Usage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `--text-caption` | 12px | 18px | 400 | metadata and small labels |
| `--text-body-sm` | 14px | 20px | 400 | nav, helper copy, secondary text |
| `--text-body` | 16px | 24px | 400 | main body and links |
| `--text-subheading` | 20px | 28px | 600-700 | feature labels |
| `--text-heading-sm` | 24px | 32px | 600-700 | compact headings |
| `--text-heading` | 32px | 41px | 600-700 | section headings |
| `--text-display` | 44px | 55px | 700 | hero headline |

## Rules

- Use one geometric sans family throughout.
- Keep normal body text at weight 400.
- Use weight 600 for button labels and most emphasis.
- Use weight 700 sparingly for feature card labels and display.
- Keep letter spacing at 0.
