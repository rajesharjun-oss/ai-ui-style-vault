# Typography

## Founders Grotesk

- **Primary role:** Workhorse grotesque for display, body, nav, meta, labels, and project descriptions.
- **Fallback:** Sohne, Inter, GT America, Neue Haas Grotesk, ui-sans-serif, system-ui
- **Weights:** 300 and 400
- **OpenType features:** `ss01`, `ss02`, `tnum`
- **Rule:** weight 300 is only for display and section titles at 40px and above.

## Editorial Neue

- **Role:** Rare editorial serif accent for pull-text or specific body-accent moments.
- **Fallback:** Tiempos Text, Lyon Display, Source Serif Pro
- **Weight:** 400
- **Size:** 16px
- **Line height:** 0.90
- **Letter spacing:** -0.0600em

| display | 70px | 0.88 | 300 | -0.03px | `--text-display` |
| display-xl | 101px | 0.8 | 300 | -0.06px | `--text-display-xl` |

## Rules

- Use uppercase for nav links, case study meta, project titles, and action labels.
- Keep display type architectural: huge, light, tight, and compressed.
- Keep body at weight 400.
- Do not use line-height above 1.20 anywhere.
- Do not use underlines for links.
