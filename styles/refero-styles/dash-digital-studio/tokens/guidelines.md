# Guidelines

## Do

- Use only achromatic colors.
- Keep the page background Bone `#f0f0f0`.
- Use Carbon Black `#2a2a2a` for primary text.
- Use giant uppercase display headings.
- Set display headings in weight 300.
- Keep body text in weight 400.
- Use 0.80-0.88 line-height for display.
- Use tight negative tracking.
- Use 0px radius for images and cards.
- Use 999px radius only for pill buttons and tags.
- Use 1px hairline rules sparingly.
- Present work through full-bleed product photography.

## Don't

- Do not introduce color accents.
- Do not use colored CTA buttons.
- Do not use weight 300 for body.
- Do not round product images.
- Do not apply shadows, glows, gradients, or glass effects.
- Do not underline text links.
- Do not add decorative icons, illustrations, or overlays.
- Do not crowd the layout with dense controls.

## Accessibility Notes

- Carbon Black on Bone gives strong text contrast.
- Avoid using Stone or Clay for long body copy.
- Keep focus states visible with monochrome outlines or border changes.
- Preserve readable heading wraps on small screens while retaining the compressed editorial feel.
