# Components

## Filled Action Button

- Role: primary CTA
- Background: `#0d111b`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 100px
- Padding: 8px 12px or 6px 14px in compact nav contexts
- Font: Open Runde, 14px, weight 500
- Tracking: -0.02em
- Shadow: dark 1px ring plus tiny drop shadow.

## Secondary Dark Button

- Background: `#0f0f0f`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 100px
- Padding: 6px 10px or 6px 14px
- Use for compact nav-level actions and dense button rows.

## Outline Link Button

- Background: `#ffffff`
- Text: `#1e1e1e`
- Radius: 100px
- Padding: 8px 12px
- Shadow: none
- Use as nav links inside the pill-shaped header.

## Feature Card

- Background: `#fafafa` or `#ffffff`
- Radius: 16px
- Padding: 24px
- Shadow: none
- Heading: Open Runde 24px, weight 600, tight tracking
- Body: Open Runde 16px, weight 500, `#666666`

## Elevated Card

- Background: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 20px
- Shadow: subtle layered shadow
- Padding: content may fill the card
- Use for invoice previews and testimonial quote cards.

## Large Feature Panel

- Background: `#fafafa`
- Radius: 32px
- Padding: 96px 48px
- Shadow: none
- Use for spacious editorial feature sections.

## Tinted Accent Card

- Background: Ice or lightly transparent Electric Blue
- Radius: 16px
- Shadow: none
- Use for payment callouts and highlighted info blocks.

## Invoice Mockup Card

- Background: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 16px
- Shadow: tiny paper shadow
- Include uppercase invoice labels, from/to blocks, brand-color circular icons, table rows, muted labels, and dark values.
- Stack multiple cards with slight rotation and overlap.

## Payment Rate Badge

- Inline row with no background, border, or padding.
- Electric Blue circular checkmark icon.
- Label text in Ink at 16px weight 500.
- Rate value in strong dark text.

## FAQ Accordion Row

- Full-width row.
- No background.
- No radius.
- 1px bottom border in Mist.
- Question text: 16px Open Runde weight 500 in Ink.
- Plus icon right-aligned.
- Vertical padding around 16px.

## Pill Header Container

- Background: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 100px
- Contains logo, pill nav links, login text, and filled CTA.
- Use 8px to 12px gaps.
- Float above the hero with subtle shadow.

## Eyebrow Label

- Font: Open Runde, 11px, weight 600
- Transform: uppercase
- Tracking: 0.02em
- Color: `#0098f2`
- No background or border.
