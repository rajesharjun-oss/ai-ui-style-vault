# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Open Runde weight 600 for headings at 20px and above.
- Apply tight negative tracking to headings and display text.
- Use Midnight filled pill buttons for primary CTAs.
- Use Electric Blue for checkmarks, rates, eyebrow labels, and inline highlights.
- Use 16px radius for content cards and 32px for large panels.
- Keep the page canvas pure white with subtle Snow or Soft Surface bands.
- Use Caveat only for testimonial attribution.
- Stack invoice mockup cards with subtle rotation and overlap.

## Do Not

- Do not use Electric Blue, Iris, or Magenta as primary button backgrounds.
- Do not use pure black for text or buttons.
- Do not apply box shadows to normal feature cards.
- Do not use border radius below 10px on interactive elements.
- Do not use gradient backgrounds.
- Do not set readable body text lighter than Smoke.
- Do not use more than two chromatic accents on one screen.
- Do not turn handwritten type into a general-purpose font.
