# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a max-width around 1200px.
- Keep section gaps generous, around 96px.
- Center hero content on a bright white canvas.
- Use a floating pill-shaped header above the hero.
- Use large rounded feature panels for major editorial sections.
- Use 16px feature cards in grids or comparison blocks.
- Use FAQ rows with simple divider lines.
- Let invoice mockup stacks be the main dimensional object.

## Imagery

- Use flat-lay office objects such as keyboards, clips, binders, monitors, paper, and desk details.
- Crop objects at page edges as atmospheric context.
- Prefer actual invoice UI mockups over illustrations.
- Use simple geometric icons, circles, and rounded squares.
- Avoid lifestyle photography, generic 3D renders, and decorative illustrations.

## Surfaces

| Level | Name | Value | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 0 | Paper | `#ffffff` | Main page canvas and card surface |
| 1 | Soft Surface | `#fafafa` | Feature cards and alternating bands |
| 2 | Snow | `#f7fafc` | Subtle section background |
| 3 | Ice | `#cfeafa` | Tinted payment highlights |
| 4 | Lavender | `#e1e0fc` | Secondary feature tint |
| 5 | Blush | `#f6d2f4` | Pink decorative tint |

## Motion

- Keep transitions short and simple.
- Use subtle opacity, transform, and shadow changes.
- Avoid dramatic parallax, elastic motion, and gradient animation.
