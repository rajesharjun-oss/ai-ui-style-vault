# Implementation Prompt

Build a bright invoicing and payments interface in the Acctual style.

Use a white paper canvas with subtle Snow section bands. Set primary text in near-black Ink `#1e1e1e`, secondary copy in Smoke `#666666`, and primary CTA fills in Midnight `#0d111b` with white text. Do not use pure black for the main voice.

Use Open Runde or a rounded geometric sans such as Inter, DM Sans, or Outfit. Headings from 20px upward should be weight 600 with about -0.03em tracking. Body text should be weight 500 with slightly tight tracking. Use small 11px uppercase eyebrow labels in Electric Blue `#0098f2`.

Use accent colors sparingly. Electric Blue is for checkmarks, payment rates, inline highlights, and eyebrow labels. Iris, Magenta, Leaf, and Coral are decorative or secondary cues. Do not use these colors as primary button backgrounds or large decorative fills.

Make controls pill-shaped with 100px radius. Use 16px radius for content cards, 20px for invoice mockup cards and images, 32px for large feature panels, and fully round icon dots. Layer invoice cards with subtle shadows, slight rotation, and overlapping offsets. Keep feature cards flat.

Avoid gradients, heavy shadows, pure black, sharp interactive corners, dense gray body text below `#666666`, and too many accents on one screen. The result should feel like a modern billing product built out of white paper, rounded documents, and small blue finance signals.
