# Acctual - Style Reference

Acctual feels like paper invoices on frosted glass: bright canvas, precise dark type, soft document cards, and rare electric-blue signals. The system is friendly but not whimsical, financial but not cold.

## Visual Thesis

- Keep the interface on a white paper canvas.
- Use near-black text and dark navy-black pill CTAs.
- Use Electric Blue only as functional punctuation.
- Use generous rounded forms: 16px cards, 20px document mockups, 32px large panels, 100px pills.
- Use layered invoice cards with offset and rotation as the signature visual.
- Keep shadows subtle and reserved for buttons, invoice mockups, and testimonial cards.

## Core Ingredients

- Canvas: Paper `#ffffff` with Snow `#f7fafc` or `#fafafa` section bands.
- Primary text: Ink `#1e1e1e`.
- CTA fill: Midnight `#0d111b`.
- Accent: Electric Blue `#0098f2`.
- Typography: Open Runde-style geometric sans with tight negative tracking.
- Human contrast: Caveat used only for testimonial signatures.
- Visual asset: invoice UI cards, not generic illustrations.

## Page Composition

Use a centered hero with a pill-shaped floating nav, compressed headline, dark pill CTA, and a stack of invoice mockup cards. Follow with white or near-white feature sections, 16px cards, 32px large panels, inline payment-rate badges, FAQ rows, testimonial cards, and sparse flat-lay office objects cropped around the page edges.

## Color Behavior

Most screens should be achromatic. Electric Blue, Iris, Magenta, Leaf, and Coral are accents, not main surfaces. Use at most two chromatic accents on a screen. The primary button never uses a bright accent fill.

## Type Behavior

Open Runde carries nearly everything. Use weight 600 for headings 20px and above, with tight negative tracking. Use weight 500 for body text. Use 11px uppercase labels with light positive tracking. Caveat appears only for small testimonial attribution moments.
