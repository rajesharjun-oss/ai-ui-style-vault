# Layout And Imagery

## Page Structure

1. Sticky top nav, 56px tall.
2. White centered hero with headline, support copy, and primary CTA.
3. Product browser mockup or 3-column feature card row.
4. Monochrome logo strip.
5. Alternating centered text sections and two-column product sections.
6. Tinted band or dark inverted callout where useful.
7. Footer or final CTA.

## Grid

- Desktop rail: 1200px.
- Desktop layout: 12-column grid.
- Hero card row: 3 columns.
- Two-column sections: text left, product visual right.
- Mobile: single-column stacks.

## Imagery Rules

Use UI and product proof instead of mood imagery.

Preferred:

- Browser chrome mockups.
- Product UI captures.
- Editor screenshots.
- Dashboard-like product screens.
- Monochrome customer logos.
- Flat inline illustrations with constrained accent colors.

Avoid:

- Lifestyle photography.
- Decorative gradients.
- 3D blobs or floating objects.
- Full-color logo clutter.
- Abstract scenes that hide the product.

## Motion

Motion should be short and professional:

- Button hover: color or opacity shift.
- Card hover: optional shadow.
- Mockup hover: subtle lift at most.
- No springy or playful motion.
