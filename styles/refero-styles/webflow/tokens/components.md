# Components

## Sticky Top Nav

- Height: 56px.
- Background: white.
- Border: optional 1px `#d8d8d8` bottom rule.
- Left: logo or wordmark in `#146ef5`.
- Center or left cluster: simple nav links, 14px.
- Right: login link plus primary CTA.
- CTA radius: 4px.
- Keep the nav single-row and compact. Do not add a mega-menu unless the product requires it.

## Primary Button

- Background: `#146ef5`.
- Text: white.
- Radius: 4px.
- Font: 14-16px, weight 600.
- Padding: 10px 16px or 12px 20px depending on prominence.
- Border: 1px solid `#146ef5`.
- Hover: slightly darker blue or subtle opacity.

Do not make it pill-shaped.

## Secondary Button

- Background: white.
- Text: `#080808`.
- Border: 1px solid `#d8d8d8`.
- Radius: 4px.
- Hover: light tint or border darkening.

## Hero Section

- Background: white.
- Alignment: centered.
- Max width: 1200px rail.
- Headline: 56-80px, weight 600, tight tracking.
- Support text: 20px, muted text, max width around 560px.
- CTA row: primary blue button plus optional secondary link/button.
- Below hero: 3-column card row or browser-frame product mockup.

No hero photography or decorative gradient is needed.

## Feature Card

- Background: white.
- Border: 1px solid `#d8d8d8`.
- Radius: 8px.
- Padding: 24px.
- Gap: 16px.
- Heading: 20-24px, weight 600.
- Body: 16px, muted text.
- Resting shadow: none.
- Hover: optional shadow.

## Product Browser Mockup

- Purpose: show the product UI through realistic browser chrome.
- Frame: white or very light neutral, 1px gray border, 8px radius.
- Shadow: allowed because this is a display object.
- Content: actual product UI screenshot or recreated editor interface.
- Use this instead of abstract hero illustration.
