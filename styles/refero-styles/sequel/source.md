## Capture Notes

This bundle paraphrases the Refero style reference into implementation notes for AI-assisted UI building. It preserves the system intent, color roles, typography behavior, spacing, component patterns, layout rules, and visual constraints needed to recreate the style without copying the page text wholesale.

## What To Preserve

- Pure black canvas with warm off-white actions.
- Cinematic portrait or film still imagery.
- No chromatic accent color.
- Soft editorial typography with a restrained serif italic accent.
- Pill geometry for buttons, badges, chips, and controls.
- 10px media/card radius and shadowless content surfaces.
