# Guidelines

## Do

- Use pure black as the default page canvas.
- Use Lamp Cream for the single most important filled action per screen.
- Use 9999px radius on buttons, badges, chips, and pill controls.
- Use 10px radius on cards and media.
- Use VisueltPro weight 300 for quiet 54px narrative headlines.
- Use VisueltPro weight 500 for stronger 57px to 128px display moments.
- Use Bradford italic for one poetic word in a headline.
- Use uppercase labels with 0.05em to 0.08em positive tracking.
- Let warm cinematic photography provide emotion and contrast.
- Use Charcoal panels to imply elevation without shadows.
- Keep vertical rhythm generous, especially between sections.

## Do Not

- Do not use any chromatic color.
- Do not add gold as a luxury shortcut.
- Do not use shadows on cards, panels, images, or content sections.
- Do not use sharp corners on buttons, badges, or chips.
- Do not use bold weights above 500.
- Do not fill sections with decorative gradients.
- Do not use Bradford for full paragraphs or navigation.
- Do not brighten the page background away from pure black.
- Do not crowd the hero with too much text.
- Do not frame photography with heavy borders.
