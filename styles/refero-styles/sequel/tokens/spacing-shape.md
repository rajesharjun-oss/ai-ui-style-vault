## Shadow

Use shadows only for cream buttons and glass badges when the interaction needs depth. Do not shadow cards, images, panels, or content sections.

```css
--shadow-lg: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.15) 0px 4px 20px 0px;
--shadow-xl: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35) 0px 10px 30px 0px, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08) 0px 1px 0px inset;
```
