# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Canvas: pure black.
- Content width: 1200px max.
- Section gap: 96px to 120px.
- Hero: cinematic media first, with navigation floating above.
- Navigation: transparent over hero, optionally black after scroll.
- Cards: sparse, flat, and tonal.
- Headline blocks: large, left-aligned, and editorial.
- Media: full-bleed inside 10px-radius containers.

## Imagery

Use warm, dim, human photography:

- Founder portraits.
- Member story portraits.
- Private dinner, cinema, boardroom, or backstage scenes.
- Documentary-style lighting.
- Warm skin tones and controlled black levels.

Avoid:

- Abstract gradients.
- Neon 3D visuals.
- Generic stock business meetings.
- Bright color overlays.
- Product screenshots that overpower the cinematic mood.

## Overlay Rules

Text can sit over media only with a subtle bottom scrim. Glass badges can sit over imagery if they feel functional, such as labels, dates, or categories. Do not scatter glass elements for decoration.
