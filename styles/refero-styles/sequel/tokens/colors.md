# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lamp Cream | `#f5f5f0` | `--color-lamp-cream` | Primary filled action, warm lamplight accent |
| Void Black | `#000000` | `--color-void-black` | Page canvas, nav background, deepest icon fill |
| Charcoal | `#202020` | `--color-charcoal` | Elevated card and panel surface |
| Graphite | `#333333` | `--color-graphite` | Hairline borders, badge outlines, subtle dividers |
| Smoke | `#999999` | `--color-smoke` | Muted text, captions, secondary labels |
| Pure White | `#ffffff` | `--color-pure-white` | Primary text, ghost button border, icon strokes |

## Usage

Lamp Cream is the only accent color and should appear mostly on filled primary actions. Do not introduce blue, purple, gold, green, red, or any other chromatic color. The visual richness should come from warm photography, white text, glass badges, and black-to-charcoal tonal layering.
