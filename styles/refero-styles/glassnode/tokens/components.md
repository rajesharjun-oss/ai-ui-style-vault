# Component Recipes

## Primary Filled Button

- Role: highest-emphasis action on light surfaces.
- Background: `#000000` or `#1a1a1a`.
- Text: `#ffffff`.
- Font: Inter, 16px, weight 500.
- Padding: 12px 24px.
- Radius: 2px.
- Shadow: subtle 2px drop.
- No border and no gradient.

## Ghost Outline Button

- Role: secondary action paired with primary.
- Background: transparent or `#ffffff`.
- Text: `#000000`.
- Border: `1px solid #000000` or `#dedfe1`.
- Font: Inter, 14-16px, weight 500.
- Padding: 10px 20px.
- Radius: 2px.
- Invert to white on dark bands.

## Dark Band CTA

- Role: action on obsidian sections.
- Background: `#ffffff`.
- Text: `#000000`.
- Font: Inter, 16px, weight 500.
- Padding: 12px 24px.
- Radius: 2px.
- No shadow.

## Hero Chart Widget

- Background: `#ffffff`.
- Border: `1px solid #dedfe1`.
- Radius: 2px.
- Shadow: layered hero widget shadow.
- Include tab header, chart body, pale lavender area fill, blue line stroke, and compact search/select input.
- Treat as credibility artifact, not decoration.

## Partner Report Card

- Background: `#ffffff`.
- Border: `1px solid #dedfe1`.
- Radius: 2px.
- Shadow: none.
- Cover image fills top.
- Content padding: 16px.
- Label: Inter 12px uppercase, `#6f6f6f`.
- Title: Inter 20px, weight 700, `#000000`.
- Description: Inter 14px, `#5a5a5a`, clamp to 3 lines.

## Dark Feature Block

- Background: `#1a1a1a`.
- Layout: two-column grid.
- Heading: Inter 24px, weight 700, `#ffffff`.
- Description: Inter 16px, `#a0a0a0`.
- Visual: abstract chart illustration or line graph.
- Icons: white or neutral strokes only.

## Top Navigation

- Background: `#ffffff`.
- Bottom border: `1px solid #dedfe1`.
- Height: about 64px.
- Logo: Inter 20px, weight 700.
- Nav links: Inter 14px, weight 500, `#333333`.
- Right actions: one ghost outline and one filled dark button.

## Subscribe Form

- Band: `#1a1a1a`.
- Input: `#ffffff`, `1px solid #dedfe1`, 2px radius.
- Placeholder: `#808080`.
- Submit: white fill, black text, 2px radius.

## Badge / Tag

- Inter 12px, weight 500, uppercase.
- Text: `#6f6f6f`.
- No background, no border, no chip shape.
