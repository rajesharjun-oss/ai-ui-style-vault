# Guidelines

## Do

- Use neutral black and white action styles.
- Keep all radii at 2px.
- Use `#dedfe1` borders to separate surfaces.
- Alternate light and dark bands.
- Use Inter weight 500 for controls and weight 700 for headings.
- Reserve Fraktion for the 56px hero H1.
- Keep the canvas `#edeff2` and elevated cards `#ffffff`.
- Use `#e2e7fc` only as a soft wash or chart fill.
- Make charts and data widgets the primary imagery.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce saturated brand colors.
- Do not add colorful gradients or icon fills.
- Do not use radius above 2px.
- Do not use stacked colored shadows or glow effects.
- Do not use Fraktion for small UI.
- Do not put white cards on white backgrounds without borders.
- Do not break the 8px spacing scale.
- Do not use emoji, photography, or playful illustration in product UI.

## Implementation Heuristic

If the screen feels too plain, add better data structure, stronger table/chart hierarchy, or clearer band rhythm. Do not add decorative color.
