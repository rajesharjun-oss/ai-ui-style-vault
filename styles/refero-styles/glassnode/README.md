## Style Summary

Glassnode is an institutional research-console style: cool gray canvas, monochrome UI, Inter everywhere, one custom hero display face, 2px geometry, hairline borders, and chart-native imagery. It feels like a Bloomberg terminal adapted into a product launch page, with light data bands alternating against near-black editorial sections.

Use this style when an AI needs a financial research site, crypto analytics landing page, market intelligence dashboard, report library, or data-heavy institutional product page.

## What To Borrow

- Cool gray canvas `#edeff2` with white card surfaces.
- Near-black dark bands `#1a1a1a` for statement sections and footer.
- Pure black or obsidian buttons, not colorful CTAs.
- Pale lavender `#e2e7fc` only as soft accent wash and chart fill.
- Inter for all UI, body copy, cards, nav, and labels.
- Fraktion or a geometric bold display face only for the 56px hero H1.
- 2px radius across nav, tags, cards, inputs, and buttons.
- 1px `#dedfe1` borders to define surfaces.
- Chart widgets, line graphs, report cards, and monochrome logo strips.
- Shadow only for primary buttons and the hero chart widget.

## Avoid

- Introducing saturated brand colors or colorful CTAs.
- Using rounded cards, pills, or large radii.
- Adding glow effects or decorative gradients.
- Using the display face outside the hero headline.
- Placing white cards on white backgrounds without a hairline border.
- Breaking the 8px spacing scale.
- Using emoji, colored illustrations, or photography as product UI texture.
