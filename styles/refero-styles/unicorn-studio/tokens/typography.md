# Typography

## Primary Display

- Family: Overused Grotesk
- Substitute: Inter Tight, General Sans, or Sohne Breit
- Weights: 400 and 500
- Sizes: 26px, 32px, 48px, 64px, 215px
- Line-height: 0.99 to 1
- Tracking: -0.030em at 26px, -0.040em at 32-48px, -0.063em at 64px and above
- Use for main headings and display moments.

## Secondary Display

- Family: Sprat
- Substitute: Migra, Editorial New, or Tobias
- Weight: 500
- Sizes: 46px and 61px
- Line-height: 1
- Tracking: -0.042em
- Use sparingly for editorial variety, not as a workhorse.

## UI And Body

- Family: system stack
- Substitute: Inter, -apple-system, system-ui
- Weights: 400, 500, 600
- Sizes: 13px, 14px, 16px, 18px
- Line-height: 1.2 to 1.4
- Tracking: -0.010em
- Use for body, navigation, buttons, forms, cards, and normal UI.

## Type Scale

| Token | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `caption` | 13px | 400-500 | 1.4 | -0.13px | captions and badges |
| `body` | 16px | 400 | 1.4 | -0.16px | body copy |
| `subheading` | 18px | 400 | 1.4 | -0.18px | subheads |
| `heading-sm` | 26px | 500 | 1 | -0.78px | small display heading |
| `heading` | 32px | 500 | 1 | -1.28px | section heading |
| `heading-lg` | 48px | 500 | 1 | -1.92px | large heading |
| `display` | 64px | 500 | 0.99 | -4.03px | hero/display heading |

## Rules

- Use size and tracking for emphasis, not heavy weight.
- Do not use font weight above 600.
- Keep body line-height at or below 1.4.
- Keep display line-height around 1.
