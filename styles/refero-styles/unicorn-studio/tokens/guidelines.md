# Guidelines

## Do

- Use the neutral surface stack for all depth.
- Set headline tracking to -0.063em at 64px and above.
- Set headline tracking to -0.040em at 32-48px.
- Use 10px radius only for cards.
- Use 3px radius for buttons, tags, and inputs.
- Reserve Lavender Beam for underlines, focus states, and the aurora.
- Keep body text in the system stack at 16px, line-height 1.4, and tight tracking.
- Apply the Iron key-line shadow to primary filled buttons.
- Use cream-filled primary actions.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce filled colored buttons.
- Do not use radii larger than 10px.
- Do not use font weight above 600.
- Do not add chromatic colors beyond the violet family.
- Do not use body line-height above 1.4.
- Do not use display line-height below 1.
- Do not apply drop shadows to static elements.
- Do not use color alone for hover, active, or disabled states.
