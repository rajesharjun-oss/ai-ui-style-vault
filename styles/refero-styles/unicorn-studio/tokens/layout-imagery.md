# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a centered max-width around 1200px.
- Keep the dark canvas full-bleed.
- Hero should feel 80vh or taller.
- Center the hero stack horizontally and vertically.
- Use a logo strip between hero and first content section.
- Use 96px section gaps.
- Content bands are max-width contained.
- Feature blocks can be centered stacks or text-plus-visual grids.
- Avoid sidebar or dashboard-like asymmetry on marketing pages.

## Imagery

- Dominant image is the WebGL aurora, not photography.
- Use a radial violet-to-iris gradient from the lower center.
- Use monochrome logo strips.
- Use product screenshots only if they remain secondary to the hero atmosphere.
- Avoid decorative photography, illustrations, and extra graphics.

## Surfaces

| Level | Name | Value | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 0 | Canvas | `#08080a` | Page background and deepest void |
| 1 | Panel | `#0d0d12` | Section containers and modal surfaces |
| 2 | Card | `#17171c` | Nested cards and feature blocks |
| 3 | Elevated | `#25252d` | Hover states, selected items, popovers |

## Motion

- Motion may come from WebGL aurora or subtle shader movement.
- Keep UI transitions restrained.
- Avoid loud page animations, bouncy movement, and decorative color cycling.
