# Components

## Filled Black Button

Obsidian fill, Pure White text, 6px radius, 12px to 20px horizontal padding, Geist Sans 14px/400. Strongest action.

## Ghost Outline Button

Transparent fill, Charcoal text, Hairline ring, 6px radius, 20px padding, Geist Sans 14px/400. Secondary action.

## Pill Button

Obsidian or Pure White fill with contrasting text, 9999px radius, compact 12px horizontal padding. Use only in tight nav/header contexts.

## Text Link Button

No background, no border, 0px radius, Obsidian or Charcoal text, Geist Sans 14px to 16px. Use for nav items and inline references.

## Bordered Card

Pure White background, 6px radius, 16px padding, and stacked hairline rings: `0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,0.08), 0 0 0 2px #fafafa`.

## Inverted Card

Obsidian fill, Pure White text, 6px radius. Use sparingly as visual punctuation.

## CLI Output Panel

Pure White panel with Geist Mono 12px to 13px text. Use triangle command prefixes and Terminal Green checkmark confirmations.

## Logo Strip Row

Horizontal grayscale customer logos with 24px to 32px gaps. Use Slate or Ash for muted brand treatment.

## Eyebrow Label

Geist Mono 11px/400, uppercase, 0.071em tracking, Obsidian, with 12px spacing before the heading.

## Top Nav Bar

64px sticky Paper White bar, optional backdrop blur, wordmark left, center-left nav links, right action cluster, no bottom border.

## Hero Composition

Asymmetric three-zone hero: tight headline left, black triangle mark center/offset, eyebrow stack right, no background image.

## Feature Card Grid

Two-up or three-up card grid with bordered cards, 16px to 24px padding, 30px headings, Charcoal descriptions, and embedded product proof.
