## Elevation

| Name | Value | Token |
|---|---|---|
| Subtle ring | `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08) 0px 0px 0px 1px, rgb(250, 250, 250) 0px 0px 0px 2px` | `--shadow-subtle` |
| Hairline ring | `rgb(235, 235, 235) 0px 0px 0px 1px` | `--shadow-subtle-2` |

These are border rings, not shadows. Do not use blur, offset, or soft drop-shadow depth.
