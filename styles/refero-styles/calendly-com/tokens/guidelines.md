# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Ink Navy for regular text, headings, icons, and links.
- Use Signal Blue for filled primary CTAs and selected states.
- Use Ink Navy for secondary dark buttons.
- Use 16px radius for product cards.
- Use 24px radius for larger feature panels.
- Use blue-tinted shadows for elevated surfaces.
- Use 50px to 80px bold headings for major sections.
- Place product visuals over Coral Magenta or Sky Cyan blobs.
- Keep button radius at 8px.

## Do Not

- Do not use pure black for normal text.
- Do not use neutral black shadows.
- Do not use Coral Magenta or Sky Cyan as UI fills.
- Do not make buttons pill-shaped.
- Do not make headings timid or undersized.
- Do not use Deep Cobalt as the primary action fill.
- Do not add page background gradients.
- Do not rely on photography or lifestyle imagery.
