# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a centered 1200px max-width layout.
- Keep section gaps around 48px to 64px.
- Build the hero as a two-column split.
- Put text, body copy, and sign-in buttons on the left.
- Put a booking widget or product card on the right.
- Use a trust logo strip under the hero.
- Use centered section headers before feature blocks.
- Prefer two-column feature sections over dense grids.
- Use a 64px sticky top nav with logo, menu, and CTA cluster.

## Imagery

Use product UI screenshots and scheduling widgets as the primary visuals. Keep them on white rounded cards with blue-tinted elevation.

Decorative blobs:

- Use Coral Magenta or Sky Cyan.
- Offset behind the product card by 20px to 40px.
- Keep them atmospheric and blurred.
- Never use them as UI component fills.

Avoid:

- Photography.
- Lifestyle imagery.
- Heavy illustrations.
- Decorative gradients across the whole page.
