# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Electric Violet for action, active state, and focus.
- Keep violet sparse and intentional.
- Use Britti Sans only for display headings.
- Use Inter for all functional UI.
- Use JetBrains Mono only for code and schema fragments.
- Frame product screenshots in white cards with light borders.
- Use Mist Violet and Cloud for soft section structure.
- Keep buttons 8px radius and cards 12px radius.

## Do Not

- Do not use violet for body copy.
- Do not turn the page into a purple gradient system.
- Do not use Britti Sans for paragraphs, nav, or forms.
- Do not default the whole site to dark mode.
- Do not use lifestyle photos as the main imagery.
- Do not use mascots or cartoon illustration as the primary identity.
- Do not over-round cards or make everything pill-shaped.

## Accessibility Notes

- Electric Violet should have visible hover and focus states.
- Do not rely only on violet to communicate active state.
- Product screenshot text may be small; provide real text summaries nearby.
- Code blocks need enough contrast for syntax colors.
