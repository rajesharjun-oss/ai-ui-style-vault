# Implementation Prompt

Build a dark engineering SaaS interface in the Sauce Labs style.

Use Obsidian Shell `#132322` as the primary page canvas and navigation background. Use Deep Abyss `#0e1a19` for nested dark cards and logo tiles. Use Mint Frost `#edf7f5` for large light content cards that float on the dark page. The page should alternate between dark structural bands and light rounded cards rather than relying on dividers or heavy shadows.

Use Neon Pulse `#3ddc91` only for primary CTA fills, active tab states, announcement bars, stat highlights, and selected navigation states. Do not use green as decoration. Mint Whisper `#97ddbc` and Signal Yellow `#ffcd48` may appear inside isometric product illustrations or product mockups, but should not become global action colors.

Use Aeonik for body, buttons, nav items, card content, and form fields at weight 400. Use AeonikFono for headings, display, stat numbers, and uppercase eyebrow labels. Use 9px tracked uppercase eyebrows above section titles. Keep display weights at 400-500 only.

Make every button and tab a 56px pill. Use 60px radius for large content cards, 20px for smaller nested cards, and 10px for small controls. Use 24px card padding, 24px element gaps, 80px section gaps, and max-width around 1200px.

Use isometric product illustrations, 3D phone or dashboard renderings, dark logo tiles, tabbed feature cards, and realistic product screenshot panels. Avoid gradients, heavy shadows, square buttons, extra chromatic colors, and placing light cards next to light cards without a dark band between them.
