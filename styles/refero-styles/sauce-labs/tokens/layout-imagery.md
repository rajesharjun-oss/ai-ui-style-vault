# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Full-bleed dark canvas with max-width 1200px content.
- Hero is a split composition: text and CTAs left, isometric product illustration right.
- Dark navigation bar sits on the dark canvas.
- Feature rhythm alternates dark structural bands and large Mint Frost cards.
- Logo bars use dark rounded tiles in a single row.
- Stats use a horizontal row of green numbers on dark.
- Feature sections use centered heading stacks and tab-pill navigation.

## Imagery

- Use isometric product illustrations.
- Use 3D phone or dashboard renderings with stacked module cards.
- Use flat geometric cards in Neon Pulse, Mint Whisper, Signal Yellow, and light gray.
- Use realistic product screenshots inside dashboard panels.
- Use white monochrome client logos inside dark rounded tiles.
- Do not use photography.

## Surfaces

| Level | Name | Value | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Obsidian Canvas | `#132322` | Primary dark structural plane |
| 2 | Deep Card | `#0e1a19` | Nested dark cards, logo tiles, stat containers |
| 3 | Mint Card | `#edf7f5` | Light content cards floating on dark canvas |
| 4 | White Panel | `#ffffff` | Product screenshots and dashboard previews |

## Motion

- Use restrained hover brightening for Neon Pulse CTAs.
- Keep card and tab transitions simple.
- Avoid animated gradients, parallax, or decorative motion.
