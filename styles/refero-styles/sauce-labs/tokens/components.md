# Components

## Primary CTA Button

- Background: `#3ddc91`
- Text: `#132322`
- Radius: 56px
- Padding: 14px 28px
- Font: Aeonik, 16px, weight 400
- Tracking: -0.08px
- Border: none
- Hover: slight brightness lift.

## Ghost CTA Button

- Background: transparent
- Border: 1.5px solid `#ffffff`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 56px
- Padding: 14px 28px
- Font: Aeonik, 16px, weight 400
- Pair beside the green primary CTA.

## Dark Text Button

- Background: none
- Border: none
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Font: Aeonik, 15px, weight 400
- Use for tertiary nav actions.

## Announcement Banner

- Full-width bar.
- Background: `#3ddc91`
- Text: `#132322`
- Font: Aeonik, 14px, weight 400
- Include a small close affordance on the right.

## Navigation Bar

- Full-width dark bar.
- Background: `#132322`
- Logo left.
- Nav links centered in white at 15px.
- CTA cluster right.
- No visible border or shadow.

## Stat Block

- Number: Neon Pulse, AeonikFono 48px, weight 500.
- Label: white at reduced opacity, AeonikFono 9px, uppercase, 0.45px tracking.
- Layout: horizontal row on dark background.
- No container required.

## Logo Bar Tile

- Background: `#0e1a19`
- Radius: 60px
- Contains centered white monochrome logo.
- Use in an evenly spaced horizontal row.

## Light Content Card

- Background: `#edf7f5`
- Radius: 60px
- Padding: 48px internal
- Shadow: subtle md shadow
- Contains headline, description, tabs, product screenshots, or dashboards.

## Tab Pill Navigation

- Active tab: `#3ddc91` background, `#132322` text.
- Inactive tabs: transparent, Slate Text.
- Radius: 56px
- Padding: 12px 24px
- Font: Aeonik 16px, weight 400.
- Optional chevrons for overflow.

## Eyebrow Label

- Font: AeonikFono, 9px, weight 400
- Transform: uppercase
- Tracking: 0.45px
- Color: `#132322` on light cards or `#3ddc91` on dark sections.
- Place above section headlines as a structural anchor.

## Hero Headline

- Font: AeonikFono
- Size: 64px
- Weight: 500
- Line-height: 1.10
- Color: `#ffffff`
- Usually left-aligned in a split hero.

## Isometric Product Illustration

- 3D phone or dashboard rendering.
- Use stacked cards in Neon Pulse, Mint Whisper, Signal Yellow, and light gray.
- Add connector lines and small labels for test, build, deploy, or operate concepts.
- Place on the right side of the hero.

## Product Screenshot Panel

- Use inside Mint Frost content cards.
- Screen area: `#ffffff`.
- Chrome/frame: `#0e1a19`.
- Show realistic dashboards, widgets, and data views.
