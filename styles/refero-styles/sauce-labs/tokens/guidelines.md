# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Neon Pulse only for primary actions, active states, and announcement bars.
- Set all buttons and tabs to 56px radius.
- Use 60px radius for large content cards and 20px for nested cards.
- Place uppercase AeonikFono 9px eyebrow labels above section headlines.
- Alternate between dark Obsidian Shell bands and Mint Frost cards.
- Set Aeonik body text to weight 400 with tight tracking at 16px.
- Use Deep Abyss for nested dark surfaces inside the obsidian shell.
- Let luminance contrast create section rhythm.

## Do Not

- Do not use green for decorative non-interactive elements.
- Do not use square or slightly rounded buttons.
- Do not place light cards directly beside light cards without a dark band.
- Do not use heavy box shadows.
- Do not use gradients.
- Do not use body weights above 400 or display weights above 500.
- Do not introduce new chromatic accents beyond the defined palette.
- Do not rely on photography.
