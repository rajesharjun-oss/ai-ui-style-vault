# Sauce Labs - Style Reference

Sauce Labs feels like a neon-lit command center for engineering teams. The interface is dark, polished, and technical, with one bright green signal marking anything live, active, or conversion-focused.

## Visual Thesis

- Use an obsidian green-black canvas as the main stage.
- Alternate dark structural sections with large Mint Frost cards.
- Use Neon Pulse only where action or live signal matters.
- Make buttons and tabs fully pill-shaped.
- Use large rounded content cards as bright counterpoints to the dark page.
- Let isometric product illustrations and product screenshots carry visual complexity.
- Use luminance contrast, not decoration, to organize sections.

## Core Ingredients

- Canvas: Obsidian Shell `#132322`.
- Nested dark surface: Deep Abyss `#0e1a19`.
- Light content card: Mint Frost `#edf7f5`.
- Action accent: Neon Pulse `#3ddc91`.
- Supporting accent wash: Mint Whisper `#97ddbc`.
- Warm illustration accent: Signal Yellow `#ffcd48`.
- Shape: 56px buttons/tabs, 60px large cards, 20px small cards.
- Type: Aeonik body and AeonikFono headings/eyebrows.

## Page Composition

Use a full-width dark navigation bar, then a split hero: left side for headline, copy, and CTAs; right side for an isometric product illustration. Follow with stats, logo bars, and feature sections where large light cards float on the dark canvas. Feature cards contain tabs, product preview panels, and realistic screenshots.

## Color Behavior

Green is a signal. Neon Pulse should be limited to primary actions, active tab states, stat numbers, announcement bars, and focused conversion moments. Mint Whisper and Signal Yellow may appear inside product illustrations, but they should not become global UI accents.

## Type Behavior

Aeonik is the workhorse for body, nav, form fields, and button labels. AeonikFono handles display, headings, and tiny uppercase structural labels. Keep weights light: 400 for most text and 500 for large display. The system avoids bold shouting.
