## Notes

This bundle summarizes the Refero style page into implementation-ready guidance. It is not a verbatim archive. The style combines an editorial broadsheet headline system with developer-tool UI details: black and white structure, soft pastel technical surfaces, compact mono metadata, and one sharp orange accent.

## What To Preserve

- Black pill buttons for primary actions.
- Signal orange as headline punctuation and footer color, not a button fill.
- Large GT Planar-style display headlines with tight tracking.
- Mono 14px uppercase labels for metadata.
- Lavender, blue-gray, sky, cream, and off-white panels for grouping.
- Flat cards with small radii and 1px borders instead of shadows.
- Pixel/dot/terminal imagery instead of photography.
