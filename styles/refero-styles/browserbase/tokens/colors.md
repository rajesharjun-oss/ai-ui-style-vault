# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink Black | `#000000` | `--color-ink-black` | Primary text, button fill, borders, dividers |
| Paper White | `#ffffff` | `--color-paper-white` | Canvas, card surfaces, text on dark fills |
| Graphite | `#686562` | `--color-graphite` | Muted body copy and secondary borders |
| Signal Orange | `#ff4500` | `--color-signal-orange` | Headline highlight, focused edges, footer |
| Blue Gray Mist | `#c5d3e8` | `--color-blue-gray-mist` | Hero tint, code-card surfaces, soft borders |
| Sky Tint | `#c4edff` | `--color-sky-tint` | Terminal blocks, inline washes, light badge fills |
| Lavender Mist | `#e2e9f3` | `--color-lavender-mist` | Feature cards and section tints |
| Faint Slate | `#f8fafc` | `--color-faint-slate` | Logo strip cards and quiet panels |
| Cream Wash | `#fffde6` | `--color-cream-wash` | Warm callouts, badges, secondary cards |

## Color Behavior

Signal Orange is not a normal CTA color. Use it for headline highlight boxes, small emphasis, and the footer band. Primary buttons stay Ink Black.

## Pairings

- Body text: Ink Black or Graphite on Paper White.
- Primary button: Paper White on Ink Black.
- Feature cards: Ink Black and Graphite on Lavender Mist.
- Code cards: Ink Black on Blue Gray Mist or Sky Tint.
- Footer: Ink Black on Signal Orange.

## Avoid

- Browser default link blue.
- Orange hover floods.
- Multiple accent colors.
- Chroma-heavy body text.
