# Implementation Prompt

Build a polished interface in the BMW.com style: a premium automotive gallery page where the product imagery is the visual system and the UI is a restrained typographic frame.

Use a light theme with a pure white canvas, Carbon text (`#262626`), optional Fog section bands (`#f1f1f1`), and a full-width Carbon footer. Do not introduce brand accent colors, gradients, color-coded states, shadows, card chrome, or rounded corners.

Typography should use `BMWTypeNextLatin` if available, otherwise `Inter` or a similar geometric sans. Use one large centered display heading at 60px, weight 300, line-height 1.3. Keep navigation, footer links, and small actions at 16px regular weight. Let text feel plain and precise.

Layout should be single-column. Use a flat white top nav with brand mark/tagline on the left and text links plus a search icon on the right. Use full-bleed product images with no overlays, no borders, and no radius. Under major imagery, place a centered light display heading and a text-only inline action. Finish with a dark footer band containing plain link columns and a language row.

Images should be dramatic product closeups with dark studio lighting, sculpted metal, glossy details, and controlled contrast. They should occupy a large share of the page and carry the only meaningful color.

Before finishing, check that:

- All corners are 0px.
- There are no shadows.
- No decorative accent color has been invented.
- Hero imagery has no text or controls over it.
- The largest heading is light, not bold.
- Navigation, actions, and footer items are mostly plain text.
