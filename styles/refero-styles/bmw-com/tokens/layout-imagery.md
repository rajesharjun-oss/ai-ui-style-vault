# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

Use a single-column editorial flow with a 1200px center rail for text and footer content. Allow hero and product images to break out to the full viewport width.

Suggested order:

1. Flat white navigation.
2. Full-bleed product image.
3. Centered light display heading.
4. Centered inline text action.
5. Optional Fog band.
6. Full-width dark footer.

## Composition

The page should feel more like a gallery wall than a software interface. Keep large areas of empty white space around typography. Let image sections create the main rhythm.

## Imagery

Use product photography that feels sculpted, controlled, and technical:

- closeups of wheels, glass, badges, grills, and panels
- dark studio backgrounds
- blue-black metallic highlights
- sharp reflections and precise lighting
- isolated objects instead of lifestyle scenes

## Avoid

- Soft generic stock photos.
- Rounded image cards.
- Text overlays.
- Decorative image frames.
- Colorful background art.
- Busy multi-column galleries unless the product page specifically needs them.
