# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon | `#262626` | `--color-carbon` | Primary text, heading text, footer background |
| Pure White | `#ffffff` | `--color-pure-white` | Page canvas, header background, footer text |
| Fog | `#f1f1f1` | `--color-fog` | Subtle alternate surface |
| Concrete | `#bbbbbb` | `--color-concrete` | Muted text and quiet divider color |

## Color Behavior

This palette is intentionally small. Do not add a CTA blue, success green, warning orange, or decorative gradient unless a product requirement absolutely demands it. Most interaction should be communicated through text treatment, spacing, underlines, and focus outlines rather than hue.

## Pairings

- Primary text: Carbon on Pure White.
- Footer text: Pure White on Carbon.
- Subtle bands: Carbon on Fog.
- Muted details: Concrete on light surfaces or in the footer.

## Photography

Photography supplies the missing color. Use dark, metallic, high-contrast product images with blue-black highlights, chrome reflections, and sculptural closeups.
