# Guidelines

## Do

- Use a pure white page canvas.
- Use Carbon for almost all text.
- Use one large light display heading as the typographic signature.
- Let full-bleed product photography provide color and drama.
- Keep components flat, square, borderless, and shadowless.
- Use simple text links for navigation and actions.
- Reserve the dark footer for the strongest surface contrast.

## Don't

- Do not add gradients, colorful CTA fills, or decorative accent colors.
- Do not round corners.
- Do not add card shadows or elevated panels.
- Do not bold the display heading.
- Do not overlay text, buttons, chips, or badges on top of hero photography.
- Do not create dense app layouts from this style.
- Do not use the light display font for body copy.

## Accessibility Notes

- Carbon on white has strong contrast for body text.
- Keep focus indicators visible even if the style is minimal.
- If color states are avoided, use underline, outline, text weight, or clear labels for interaction feedback.
- Ensure full-bleed images include useful alt text or are marked decorative when appropriate.
