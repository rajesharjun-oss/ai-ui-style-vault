## Notes

This bundle summarizes the Refero style page into implementation-ready guidance. It is not a verbatim archive. The style is centered on a luxury automotive showcase: monochrome UI, sharp geometry, soft display weight, and full-width product photography as the only expressive color source.

## What To Preserve

- Pure white canvas and near-black structural text.
- Borderless, shadowless components.
- Zero-radius geometry.
- One large 300-weight display heading per page.
- Full-bleed, tightly cropped product photography.
- Text-only navigation, links, and footer items.
- A single dark footer band as the main contrast surface.
