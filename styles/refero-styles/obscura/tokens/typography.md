# Typography

## Families

- Display: `Jersey 10`
- Body and UI: `Manrope Variable`
- Display substitutes: `Press Start 2P`, `VT323`, `Pixelify Sans`
- Body substitutes: `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `DM Sans`

Jersey 10 is the brand voice. Manrope is the product interface.

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Line Height | Tracking | Family | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Caption | 12px | 1.5 | 0 | Manrope | Captions and fine UI |
| Body small | 14px | 1.43 | 0 | Manrope | Nav, utility text |
| Body | 16px | 1.5 | 0 | Manrope | Main readable copy |
| Subheading | 18px | 1.56 | 0 | Manrope | Short section intro |
| Heading small | 36px | 1.11 | -0.9px | Jersey 10 | Feature card title |
| Heading | 48px | 0.85 | -1.2px | Jersey 10 | Section headline |
| Heading large | 60px | 0.85 | -1.5px | Jersey 10 | Large section headline |
| Display | 96px | 0.8 | -3px | Jersey 10 | Hero headline |

## Rules

- Use Jersey 10 only at 36px and above.
- Use Manrope for all body text, button labels, nav links, and captions.
- Use tight line-height for multiline pixel headlines.
- Use 1.11 line-height when a single-line Jersey heading needs more air.
- Use Manrope 500 for buttons and nav.
- Use Manrope 400 for paragraphs.
- Use Manrope 600-700 for emphasis.

## Responsive Notes

On mobile, reduce the hero display to 56-64px and keep line-height tight. Do not squeeze Jersey into tiny labels; switch to Manrope for anything compact.
