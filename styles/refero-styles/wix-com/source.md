## Notes

This bundle summarizes the Refero style page into implementation-ready guidance. It is not a verbatim archive. The style is a bright website-builder product system: oversized Madefor Display typography, airy white space, flat rounded components, product mockups, template cards, and a single electric-blue action color.

## What To Preserve

- Pure white canvas and black text as the dominant pair.
- Madefor Display for large headlines, tightly tracked as size increases.
- Madefor Text for body, nav, inputs, and descriptions.
- Electric Blue only for primary action buttons and selected action indicators.
- Black filled buttons as the alternate action fill.
- 50px radius for interactive buttons and nav pills.
- 20px cards, 8.9px images and product mockups.
- 80px section rhythm.
- Lavender Mist gradients and Lime Highlight washes as section punctuation.
