# Implementation Prompt

Build a polished wix.com-style interface: a bright, spacious, creator-friendly product page with oversized Madefor Display typography, rounded flat components, product mockups, template previews, and one electric-blue primary action color.

Use Pure Canvas (`#ffffff`) as the main page background, Carbon Ink (`#000000`) for text, icons, borders, and black-filled secondary buttons, Graphite (`#1c1d21`) for softened display headings, Ash Border (`#d0d0d0`) and Fog Border (`#e8e6e6`) for hairlines, and Slate Mist (`#f1f5f9`) for inputs and subtle panels. Electric Blue (`#166aea`) is reserved for primary CTA fills and selected indicators. Do not use it broadly for decorative text or icons. Deep Violet, Indigo Pulse, Lime Highlight, Lemon Note, and Lavender Mist are section or accent colors only.

Typography should use Madefor Display for all large headings from 31px upward and Madefor Text for body, nav, inputs, and descriptions. Use tight negative tracking on display text: around -0.02em at 31px to 48px, and -0.03em at 82px and above. Hero headings should be 82px to 104px, with line-height near 0.95 to 1.0. Body copy should be 16px with airy 1.5 line-height.

Layout should use a 1200px centered content rail with full-bleed color-wash sections. Use 80px section gaps, 24px card padding, and around 12px element gaps. Alternate white canvas sections with Lavender Mist to white gradients, and use Lime Highlight or Lemon Note only as small callout surfaces.

Components should be flat and rounded: 50px pill buttons for primary, secondary, ghost, and nav actions; 20px cards; 8.9px images and product mockups; 8px to 12px inputs. Avoid visible shadows. Use product UI screenshots, browser-framed Wix editor mockups, AI prompt panels, and template gallery cards as the visual system.

Before finishing, check that:

- Electric Blue appears only on primary actions or selected action indicators.
- Black filled buttons are the only alternate filled CTA style.
- Deep Violet and Indigo Pulse are not used for buttons.
- Cards and buttons do not have visible drop shadows.
- Headline line-height is tight at large sizes.
- Images and product mockups use 8.9px radius.
- Section rhythm has generous breathing room.
