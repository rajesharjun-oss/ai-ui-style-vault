# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Madefor Display at 48px to 104px for headlines.
- Use Electric Blue only for primary action buttons and selected action indicators.
- Use black filled pills as the alternate action system.
- Use 50px radius for buttons and nav pills.
- Keep section gaps around 80px.
- Use Lavender Mist to white gradients for full-bleed section breaks.
- Use 8.9px radius on images and product mockups.
- Use product mockups and template previews as primary visuals.

## Don't

- Do not use Deep Violet or Indigo Pulse for action buttons.
- Do not add visible drop shadows to cards or buttons.
- Do not use radius below 8px on cards or below 20px on buttons.
- Do not set headline line-height above 1.2 at 48px and above.
- Do not apply Lime Highlight or Lemon Note to long text blocks.
- Do not introduce extra accent colors.
- Do not center long body paragraphs.
- Do not make cards feel heavy or dashboard-like.

## Accessibility Notes

- Check white text contrast on Electric Blue.
- Keep focus states visible on blue, black, and ghost buttons.
- Do not rely on color alone for selected states.
- Provide clear alt text for template and product screenshots when meaningful.
