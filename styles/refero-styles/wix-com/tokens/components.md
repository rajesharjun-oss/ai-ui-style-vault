# Components

## Primary Action Button

- Electric Blue fill.
- Pure Canvas text.
- 50px radius.
- Madefor Text 16px.
- Compact horizontal padding.
- Use one per section maximum.

## Secondary Action Button

- Carbon Ink fill.
- Pure Canvas text.
- 50px radius.
- Madefor Text 16px.
- Use as the alternate filled action.

## Ghost Button

- Transparent background.
- 1px contextual border.
- Madefor Text 15px to 16px.
- 50px radius.
- Use for tertiary actions.

## Rounded Button

- 20px radius.
- Same blue, black, or ghost color system.
- Good for cards and tight layouts.

## Template Preview Card

- 20px radius.
- 8.9px inner image radius.
- Minimal or no shadow.
- No heavy border.
- Image dominates; label below with arrow.
- Works in 4-column desktop grids.

## Product Mockup Card

- 8.9px radius.
- Subtle border or no border.
- Browser chrome, editor panels, URL bars, or AI prompt UI.
- Sits directly on white or gradient canvas.

## Navigation Bar

- Transparent or white surface.
- Logo left.
- Product, Solutions, Resources, Pricing links.
- Login ghost link.
- Electric Blue Get Started pill.
- Simple dropdown indicators.

## Input Field

- Pure Canvas or Slate Mist background.
- 1px border.
- Focus border Input Blue.
- Madefor Text 15px to 16px.
- 8px to 12px radius.
- Light gray placeholder.

## Hero Headline Block

- Madefor Display 82px to 104px.
- Line-height 0.95 to 1.0.
- Tight negative tracking.
- Subtitle in Madefor Text 18px to 21px.
- CTA 24px to 32px below subtitle.

## Section Heading

- Madefor Display 48px.
- Line-height 1.1.
- -0.96px tracking.
- Supporting copy to the right or below.

## Gradient Section Background

- Full-bleed Lavender Mist to white gradient.
- Use to break up white sections.
- Keep soft and atmospheric.

## Badge Or Tag

- 20px radius.
- Madefor Text 13px to 14px.
- Usually ghost or lightly filled.
- Use for categories, metadata, and template labels.

## Side Panel

- Pure Canvas surface.
- 20px radius.
- Subtle border or soft shadow.
- Contextual AI assistant or editor tool panel.
- Fixed or collapsible on wide screens.
