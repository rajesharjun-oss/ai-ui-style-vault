# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

Use a centered 1200px content rail with full-bleed color-wash sections. The page should feel airy, not dense.

Suggested order:

1. Horizontal navigation.
2. Large hero headline with subtitle and CTA.
3. Product mockup or editor screenshot.
4. Lavender gradient section.
5. Two-column feature section.
6. Template gallery grid.
7. Lime or lemon callout wash.
8. Footer.

## Composition

Use generous white space and large type. Product mockups often sit centered below their headlines and can overlap into the next gradient section for flow.

## Imagery

Use:

- Wix editor screenshots
- browser-framed product mockups
- AI prompt interfaces
- template preview cards
- template photography for commerce, portfolio, interior, fashion, and architecture examples
- soft lavender and periwinkle gradient backgrounds

## Avoid

- Heavy illustration systems.
- Dense dashboards.
- Harsh geometric layouts.
- Visible card shadows.
- Overly colorful UI controls.
- Stock-looking hero photography unrelated to templates.
