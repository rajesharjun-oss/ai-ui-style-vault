# Guidelines

## Do

- Use weight 400 for headlines 32px and above.
- Set primary actions with Bone fill and Ink text.
- Apply 0.5px borders for panel outlines and dividers.
- Enable Inter `ss01` and `ss03`.
- Stack surfaces from Void to Graphite to subtle white-alpha hovers.
- Use tight body tracking.
- Reserve Signal Blue for active states, links, and icon accents.

## Do Not

- Do not use headline weights of 600 or above.
- Do not use blue as a CTA fill.
- Do not use 1px or thicker borders for the default hairline language.
- Do not add heavy drop shadows.
- Do not introduce big gradients.
- Do not make the page airy, decorative, or marketing-heavy.
- Do not use chromatic colors as large surface fills.

## AI Builder Checks

- Does it feel like dark operations software rather than a neon dashboard?
- Are CTAs neutral off-white instead of blue?
- Are borders thin enough to feel like frost/glass hairlines?
- Are headings light and quiet?
- Are blue accents limited to link and active-state roles?
