## What To Preserve

Caldera is not a generic crypto gradient system. It is chunky, warm, and technical. The signature comes from large compressed headlines, lava-black type, rounded 40px panels, saturated ember controls, and a violet halftone graphic language.

The page relies on:

- Pumice paper canvas instead of white.
- Huge PP Neue Montreal-style display type.
- DM Sans for all UI and body text.
- Ember Orange as the main product/action color.
- Violet and sulfur only as controlled supporting accents.
- Oversized rounded cards and 800px pill geometry.
- Thin black line art and small process/network diagrams.
