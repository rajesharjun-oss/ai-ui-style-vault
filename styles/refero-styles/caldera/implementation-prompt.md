# AI Implementation Prompt

Build a polished UI in the Caldera style.

Use a warm volcanic infrastructure design system: `#e3ded8` Pumice page canvas, `#000000` Lava Black text, `#ff6828` Ember Orange primary actions, `#6c35ff` Violet Rift graphics, and small `#e8ff50` Sulfur tags. The product should feel like blockchain infrastructure, rollup tooling, or a developer network platform.

Typography:

- Use PP Neue Montreal-style display type for major headlines.
- Use DM Sans for body text, buttons, nav, cards, labels, inputs, and captions.
- Keep headline tracking tight and sizes large.
- Do not use serif type.

Layout:

- Use a 1280px centered container.
- Use large section gaps around 160px.
- Use poster-like hero composition with huge compressed heading, short body copy, one Ember CTA, and a violet halftone or node-graph visual.
- Use large rounded cards rather than dense small tiles.
- Alternate Pumice and Chalk surfaces.

Components:

- Primary buttons: Ember Orange fill, 40px radius, DM Sans 16px/600.
- Secondary buttons: transparent or Chalk fill, 1px Lava Black border, 40px radius.
- Cards: Chalk or Pumice fill, 40px radius, 40px padding, thin border.
- Tags: 800px pill radius, Sulfur or Ember fill, compact DM Sans label.
- Inputs: Chalk fill, 32px radius, Stone border, Ember focus.
- Diagrams: thin Lava Black strokes, circular nodes, Violet/Ember highlights, flat shapes.

Strict rules:

- Do not use white as the main canvas.
- Do not add extra saturated colors.
- Do not use glassmorphism, neon glow, or generic crypto coin art.
- Do not use small 8px SaaS cards.
- Do not make everything purple. Orange is the main action color.
