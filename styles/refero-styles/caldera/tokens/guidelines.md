# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Pumice as the broad canvas.
- Make headlines huge and tightly tracked.
- Use Ember Orange for the primary action and key active states.
- Keep Violet Rift in graphic, halftone, and developer-diagram moments.
- Use Sulfur only for small tags and signal sparks.
- Use 40px rounded cards and buttons.
- Use flat technical diagrams and black line art.
- Keep spacing generous.

## Don't

- Do not use pure white as the main background.
- Do not add extra saturated accent colors.
- Do not use small 8px SaaS cards.
- Do not use glassmorphism, neon glow, or glossy gradients.
- Do not use generic blockchain coin imagery.
- Do not make every accent violet.
- Do not use serif type.
- Do not make the page feel cold or corporate.
