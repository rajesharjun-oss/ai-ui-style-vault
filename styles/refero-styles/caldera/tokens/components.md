# Components

## Primary Pill Button

Ember Orange fill, Lava Black or Steam text, 40px radius, DM Sans 16px/600, generous horizontal padding. Use for the main CTA.

## Secondary Outline Button

Transparent or Chalk fill, 1px Lava Black border, 40px radius, Lava Black text. Use beside the orange CTA or in navigation.

## Rounded Card

Chalk or Pumice fill, 1px Lava Black or Stone border, 40px radius, 40px padding. Use for major content modules.

## Hero Halftone Panel

Violet Rift and Deep Violet halftone geometry over Pumice or Chalk. Pair with giant Lava Black PP Neue-style type and one orange action.

## Network Badge

Sulfur fill, Lava Black text, 800px radius, small DM Sans label. Use only for tags, status chips, and small signal markers.

## Input

Chalk fill, Stone border, 32px radius, 16px DM Sans text, 16px by 24px padding. Focus border should use Ember Orange.

## Process Diagram

Thin Lava Black strokes, circular nodes, occasional Violet or Ember fill, and short DM Sans labels. Keep it flat and clear.
