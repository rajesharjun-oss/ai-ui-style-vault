# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Max width: 1280px centered.
- Page canvas: full-bleed Pumice.
- Navigation: simple warm-paper nav with black labels and rounded action.
- Hero: poster-like composition with huge compressed headline, short DM Sans copy, Ember CTA, and violet halftone or node-graph visual.
- Sections: very large 160px vertical rhythm.
- Content: use large rounded cards and wide panels rather than dense card grids.

## Imagery

Use:

- Violet halftone fields.
- Flat network maps and developer diagrams.
- Thin black line art.
- Circular nodes and simple protocol flow graphics.
- Occasional sulfur labels or orange signal marks.

Avoid:

- Generic crypto coins.
- Glossy 3D cubes.
- Blue-purple cyberpunk gradients.
- Glassmorphism.
- Lifestyle photography.
- Photoreal volcanic imagery.
