# Colors

## Brand And Accent

| Name | Hex | Token | Usage |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Signal Orange | `#ff3b00` | `--color-signal-orange` | Sole chromatic accent for small CTA/icon/active moments |
| Deep Teal | `#042a2b` | `--color-deep-teal` | Full-bleed hero and dark editorial sections |

## Neutrals

| Name | Hex | Token | Usage |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Warm Parchment | `#f0f0eb` | `--color-warm-parchment` | Main canvas and broad section backgrounds |
| Gallery White | `#ffffff` | `--color-gallery-white` | Cards, panels, and reversed button text |
| Soft Linen | `#e1e1db` | `--color-soft-linen` | Secondary grouped panels |
| Mist Gray | `#d7d7cf` | `--color-mist-gray` | Quiet fills and dividers |
| Pale Stone | `#cbcbc5` | `--color-pale-stone` | Light hairline borders |
| Ash Gray | `#bfbfb8` | `--color-ash-gray` | Icon strokes and muted borders |
| Graphite Warm | `#575753` | `--color-graphite-warm` | Secondary text and labels |
| Charcoal Warm | `#464643` | `--color-charcoal-warm` | Strong secondary copy and section rules |
| Ink | `#111110` | `--color-ink` | Primary text, primary button fill, structural borders |

## Palette Behavior

- Treat the system as cream, white, ink, and one orange accent.
- Use Deep Teal only for major editorial contrast.
- Use borders and layered neutrals for hierarchy.
- Avoid broad orange surfaces and avoid additional accent colors.
