# Components

## Top Navigation Bar

- Background: Warm Parchment.
- Height: about 64px.
- Left: logo plus dropdown-like nav links.
- Right: pricing link, language selector, ghost Login pill, filled Demo pill.
- Use 1px Ink borders for pills.

## Dark Hero Section

- Full-bleed Deep Teal.
- Center content in a 1200px max-width container.
- Heading: Cranny, 80-96px, weight 300, white.
- Copy: ABC Diatype, 18px, white at soft opacity.
- Add filled Ink or white buttons depending on contrast.
- Optional product preview image uses 18px radius.

## Buttons

- Filled dark: Ink background, Gallery White text, 12px 20px padding, 24-40px radius.
- Ghost: transparent fill, 1px border, 12px 20px padding, 24px radius.
- Text: ABC Diatype, 14px, weight 500, slight positive tracking.

## Logo Bar

- Cream canvas.
- Black or grayscale partner logos.
- Horizontal spacing should feel generous.
- Do not put each logo in a card.

## Media Library Card

- Gallery White surface.
- 8px radius.
- 32px padding.
- Breadcrumb header and 4x2 image grid.
- Use asset thumbnails, pills, and subtle hairlines.

## Upload Drop Zone

- Cream or white surface.
- 1px dashed Pale Stone border.
- 8px radius.
- Center icon and label stack.
- Minimum height around 320px.

## Overlapping Image Tiles

- 18px radius.
- Slight 2-3 degree rotation.
- Small overlap between images.
- No drop shadow.

## CTA Card

- Photography-led composition.
- Optional translucent white overlay with 18px radius.
- Signal Orange appears as a compact action control or icon mark only.
