# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Model

- 1200px centered max-width.
- Warm Parchment page canvas.
- Full-bleed Deep Teal hero for the first viewport.
- Cream sections with 80px vertical rhythm.
- White product cards placed on cream, separated by hairline borders.
- Logo bars and feature heading blocks should feel editorial, not dashboard-heavy.
- Product visuals can be staged as media-library cards, upload zones, or brand-board grids.

## Imagery

- Use real product UI, brand asset grids, thumbnails, campaign imagery, and creative-team/lifestyle visuals.
- Keep imagery warm, editorial, content-rich, and easy to inspect.
- Media tiles can overlap and rotate slightly for a brand-board feel.
- Round imagery more than cards: 18px image radius vs 8px card radius.

## Avoid

- Abstract gradient backgrounds.
- Decoration-only illustrations.
- Heavy photo blur.
- Monochrome UI with no content signal.
- Overly dark pages outside the hero/editorial sections.
