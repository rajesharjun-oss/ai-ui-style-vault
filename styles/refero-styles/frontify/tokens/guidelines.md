# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Cranny weight 300 for all major display headlines.
- Set interface text in ABC Diatype with slight positive tracking.
- Use Warm Parchment as the page canvas and Gallery White for cards.
- Apply 8px radius to cards and 24-40px radius to buttons.
- Use Signal Orange only for small functional moments.
- Use Deep Teal sparingly for full-bleed editorial contrast.
- Separate components with 1px hairline borders instead of shadows.
- Use warm neutral layering to create depth.

## Don't

- Do not use drop shadows or stacked elevation.
- Do not use Cranny for body copy, nav text, or labels.
- Do not make Signal Orange a large fill, section background, or long text color.
- Do not introduce a second accent color.
- Do not center long body copy.
- Do not use 4px or 6px card radius.
- Do not use glossy gradients or blue/purple SaaS effects.
- Do not rely on abstract illustration when real brand/product imagery is needed.
