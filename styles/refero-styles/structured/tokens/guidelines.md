# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Davinci-style serif for all major display moments.
- Set the hero wordmark around 374px.
- Let the wordmark crop at the viewport edges.
- Alternate Putty light sections and Ink dark sections with hard cuts.
- Use 28.8px radius for filled pill CTAs.
- Use circular crops for feature imagery.
- Use hexagonal indicators as the secondary geometry.
- Keep Helvetica Now style utility type small and functional.
- Use classical painting imagery as the only decorative language.

## Don't

- Do not introduce saturated colors.
- Do not use shadows or gradients.
- Do not use stock photography.
- Do not use modern digital illustrations.
- Do not set body text in the serif.
- Do not add visible menu bars or heavy navigation chrome.
- Do not use standard 4px, 8px, or 12px radii on main components.
- Do not make the page look like a conventional crypto dashboard.

## Accessibility Notes

- Ensure Paper text on Ink sections is high contrast.
- Ensure Ink text on Putty remains readable at small sizes.
- Avoid making all content dependent on painting imagery.
- Keep the huge cropped wordmark decorative or provide an accessible text equivalent.
