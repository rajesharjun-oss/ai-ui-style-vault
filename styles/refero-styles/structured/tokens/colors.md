# Colors

## Core Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Use |
|------|-------|-------|-----|
| Putty | `#c4c3b6` | `--color-putty` | Dominant warm gray-beige canvas for hero and most light sections. |
| Ink | `#000000` | `--color-ink` | Dark rooms, text, borders, separators, and filled pill CTAs. |
| Bone | `#e7e5e4` | `--color-bone` | Elevated cards and secondary light surfaces. |
| Chalk | `#ebebeb` | `--color-chalk` | Footer and lightest surface tier. |
| Vellum | `#dfdcd5` | `--color-vellum` | Hairline borders and printed-page edge details. |
| Graphite | `#595855` | `--color-graphite` | Muted secondary text and subtle image backgrounds. |
| Ash | `#808080` | `--color-ash` | Image placeholders and mid-neutral surfaces. |
| Paper | `#ffffff` | `--color-paper` | Reverse text on dark sections and rare pure-white marks. |

## Surface Levels

| Level | Name | Value | Purpose |
|-------|------|-------|---------|
| 0 | Putty Canvas | `#c4c3b6` | Default background for light sections. |
| 1 | Bone Card | `#e7e5e4` | Elevated cards and secondary light surfaces. |
| 2 | Chalk Footer | `#ebebeb` | Footer and section bases. |
| 3 | Ink Room | `#000000` | Dark feature and explainer canvas. |

## Rules

- Do not introduce saturated color.
- Use black and warm neutrals as the whole chromatic system.
- Use Paper mainly as reverse text on dark sections.
- Use Putty as the dominant light section background.
- Keep borders subtle with Vellum.
