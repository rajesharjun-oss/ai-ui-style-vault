# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Instrument Serif italic at 64px to 96px for major headings.
- Use a 400px-scale ghost wordmark that bleeds off the viewport.
- Use DM Mono for labels, tags, buttons, metadata, and product stamps.
- Keep Electric Cyan to 2 or 3 appearances per viewport.
- Use fully rounded pills for tags and the download CTA.
- Use 18px to 40px radius for tangible product cards.
- Reserve complex shadows for the product device and stamp.
- Use Geist or a similar sans for readable paragraph copy.

## Don't

- Do not use warm accent colors.
- Do not use heavy text shadows.
- Do not use Inter or geometric sans for major headings.
- Do not use Instrument Serif for small routine UI text.
- Do not turn cyan into a broad background fill or generic CTA color.
- Do not use sharp corners below 4px.
- Do not center body paragraphs except in testimonials.
- Do not add lifestyle photography or people imagery.

## Accessibility Notes

- Keep muted Steel Gray body copy large enough and high enough contrast on black.
- Ensure the ghost wordmark remains background-only and does not interfere with reading.
- Add visible focus outlines to white pills and ghost buttons.
- Do not rely on cyan alone for state; pair it with text or structure.
