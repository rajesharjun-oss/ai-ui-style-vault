# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

Use a full-bleed dark canvas with a 1200px content rail. The hero should feel split and theatrical, with large editorial type on one side and a product or video object on the other.

Suggested order:

1. Ghost wordmark behind hero.
2. Left editorial headline and body copy.
3. White download pill.
4. Product device card or video card.
5. Centered editorial section.
6. Two-column feature rows.
7. Product screenshot or email composition cards.
8. Minimal testimonial block.
9. Language support row.

## Composition

The type should own the page. Avoid busy grids. Use the product device as the central object and keep everything else quiet.

## Imagery

Use:

- physical product device card
- teal screen gradients
- dot-matrix textures
- email composition screenshots
- dark water-like or fabric-like background texture
- typography as atmosphere

## Avoid

- Lifestyle photography.
- People shots.
- Generic SaaS illustrations.
- Multi-color gradients.
- Busy icon systems.
- Cartoon mascots.
- Dense dashboard imagery.
