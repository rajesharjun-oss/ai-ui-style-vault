# Components

## Brand Wordmark Background

- Instrument Serif italic.
- Around 400px.
- Ghost Gray or subtle light gradient.
- Absolutely positioned behind content.
- Overflow viewport edges.
- Do not treat as a normal heading.

## Product Device Card

- Paper White shell.
- About 360px wide.
- 40px radius.
- Dark teal gradient screen.
- Dot-matrix display texture.
- Speaker grille rendered as black dots.
- Download pill inside the screen.
- Layered inset and outset shadows.
- Stamp badge overlapping the top edge.

## Every Stamp Badge

- Deep navy or teal badge.
- Around 140px wide.
- Wavy or scalloped stamp edge.
- DM Mono white text.
- Heavy inset shadow.
- Overlaps product device top.

## Download Button

- Paper White fill.
- Void Black text.
- Optional Apple icon.
- DM Mono 12px.
- Fully rounded radius.
- Around 120px by 32px.
- No border or shadow.

## Watch Video Card

- Teal-tinted dark surface.
- 8px radius.
- Small video thumbnail.
- Play affordance.
- Italic serif label.
- Secondary action only.

## Section Heading

- Instrument Serif italic.
- 64px to 72px.
- Paper White.
- Negative tracking.
- Left-aligned.
- Pair with Geist 17px supporting copy.

## Feature Annotation Row

- Two columns.
- Left side: DM Mono label plus Geist description.
- Right side: product screenshot card.
- Use 10px row gap between label and description.

## Email Composition Card

- Midnight Surface or Graphite background.
- 8px to 12px radius.
- DM Mono fields.
- Geist content text.
- Teal-to-dark content gradient allowed.

## Testimonial Block

- Centered.
- Instrument Serif italic quote at 24px to 28px.
- Small avatar.
- DM Mono name and role metadata.
- No card background.

## Language Pill Row

- Horizontal row.
- DM Mono 12px language names.
- No background or border.
- About 20px gaps.
- Italic serif label can anchor the row.

## Accent Text Link

- Electric Cyan text.
- Inline emphasis only.
- Use sparingly.
- Do not fill large surfaces with cyan.

## Ghost Button

- Transparent background.
- 1px white or gray border.
- White DM Mono 12px text.
- 8px radius.
- Compact padding.
