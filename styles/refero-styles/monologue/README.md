## Use This When

Use this style when a product should feel like a late-night editorial artifact: quiet, typographic, expensive, and software-native. It works well for writing tools, audio products, AI note products, creative utilities, boutique productivity tools, and product pages where one physical-looking device or screen can carry the hero.

The key signature is contrast between a dramatic Instrument Serif italic headline and small DM Mono system chrome. The palette is almost entirely black and gray, with Electric Cyan used only as a cold spark.
