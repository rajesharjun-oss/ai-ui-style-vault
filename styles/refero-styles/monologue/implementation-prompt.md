# Implementation Prompt

Build a polished Monologue-style interface: a dark editorial product page with a giant ghosted serif wordmark, italic Instrument Serif headlines, DM Mono system chrome, a white pill download CTA, and one cold cyan accent.

Use Void Black (`#000000`) as the base canvas. Layer near-black surfaces such as Obsidian (`#010101`), Midnight Surface (`#191919`), Graphite (`#282828`), Charcoal (`#363636`), and Slate (`#3f3f3f`) only when a card or product screenshot needs structure. Use Paper White (`#ffffff`) for primary text, the download CTA, and the product device shell. Use Electric Cyan (`#19d0e8`) and Sky Signal (`#44ccff`) sparingly for inline accents, thin borders, video/product glow, and teal screen gradients. Do not introduce warm accent colors.

Typography should be strongly separated by role. Use Instrument Serif italic for headings, section titles, and the huge ghost wordmark. Use DM Mono for labels, tags, metadata, buttons, and product stamps. Use Geist or Inter-style sans only for 17px paragraph copy. Major headings should be 64px to 96px with negative tracking. The ghost wordmark can be around 400px and should overflow the viewport.

Layout should be a full-bleed dark canvas with a 1200px max content rail. Start with a split hero: left side editorial headline and body copy, right side video or product accent, giant wordmark behind the layout, and a high-contrast white download pill. Follow with centered editorial text sections, two-column feature rows, dark product screenshot cards, a minimal testimonial, and a language support row.

Components should use fully rounded pills for the primary download action and tags. Cards use 8px to 18px radius. The product device uses a 40px radius, a white shell, teal gradient screen, dot-matrix texture, speaker grille, overlapping stamp badge, and layered inset/outset shadows so it feels like a physical object.

Before finishing, check that:

- Instrument Serif italic is used for major display moments.
- DM Mono is used for all UI chrome and metadata.
- Electric Cyan appears only a few times per viewport.
- The product device is the only heavy-shadow object.
- Body paragraphs are left-aligned outside testimonials.
- No warm accent color, generic neon gradient, or ordinary SaaS card style slipped in.
