## Notes

This bundle summarizes the Refero style page into implementation-ready guidance. It is not a verbatim archive. The style is a dark editorial product page with a single cyan spark, oversized italic serif display type, mono UI metadata, and a rounded product device that feels like a physical object on the void.

## What To Preserve

- Void Black canvas with subtle near-black layers.
- Instrument Serif italic for all major headings and the ghost wordmark.
- DM Mono for UI chrome, labels, tags, buttons, metadata, and stamps.
- Electric Cyan used only a few times per viewport.
- Ghosted full-viewport wordmark as background atmosphere.
- Fully rounded pills for the download CTA and tags.
- 18px to 40px radii on cards and the product device.
- Layered inset and outset shadows only for the physical product device and stamp.
