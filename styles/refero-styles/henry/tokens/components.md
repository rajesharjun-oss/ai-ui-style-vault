# Components

## Masthead Display Headline

Louize Display at 116-132px, weight 400, line-height around 0.8. Use Headline Ink on Paper or Paper on Ink. Treat the type itself as the main visual element.

## Stamped Display Section Header

Manuka at 226-371px, uppercase, weight 400, line-height 0.75-0.8. Use as a full-bleed typographic slab, not as a small label.

## Top Ticker Banner

Full-bleed announcement strip with Neue Montreal 12px uppercase text, tight tracking, and 1px horizontal rules. Keep vertical padding very small.

## Inverted Editorial Letter

Full-bleed Ink section with centered Louize Display/Louize copy in Paper. Eyebrows use tiny uppercase Neue Montreal with reduced opacity.

## Nav Link Uppercase

Neue Montreal 12-16px, bold, uppercase, Headline Ink. No underline, no background, no color-change active state. Use scale for active emphasis.

## Section Divider Rule

1px full-bleed line in Headline Ink or Paper. Use as typographic structure, not decoration.

## Brand Ticker Strip

Full-bleed Ink band with repeated Louize wordmarks in Paper. Keep it dense and horizontal.

## Coming Soon Tag

Outlined ghost tag with 12px radius, uppercase 12px Neue Montreal, 1px Paper border, and 4px 8px padding. No fill.

## Coordinate Footer

Small uppercase metadata line in Neue Montreal 12px with tight tracking. Use as location/time/byline texture.

## Hero Halftone Plate

Square monochrome halftone illustration on Paper or Ink. No radius, no color, no caption, no icon overlay.

## Footnotes And Meta Line

Small all-caps caption in Neue Montreal 12px with tight tracking. Use beneath major work entries.
