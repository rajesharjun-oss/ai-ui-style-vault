| --- | --- |

| Section gap | 64-96px |

## Radius

| Element | Value | Token |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tags | 12px | `--radius-tags` |
| cards | 12px | `--radius-cards` |
| buttons | 12px | `--radius-buttons` |

## Shape Rules

- 12px is the only allowed radius.
- No square 0px cards.
- No pill shapes or 9999px tags.
- No asymmetric rounding.
