# Colors

## Warm Monochrome

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | `#fafafa` | `--color-paper` | Cream-white canvas, card surface, and inverted text on Ink. |
| Headline Ink | `#2a2722` | `--color-headline-ink` | Primary type, dark section background, borders, and rules. |
| Sepia | `#3e3b36` | `--color-sepia` | Strong secondary borders and warm near-black support. |
| Ash | `#666666` | `--color-ash` | Muted UI text, low-emphasis rules, and secondary borders. |
| Midstone | `#9f9f9f` | `--color-midstone` | Muted borders and light decorative borders on Ink. |
| Pebble | `#b3b3b3` | `--color-pebble` | Inactive nav text and quiet border states. |
| Hairline | `#eeeeee` | `--color-hairline` | Subtle tile/card borders on Paper. |

## Usage

- The system is fully monochrome.
- No blue links, red warnings, green success fills, or decorative accent colors.
- Use Paper and Headline Ink for almost every visible decision.
- Support neutrals are for borders, inactive states, and muted metadata only.
