# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Louize Display at 77px or larger for section-defining headlines.
- Use Neue Montreal for UI/body at 12, 16, 20, 24, and 32px with tight tracking.
- Alternate Paper and Ink as full-bleed bands.
- Use 12px as the only border radius.
- Carry all major type in Headline Ink on Paper or Paper on Ink.
- Use Manuka only for huge section mastheads.
- Let most of the page remain empty Paper or Ink.

## Don't

- Do not introduce any chromatic accent color.
- Do not use filled colored CTA buttons.
- Do not use box shadows, drop shadows, glow, gradients, blur, or elevated panels.
- Do not set long body copy in oversized display serif sizes.
- Do not use any radius other than 12px.
- Do not add gray panels, tinted surfaces, or light/dark gradients.
- Do not center-align Neue Montreal body copy.
- Do not add icon systems, generic UI pictograms, or stock photography.

## Accessibility Notes

- Oversized display type must wrap intentionally on mobile.
- Preserve contrast between Paper and Headline Ink.
- If action states are needed, use underline, scale, border, or inversion rather than color.
- Avoid relying on tiny 12px uppercase text for critical instructions.
