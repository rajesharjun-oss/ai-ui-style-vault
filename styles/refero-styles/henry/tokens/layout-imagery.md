# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Full-bleed editorial sections.
- Paper and Ink bands alternate, but never blend.
- Hero can use a left-heavy display headline with a right-side halftone plate.
- Inverted letter sections are centered and typographic.
- Ticker bands can be dense and horizontal.
- Navigation is minimal and text-only.
- Avoid cards, feature grids, pricing tables, dashboards, and conventional SaaS sections.

## Imagery

- Use monochrome halftone illustration only.
- Keep imagery square-cornered.
- Use black-on-Paper or Paper-on-Ink treatment.
- No photography.
- No product screenshots.
- No gradients.
- No icons or UI pictograms.

## Composition Checks

- Is the design still monochrome?
- Are Paper and Ink the only dominant surfaces?
- Is the main visual impact coming from type scale?
- Are all radii exactly 12px?
- Are shadows and gradients absent?
- Is any dense content limited to ticker/byline areas?
