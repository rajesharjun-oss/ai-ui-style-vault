# Implementation Prompt

Build a fashion-editorial product gallery in the LUNCH style.

Use only four colors: Ink Black `#000000`, Warm Parchment `#f4f1e4`, Ivory Mist `#fcfaf1`, and Lilac Veil `#b8aad0`. Use Ink Black for all text, underlines, borders, and structural linework. Use Warm Parchment for post-hero content sections. Use Ivory Mist as a warm light surface variant. Use Lilac Veil only for the announcement bar, footer band, and sold-out product status.

Use Good Sans or a close editorial sans such as Sohne, Inter, or Untitled Sans across the interface. Use weight 400 for body and labels, weight 700 for brand names, product prices, and emphasis. Keep sizes modest: 12px captions, 16px body, 24px subheadings, 32px headings. Use a Redaction-style display face only once for the hero wordmark at 72px or larger, treated like a 3D/chrome visual object rather than reusable text styling.

Make the hero full-bleed and dark, with fashion photography filling the viewport edge-to-edge. Overlay the brand wordmark across the image, allowing it to intersect the subject. After the hero, switch to a warm parchment catalog: centered intro copy, underlined text link, then a tight 4-5 column product grid with 5px gaps.

Do not use filled buttons, pills, rounded corners, gradients, drop shadows, glassmorphism, colored borders, icon ornament, or UI badges. Product images and cards have 0px radius and no wrapper styling. The UI should feel like a printed catalog where photography and one wordmark carry the drama.
