# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Value | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Lilac Veil | `#b8aad0` | Announcement bar, footer band, sold-out text, rare brand punctuation |
| Ink Black | `#000000` | Text, nav, underlines, borders, linework, product metadata |
| Warm Parchment | `#f4f1e4` | Editorial and product-section background after the hero |
| Ivory Mist | `#fcfaf1` | Warm light surface variant, hero-context UI, product panel surface |

## Rules

- Use only these four colors.
- Use Ink Black for all structural lines.
- Use Lilac Veil as a punctuation strip or status text, not as a CTA fill.
- Do not create lighter or darker lilac variants.
- Do not add colored borders or decorative chromatic UI.
- Let photography provide depth and emotion.
