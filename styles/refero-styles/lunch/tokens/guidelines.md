# Guidelines

## Do

- Use the four-token palette only.
- Set all text in Good Sans except the hero wordmark.
- Use font weight for hierarchy before increasing size.
- Make every interactive element an underlined text link.
- Let product and editorial photography carry the visual weight.
- Use one Redaction-style wordmark as the only typographic spectacle.
- Pair dark full-bleed photography with Warm Parchment product sections.
- Keep card and image edges sharp.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce filled buttons, pill shapes, or rounded corners.
- Do not use Lilac Veil as a CTA background.
- Do not add gradients, drop shadows, or glassmorphism.
- Do not scale body text above 32px outside the hero wordmark.
- Do not use colored borders or chromatic UI elements.
- Do not split Lilac Veil into multiple variants.
- Do not add decorative icons, badges, or UI ornament.
- Do not let UI chrome compete with photography.
