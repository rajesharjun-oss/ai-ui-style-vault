# Typography

## Workhorse Typeface

- Family: Good Sans
- Substitute: Sohne, Inter, Untitled Sans, or a restrained editorial sans
- Weights: 400 and 700
- Sizes: 12px, 16px, 24px, 32px
- Line-height: 1 for nav, 1.8 for body and editorial copy
- Letter spacing: 0.005em to 0.013em, slightly larger at larger sizes

Good Sans handles navigation, body copy, product metadata, links, prices, labels, and normal headings.

## Hero Wordmark Typeface

- Family: Redaction
- Substitute: GT Sectra Display Bold, Domaine Display Bold, or Editorial New Bold
- Weight: 700
- Size: 72px
- Line-height: 1.8
- Letter spacing: 0.002em
- Use exactly once per page for the hero wordmark.

## Type Scale

| Token | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `caption` | 12px | 400 | 1 | 0.6px | product metadata and nav details |
| `body` | 16px | 400 | 1.8 | 0.08px | intro and body copy |
| `subheading` | 24px | 700 | 1.8 | 0.12px | small editorial headings |
| `heading` | 32px | 700 | 1.8 | 0.16px | major non-hero headings |
| `display` | 72px | 700 | 1.8 | 0.144px | hero wordmark only |

## Rules

- Reach for weight before size.
- Keep normal interface text modest.
- Do not use display type outside the hero wordmark.
- Do not scale body text above 32px.
