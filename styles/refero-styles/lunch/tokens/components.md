# Components

## Announcement Bar

- Full-width strip.
- Background: `#b8aad0`
- Text: `#000000`
- Font: Good Sans, 12px to 16px, weight 400
- Line-height: 1.8
- Centered single sentence.
- No border, no radius, no icon.

## Primary Navigation

- Horizontal text-only link bar.
- Brand name left, links right.
- Text over hero: `#fcfaf1`
- Text over light sections: `#000000`
- Font: Good Sans, 12px to 16px.
- Brand name can use weight 700.
- No background, borders, icons, or filled active states.
- Gap: 20px to 30px.

## Hero With 3D Wordmark

- Full-viewport dark fashion photograph.
- Image bleeds to every edge.
- Large brand wordmark spans the width.
- Wordmark may use chrome or metallic 3D treatment.
- Wordmark intersects the subject rather than sitting politely above it.
- Use this spectacle once.

## Intro Text Block

- Background: Warm Parchment.
- Centered column.
- Max width: 600px to 700px.
- Body: 16px Good Sans, line-height 1.8, Ink Black.
- Follow with centered underlined link at 12px uppercase.

## Text Link

- Font: Good Sans, 12px to 16px.
- Weight: 400.
- Color: Ink Black.
- Decoration: 1px underline.
- Underline offset: 2px to 4px.
- No fill, border, radius, or icon.

## Product Card

- Radius: 0
- Border: none
- Shadow: none
- Wrapper padding: none
- Image sits flush on Ivory Mist or white panel.
- Brand name: 12px Good Sans, weight 700, Ink Black.
- Product name: 12px Good Sans, weight 400, Ink Black.
- Price: 12px Good Sans, weight 700, Ink Black.
- Sold out: 12px Good Sans, Lilac Veil.

## Product Grid

- 4-5 columns.
- Gap: about 5px.
- No card gutters beyond the grid gap.
- Full-width up to about 1280px.
- Images should align tightly in a gallery rhythm.

## Sold Out Status

- Inline text only.
- Color: Lilac Veil.
- Font: 12px Good Sans.
- No background, badge, border, or icon.

## Cookie Consent Banner

- Small white utility card.
- Position: bottom-right.
- Text: 12px black.
- Include an underlined learn-more link.
- Keep close affordance minimal.

## Footer Band

- Full-width Lilac Veil strip.
- Text: Ink Black.
- Can contain centered navigation or brand text.
- Mirrors the announcement bar to bookend the page.
