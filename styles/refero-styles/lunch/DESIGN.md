# LUNCH - Style Reference

LUNCH feels like a printed fashion editorial moved onto the web. The first screen is confrontational and cinematic. Everything after it becomes quiet, catalog-like, and almost anti-interface.

## Visual Thesis

- Open with one full-bleed dark photographic hero.
- Use one oversized 3D or chrome brand wordmark as the spectacle.
- Move into warm parchment product sections that feel like a printed catalog.
- Keep UI interaction as underlined text, not filled controls.
- Use Lilac Veil only for announcement bars, footer bands, and sold-out text.
- Keep all product cards, images, badges, and buttons at 0px radius.

## Core Ingredients

- Palette: Ink Black, Warm Parchment, Ivory Mist, and Lilac Veil only.
- Typography: Good Sans as the workhorse, Redaction-style display only for the hero wordmark.
- Shape: square, flush, and unframed.
- Interactions: text links with 1px underlines.
- Imagery: fashion photography carries the brand.
- Layout: full-bleed hero followed by centered intro and tight product grid.

## Page Composition

The rhythm is direct: lilac announcement strip, text-only navigation over a dark hero, full-viewport editorial photograph with a huge wordmark, a centered intro paragraph on warm parchment, a tight 4-5 column product grid, then a lilac footer band.

## Color Behavior

Ink Black is every line and text element. Warm Parchment is the main editorial background. Ivory Mist is a warm light variant for hero-context UI or product image panels. Lilac Veil is a whisper: use it to bookend the page and mark sold-out states. Do not make lilac a CTA fill or decorative theme.

## Type Behavior

Good Sans carries navigation, body, metadata, product names, prices, and links. Use weight 400 for regular text and 700 for brand names, prices, and emphasis. The Redaction-style display face appears exactly once as the hero wordmark.
