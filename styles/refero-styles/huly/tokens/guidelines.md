# Guidelines

## Do

- Use `9999px` radius for buttons, tags, and pill controls.
- Reserve Esbuild for display moments at `28px` and above.
- Choose the background mode first: dark canvas for product-heavy screens, white for editorial sections.
- Use Electric Iris for the single most important action on a screen.
- Use Ember Pulse as warm punctuation in tags, dots, and gradient stops.
- Apply the aurora gradient as a narrow vertical or radial beam.
- Set body text around `14px`, `1.5` line-height, and subtle negative tracking.
- Stack dark and light sections as alternating bands with `96px` vertical rhythm.

## Don't

- Do not use sharp corners on buttons or tags.
- Do not pair Inter display weights with Esbuild in the same large display hierarchy.
- Do not apply the aurora gradient as a full-surface background.
- Do not introduce a third accent color; the iris/ember pair should carry the chromatic identity.
- Do not rely on heavy drop shadows for elevation on dark cards.
- Do not use Esbuild below `28px` or in body copy.
- Do not place low-contrast text on dark or light surfaces without checking readability.
