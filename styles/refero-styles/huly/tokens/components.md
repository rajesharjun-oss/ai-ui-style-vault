# Components

## Primary Pill Button

- **Role:** Main CTA or top-level conversion action.
- **Style:** `#5683da` background, white text, Inter `14px`/`500`, `9999px` radius, `12px 24px` padding.
- **Behavior:** Add a restrained Electric Iris glow on hover.

## Ghost Pill Button

- **Role:** Secondary CTA or nav action.
- **Style:** Transparent fill, `1px` dark border on dark surfaces, white text, Inter `14px`/`500`, `9999px` radius, `10px 20px` padding.
- **Behavior:** Can become a stronger white-on-charcoal treatment on hover.

## White Pill Button

- **Role:** Light-foreground hero CTA or dark-hero secondary action.
- **Style:** `#ffffff` fill, `#090a0c` text, `9999px` radius, `12px 24px` padding.
- **Rule:** White works as a foreground CTA in dark contexts; do not overuse it as a primary action color elsewhere.

## Feature Card

- **Role:** Product capability card in grids.
- **Style:** `#111111` or gradient-tinted dark surface, `12px` radius, `24px` padding, optional `1px #4a4b50` border.
- **Effect:** A subtle radial coral/amber glow may bleed from one corner.

## Highlight Card

- **Role:** Large feature spotlight card.
- **Style:** Deep dark base, `12px` radius, `16px` to `20px` padding, Esbuild `32px` heading in white.
- **Effect:** Use a single soft radial warm or cool glow as the visual hook.

## Product Screenshot Frame

- **Role:** Hero and feature evidence.
- **Style:** Dark product UI wrapped in a `12px` radius frame with `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5) 0 6px 25px` shadow.
- **Rule:** The product UI is the hero asset. Use real app surfaces rather than stock photography.

## Top Navigation

- **Role:** Site-wide header.
- **Style:** Transparent over hero, Inter `14px` center nav, pill auth/CTA controls on the right.
- **Behavior:** Can become more opaque with slight backdrop blur on scroll.

## Aurora Hero Background

- **Role:** Signature hero treatment.
- **Style:** Narrow vertical light beam on `#090a0c`, moving from Electric Iris through Ember Pulse to white.
- **Rule:** Use once as a signature moment. Do not fill every section with the gradient.

## Tag Or Chip

- **Role:** Category labels, issue tags, filters.
- **Style:** `9999px` radius, `4px 10px` padding, Inter `11px`/`500`.
- **Color:** Text uses category color; background uses the same color at about 12% opacity.

## Stat Counter

- **Role:** Large metric/date/time display.
- **Style:** Esbuild `80px` numeral in white inside a `30px`-radius or circular container.

## Light Section Band

- **Role:** Alternating content sections below dark hero.
- **Style:** White or warm linen background, dark headings, dark body text, content constrained to `1200px`.

## Kanban Board Preview

- **Role:** Product illustration card.
- **Style:** Mini dark-mode kanban inside a `12px` card frame with subtle shadow, tags, avatars, and product-scale details.

## Inbox Or Chat Panel

- **Role:** Right-side feature preview.
- **Style:** Dark panel with avatars, `12px` radius, white names, ash/smoke message previews, unread pills, and iris status dots.
