# Colors

## Brand

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Electric Iris | `#5683da` | `--color-electric-iris` | Primary action background, active nav indicator, cool aurora stop, and the main vivid accent against graphite surfaces. |
| Ember Pulse | `#ff8964` | `--color-ember-pulse` | Secondary accent, warm aurora stop, notification dot, and small highlight color. |

## Accent

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Molasses | `#5a250a` | `--color-molasses` | Deep warm accent for dark-context borders, icon strokes, and tag fills when coral is too bright. |

## Neutrals

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Void | `#090a0c` | `--color-void` | Deepest surface for hero gradients, modal backdrops, and disappearing dark borders. |
| Charcoal Card | `#111111` | `--color-charcoal-card` | Elevated card and panel surface above the canvas. |
| Obsidian Canvas | `#303236` | `--color-obsidian-canvas` | Dominant page background for dark sections. |
| Slate Edge | `#4a4b50` | `--color-slate-edge` | Hairline borders and dividers on dark surfaces. |
| Iron Veil | `#6b6c6d` | `--color-iron-veil` | Muted backgrounds for tags, list fills, and disabled states. |
| Smoke | `#95979e` | `--color-smoke` | Icon strokes, secondary text, and inactive controls. |
| Ash | `#a9a9aa` | `--color-ash` | Tertiary text and subtle body borders in dense lists. |
| Frost | `#d1d1d1` | `--color-frost` | Light-mode borders, input fields, and secondary CTA borders. |
| Linen | `#e5e5e7` | `--color-linen` | Light-mode surface tint and subtle section dividers. |
| Snow | `#ffffff` | `--color-snow` | Hairline borders, dividers, input outlines, and light surfaces. |
