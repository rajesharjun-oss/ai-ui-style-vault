# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Rhythm

- Hero should be full-bleed and dark, with a narrow aurora beam and product UI evidence.
- Content sections should use a max width around `1200px`.
- Below the hero, alternate dark and light bands.
- Use `96px` section gaps to keep dense SaaS content breathable.
- Feature areas work well as 3-column or 4-column card grids.
- Larger feature sections can break the grid with mixed-size cards or a mosaic layout.

## Imagery

- Use product UI screenshots, dashboards, kanban boards, inbox panels, calendars, or app surfaces as the main visuals.
- Avoid lifestyle photography, generic stock imagery, and unrelated 3D renders.
- Use monochrome line icons in smoke/white tones rather than multicolor icon sets.
- Localized glows should support components without competing with the hero aurora.

## Gradient System

- **Aurora beam:** narrow hero-only linear gradient from Electric Iris to Ember Pulse to white.
- **Radial sunburst:** warm amber/yellow glow from one card corner, low opacity.
- **Section transition:** rare light-to-violet tint bridges between major bands.
- Never stack multiple full-opacity gradients in one viewport.

## Similar Design Neighborhood

- Linear-like dark productivity aesthetic with one strong accent.
- Vercel-like dramatic gradient hero restraint.
- Resend-like dark/light section alternation and pill controls.
- Stripe-like product evidence floating over atmospheric backgrounds.
