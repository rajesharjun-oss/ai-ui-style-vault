# Implementation Prompt

Build a polished, responsive SaaS interface in the Amplemarket Refero style.

Use a light editorial/product system: Canvas White `#ffffff` as the main page, Cream Wash `#f6f5f3` for alternating soft sections, Ink `#111111` for text and filled actions, Phoenix Orange `#e8400d` for brand moments, and dark contrast bands in Charcoal `#272625` or Midnight Indigo `#10054d`.

Typography must use `Labil Grotesk Variable` or a geometric grotesque fallback such as Inter, DM Sans, or General Sans Variable. Use weight 400 for most headings up to 56px. Reserve 84px, weight 900, uppercase display text for poster-like hero moments. Apply negative tracking at large sizes and normal tracking for body/UI.

Layout rules:

- Center content in a 1200px max-width container.
- Use 80-120px section gaps.
- Use 20px card padding and 8-12px element gaps.
- Use 8px button radius, 12px card/input radius, and 9999px badge radius.
- Alternate white/Cream Wash sections with occasional dark full-bleed panels.
- Use pastel category cards in flat fills: Petal Pink, Mint Green, Canary Yellow, and Soft Violet.

Component rules:

- Primary buttons are Ink-filled, 8px radius, 12px 16px padding.
- Email capture pairs a white 48px-high input with a filled Ink button.
- Feature cards are flat, rounded, and color-coded.
- Dark testimonial cards use Charcoal, white quote text, and Ash metadata.
- Hero can use a radial gradient from Phoenix Orange through warm cream to violet/cyan.
- Product media should sit inside dark rounded video/screenshot containers.

Do not use heavy shadows, glossy gradients on cards, extra accent colors, pure black text, oversized radii, or pastel colors as decoration-only flourishes.
