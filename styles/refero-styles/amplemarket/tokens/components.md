# Components

## Primary Filled Button

- Background: Ink.
- Text: Canvas White.
- Radius: 8px.
- Padding: 12px 16px.
- Font: 16px Labil Grotesk Variable, weight 400.
- Use for the highest-emphasis CTA.

## Ghost Navigation Button

- Transparent background.
- Radius: 8px.
- Padding: 12px 16px.
- Use softened white on dark surfaces and Ink on light surfaces.

## Neutral Filled Button

- Background: Pearl.
- Text: Ink.
- Radius: 8px.
- Padding: 12px 16px.
- Use for secondary actions that need a visible surface.

## White Pill Badge

- Background: Canvas White.
- Text: Ink.
- Radius: 12px.
- Compact icon-plus-text lockup.

## Email Capture Input

- Background: Canvas White.
- Border: 1px rgba(17,17,17,0.08).
- Radius: 12px.
- Height: 48px.
- Padding: 0 16px.
- Join inline with a filled Ink button.

## Pastel Category Card

- Background: Petal Pink, Mint Green, Canary Yellow, or Soft Violet.
- Radius: 12px.
- Padding: 16px 20px.
- No shadow, no border.
- Use color to communicate product category.

## Dark Testimonial Card

- Background: Charcoal.
- Radius: 12px.
- Quote text: Canvas White.
- Metadata: Ash.
- Include a small avatar and compact author stack.

## Light Feature Card

- Background: Canvas White.
- Radius: 12px.
- Padding: 16px 20px.
- Use a hairline border only when separation is needed.

## Logo Grid Card

- Transparent or Canvas White surface.
- Radius: 12px.
- Centered logo with Ash caption.
- Works best in a 4-column grid.

## Sticky Navigation Bar

- Height: about 62px.
- Logo left, nav links center, action cluster right.
- Can become white with backdrop blur on scroll.

## Hero Video Container

- Dark/black surface.
- Radius: 12px.
- Near full container width.
- Aspect ratio around 16:9.
- Center a play button or product video frame.

## Dark Section Panel

- Background: Charcoal or Midnight Indigo.
- Padding: 80-120px vertical.
- Use white primary text and Ash secondary copy.
