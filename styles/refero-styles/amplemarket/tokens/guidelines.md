# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Labil Grotesk Variable weight 400 for headings up to 56px.
- Apply weight 900 uppercase only for 84px poster/display moments.
- Use the pastel palette as flat card fills for category differentiation.
- Set border radius to 8px on buttons and 12px on cards and inputs.
- Use Ink for primary filled buttons.
- Use Charcoal and Midnight Indigo for dark contrast panels.
- Apply negative letter spacing proportionally as type gets larger.
- Alternate Canvas White and Cream Wash sections with occasional dark bands.

## Don't

- Do not use weight 500 or 700 for normal large headings.
- Do not add shadows to pastel category cards.
- Do not use pastel colors as gradients.
- Do not set card radius above 12px or button radius below 8px.
- Do not use pure black for primary text.
- Do not add heavy drop shadows to white cards.
- Do not scatter orange or pastel colors randomly as decoration.
- Do not make the whole page dark; darkness is for panels and rhythm.
