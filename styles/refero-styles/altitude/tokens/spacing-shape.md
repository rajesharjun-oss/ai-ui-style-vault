## Elevation

Use shadows sparingly. Structure should come from surface steps and hairline borders first.

- Card: subtle charcoal shadow, useful only when a panel needs mild lift.
- Button: shadow plus 1px ring, only for pressed or raised controls.
- Modal: dark overlay shadow, keep it restrained and crisp.
