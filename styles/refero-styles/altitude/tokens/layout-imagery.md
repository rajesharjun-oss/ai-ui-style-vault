# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Max width: 1200px centered.
- Main canvas: full-bleed Carbon Canvas.
- Top navigation: 64px tall, simple wordmark left, nav links centered, login/action right.
- Hero: centered single-column stack with serif display headline, muted subhead, quiet action, and ridge-line graphic.
- Section rhythm: 80px vertical gaps.
- Product sections: dark copy and terminal/product visuals.
- Contrast section: one light workflow-card grid to interrupt the dark flow.

## Imagery

Use:

- Muted alpine or mountain landscape photography.
- Thin mountain ridge line art, ideally 1px and Fog colored.
- Terminal screenshots or product panes over landscape cards.
- Monochrome outline icons, 1.5px stroke, in Fog or Mid Navy.

Avoid:

- Gradient art.
- Abstract bokeh or blobs.
- 3D renders.
- Lifestyle stock photos.
- Overly colorful charts.
- Decorative color systems beyond Voltage Blue.
