# Guidelines

## Do

- Use the five-step dark gray surface stack as the base.
- Use Libre Baskerville for large editorial statements only.
- Use Inter for body copy and every functional UI element.
- Use Fira Code for terminal, code, query, and data identifier moments.
- Keep controls rectangular: 4px for buttons, inputs, and badges; 8px for cards; 16px for large images.
- Reserve Voltage Blue for functional emphasis such as links, active state, focus ring, and terminal highlights.
- Use mountain ridge line art or muted alpine photography for visual storytelling.
- Let borders, tracking, and weight do more work than color.

## Don't

- Do not add extra saturated accents.
- Do not use bold serif headlines.
- Do not use the serif font for body text or controls.
- Do not add gradient meshes, glowing orbs, glassmorphism, or 3D decoration.
- Do not use heavy shadows.
- Do not make everything black; keep the light workflow interruption.
- Do not make all badges and buttons pill-shaped.
- Do not use generic lifestyle stock photography.
