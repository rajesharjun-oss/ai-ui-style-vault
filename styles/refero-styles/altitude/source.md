## What To Preserve

Altitude combines editorial finance, terminal density, and alpine restraint. Its visual identity comes from the contrast between a dark gray surface stack and refined serif headlines. The interface should feel like an investment research product or AI intelligence terminal, not a generic SaaS landing page.

The page relies on:

- Layered gray surfaces from `#111111` through `#323232`.
- Bone-colored primary type and fog-colored helper copy.
- Libre Baskerville for display moments only.
- Inter for functional interface text.
- Fira Code for code, command, terminal, and data-identifier moments.
- Voltage Blue as the only saturated accent.
- Mountain ridge line art, landscape photography, and product terminal screenshots as the visual vocabulary.
