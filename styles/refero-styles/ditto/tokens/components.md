# Components

## Primary CTA Button Filled Yellow

Hi-Yellow background, Deep Ink or Charcoal text, Inter 500 16px, full pill radius, 12px by 24px padding, and no shadow. Use one primary yellow CTA per viewport.

## Secondary Button Dark Pill

Deep Ink background, white text, Inter 500 16px, full pill radius, 12px by 22px padding. Use alongside primary CTA.

## Email Input Field

White fill, 1px Onyx border, Deep Ink text, Slate placeholder, Inter 16px, full pill radius, and 12px by 22px padding.

## Logo Lockup

Organic mark plus wordmark in Deep Ink. Use inside nav or footer.

## Nav Bar

Soft Meadow background, logo left, centered Inter 500 nav links, and CTA pair right. No shadow.

## Hero Card Product Mockup Container

White product card with 24px radius placed over organic blobs in Moss Green, Fuchsia, Hi-Yellow, and Deep Ink.

## Feature Card

Soft Meadow card, 24px radius, 24-48px padding, serif heading, Inter body, and no border or shadow.

## Customer Logo Card

Soft Meadow card with centered monochrome logo and small case-study link.

## Testimonial Card

Soft Meadow card with 32px padding, 24px radius, quote text, and author metadata block.

## Pagination Dot

Inactive marker in Slate. Active marker uses Hi-Yellow and pill shape.

## Small Caps Label

Inter 500 at 10-12px, uppercase, tight tracking, Slate or Deep Ink.

## Hero Headline

Hedvig Letters Serif 48-64px, weight 700, Deep Ink, tight tracking, line-height around 1.
