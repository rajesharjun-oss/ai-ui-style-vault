# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- 1200px centered content width.
- Generous side padding.
- Hero is a two-column split.
- Left hero column: headline, subcopy, email capture, trust badge.
- Right hero column: product mockup card with organic blob backdrop.
- Customer trust strip sits on Soft Meadow.
- Feature sections can use three-card grids.
- Testimonials use a horizontal carousel.
- Sections alternate Canvas and Soft Meadow bands.

## Imagery

- Product UI screenshots are primary.
- Organic blobs are flat, irregular, overlapping, and decorative.
- Photography can be warm outdoor divider imagery, but should not dominate the UI.
- Customer logos are monochrome Deep Ink.
- No 3D renders or people illustrations.

## Iconography

- Minimal line icons.
- Keep icons in Deep Ink, Slate, or Onyx.
- Do not make icons green or fuchsia.

## Composition Checks

- Is Soft Meadow used for cards?
- Is yellow limited to one primary CTA per viewport?
- Are green and fuchsia only decorative?
- Are headings in the serif?
- Are buttons and inputs pill-shaped?
