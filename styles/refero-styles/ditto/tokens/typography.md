# Typography

## Font Families

| Role | Font | Token | Fallback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Hedvig Letters Serif | `--font-hedvig-letters-serif` | DM Serif Display, Source Serif 4, Libre Caslon Text, Georgia, serif |
| UI/body | Inter | `--font-inter` | Inter, system-ui, sans-serif |

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Token |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| caption | 10px | 500 | 1.2 | -0.2px | `--text-caption` |
| body-sm | 14px | 400 or 500 | 1.5 | -0.14px | `--text-body-sm` |
| body | 16px | 400 | 1.5 | -0.16px | `--text-body` |
| subheading | 18px | 400 | 1.5 | -0.18px | `--text-subheading` |
| heading-sm | 22px | 400 or 700 | 1.25 | -0.22px | `--text-heading-sm` |
| heading | 32px | 700 | 1.15 | -0.32px | `--text-heading` |
| heading-lg | 48px | 700 | 1.1 | -0.48px | `--text-heading-lg` |
| display | 64px | 700 | 1 | -0.64px | `--text-display` |

## Rules

- Use Hedvig Letters Serif for all headings at 22px and above.
- Use Inter for UI, body, nav, labels, forms, and cards.
- Do not use Hedvig for body copy or UI labels.
- Use tight negative tracking on headings and small caps labels.
- Inter may use weights 400, 500, and 600.
