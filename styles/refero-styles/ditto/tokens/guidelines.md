# Guidelines

## Do

- Use pill radius on every button, input, nav link, tag, and icon container.
- Pair yellow CTA with a dark pill for hierarchy.
- Use Hedvig Letters Serif for headings at 22px and larger.
- Use Inter for everything below heading level.
- Set primary body text in Deep Ink.
- Preserve Canvas and Soft Meadow surface temperatures.
- Use decorative blobs only behind product visuals.
- Tighten heading letter spacing and small caps labels.

## Don't

- Do not use sharp corners on buttons, inputs, or nav items.
- Do not introduce extra UI accent colors.
- Do not use Moss Green or Fuchsia in buttons, badges, icons, or status indicators.
- Do not use pure white for card surfaces when Soft Meadow is intended.
- Do not place two yellow CTAs in the same viewport.
- Do not use Inter for display headlines or Hedvig for UI labels.
- Do not add drop shadows to cards or buttons.
- Do not use Slate for primary body text.

## Accessibility Notes

- Yellow CTAs should use Deep Ink or Charcoal text for strong contrast.
- Do not rely on blob colors to convey status or meaning.
- Keep form labels and error/help text distinct through copy, border, and ARIA state, not new colors.
- Serif headlines should reflow cleanly on mobile.
