## Use This Style When

Use Peak Design when an interface should feel like a premium product-commerce gallery: monochrome, engineered, photographic, and editorial. It fits gear stores, hardware catalogs, accessories brands, camera products, luggage, industrial design portfolios, and any commerce surface where the product photography should be the hero.

## Quick DNA

- Near-monochrome palette with white sections and deep near-black panels.
- Product photography does the visual work.
- Editorial split panels pair dark type blocks with full-bleed images.
- Exposure-style serif is reserved for hero and section display headlines.
- Geist handles readable body, product text, forms, and footer content.
- Bryant-style condensed uppercase labels define nav, buttons, filters, and eyebrows.
- Product cards are flat, borderless, shadowless, and spaced apart.
- Ember Red is rare, single-instance emphasis only.
