# AI Implementation Prompt

Build a Peak Design-inspired commerce interface.

Use a near-monochrome gallery palette: Paper White `#ffffff`, Carbon Ink `#1a211e`, Obsidian `#0c0c0c`, Mist `#e0e0e0`, Ash Border `#cccfcd`, Graphite `#606562`, Slate `#363537`, Pewter `#4e4e4e`, and rare Ember Red `#cc2e39`. The UI should feel engineered, photographic, and editorial.

Use three type voices:

- Exposure-style serif for hero and section display headlines only, 48px and above, weight 400, tight negative tracking.
- Geist for body, product details, descriptions, forms, links, and footer content.
- Bryant-style condensed uppercase sans for navigation, buttons, filters, labels, and eyebrows.

Core layout:

- Sticky announcement strip and white main nav with hairline border.
- Horizontal category filter pills below the nav.
- Full-width 50/50 split hero with a near-black text panel and full-bleed product image.
- Product grids with 4 columns, 24px to 32px gaps, borderless product cards, and 8px-radius product images.
- Alternating white and near-black editorial sections with 80px vertical rhythm.

Core components:

- Dark announcement bar with uppercase white labels.
- Search field in nav with Fog fill, Ash Border, 4px radius, and Graphite placeholder.
- Active filter pill with Carbon Ink fill and white uppercase text.
- Inactive filter pill with transparent fill, Ash Border, and Slate text.
- Product card with no shadow or border, 1:1 product image, compact title/variant/price stack.
- White filled button on dark panel with 4px radius and uppercase label.
- Transparent outlined button on dark panel with white border.
- Ghost text button on light surfaces.
- Carousel dots with active pill dot and inactive gray circles.

Do not use colorful CTA fills, gradients, shadows, soft glass, decorative illustration, or extra accent colors. Use Ember Red only for one sale marker, urgency badge, or brand punctuation per viewport.
