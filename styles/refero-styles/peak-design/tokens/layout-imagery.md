# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use full-width sections that alternate between Paper White and deep near-black.
- Keep internal content centered at about 1440px max width.
- Build heroes as 50/50 split panels.
- Use product grids with 4 equal columns and 24px to 32px gaps.
- Place category filter chips directly below the main nav.
- Use 80px vertical section rhythm.
- Keep body copy left-aligned.
- Reserve centered alignment for hero headlines and small section labels only.

## Imagery

Product photography dominates the design.

Use:

- Studio-grade product photos on pure white.
- Consistent lighting and angle across product grids.
- Close detail crops that show material and hardware.
- Lifestyle images only in full-bleed editorial split sections.

Avoid:

- Illustrations.
- Abstract graphics.
- 3D renders.
- Decorative icon art.
- Generic lifestyle stock.
- Image overlays with gradients.

## Product Card Imagery

Use 1:1 images with 8px radius and no surrounding card border. Let the white canvas and whitespace frame the product.
