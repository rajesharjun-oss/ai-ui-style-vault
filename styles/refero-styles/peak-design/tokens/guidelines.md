# Guidelines

## Do

- Use the Exposure-style serif only for hero and section-display headlines.
- Use Geist 400 at 16px with 1.5 line height for body text.
- Use Bryant-style uppercase labels for nav, filters, buttons, and eyebrows.
- Alternate white and near-black full-bleed sections.
- Keep product cards borderless and shadowless.
- Use 8px radius for cards and product images.
- Use 4px radius for buttons, inputs, and nav elements.
- Use 9999px radius only for badges and filter pills.
- Use Ember Red only for a single low-frequency emphasis.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce colored backgrounds for UI components.
- Do not add box shadows.
- Do not use the display serif for body or UI.
- Do not use Bryant-style labels in mixed case.
- Do not repeat Ember Red across a viewport.
- Do not use card or image radii larger than 8px.
- Do not center-align product descriptions.
- Do not use illustrations, 3D renders, or abstract decorative graphics.
