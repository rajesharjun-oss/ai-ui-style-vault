# Components

## Announcement Bar

Dark utility strip, about 32px tall, centered uppercase Bryant-style text, white labels, and no decorative chrome.

## Primary Navigation

White 64px header with hairline bottom border, logo, uppercase category links, search field, support link, account icon, and cart icon.

## Category Filter Bar

Horizontal pill row below the nav. Active pills use Carbon Ink fill and white uppercase text. Inactive pills use transparent fill, Ash Border, and Slate text.

## Product Card

Borderless and shadowless card. Use an 8px-radius product image, then a compact Geist stack for title, variant, and price.

## New Badge

Pewter pill badge over product image with white uppercase Bryant-style text.

## Hero Split Panel

50/50 layout with a deep text panel on one side and full-bleed product or lifestyle image on the other.

## Filled Button On Dark

White fill, Carbon Ink uppercase label, 4px radius, 12px by 20px padding, no shadow.

## Outlined Button On Dark

Transparent fill, white border, white uppercase label, 4px radius, and no elevation.

## Ghost Button On Light

No background, no border, uppercase Carbon Ink label. Use for annotation-like actions.

## Carousel Pagination Dots

Active Carbon Ink pill dot and inactive Ash Border circles, centered below product rows.

## Search Field

Fog background, Ash Border, 4px radius, 40px height, Graphite icon and placeholder.

## Section Heading Block

Large left-aligned Exposure-style heading, Carbon Ink, 48px to 80px, with tight tracking.

## Navigation Icon Button

Icon-only control with no fill or border, 20px to 24px icon size, and Carbon Ink stroke.
