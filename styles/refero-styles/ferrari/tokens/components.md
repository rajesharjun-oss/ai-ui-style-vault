# Components

## Ghost Navigation Link

Transparent text-only navigation. Use 11-12px uppercase white type with wide tracking, 0px radius, no fill, and no border. Hover changes text color to red or white.

## Dark Cinematic Hero Panel

Full-viewport black or near-black section with edge-to-edge product photography. Copy is minimal, centered or gently overlaid, uppercase, white, and no larger than 16px in the reference system.

## Section Label Button

Transparent uppercase label control with 5px vertical padding, 0px radius, and optional 1px underline on hover. Do not use a filled button surface.

## Carousel Indicator Dot

Small 8-10px indicator. Use muted gray for inactive, white or red for active. Keep it geometric and quiet.

## Footer Block

Full-width near-black footer. Use tiny uppercase column labels, white link text, generous spacing, and white outlined social icons.

## Featured Content Card

Flat rectangle on black or near-black. No radius, border, shadow, or elevation. Image bleeds to the card edge.

## Utility Icon Button

16px white outlined icon with transparent background, 10px padding, no fill, no border, and 0px radius.

## Category Pill

Transparent full-radius pill with a 1px white hairline border and uppercase 9-11px text. Use sparingly.

## Hairline Divider

1px solid `#303030` on dark backgrounds or `#d2d2d2` on light backgrounds.

## Full-Bleed Photography Frame

Image container with no inset, no rounding, no border, and no extra chrome.

## Body Text Link

Uppercase white text link with red hover state. Avoid underline by default.

## CTA Arrow Link

Text-only action with a simple arrow affordance. Never treat this as a filled CTA button.
