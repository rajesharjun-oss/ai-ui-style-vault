# Colors

## Brand

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rosso Corsa | `#da291c` | `--color-rosso-corsa` | Red interaction signal for hover, focus, active, and tiny accents. |
| Rosso Scuro | `#9d2211` | `--color-rosso-scuro` | Deeper red for small supporting states. |

## Neutrals

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Carbone Nero | `#000000` | `--color-carbone-nero` | Primary cinematic background and hero void. |
| Notte Profonda | `#181818` | `--color-notte-profonda` | Secondary dark surface and footer. |
| Grafite | `#303030` | `--color-grafite` | Dark hairlines, dividers, and subtle borders. |
| Piombo | `#666666` | `--color-piombo` | Subdued copy and quiet label text. |

| Argento Chiaro | `#d2d2d2` | `--color-argento-chiaro` | Light surface dividers and disabled outlines. |
| Cemento | `#ebebeb` | `--color-cemento` | Pale alternate section background. |
| Velo di Luce | `#f7f7f7` | `--color-velo-di-luce` | Near-white wash for light surfaces. |
| Bianco Ferrari | `#ffffff` | `--color-bianco-ferrari` | Primary text, white canvas, icons, and links. |

## Usage

- Red is an interaction state, not a filled surface.
- White text and muted gray carry almost all copy.
- Black and near-black should dominate the viewport.
- Light surfaces are rare and should stay flat, clean, and sharp.
