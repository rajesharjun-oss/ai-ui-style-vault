# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Full-bleed vertical scroll.
- First viewport should be a cinematic product photograph or a black hero panel.
- Navigation is flat and transparent over the hero.
- Content rhythm alternates between large image-led panels and quiet black sections.
- Composition is usually centered and single-column.
- Secondary card grids are allowed after the fold, but must stay flat and sharp.

## Imagery

- Use cinematic product or vehicle photography.
- Favor dark studio, night, or high-contrast environments.
- Images should occupy 70-90% of a viewport section.
- The product should be isolated and dominant.
- Avoid lifestyle scenes, generic stock images, illustrations, abstract graphics, and product screenshots.

## Iconography

- Minimal white outlined SVG icons.
- 16px icon size works well for search, close, menu, and social actions.
- Keep icon buttons transparent and borderless.

## Composition Checks

- Does the image bleed to the edge?
- Is the type quiet and precise?
- Is red used only as a signal?
- Are all primary surfaces flat?
- Are corners sharp?
