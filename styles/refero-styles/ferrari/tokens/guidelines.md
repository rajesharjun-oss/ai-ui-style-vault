# Guidelines

## Do

- Use black and near-black as full-bleed backgrounds.
- Let product photography reach the viewport edge.
- Keep type small, uppercase, and widely tracked.
- Use `#da291c` only for hover, focus, active states, or tiny status signals.
- Keep all core UI geometry at 0px radius.
- Use 1px hairline dividers for structure.
- Keep cards and panels flat.

## Don't

- Do not use red as a filled button or large decorative background.
- Do not add rounded cards, rounded buttons, glass panels, or soft UI.
- Do not add shadows, elevation, glow, or gradients.
- Do not wrap hero photography in padded frames.
- Do not introduce a broad accent palette.
- Do not make interface copy large and loud.
- Do not let UI chrome compete with the image.

## Accessibility Notes

- Tiny uppercase type can become hard to read. Use at least 11px for functional controls.
- Preserve high contrast between white text and black backgrounds.
- Red hover states should be paired with another state cue such as focus outline, underline, or active indicator.
- Provide visible keyboard focus even when the visual language is minimal.
