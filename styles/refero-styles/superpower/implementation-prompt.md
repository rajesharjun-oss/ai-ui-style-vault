# Implementation Prompt

Build a polished clinical health interface inspired by the Superpower Refero style system.

Use dark cinematic hero photography, a floating Carbon Black pill nav, tight NB International-style typography, crisp white clinical sections, zinc-gray support copy, and a low-frequency Sunrise Coral action accent. The experience should feel like a premium health command center rather than a soft wellness landing page.

## Required Style Decisions

- Hero: full-bleed dark photography with readable white overlay text.
- Page background: Paper White `#ffffff`.
- Primary text and nav fill: Carbon Black `#18181b`.
- Muted copy: Zinc Gray `#71717a`.
- Dividers and borders: Mist Gray `#e4e4e7`.
- Muted surfaces: Fog Gray `#f4f4f5`.
- Primary action: Sunrise Coral `#fc5f2b` filled pill with white text.
- Special visual only: Coral Glow marbled membership card.
- Buttons: 9999px radius.
- Cards: 15px radius.
- Small thumbnails/cards: 5px radius.
- Floating nav: 15px radius.
- Heavy shadows: none.

## Typography Direction

Use NB International Pro or a close substitute for all sans typography. Use weight 400 for all display and major headings. Use weight 700 only for button text and small labels. Apply negative tracking everywhere, strongest at display sizes.

Use NB International Mono Pro sparingly for clinical metadata, tabular labels, and technical annotations.

## Layout Direction

Create:

- A full-bleed dark photo hero.
- A floating dark pill navigation capsule over the hero.
- Centered hero headline at 66px with tight tracking.
- A Sunrise Coral filled CTA with a trust badge row below.
- White content sections with 75px section rhythm.
- Feature cards with 15px radius and hairline borders.
- A single marbled coral membership card visual.
- Photo thumbnail strips with one coral active border.

## Avoid

- Extra accent colors.
- Heavy shadows or glowing effects.
- Coral body text or coral broad backgrounds.
- Weight 700 display headings.
- Flat top nav bars.
- Square buttons.
- Reusing the coral gradient outside the membership card.
- Body text directly on hero photography without a dark scrim.
