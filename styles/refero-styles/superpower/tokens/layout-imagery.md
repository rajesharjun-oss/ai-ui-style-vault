# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Hero: full-bleed dark photographic section.
- Navigation: floating Carbon Black pill over the hero.
- Main content: Paper White sections.
- Max width: 1200px.
- Section rhythm: about 75px.
- Card padding: 19px.
- Trust row: immediately below primary CTA.
- Membership visual: one marbled coral card.

## Imagery

Use:

- Dark cinematic hero photography.
- Human silhouette or figure against deep teal-blue darkness.
- Clean product screenshots.
- Membership card artwork.
- Small app/dashboard thumbnail strips.

Avoid:

- Generic wellness stock photography.
- Illustrations.
- 3D renders.
- Abstract graphic backgrounds.
- Broad coral gradients outside the membership card.

## Overlay Rules

Hero text must sit on a dark enough image area or scrim. White text should feel crisp and intentional, not low-contrast. Product imagery below the hero should be contained and clinical.
