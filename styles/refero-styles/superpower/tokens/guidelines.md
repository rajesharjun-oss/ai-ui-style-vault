# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Sunrise Coral for filled action pills, brand marks, and active states.
- Set display headlines at weight 400 with -0.025em tracking and line-height 1.
- Float navigation as a dark pill over imagery.
- Use 9999px radius for buttons and nav CTAs.
- Layer surfaces as Paper White, Fog or Mist Gray, and Carbon Black.
- Use the marbled coral gradient only for the membership card.
- Pair every primary CTA with a trust badge row.
- Use dark scrims when placing text over hero photography.
- Keep most cards flat with hairline borders.

## Do Not

- Do not use weight 700 for display headings.
- Do not introduce additional chromatic colors.
- Do not use coral for body text, icons, or broad backgrounds.
- Do not use heavy drop shadows.
- Do not place navigation as a flat top bar.
- Do not use square CTA buttons.
- Do not reuse the marbled gradient as a section background.
- Do not place body copy on hero images without sufficient contrast.
