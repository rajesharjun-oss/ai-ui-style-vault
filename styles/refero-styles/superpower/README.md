## Use This When

Use this style for health-tech, diagnostics, longevity, clinical membership, wellness subscriptions, lab testing, personal health dashboards, and high-trust consumer medical products that need to feel precise, cinematic, and warm without becoming soft.

## Core Idea

Superpower combines a dark cinematic photo hero with crisp white clinical content. The system is mostly black, white, and zinc gray, with Sunrise Coral used as low-frequency functional punctuation for actions and brand moments. Tight geometric typography and pill controls keep the interface sharp, while the coral membership card adds the one expressive visual flourish.
