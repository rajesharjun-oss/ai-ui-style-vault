## Radius

| Element | Value | Token |
| --- | --- | --- |
| inputs | `5px` | `--radius-input` |
| ghost buttons | `5px` | `--radius-ghost-button` |
| cards | `12px` | `--radius-card` |

## Borders And Elevation

- Card border: `1px solid #4d4d4d`.
- Ghost button border: `2px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.6)`.
- Overlay blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.
- Elevation: translucency and blur only.
- Shadows: none.
