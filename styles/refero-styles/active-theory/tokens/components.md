# Component Tokens

## Ghost Navigation Button

- **Role:** Top-right navigation chrome.
- **Background:** transparent.
- **Border:** `2px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.6)`.
- **Radius:** `5px`.
- **Padding:** `1px 6px`.
- **Text:** uppercase nbarchitekt, `10px` to `12px`, weight `400`, `#ffffff`.
- **Placement:** Floating top-right over the black canvas.

## Pill Primary Button

- **Role:** Singular dominant CTA.
- **Background:** `#343755`.
- **Text:** usually high-contrast dark or white depending on context; keep one treatment consistent.
- **Radius:** `500px`.
- **Padding:** `4px 18px`.
- **Font:** nbarchitekt `14px`, weight `700`.
- **Rule:** Use sparingly.

## Translucent Cookie Banner

- **Background:** `rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`.
- **Backdrop:** `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.
- **Radius:** `12px`.
- **Padding:** `16px 28px 32px`.
- **Shadow:** none.
- **Body:** Times `16px`, line-height `1.88`, `#ffffff`.
- **Links:** `#c6c6c6`.

## Ghost Project Card

- **Background:** transparent or `rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`.
- **Border:** `1px solid #4d4d4d`.
- **Radius:** `12px`.
- **Padding:** `28px`.
- **Shadow:** none.
- **Title:** nbarchitekt `14px`, weight `700`, `#ffffff`.
- **Description:** Times `16px`, line-height `1.88`, `#ffffff`.

## Full-Viewport Immersive Hero

- **Canvas:** `#000000`.
- **Layout:** No visible max-width container.
- **Visual:** Centered WebGL or 3D portal/geometric composition.
- **Chrome:** Floating ghost nav, minimal consent, no heavy controls.
- **Atmosphere:** Aurora wash or particle glow belongs to the rendered scene, not UI chrome.

## Inline Text Link

- **Text:** `#c6c6c6`.
- **Font:** Times or nbarchitekt depending on context.
- **Decoration:** Optional subtle underline or no decoration.
- **Hover:** Fade to `#ffffff`.
