# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Grammar

Use a full-bleed immersive canvas with no visible max-width constraint. The page reads as a single viewport of rendered 3D space, not as a conventional scrolling document.

## Hero Structure

- Pure black page canvas.
- Centered 3D or WebGL composition.
- Floating ghost navigation pinned to the top-right.
- Minimal cookie or consent overlay when required.
- Near-zero content density above the fold.

## Conventional Sections

When project listings, case studies, contact forms, or legal content appear, they should overlay the black scene as translucent panels. Do not switch to a white content layout.

## Imagery

- Use rendered real-time 3D, particle fields, geometric portals, wireframes, aurora glows, or shader-driven visuals.
- Avoid photography, stock illustration, flat icon grids, and conventional mockup cards.
- UI iconography should be geometric and line-based.

## Composition Signatures

- Central luminous portal or abstract geometric core.
- Low-opacity aurora wash from one corner.
- Particle fields or constellation-like specks.
- Ghost chrome that seems to dissolve into the void.
