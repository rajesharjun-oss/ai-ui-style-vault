# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

Use a full-bleed Obsidian canvas with a centered 1200px content rail. Alternate between dark and Bone sections, with generous 80px vertical gaps.

Suggested order:

1. Bone announcement bar.
2. Transparent nav over Obsidian.
3. Dark hero with giant centered serif headline.
4. Floating Solstice Gradient abstract shape.
5. Light Bone product feature section.
6. Dark stats credibility row.
7. Product cards or case studies.
8. Restrained closing CTA.

## Composition

Let headings and whitespace dominate. Imagery should occupy less of the viewport than the typography. Avoid dense grids except for product protection cards.

## Imagery

Use two modes:

- Abstract 3D organic blob with Solstice Gradient.
- Clean product-object crops inside Bone or Paper containers.

Product imagery should feel isolated and showroom-like, not lifestyle-heavy.

## Avoid

- Stock photography.
- Heavy icon systems.
- Generic SaaS illustrations.
- Neon effects.
- Gradient UI controls.
- Dense visual clutter.
