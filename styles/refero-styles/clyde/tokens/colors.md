# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Obsidian | `#000000` | `--color-obsidian` | Main dark canvas, dark borders, inverted UI |
| Char | `#1a1a1a` | `--color-char` | Near-black edge and subtle dark surface layer |
| Slate | `#7d7d7d` | `--color-slate` | Mid-gray controls, active filters, muted fills |
| Bone | `#f6f6f4` | `--color-bone` | Warm text, light surface, ghost borders |
| Paper | `#ffffff` | `--color-paper` | Inner product-image containers and bright cards |
| Solstice Gradient | `linear-gradient(90deg, #feed7a 0%, #ff8400 48.96%, #df91f7 100%)` | `--gradient-solstice` | Decorative 3D shape and limited product accent |

## Color Behavior

The palette should feel 99 percent neutral. Use the gradient as one carefully placed spotlight. Do not use it for CTA fills, text, borders, form states, or active navigation.

## Pairings

- Dark page: Bone text on Obsidian.
- Light sections: Obsidian text on Bone.
- Product containers: product image on Paper inside Bone cards.
- Ghost controls: Bone border and text on Obsidian.
- Filled controls: Obsidian fill with Bone text on light surfaces.

## Avoid

- New chromatic accents.
- Gradient buttons.
- Neon glows.
- Color-coded UI states.
- Shadows for contrast.
