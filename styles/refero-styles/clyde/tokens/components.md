# Components

## Announcement Bar

- Full-width Bone strip.
- Centered 15px Oldschool Grotesk text.
- Obsidian text.
- 12px vertical padding.
- No border or shadow.

## Dark Navigation Header

- Transparent over Obsidian.
- Bone nav links.
- Oldschool Grotesk 15px regular.
- Minimal logo lockup.
- Login and demo actions on the right.
- No bottom border or nav fill.

## Hero Display Section

- Full-bleed Obsidian background.
- Centered Recoleta-style headline at 80px to 125px.
- Bone text.
- Tight line height around 1.
- Floating Solstice Gradient abstract shape.
- Supporting body copy in Oldschool Grotesk.

## Filled Pill Button

- Use on Bone or Paper surfaces.
- Obsidian fill.
- Bone text.
- 100px radius.
- 20px 28px padding.
- No shadow, no gradient.

## Ghost Pill Button

- Use on dark surfaces.
- Transparent fill.
- 1px Bone border.
- Bone text.
- 100px radius.
- 20px 28px padding.

## Product Protection Card

- Bone background.
- 16px radius.
- 24px padding.
- Inner Paper product-image container.
- 21px Oldschool Grotesk heading.
- 15px lightweight feature list.

## Program Performance Card

- Bone background.
- 16px radius.
- 44px horizontal padding.
- Filter tab pills at top.
- Flat chart panel below.
- Use Slate for active tabs or muted UI fills.

## Stats Feature Bar

- Dark row on Obsidian.
- Four or five items.
- 1px Bone dividers.
- Small circular check icon plus 15px Bone text.

## Case Study Card

- Char or Obsidian background.
- 16px radius.
- Bone copy.
- Client logo box.
- Ghost pill CTA.
- Product illustration area with very limited gradient accent.

## Filter Tab Pill

- 100px radius.
- 10px 16px padding.
- Inactive: transparent with border.
- Active: Slate fill.

## Section Heading Block

- Left-aligned Recoleta-style heading.
- 47px to 80px.
- Bone on dark, Obsidian on light.
- No eyebrow needed.

## Body Description Block

- Max width around 520px.
- 16px Oldschool Grotesk.
- Weight 300 or 400.
- Quiet opacity on dark surfaces.
