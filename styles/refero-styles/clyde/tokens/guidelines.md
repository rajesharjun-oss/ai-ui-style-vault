# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Recoleta-style serif headings at 36px and above.
- Use increasingly negative letter spacing as type gets larger.
- Use 100px radius for all interactive controls.
- Keep the UI almost entirely achromatic.
- Use Solstice Gradient only for decorative shapes and tiny product accents.
- Use 1px Bone or Char hairline borders for separation.
- Use Oldschool Grotesk weight 300 for descriptive text.
- Use Oldschool Grotesk weight 400 for nav, buttons, and primary UI.

## Don't

- Do not introduce extra chromatic colors.
- Do not use the gradient for buttons, text, borders, or large UI surfaces.
- Do not use Recoleta-style serif for body copy below 36px.
- Do not use box shadows for card elevation.
- Do not loosen display letter spacing.
- Do not invent radius values outside 4px, 12px, 16px, 38px, and 100px.
- Do not mix chromatic CTA styles.

## Accessibility Notes

- Bone on Obsidian provides strong contrast, but keep opacity reductions conservative for body text.
- Make focus states visible with Bone or Obsidian outlines depending on surface.
- Do not rely on the gradient alone to communicate state.
- Product images should include useful alt text when they explain the offering.
