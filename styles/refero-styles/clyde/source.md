## Notes

This bundle summarizes the Refero style page into implementation-ready guidance. It is not a verbatim archive. The style reads as a luxury editorial showroom for product protection: near-black canvas, warm cream text, high-contrast light product cards, serif display scale, and a single decorative gradient.

## What To Preserve

- Obsidian dark canvas with Bone text and borders.
- Recoleta-style serif display headings at 36px and above.
- Oldschool Grotesk-style UI and body text.
- 100px pill radius for all interactive elements.
- Border-based elevation, no box shadows.
- Light Bone product cards with inner Paper containers.
- Solstice Gradient only for abstract 3D shapes and small product accents.
