# Guidelines

## Do

- Use PolySans-style headings at weight 400 only.
- Use -0.02em tracking on PolySans-style text.
- Use Inter for body and UI support text.
- Keep pages about 95 percent achromatic.
- Use Ember Orange only as punctuation.
- Use square buttons, asymmetric cards, rounded data widgets, and pill navs.
- Use 20px element gaps and 80px section gaps.
- Alternate Canvas White and Ash section bands.
- Use flat data cards as the main imagery.

## Don't

- Do not bold PolySans-style headings.
- Do not use Ember Orange as a filled button background.
- Do not add shadows to cards or buttons.
- Do not make every card symmetrically rounded.
- Do not introduce extra chromatic accents.
- Do not use display line-height above 1.25.
- Do not crowd the layout with decoration.

## Accessibility Notes

- Graphite on white and Ash gives strong contrast.
- Use Ember Orange sparingly and check contrast when it is used for text.
- Keep focus states visible with Graphite or Ember outline treatment.
- Avoid relying on chart color alone; include labels or patterns when data meaning matters.
