# Guidelines

## Do

- Use GeistSans for all text.
- Use GeistMono only for tiny technical annotations.
- Apply negative tracking to headings 24px and larger.
- Keep the palette strictly achromatic.
- Use 1px solid Line borders for dividers and structure.
- Use solid fill for primary, border for secondary, and text-only for tertiary actions.
- Keep at least about 96px between major sections.
- Keep the main prompt input visually central.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce saturated colors into UI chrome.
- Do not use system fonts or other brand fonts when Geist is available.
- Do not use shadows on buttons or inputs.
- Do not use gradients or background images.
- Do not use 700+ font weights.
- Do not create dense layouts.
- Do not use border radii outside 6px, 8px, 12px, and 9999px.
