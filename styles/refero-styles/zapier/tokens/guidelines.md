# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Cream Paper or Parchment for all page and card backgrounds.
- Apply Ember Orange only to filled buttons, the logo glyph, and small icon accents.
- Keep Ember Orange under about 5 percent of any layout.
- Use Degular Display for hero and section headings with positive tracking.
- Use GT Alpina weight 250 on one editorial heading per page.
- Set button radius to 20px.
- Layer warm neutrals for text hierarchy.
- Follow the 4px spacing rhythm.

## Do Not

- Do not use stark white or pure black as primary surfaces.
- Do not apply negative letter spacing to Degular Display headings.
- Do not use Iris Ink in UI chrome, buttons, or text.
- Do not add heavy drop shadows.
- Do not let Ember Orange fill large background areas.
- Do not use cool blue-grays.
- Do not overuse GT Alpina.

## AI Implementation Notes

- If the screen feels too enterprise, warm the surface and type colors before adding decoration.
- If it feels too orange, reduce orange to one CTA and a few icons.
- If hierarchy feels weak, use Degular Display or spacing instead of more color.
- If a card feels heavy, remove the shadow and use a Stone Linen border.
