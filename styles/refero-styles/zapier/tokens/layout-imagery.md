# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a 1200px max-width centered container.
- Use a full-bleed announcement bar when needed.
- Keep navigation sticky at 64px height.
- Hero commonly uses a 2-column split: text and CTAs on the left, illustration or product visual on the right.
- Use a centered grayscale partner logo strip.
- Product showcase sections can break full-bleed with a warm photographic blur background.
- Template sections use 3-column card grids on cream.
- Maintain 80px to 120px between major bands.
- Alternate left-aligned editorial stacks with centered showcase compositions.

## Section Patterns

- Hero: open-tracked Degular headline, Inter body, orange filled CTA, outlined secondary CTA.
- Template grid: Cream Paper cards with app icons and workflow headings.
- Product showcase: centered screenshot frame over warm photographic blur.
- Trust strip: grayscale logos with wide gaps.
- Feature card: Parchment panel with small orange icon accent.
- Footer: warm text hierarchy with compact link groups.

## Imagery

- Use workflow builder screenshots, product UI frames, app icons, integration diagrams, and grayscale partner logos.
- Illustration should use Ember Orange, Iris Ink, and Cream Paper.
- Product screenshots are dominant.
- Avoid lifestyle photography, stock imagery, cool-toned graphics, and colorful badges.
- Keep visual density moderate, with one large illustration or product screenshot per section.
