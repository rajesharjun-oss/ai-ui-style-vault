## Use This Style For

- Automation platforms, workflow builders, integration marketplaces, productivity SaaS, and creator/business tools.
- Interfaces that need to feel practical, friendly, editorial, and human without looking playful.
- Product pages where app icons, workflow templates, integration cards, and warm product screenshots are the core visuals.

## Design Memory

Zapier feels like a warm maker notebook instead of cold enterprise software. Cream Paper and Parchment replace stark white, Ink Brown replaces cool black, and Ember Orange is the one vivid action color. Inter carries functional UI, Degular Display creates open-tracked editorial headings, and GT Alpina appears sparingly for one magazine-like serif moment.
