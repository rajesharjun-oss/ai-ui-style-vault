# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Patterns

- 1280px centered content column.
- 64px outer gutters.
- Editorial hero with left-aligned heading and right-side body copy.
- Large full-width feature panel below hero.
- Sections alternate between eggshell canvas and taupe bands.
- Minimal top bar, no mega-menu.
- Trust logos on open canvas rather than cards.

## Imagery

- Primary imagery is product visuals, especially audio spheres.
- Use radial violet/orange/pink blends only for product-art moments.
- Use monochrome icons.
- Use grayscale logos.
- Avoid lifestyle photography and multicolor icon sets.

## Responsive Notes

- Stack hero columns on mobile.
- Keep pill buttons intact.
- Preserve 20px card radius and warm surfaces.
- Keep audio sphere visuals large enough to feel like product art.
