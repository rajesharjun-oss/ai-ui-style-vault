# Colors

## Brand And Accent

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Violet Spark | `#0447ff` | `--color-violet-spark` | Product visual accent inside audio spheres and decorative product icons only |
| Ember Orange | `#ff4704` | `--color-ember-orange` | Product visual accent paired with Violet Spark inside artwork only |

## Neutrals

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Ink | `#000000` | `--color-ink` | Primary text, filled buttons, nav, and links |
| Graphite | `#44403b` | `--color-graphite` | Strong secondary text and section labels |
| Smoke | `#777169` | `--color-smoke` | Body text, muted descriptions, and captions |
| Ash | `#a59f97` | `--color-ash` | Helper text and tertiary descriptions |
| Stone | `#ebe8e4` | `--color-stone` | Hairline borders, dividers, and icon plates |
| Warm Taupe | `#f5f3f1` | `--color-warm-taupe` | Section bands, feature cards, and secondary surfaces |
| Eggshell | `#fdfcfc` | `--color-eggshell` | Page canvas, card surfaces, and outline button fill |

## Rules

- Use Eggshell instead of pure white.
- Use Ink for buttons and links.
- Use violet and orange only in product visuals.
- Do not introduce additional accent colors.
- Use Stone for borders and dividers.
