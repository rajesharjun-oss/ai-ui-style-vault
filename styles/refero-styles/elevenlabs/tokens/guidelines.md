# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Waldenburg weight 300 for all display headlines at 32px and up.
- Set all buttons, tags, and tab pills to 9999px radius.
- Use black filled buttons paired with eggshell outline buttons as the only button hierarchy.
- Reserve violet and orange exclusively for product visuals.
- Use 1px Stone hairline borders for section separation.
- Apply -0.02em tracking on Waldenburg headings.
- Apply +0.01em tracking on Inter body at 14px to 16px.
- Stack surfaces as eggshell, taupe, then stone.

## Don't

- Do not bold or semibold Waldenburg.
- Do not use violet or orange for buttons, links, badges, borders, or interactive UI.
- Do not add heavy drop shadows.
- Do not introduce new accent colors.
- Do not use sharp corners under 8px on cards or feature panels.
- Do not use pure white backgrounds.
- Do not use display-weight fonts for body copy.
