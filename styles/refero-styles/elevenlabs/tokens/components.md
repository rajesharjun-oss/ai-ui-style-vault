# Components

## Filled Pill Button

- Background: `#000000`.
- Text: `#ffffff`.
- Radius: `9999px`.
- Padding: `16px` horizontal.
- Font: Inter 14px weight 500.
- Border: `1px solid #e5e5e5` when needed for legacy support.

## Outline Pill Button

- Background: `#fdfcfc`.
- Text: `#000000`.
- Radius: `9999px`.
- Padding: `14px` horizontal.
- Border: `1px solid #e5e5e5`.
- Font: Inter 14px weight 500.

## Ghost Link Button

- Transparent fill.
- Black text.
- Radius: `9999px`.
- Border: `1px solid #e5e5e5`.
- Font: Inter 14px weight 500.
- Used for tertiary nav and inline actions.

## Feature Card

- Background: `#f5f3f1`.
- Radius: `20px`.
- Padding: `32px`.
- Shadow: none.
- Border: none.

## White Card With Whisper Shadow

- Background: `#fdfcfc`.
- Radius: `20px`.
- Padding: `16px`.
- Shadow: very subtle hard edge and low-opacity blur.
- Use sparingly for cards that need to sit above other content.

## Large Feature Card

- Background: `#f5f3f1`.
- Radius: `24px`.
- Padding: generous, usually at least `32px`.
- Used for flagship feature showcases.

## Tab Pill

- Background: white/eggshell.
- Text: black.
- Radius: `9999px`.
- Border: `1px solid #ebe8e4`.
- Active marker: small colored dot for product category only.

## Hairline Divider

- Border: `1px solid #ebe8e4`.
- Preferred over shadows for separation.

## Audio Sphere Visual

- Approximate size: 200px circle.
- Soft radial blend of `#0447ff`, `#ff4704`, pink, and warm tones.
- Center play icon in white circle.
- No hard edge.

## Top Nav Bar

- Transparent on eggshell canvas.
- Height around 50px.
- Logo left, links center-left, auth buttons right.
- No filled nav background.

## Trust Logo Grid

- Grayscale partner logos.
- Low contrast.
- No boxed cards.
- Generous padding on eggshell canvas.
