# Typography

## Families

| Family | Token | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Waldenburg | `--font-waldenburg` | Display and heading type at 32px and above |
| Inter | `--font-inter` | Body, nav, buttons, links, captions, inputs, and cards |
| Geist Mono | `--font-geist-mono` | Code-adjacent or technical microcopy |

## Rules

- Use Waldenburg weight 300 only.
- Never bold or semibold Waldenburg.
- Use Inter 400 by default and Inter 500 for buttons or emphasized links.
- Apply -0.02em tracking to Waldenburg headings.
- Apply +0.01em tracking to Inter body at 14px to 16px.
