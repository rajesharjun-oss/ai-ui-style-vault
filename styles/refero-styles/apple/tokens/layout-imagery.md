# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Patterns

- Full-bleed page model with edge-to-edge sections.
- Centered product hero: name, tagline, two pill actions, product render.
- Internal text width around 980px; global page/section width can extend to 1440px and beyond.
- Sticky global nav plus product mini-nav.
- Sections alternate between Frost and product-specific color washes.
- Service card rows use full-bleed photographic tiles.
- No sidebars, no complex grids, no dashboard layouts.

## Imagery

- Product photography is the dominant visual language.
- Product renders should be isolated on white, Frost, or soft gray backgrounds.
- Lifestyle photography can appear in full-bleed feature banners.
- Color washes are section backgrounds for products, not decoration.
- No abstract illustration.
- No generic 3D beyond the product itself.
- Icons are minimal line icons, 1px to 2px, in neutral grays.

## Responsive Notes

- On mobile, preserve centered stack but reduce display size.
- Keep pill actions visible and paired.
- Let product imagery remain wide, even if cropped.
- Stack service cards into a horizontal scroll or one-column list.
- Preserve 8px image radius and 980px action pill radius.
