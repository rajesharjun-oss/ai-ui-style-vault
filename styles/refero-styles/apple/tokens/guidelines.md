# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Apple Blue only for filled action buttons and selected states.
- Pair every filled blue button with an outlined blue secondary action.
- Set body text at 17px with `-0.016em` letter spacing.
- Let product photography fill the full viewport width.
- Use 980px radius for every interactive button, tag, and pill.
- Set section backgrounds to full-bleed Frost or a single product color wash.
- Use weight 300 for subheads and editorial descriptors.
- Build hierarchy with scale, whitespace, surface color, and photography.

## Don't

- Do not use Apple Blue for text, borders, or decoration.
- Do not add drop shadows to cards, buttons, or nav.
- Do not set product-name headlines at weight 700.
- Do not use a card or panel inside a Frost section.
- Do not constrain main product imagery to a narrow max-width container.
- Do not use radius below 980px for buttons or above 8px for cards/images.
- Do not mix the three blues in one interactive element.
- Do not build asymmetrical dashboard layouts in this style.
