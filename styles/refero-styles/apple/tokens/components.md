# Components

## Filled Pill Button

- Role: Primary action.
- Background: `#0071e3`.
- Text: `#ffffff` or `#f4f8fb`.
- Radius: `980px`.
- Padding: `11px 15px`.
- Font: SF Pro Text `17px`, weight `400`.
- Border: none.
- Shadow: none.
- Use one per action group.

## Outlined Pill Button

- Role: Secondary action paired with a filled action.
- Background: transparent.
- Border: `1px solid #0066cc`.
- Text: `#0066cc`.
- Radius: `980px`.
- Padding: `11px 15px`.
- Font: SF Pro Text `17px`, weight `400`.

## Ghost Link

- Role: Tertiary action or inline link.
- Background: none.
- Border: none.
- Text: `#0066cc`.
- Hover: underline only.
- Weight: 400.

## Global Nav Bar

- Background: `#1d1d1f` for dark nav or white for light nav.
- Height: compact with about `8px` vertical padding.
- Links: SF Pro Text `12px`, weight `400`.
- Border: 1px hairline bottom rule in `#333333` or light equivalent.
- Layout: logo left, categories centered, utility icons right.

## Sticky Mini-Nav

- Background: white.
- Product name: `21px`, weight `600`.
- Action links: `14px`, weight `400`.
- Border: 1px bottom hairline, usually `#d2d2d7`.
- Pins below global nav.

## Product Hero Section

- Background: full-bleed `#f5f5f7`.
- Product name: SF Pro Display `56px`, weight `600`, `#1d1d1f`.
- Tagline: SF Pro Text `26px`, weight `300`.
- CTAs: filled + outlined pill pair.
- Product render fills lower portion of the viewport.

## Feature Banner

- Full-width photographic or color-washed background.
- Text overlay can be dark or white depending on image contrast.
- No card surface inside the section.
- Image is the surface.

## Service Card Grid

- Card background: full-bleed photograph.
- Radius: `8px`.
- Title: `24px` to `28px`, weight `600`, white.
- Label: `12px` to `14px`, white at lower opacity.
- Action: small pill button at bottom.
- Use horizontal scroll or grid.

## Typography-Only CTA Block

- Centered stack.
- Product name: `40px` to `56px`, weight `600`.
- Descriptor: `21px`, weight `300`.
- Optional variant word: `#2997ff`.
- CTAs below with generous spacing.

## Footer

- Background: `#f5f5f7` or `#1d1d1f`.
- Multi-column link grid.
- Links: `12px`, weight `400`, `#707070`.
- Headers: `12px`, weight `600`, `#1d1d1f`.
- Hairline dividers.

## Form Input

- Background: `#f5f5f7`.
- Border: `1px solid #d2d2d7` or `#707070`.
- Radius: `8px`.
- Text: `14px` to `17px`.
- Focus ring: `2px #0071e3`.
