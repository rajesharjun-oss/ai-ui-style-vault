# Apple Style Reference

## Creative Direction

Create a restrained premium product page where the interface disappears and the product takes over. The page should feel bright, quiet, and precise: large centered product names, short subheads, paired pill actions, full-bleed product imagery, and soft gray section canvases.

## Visual Priorities

1. Use `#f5f5f7` as the signature light gray page canvas.
2. Use `#1d1d1f` as the dominant ink color.
3. Use `#0071e3` only for filled action buttons and selected states.
4. Use `#0066cc` for outlined action borders and inline links.
5. Use `#2997ff` only as a decorative or atmospheric blue accent.
6. Let product imagery fill the full viewport width.
7. Use full-bleed section backgrounds instead of nesting cards inside sections.
8. Use hairline borders and surface shifts instead of shadows.

## Screen Composition

Use a full-bleed page model. Sections stretch edge-to-edge, while text blocks center internally around 980px. Heroes are symmetrical and stacked: product name, one-line tagline, two pill actions, then a product render that fills the remaining viewport. Service cards can use full-bleed photography with text overlays and 8px radius.

## Material Language

Chrome is minimal. Buttons are pills, cards/images are 8px, inputs are 8px, and sections remain flat. Hierarchy comes from scale, whitespace, typography, and photography rather than borders, shadows, or decoration.

## AI Implementation Notes

When generating a UI from this style, do not build a dashboard. Build a product showcase. Keep every section centered, symmetrical, and generous. Use product photography, isolated renders, or lifestyle imagery as the hero. Pair every filled blue action with an outlined blue action.
