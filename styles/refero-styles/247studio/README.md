## Style Summary

247Studio is a severe editorial agency style: white gallery canvas, black ink typography, almost no color, rounded flat containers, and a printed-monograph sense of spacing. The emotion comes from scale, alignment, font alternates, and stark tonal contrast rather than gradients, shadows, or decorative color.

Use this style when an AI needs a refined branding-agency page, portfolio system, studio case-study index, client logo grid, or monochrome editorial landing page.

## What To Borrow

- Pure black and white as the whole brand language.
- `247 grotesk` or a close grotesk substitute for large display typography.
- `Ntbau` or a clean neo-grotesk substitute for small body copy.
- Huge 73px display lines with one muted italic line.
- Left rail composition with a narrow body-copy measure.
- Rounded white cards with 1px gray borders.
- Flat tonal hierarchy instead of shadows.
- Large whitespace, sparse copy, and hairline dividers.
- Rotated documentary labels as quiet editorial accents.
- Client logo grids with small numbered cells and grayscale marks.

## Avoid

- Adding a colored CTA to "warm up" the system.
- Using shadows, glow, glassmorphism, or depth effects.
- Letting body copy get wide or dense.
- Mixing several typefaces beyond the display and body pair.
- Rounding buttons less than 32px when they need to feel like pills.
- Using gradients as background decoration.
- Treating the logo mark like a generic text wordmark.
