# Component Recipes

## Hero

- Background: `#ffffff`.
- Container: max 1440px.
- Desktop offset: about 120px from the left edge.
- Headline: two lines, flush left.
- Line 1: black, `247 grotesk`, 73px, line-height 1, weight 400.
- Line 2: `#999999`, italic, same size and line-height.
- Supporting copy: `Ntbau`, 14px, line-height 1.89, max width 350px.
- CTA, if needed: black/white only.

## Client Logo Grid Cell

- Height: around 80px.
- Background: white.
- Border: bottom `1px solid #e6e6e6`.
- Number: top-left, 10px, `#999999`.
- Logo: centered, grayscale, about 32px high, 60% opacity.
- Interaction: slight opacity change only.

## Office Info Card

- Width: around 280px.
- Background: white.
- Border: `1px solid #cccccc`.
- Radius: 33.76px.
- Padding: 24px.
- Top media block: square or near-square ash image placeholder.
- Label: 11px uppercase in display face.
- Address: 11px body face, line-height 1.3.
- Email: underlined, black.

## Documentary Sidebar Label

- Position: absolute on the right edge of an editorial section.
- Text: uppercase, 10px, display face, weight 500.
- Rotation: 90deg.
- Color: black.
- Add a short pale-mist hairline as an anchor.

## Section Header Eyebrow

- Align to the same left rail as hero content.
- Use 11px uppercase display text.
- Weight 500.
- Color black.
- Bottom margin around 40px.
- Keep to one or two short lines.

## Brand Mark

- Use the custom `247` mark where available.
- Keep black on light and white on dark.
- Preserve the stylistic alternate feeling with `ss01`.
- Do not stretch, outline, gradient-fill, or recolor the mark.
