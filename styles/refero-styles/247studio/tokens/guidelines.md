# Guidelines

## Do

- Use black, white, and gray only.
- Make the first screen feel typographic, not decorative.
- Keep hero display text large and left aligned.
- Use the muted italic line as the key expressive move.
- Keep supporting copy narrow and quiet.
- Use 1px borders and flat surfaces.
- Use large rounded cards when a component needs containment.
- Let client logos appear as grayscale editorial material.
- Use dark sections sparingly for contrast.
- Replace source media with owned or licensed assets in production.

## Do Not

- Do not add a colored CTA.
- Do not introduce brand gradients.
- Do not use shadows as the primary depth system.
- Do not use busy illustration backgrounds.
- Do not make every section a card.
- Do not use a dense dashboard layout.
- Do not center all content by default.
- Do not use decorative icons where typography can do the job.
- Do not over-animate; movement should feel editorial and slow.

## Implementation Heuristic

If the screen feels too plain, adjust scale, spacing, or alignment first. Add new visual elements only after the typography is working.
