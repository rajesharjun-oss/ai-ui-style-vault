## Borders

- Default structural border: `1px solid #e6e6e6`.
- Strong card border: `1px solid #cccccc`.
- Strong dark border: `1px solid #000000`.

## Layout Notes

- Leave more space than seems necessary.
- Let sparse text and hairline dividers establish rhythm.
- Keep components flat; do not create stacked card systems.
- When adapting to mobile, reduce left offset before reducing the character of the typography.
