# Layout And Imagery

## Page Composition

- Use a white full-page canvas.
- Keep a strong left rail on desktop.
- Let empty right-side space remain empty when it improves the composition.
- Alternate between open editorial sections and flat rounded information cards.
- Use dark sections only as strong pauses, not as the main page style.

## Hero

- Two-line display headline.
- First line black, second line muted gray and italic.
- Narrow paragraph below or beside the headline.
- No hero image is required unless the project content needs one.

## Portfolio Imagery

- Prefer restrained project screenshots, brand marks, print-like images, or monochrome placeholders.
- Use grayscale or low-saturation imagery when possible.
- Place images in rounded containers with ash or white backgrounds.
- Keep captions small and direct.

## Client Logos

- Use a grid of low cells.
- Keep logos grayscale.
- Add tiny numbers or metadata to create editorial cadence.
- Avoid colorful partner-logo walls.

## Motion

- Fade, reveal, or slide content slowly.
- Avoid springy SaaS motion, glow trails, or attention-grabbing hover effects.
