# Typography

## Font Roles

| Font | Role | Weights | Sizes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Martinaplantijn | Hero and section display serif | 400 | 48px, 64px |
| Abcfavoritvariable | Compressed modern headings and labels | 400, 500 | 12px to 64px |
| Inter | Body, buttons, nav, forms, captions | 400, 500, 700 | 12px to 20px |

## Typography Rules

- Use Martinaplantijn for large editorial headlines.
- Italicize one phrase inside major headlines.
- Use Abcfavoritvariable for geometric headings and step labels.
- Use tight tracking on Abcfavorit at 24px and above.
- Use Inter for practical UI and body text.
- Do not use Inter or Abcfavorit for the main display headline.
