# Guidelines

## Do

- Set largest headlines in Martinaplantijn at 48px to 64px.
- Italicize one meaningful phrase inside major headlines.
- Use Electric Cobalt only for filled action buttons, active links, and one key icon.
- Pair Ink Navy with Paper White for dark feature cards and footer.
- Use 100px radius for buttons, tags, and suggestion chips.
- Use 16px radius for cards, images, and the chat widget.
- Use 24px radius only for large hero illustrations.
- Keep the canvas Warm Canvas.
- Use Cream Border instead of cool gray dividers.
- Apply tight Abcfavorit tracking at 40px and above.

## Do Not

- Do not set display headlines in Inter or Abcfavorit.
- Do not use Electric Cobalt for body text, generic borders, or large background fills.
- Do not introduce cool gray borders.
- Do not use sharp corners on buttons.
- Do not use heavy black shadows.
- Do not center-align long body copy.
- Do not treat the chat widget as optional on product pages.
