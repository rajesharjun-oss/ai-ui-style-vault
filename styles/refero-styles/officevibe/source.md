## Capture Notes

The Refero page describes Officevibe as a calm editorial HR-product system on warm cream paper. It uses a deep ink navy for serious editorial weight, a vivid electric cobalt for action, and cream borders instead of cool gray borders.

The AI-useful pieces to preserve are:

- Warm Canvas `#f9f8f6` is the page background.
- Cream Border `#f0e9e1` replaces cool gray for structure.
- Ink Navy `#0c1754` owns dark feature cards, footer, and editorial emphasis.
- Electric Cobalt `#2545ff` is reserved for filled action buttons, active links, and the single most important icon per view.
- Martinaplantijn serif headlines use italic phrase accents as the brand voice.
- Abcfavoritvariable has tight negative tracking at 24px and above.
- Buttons and suggestion chips use 100px pill radius.
- Cards, images, and chat widget use 16px radius; large hero illustrations can use 24px.
- A bottom chat widget belongs to the system.
