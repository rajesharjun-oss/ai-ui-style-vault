# AI Implementation Prompt

Build a warm food-market commerce interface inspired by arte*.

Use a mixed poster/catalog system with black-bean dark panels, cream paper surfaces, tomato red display type, olive green and mint produce accents, yellow sticker warmth, rounded product cards, and pill CTAs. The page should feel like a premium farmers market flyer translated into a shoppable modern website.

Requirements:

- Use Miniature or a characterful editorial serif for hero headlines, product callouts, section headings, and brand-like display moments.
- Use Satoshi or a clean sans for navigation, labels, buttons, body text, prices, product metadata, and forms.
- Use Cream and Eggshell for most catalog surfaces.
- Use Black Bean for hero, footer, and occasional poster panels.
- Use Salsa red for the main headline, primary CTA, logo-like text, badges, and active states.
- Use Romaine, Mint Market, and Sunbeam as produce accents, price chips, sticker badges, and small moments of warmth.
- Build product cards with 28px radius, large food imagery, Miniature titles, Cocoa body text, and pill price or CTA elements.
- Add sticker-like promo badges sparingly. They can be circular, Sunbeam or Salsa filled, and slightly rotated.
- Keep forms rounded and friendly, with pill inputs and visible borders.
- Avoid cold blues, purple tech gradients, glassmorphism, sterile square enterprise cards, and all-sans minimalism.

Hero recipe:

Create a full-width Black Bean hero with a cream or Salsa Miniature headline at 72px to 96px, warm product imagery, one Salsa pill CTA, one secondary cream/outlined pill, and one small Sunbeam sticker badge. Keep the layout poster-like but still readable.

Product grid recipe:

Create a Cream or Eggshell section with a 2- or 3-column product grid. Each card should have 28px radius, a rounded product image, a Miniature product name, Cocoa description, a Mint Market or Sunbeam price chip, and a Salsa pill action.
