# Components

## Tomato Pill CTA

- Fill: Salsa.
- Text: Cream or Eggshell.
- Radius: `999px`.
- Padding: `14px 22px`.
- Type: Satoshi, `13px`, weight `700`, uppercase, tracking `0.08em`.
- Optional treatment: tiny Charcoal offset shadow for poster-sticker energy.

## Cream Navigation Bar

- Surface: Cream.
- Brand: Miniature or display serif in Salsa.
- Links: Satoshi uppercase, `13px`, Charcoal or Black Bean.
- CTA: Salsa pill.
- Divider: thin Black Bean or Cocoa line.
- Keep the nav friendly and compact, not corporate.

## Hero Poster Block

- Surface: Black Bean.
- Headline: Miniature, `72px` to `96px`, Salsa or Cream.
- Supporting text: Cocoa on cream subpanels or Eggshell on dark.
- Imagery: food, produce, packaging, or market photography.
- Accents: one Sunbeam or Salsa sticker badge.
- Layout: poster-like, full-width, and bold.

## Product Card

- Surface: Eggshell or Cream.
- Radius: `28px`.
- Padding: `28px`.
- Image radius: `32px`.
- Title: Miniature, `24px`, Black Bean or Salsa.
- Body: Cocoa, `15px`.
- Price chip: Mint Market or Sunbeam.
- CTA: Salsa pill.

## Market Badge Or Sticker

- Fill: Sunbeam, Salsa, Romaine, or Mint Market.
- Shape: circle, soft burst, or sticker patch.
- Type: Satoshi uppercase, `11px` to `13px`, bold, wide tracking.
- Rotation: optional `-4deg` to `4deg`.
- Use one or two per view, not everywhere.

## Editorial Split Section

- Surface: Cream.
- Layout: image plus copy.
- Headline: Miniature, `54px`.
- Body: Cocoa.
- Action: pill CTA or text link.
- Use for story, sourcing, seasonal drops, or product education.

## Price Chip

- Fill: Mint Market or Sunbeam.
- Text: Black Bean or Charcoal.
- Radius: `999px`.
- Padding: `8px 12px`.
- Type: Satoshi uppercase, `13px`, weight `700`.

## Illustrated Produce Accent

- Colors: Sunbeam, Salsa, Romaine.
- Shape: dots, small marks, simple produce-like details.
- Use as tiny punctuation near headlines or badges.
- Do not let accents compete with product imagery.

## Newsletter Field

- Input fill: Eggshell.
- Border: Black Bean or Cocoa.
- Radius: `999px`.
- Padding: `14px 18px`.
- Submit: Salsa pill.
- Type: Satoshi, `15px`.

## Footer Market Block

- Surface: Black Bean.
- Heading: Miniature in Cream or Salsa.
- Links: Satoshi uppercase in Cream.
- Body: Eggshell or Cream with reduced opacity.
- Accents: tiny Sunbeam or Romaine marks.
