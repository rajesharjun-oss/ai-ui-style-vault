# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Miniature or a characterful serif for big emotional text.
- Use Satoshi or a clean sans for practical UI and body text.
- Keep Cream and Eggshell as the main catalog paper surfaces.
- Use Salsa red for the logo voice, selected headlines, CTAs, and badges.
- Use Black Bean for dramatic poster-like hero and footer sections.
- Add produce accents with Romaine, Mint Market, and Sunbeam.
- Use rounded cards, pill controls, and sticker-like badges.
- Use real food, produce, packaging, market, or pantry imagery.
- Keep the layout playful but still aligned and shoppable.

## Don't

- Do not make the interface cold, blue, purple, or techy.
- Do not use glassmorphism or polished SaaS gradients.
- Do not make product cards sharp and sterile.
- Do not remove the expressive serif layer.
- Do not overuse badges until the composition feels chaotic.
- Do not use generic corporate stock photos.
- Do not flatten everything into black and white minimalism.
- Do not use tiny product imagery; food needs appetite and scale.
