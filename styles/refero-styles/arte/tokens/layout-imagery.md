# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Model

- Start with a dark poster hero or a cream editorial opening.
- Use a max-width around `1280px` for catalog grids.
- Alternate Black Bean poster blocks with Cream or Eggshell commerce sections.
- Use 2- or 3-column product grids with rounded cards.
- Layer one or two sticker badges near hero, product drops, or promo zones.
- Use editorial split sections for origin stories, seasonal drops, or menu notes.
- End with a dark market-style footer.

## Imagery

- Use real food, produce, packaging, pantry, or market photography.
- Favor bright natural light, rich reds, greens, yellows, and warm paper tones.
- Use cutout product or produce imagery where a catalog feeling is needed.
- Use rounded image crops rather than hard photo rectangles.
- Avoid corporate office photos, generic tech screenshots, sterile medical imagery, and dark luxury product lighting.

## Motion

- Motion can be playful but should stay controlled.
- Use small badge rotations, gentle image lifts, and quick color changes.
- Avoid bouncy cartoon motion or distracting continuous animation.
