# Typography

## Font Stacks

- Display serif: `Miniature`, `PP Eiko`, `Ogg`, `Georgia`, serif
- Sans/UI: `Satoshi`, `Inter`, `Arial`, `Helvetica`, sans-serif
- Weights: `400`, `500`, `700`

## Scale

| Purpose | Size | Line height | Weight | Tracking | Token |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Micro label | `11px` | `1.1` | `700` | `0.18em` | `--text-micro` |
| Navigation | `13px` | `1.1` | `700` | `0.08em` | `--text-nav` |
| Body | `15px` | `1.47` | `400` | `0` | `--text-body` |
| Body large | `18px` | `1.4` | `400` | `0` | `--text-body-lg` |
| Card title | `24px` | `1.08` | `400` | `-0.01em` | `--text-card-title` |
| Section heading | `54px` | `1.03` | `400` | `-0.02em` | `--text-section-heading` |
| Hero display | `96px` | `0.92` | `400` | `-0.04em` | `--text-hero-display` |

## Rules

- Use the display serif for hero headlines, product names, big labels, and editorial callouts.
- Use the sans for everything operational: nav, body, labels, buttons, forms, filters, and prices.
- Keep navigation uppercase and widely tracked.
- Let hero display text feel poster-like and compressed.
- Avoid all-sans minimalism; the serif voice is essential.
