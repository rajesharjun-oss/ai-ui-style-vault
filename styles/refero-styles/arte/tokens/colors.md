# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Black Bean | `#2e0401` | `--color-black-bean` | Near-black red-brown hero, footer, and serious poster frame |
| Salsa | `#ff2000` | `--color-salsa` | Primary tomato red for display text, CTAs, badges, and active states |
| Sunbeam | `#fbaf04` | `--color-sunbeam` | Warm yellow-orange for stickers, promo notes, dots, and hover warmth |
| Romaine | `#5f831d` | `--color-romaine` | Olive green for vegetable contrast, secondary accents, and links |
| Cream | `#f7f1e4` | `--color-cream` | Warm paper page background and editorial sections |
| Eggshell | `#fff9f1` | `--color-eggshell` | Light product cards, menu panels, and form fields |
| Cocoa | `#754b32` | `--color-cocoa` | Brown body text and warm supporting copy on light surfaces |
| Tomato Wash | `#f2634c` | `--color-tomato-wash` | Softer red panels, borders, and editorial callouts |
| Mint Market | `#d6e7c4` | `--color-mint-market` | Pale green tags, price chips, and section breaks |
| Charcoal | `#111111` | `--color-charcoal` | High-contrast text and small icon marks |

## Usage

- Use Cream as the main paper background.
- Use Black Bean for poster-like hero and footer sections.
- Use Salsa as the loud brand/action color, not as every surface.
- Use Sunbeam for small sticker and market-note moments.
- Use Romaine and Mint Market to bring produce contrast into tags, chips, and small accents.
- Use Cocoa for friendly readable copy on cream surfaces.
