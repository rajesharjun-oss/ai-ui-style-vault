# arte* Design Reference

## Essence

arte* feels like a farmers market poster turned into a modern product catalog. It is warm, punchy, and food-forward: red serif display type, cream paper backgrounds, rounded product cards, produce-green accents, and sticker-like badges. It should feel tactile and appetizing without becoming messy.

## Color System

The palette is tomato, paper, produce, and dark sauce. Black Bean creates dramatic hero and footer panels. Salsa is the loud brand red for headlines, CTAs, and badges. Sunbeam adds yellow market warmth. Romaine and Mint Market create produce contrast and lighter tag surfaces. Cream and Eggshell keep the commerce areas warm and readable. Cocoa carries friendly body copy, while Charcoal keeps small marks crisp.

## Typography

Use Miniature for logo-like display moments, hero statements, product names, and editorial headings. If Miniature is unavailable, choose a characterful editorial serif such as PP Eiko, Ogg, Georgia, or a similar high-contrast serif. Use Satoshi or a clean grotesk for nav, buttons, labels, body, cards, prices, and forms. The contrast between expressive serif and modern sans is central to the style.

## Shape And Space

The geometry is round and market-friendly. CTAs, tags, price chips, and inputs are pills. Cards use 28px radius, images use 32px, and large sections can use 40px. Spacing is roomy enough for product photography and oversized display type, with 112px section gaps and 28px card padding.

## Layout Rhythm

Alternate dark poster panels with cream commerce sections. Use a full-width hero with oversized display type and product imagery. Follow with rounded product grids, editorial split sections, promo sticker modules, and a dark footer. The page can be playful, but the grid should still feel intentional and shoppable.

## Components

- Tomato pill CTA with Salsa fill, cream text, 999px radius, uppercase sans label, and optional offset poster shadow.
- Cream navigation bar with Salsa serif brand, uppercase sans nav links, and pill CTA.
- Hero poster block with Black Bean surface, oversized red or cream serif display type, product imagery, and small sticker accents.
- Product card with Eggshell or Cream fill, 28px radius, food image, Miniature title, Cocoa body text, and price chip.
- Market badge or sticker with Sunbeam, Salsa, or Romaine fill, circular or burst-like shape, uppercase sans label, and slight rotation.
- Editorial split section with product photography, large serif headline, warm body copy, and pill action.
- Newsletter field with Eggshell fill, Black Bean border, pill input, and Salsa submit button.
- Footer market block with Black Bean background, Cream and Salsa type, link columns, and small produce accents.

## Implementation Direction

Start with a cream paper canvas and a dramatic Black Bean hero or footer. Use Salsa red for brand voice and CTAs, but balance it with Cream, Eggshell, Romaine, and Mint Market. Keep product cards rounded, photography bright, and badges playful but not chaotic. Avoid cold blue tech styling, glass effects, sterile square cards, and generic minimalist black-white layouts.
