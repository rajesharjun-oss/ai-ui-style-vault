# Typography

## Font Families

| Role | Font | Token | Fallback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Telemetry | Arbeit Technik | `--font-arbeit-technik` | JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono, ui-monospace, monospace |
| Display | Inline VF | `--font-inline-vf` | Departure Mono, VT323, monospace |
| Body/prose | Arbeit Contrast | `--font-arbeit-contrast` | Inter, Sohne, Untitled Sans, system-ui, sans-serif |

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Token |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| caption | 12px | 400 | 15px | -0.6px | `--text-caption` |
| body | 16px | 400 | 20px | 0 | `--text-body` |
| subheading | 30px | 400 | 34px | 0 | `--text-subheading` |
| display | 80px | 400 | 56px | 0 | `--text-display` |

## Rules

- Use weight 400 at every size.
- Telemetry labels are always 12px.
- Hero display is 80px with compressed 56px line-height.
- Keep all line heights at or below 1.25.
- Use tight negative tracking for labels.
- Do not introduce bold, semibold, or heavy display weights.
