# Colors

## Neutrals

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight Carbon | `#12130f` | `--color-midnight-carbon` | Full-bleed page canvas and flush content surface. |
| Bone Glow | `#e4dfda` | `--color-bone-glow` | Primary text, headings, labels, buttons, and body copy. |
| Charcoal Vein | `#3c3c38` | `--color-charcoal-vein` | Subdued dividers or chrome only when absolutely needed. |

## Accent

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rose Quartz Bloom | `#f5c2c8` | `--color-rose-quartz-bloom` | 3D artifact edge-lighting only. |

## Usage

- The UI is basically two-color: Midnight Carbon and Bone Glow.
- Rose Quartz Bloom must not appear in buttons, links, borders, icons, or labels.
- Avoid pure white and pure black.
- Charcoal Vein is support chrome, not a card surface.
