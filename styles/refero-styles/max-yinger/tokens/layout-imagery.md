# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Full-bleed edge-to-edge layout.
- No max-width container.
- Top-left: masthead.
- Bottom-left: time readout, telemetry labels, and bio.
- Bottom-right: social pill links.
- Center: single 3D artifact.
- Section spacing is implicit and sparse.
- The composition is a corner-anchored z-pattern, not a column layout.

## Imagery

- Use one central 3D rendered object.
- Prefer isometric cubes, chrome/bone forms, or geometric artifacts.
- Bone Glow is the dominant material color.
- Rose Quartz Bloom may appear only as soft edge-lighting.
- Avoid photography, product screenshots, flat illustrations, icons, and extra visual assets.

## Composition Checks

- Is the page still one dark plane?
- Are content clusters anchored to corners?
- Is the 3D artifact the only depth source?
- Are gaps very compact?
- Is Rose Quartz absent from the UI controls?
