# Guidelines

## Do

- Use `#e4dfda` for all text and `#12130f` for all backgrounds.
- Use 9999px radius on social buttons, tags, and status indicators.
- Use 2px radius only on underlined inline links.
- Use 4px element gaps and 12px cluster padding.
- Use Inline VF-style display at 80px with a 56px line-height.
- Use Arbeit Technik-style 12px labels with -0.6px tracking.
- Push content to viewport edges and corners.

## Don't

- Do not introduce new accent colors.
- Do not use Rose Quartz Bloom on UI chrome.
- Do not use box shadows, drop shadows, or CSS glow.
- Do not use line-height above 1.25.
- Do not center content in a max-width container.
- Do not add borders to cards or content blocks.
- Do not introduce bold or semibold weights.
- Do not use pure white or pure black.

## Accessibility Notes

- Very compressed display type can be hard to read; use it for short readouts only.
- Keep body copy short and high contrast.
- If interactive states are needed, use underline, motion, or focus outlines without introducing new colors.
- Keep touch targets large even when visual gaps are compact.
