# Components

## Highlighter Green Action Button

- Background: Highlighter Green
- Text: Typesetter Ink
- Font: TWK Lausanne
- Size: 11px
- Weight: 550
- Text: uppercase
- Tracking: 0.01em
- Padding: 20px vertical, 30px horizontal
- Radius: 5px
- Shadow: green-tinted `--shadow-lg`

## Ghost Outline Button

- Background: transparent
- Border: 1px solid Bone White
- Text: Bone White
- Font: TWK Lausanne
- Size: 11px
- Weight: 550
- Text: uppercase
- Radius: 10px
- Padding: tall editorial menu-style padding

## Underlined Text Link

- Background: none
- Border: none
- Font: Times
- Size: 16px
- Weight: 400
- Decoration: 1px underline close to baseline
- Hover: no weight or color change

## Editorial Photo Insert

- Radius: 14px
- Layout: small rectangle floating inline between display type lines
- Treatment: grayscale-green filter
- Grid: avoid standard grids

## Stat Callout

- Color: Newsprint Gray
- Scale: display or oversized
- Decoration: none
- Purpose: editorial metric, not marketing badge

## Category Tag

- Color: Muted Sage
- Font: TWK Lausanne
- Size: 14px
- Weight: 350
- Tracking: 0.01em
- Background: none
- Border: none

## Navigation Wordmark

- Font: TWK Lausanne
- Treatment: bold wordmark with 2px Highlighter Green underline under the first word
- Icon: none
- Frame: none

## Full-Bleed Accent Band

- Background: Highlighter Green
- Width: edge-to-edge
- Height: poster-scale
- Content: minimal wordmark or single letter
- Placement: before footer

## Dark Editorial Section

- Background: Press Black
- Heading: Bone White, large TWK Lausanne
- Body: Bone White, lightweight body text
- Actions: ghost buttons instead of filled green when the surface is already dramatic

## Footer

- Background: Press Black
- Text: Bone White
- Labels: TWK Lausanne small caps
- Ending: full-bleed Highlighter Green band
