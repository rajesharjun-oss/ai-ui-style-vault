# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bone White | `#fafffa` | `--color-bone-white` | Page canvas, cards, primary text on dark sections |
| Press Black | `#121613` | `--color-press-black` | Headline color, footer background, dominant dark surface |
| Typesetter Ink | `#000000` | `--color-typesetter-ink` | Primary headings, body text, icon fills on light surfaces |
| Slate Verdant | `#232924` | `--color-slate-verdant` | Secondary dark surface and bordered sections |
| Newsprint Gray | `#516254` | `--color-newsprint-gray` | Muted captions, helper text, editorial data |
| Muted Sage | `#c8d2c8` | `--color-muted-sage` | Light text on dark surfaces and inverse labels |
| Highlighter Green | `#2bee4b` | `--color-highlighter-green` | Primary action fill, active underline, full-bleed footer band |
| Shadow Moss | `#93b799` | `--color-shadow-moss` | Green-tinted action button shadow |
| Echo Green | `#c4e4c9` | `--color-echo-green` | Low-frequency green-gray support accent |

## Usage

Highlighter Green is the only saturated color. Use it as a marker-like accent for primary action fill, active underline, and the closing footer band. Do not introduce another saturated accent. Do not use pure white for the page canvas.
