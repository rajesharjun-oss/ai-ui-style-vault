# Guidelines

## Do

- Set the hero headline in PP Mondwest at 165px to 295px.
- Use line-height 0.9 and -0.04em tracking for giant serif display type.
- Use Highlighter Green fill with green-tinted shadow for the primary action.
- Keep the canvas Bone White.
- Use TWK Lausanne 11px uppercase micro-labels with positive tracking.
- Apply the grayscale-green photo filter to all photography.
- Float image tiles inline between lines of display type.
- Anchor the page with a full-bleed green band before the footer.
- Use Press Black for dark editorial sections and footer.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce a second saturated accent color.
- Do not use 9999px radius on the primary action.
- Do not set body copy larger than 18px.
- Do not use box-shadow on cards or content blocks.
- Do not render photographs in full color.
- Do not use sans-serif for all display headlines.
- Do not exceed 1400px content width.
- Do not use pure white as the page canvas.
