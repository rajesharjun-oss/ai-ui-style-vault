# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Canvas: Bone White.
- Max width: 1400px.
- Section gap: 80px.
- Hero: huge editorial display type.
- Images: inline photo tiles between type lines.
- Dark sections: Press Black with Bone White type.
- Footer: Press Black followed by full-bleed Highlighter Green band.
- UI chrome: minimal, label-driven, hairline-based.

## Imagery

Use:

- Editorial portraits.
- Founder, market, or institutional photography.
- Cropped rectangular image inserts.
- Grayscale-green treatment.
- Photos that interrupt the text flow.

Avoid:

- Full-color photography.
- Standard image card grids.
- Decorative gradients.
- Hero background photo treatments.
- Stock SaaS visuals.

## Photo Filter

```css
filter: grayscale(1) saturate(1) invert(0.27) sepia(0.07) saturate(10.67) hue-rotate(80deg) brightness(1.02) contrast(0.83);
```
