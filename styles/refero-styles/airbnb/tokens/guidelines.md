# Guidelines

## Do

- Set body text at 14px/400 in Hof with 1.43 line-height.
- Use Rausch only for the logo, search submit, active wishlist hearts, and one primary action per surface.
- Give listing card images 12px radius and full-bleed image treatment.
- Use 9999px radius for the search bar, pill buttons, hearts, avatars, and icon controls.
- Use 12px for listing cards and 8px for ghost buttons.
- Set section headings at 22px/500 with -0.020em tracking.
- Place the page canvas at `#f7f7f7` and cards at `#ffffff`.
- Keep property imagery full-bleed within its card frame.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce additional accent colors beyond Rausch.
- Do not use shadows on listing cards.
- Do not set body text below 12px.
- Do not use inconsistent corner radii.
- Do not use borders on listing cards.
- Do not apply Rausch to headings or decorative text.
- Do not use 700 weight for body or metadata.
- Do not use illustrations, 3D renders, or abstract graphics.
