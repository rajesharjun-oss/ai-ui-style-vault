# AI Implementation Prompt

Build a severe dark research or consulting interface inspired by ThoughtLab.

Use pure black as the page canvas, white typography, one crimson action color, Sui as the only type family, monumental uppercase display type, transparent cards, hairline dividers, square buttons, and no visual decoration beyond typography and thin rules.

Requirements:

- Use Sui if licensed. Otherwise use Inter, Helvetica Neue, Arial, or another clean product sans.
- Use one font family across the entire interface.
- Use huge uppercase display type at 78px, 182px, or 319px depending on viewport and section role.
- Keep body and UI text small, usually 12px to 18px.
- Use Crimson Signal only for primary actions, active states, and tiny emphasis marks.
- Use transparent cards with hairline borders instead of filled elevated cards.
- Use 0px radius for buttons, inputs, cards, images, and sections.
- Do not use shadows, gradients, blur effects, glassmorphism, rounded cards, or extra accent colors.
- Use article grids, research modules, newsletter capture, and stat panels as editorial blocks.
- Keep the composition sparse, high contrast, and left aligned.

Hero recipe:

Create a full black hero with a minimal header, a giant uppercase Sui headline, White Mist supporting copy, and one square Crimson Signal CTA. Add a thin white hairline divider below the hero. Keep the right side mostly empty or use one transparent research module.

Card recipe:

Create a transparent topic card with 1px White Hairline border, 0px radius, 24px padding, a small uppercase label, white title, White Mist body text, and a crimson active or hover state. No shadow and no background fill.
