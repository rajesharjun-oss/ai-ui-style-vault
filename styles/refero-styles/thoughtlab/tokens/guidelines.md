# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Black Void as the dominant page surface.
- Use Sui or one clean sans family everywhere.
- Use huge uppercase type for the main visual energy.
- Use Crimson Signal only for actions, active state, and small emphasis.
- Use transparent cards and hairline borders.
- Keep every corner square.
- Use negative space and white hairlines for hierarchy.
- Keep content left aligned and editorial.

## Don't

- Do not add extra accent colors.
- Do not use rounded corners, pills, or soft cards.
- Do not use shadows, glows, gradients, bokeh, or blur effects.
- Do not introduce serif, mono, or expressive secondary typefaces.
- Do not use decorative illustrations, mascots, or stock-photo hero scenes.
- Do not fill the page with nested SaaS cards.
- Do not soften the black canvas with warm off-white or beige.
