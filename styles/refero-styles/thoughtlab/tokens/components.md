# Components

## Minimal Header

- Surface: Black Void.
- Height: `72px` to `88px`.
- Logo: White or Crimson Signal.
- Links: White or White Mist.
- Action: Crimson Signal text or square button.
- Border: optional White Hairline bottom rule.

## Hero Text Stack

- Background: Black Void.
- Text: uppercase Sui display.
- Size: `182px` to `319px` on desktop.
- Line height: `0.75` to `0.82`.
- Color: White or Crimson Signal for one word.
- Layout: left aligned or edge-aware.

## Crimson Action Button

- Fill: Crimson Signal.
- Text: White or Black Void.
- Radius: `0px`.
- Padding: `14px 22px`.
- Font: Sui, `16px`, weight `600`.
- Shadow: none.

## Transparent Topic Card

- Fill: transparent.
- Border: `1px solid` White Hairline.
- Radius: `0px`.
- Padding: `24px`.
- Title: White, `30px`, weight `600`.
- Body: White Mist.
- Active or hover: Glass Edge border or Crimson Signal text.

## Hairline Email Input

- Fill: Black Void or transparent.
- Border: `1px solid` Glass Edge.
- Radius: `0px`.
- Padding: `16px 18px`.
- Text: White.
- Placeholder: White Mist.
- Focus: Crimson Signal border.

## Editorial Article Grid

- Surface: Black Void.
- Cards: transparent with hairline separators.
- Title: White.
- Excerpt: White Mist.
- Meta: caption scale, uppercase optional.
- Dividers: White Hairline.

## Large Section Number

- Type: Sui, uppercase or numeric.
- Size: `78px` or larger.
- Color: Crimson Signal or White.
- Use as editorial marker, not decorative badge.

## Footer Link Block

- Surface: Black Void.
- Text: White and White Mist.
- Links: compact rows.
- Dividers: White Hairline.
- Accent: Crimson Signal for active or important links only.

## White Hairline Divider

- Thickness: `1px`.
- Color: White Hairline.
- Use between hero, article grids, forms, and footer.

## Filter Nav Link Row

- Text: Sui, `14px` to `18px`.
- Active: Crimson Signal.
- Inactive: White Mist.
- Shape: no pill container.
- Divider: optional hairline below.
