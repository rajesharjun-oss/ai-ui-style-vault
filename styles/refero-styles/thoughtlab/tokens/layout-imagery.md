# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Model

- Use a continuous Black Void canvas.
- Keep the header minimal and mostly text-based.
- Use massive display type as the hero image.
- Place content in stark editorial grids with hairline separators.
- Use transparent topic cards for research modules.
- Use square email capture and form elements.
- Use Crimson Signal as a small line of force, not a full palette.
- Let section gaps be large enough for the page to feel executive and deliberate.

## Imagery

- Typography is the main imagery.
- If images are used, keep them monochrome, high contrast, rectangular, and research/editorial in tone.
- Prefer diagrams, charts, or publication covers over lifestyle stock.
- Avoid decorative 3D, gradients, colorful abstract art, and generic business photography.

## Motion

- Motion should feel precise and quiet.
- Use type reveals, hairline draws, opacity changes, and crisp section transitions.
- Avoid bouncy easing, playful loops, and decorative particle animation.
