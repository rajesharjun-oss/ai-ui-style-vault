# Implementation Prompt

Build in the T1 Energy style: a quiet industrial website on warm vellum with monochrome carbon typography, whisper-light display text, large rounded factory imagery, and no decorative color.

Use these rules:

- Canvas: `#f0efe9`.
- Surface: `#ffffff`.
- Text, borders, and controls: `#322d2a`.
- Footer and deepest dark layer: `#0f0e12`.
- Muted text: `#8b8b8b`.
- Pure black: `#000000` only for SVG icon fills.
- Font: T1 Sans, with Inter or Sohne fallback.
- Display: 52px, weight 300, line-height 1, tracking 0.52px.
- Body: 16px, weight 400, line-height 1.4, tracking 0.16px.
- Labels: 12px, weight 400, line-height 1.3, tracking 0.12px.
- Universal tracking: 0.01em.
- Max width: 1200px.
- Section gap: 48px.
- Card padding: 22px.
- Element gap: 8px.
- Image/card radius: 80px.
- Interactive pill radius: 100px.
- Nav radius: 16px.
- Body container radius: 12px.
- Small radius: 8px.
- No shadows.
- No gradients.

Design structure:

1. Full-viewport factory photo hero.
2. Bottom-left white display headline and Carbon Warm filled pill button.
3. Floating Carbon Warm pill navigation near the top.
4. Vellum sections with section labels that start with a 4px square.
5. Two-column industrial image pairs, each with 80px radius.
6. Technology split section with product render and accordion list.
7. Onyx Depth footer.

Avoid:

- Accent colors.
- Pure black text.
- Bold headlines.
- Card shadows.
- Image frames.
- Decorative icons in labels.
- Lifestyle photography.
- Abstract illustrations.

The result should feel like a factory blueprint printed on warm vellum.
