## Capture Notes

The Refero page describes T1 Energy as a warm monochrome manufacturing system. It uses a vellum canvas, carbon text, dark pill navigation, 80px image radius, and ultra-light 52px display type.

The AI-useful pieces to preserve are:

- Vellum `#f0efe9` is the page canvas.
- Carbon Warm `#322d2a` owns text, borders, buttons, and dark pills.
- Onyx Depth `#0f0e12` is reserved for the footer or deepest layer.
- Pure Black is for SVG icon fills only, not text or backgrounds.
- T1 Sans weight 300 at 52px is the display signature.
- Body and labels use T1 Sans weight 400.
- Universal tracking is 0.01em.
- Image containers use 80px radius and no border or shadow.
- Interactive buttons use 100px pill radius.
- Section labels use a 4px Carbon Warm square before uppercase text.
