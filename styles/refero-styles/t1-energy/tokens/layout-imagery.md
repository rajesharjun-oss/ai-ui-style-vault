# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use 1200px centered content.
- Let hero sections break out full-bleed.
- Keep the nav as a floating dark pill, not a full-width bar.
- Use 48px vertical section gaps.
- Alternate single-column specification blocks and two-column splits.
- Put product renders next to accordion stacks.
- Use image pairs with 80px rounded containers.
- Footer is the only full-width Onyx layer.

## Imagery

Use:

- Factory floors.
- Robotic arms.
- Solar cell production lines.
- Industrial documentary photography.
- Product renders isolated on vellum.

Avoid:

- Lifestyle photography.
- Abstract illustrations.
- Duotone treatments.
- Gradients.
- Image captions.
- Borders or frames around photos.

Photographs should fill their containers edge-to-edge.
