# Components

## Pill Navigation Bar

Carbon Warm horizontal pill anchored near the top-right. Use 16px radius, 36px vertical padding, 24px horizontal padding, Paper White 14px links, and 24px gaps.

## Logo Mark

Geometric T1 wordmark in Paper White on the dark navigation surface. No tagline and no decorative element.

## Filled Primary Button

Carbon Warm fill, Paper White text, 100px radius, 18px 22px padding, T1 Sans 14px weight 400. No border and no shadow.

## Ghost Outlined Button

Transparent fill, 1px Carbon Warm border, Carbon Warm text, 100px radius, 18px 22px padding.

## Section Label

4px square in Carbon Warm followed by uppercase 11px to 12px text, weight 400, tracking 0.12em. Do not use icons.

## Display Headline

T1 Sans 52px weight 300, line-height 1, 0.52px tracking, Carbon Warm on Vellum or Paper White on hero photography.

## Case Study Card

Carbon Warm surface, 12px radius, 16px padding, white label and short headline, optional map thumbnail.

## Image Card

Full-bleed image with 80px radius. No caption, no border, no shadow, no inner padding.

## Accordion Item

Full-width row with Carbon Warm text label and 40px circular icon button. Icon button has Paper White fill, 1px Carbon Warm border, 100px radius, plus or minus mark. Rows use Carbon Warm hairline dividers.

## Hero Overlay

Full-viewport industrial photograph with bottom-left 52px headline and Carbon Warm filled button. No gradient or overlay.

## Footer

Full-width Onyx Depth surface with Paper White text and restrained 14px typography.
