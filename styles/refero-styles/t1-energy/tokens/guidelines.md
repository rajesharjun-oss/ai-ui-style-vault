# Guidelines

## Do

- Use T1 Sans weight 300 at 52px for all display headlines.
- Use Carbon Warm for text, borders, and filled interactive elements.
- Use 80px radius on all image containers.
- Use 100px radius on interactive buttons.
- Maintain 48px section gaps.
- Use 22px card padding.
- Prefix every section label with a 4px Carbon Warm square.
- Keep the palette to vellum canvas, white surfaces, and carbon text.
- Set display headline line-height exactly to 1.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce accent colors.
- Do not use gradients or decorative hues.
- Do not use Pure Black for text, backgrounds, or borders.
- Do not apply box shadows to cards, buttons, or navigation.
- Do not use bold or semibold headlines.
- Do not add icons, illustrations, or emoji to section labels.
- Do not frame or pad photographs.
- Do not use sharp corners below 8px.
