# Typography

## Font

- Primary: T1 Sans
- Fallback: Inter or Sohne
- Weights: 300, 400
- Universal tracking: 0.01em

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Letter Spacing | Token |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Label | 12px | 400 | 1.3 | 0.12px | `--text-label` |
| Body Small | 14px | 400 | 1.3 | 0.14px | `--text-body-sm` |
| Body | 16px | 400 | 1.4 | 0.16px | `--text-body` |
| Subheading | 22px | 400 | 1.3 | 0.22px | `--text-subheading` |
| Heading | 32px | 300 | 1.2 | 0.32px | `--text-heading` |
| Display | 52px | 300 | 1 | 0.52px | `--text-display` |

## Typography Rules

- Use T1 Sans everywhere.
- Use weight 300 for display and headline moments.
- Use weight 400 for body, labels, nav, and buttons.
- Do not use bold or semibold.
- Keep display line-height at exactly 1.
- Keep body line-height restrained and technical.
