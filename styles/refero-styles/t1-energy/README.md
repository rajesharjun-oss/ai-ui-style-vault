## Use This When

Use this style for industrial manufacturing, energy infrastructure, climate tech, solar products, factory automation, robotics, technical specification pages, and serious B2B sites that should feel engineered and quiet.

## Core Idea

T1 Energy is nearly colorless by design. A warm Vellum canvas, Carbon Warm typography, whisper-light display type, and very large rounded image containers create a specification-sheet rhythm. The absence of color is the brand; imagery and geometry carry the industrial credibility.
