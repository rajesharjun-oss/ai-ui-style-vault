# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a centered 1200px max-width track.
- Keep section gaps around 96px.
- Hero is left-aligned text plus a full-width floating dashboard preview.
- Use partner logos and trust/rating lines before the preview.
- Follow with testimonial rows and feature sections.
- Keep pacing editorial rather than dense.

## Imagery

- Product UI screenshots are the main imagery.
- Dashboard preview should be muted with grayscale and slight contrast reduction.
- Use a grayscale line-art mascot sticker once per section at most.
- Avoid lifestyle photography, team shots, and office/environment imagery.
- Use outline icons in Ink Black or Cyan Signal.

## Composition Rules

- The dashboard is the hero object.
- Use one cyan phrase to punctuate each headline.
- Let borders, not fills, create structure.
- Keep most surfaces quiet and neutral.
- Use cyan only for action and selected emphasis.
