# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Roobert-style headings at weight 400.
- Use Stone Canvas as the page background.
- Use Pure White only for card surfaces.
- Use exactly one cyan highlight span per headline.
- Use 1px Stone Border as the primary structural device.
- Keep buttons pill-shaped with 8px by 16px padding.
- Set body copy at 14px Inter with 1.64 line-height.
- Use mascot stickers sparingly.

## Don't

- Do not introduce new accent colors.
- Do not use heavy drop shadows on content cards.
- Do not set headlines in Inter.
- Do not use Pure White as the page background.
- Do not fill primary buttons with dark or neutral colors.
- Do not add gradients, glassmorphism, or decorative color washes.
- Do not stack multiple cyan highlight spans in one headline.

## Accessibility Notes

- Check Pure White text on Cyan Signal for CTA contrast.
- Keep Warm Gray body text at comfortable sizes.
- Use visible focus rings with Cyan Signal.
- Do not rely only on cyan for meaning in analytics screenshots.
