# Guidelines

## Do

- Use `#8052ff` exclusively for filled action buttons.
- Set major headlines at weight `400`, not bold.
- Use weight `200` for 18px body text.
- Keep every major section on pure `#000000`.
- Apply tight negative tracking on display sizes 42px and above.
- Use `24px` radius for buttons, cards/portraits, and nav elements.
- Let the particle constellation be the hero visual.
- Use amber `#ffb829` for emphasis and special links.
- Keep layouts spacious and low-density.

## Don't

- Do not use filled violet for large background blocks or full sections.
- Do not set body text at weight `400` when the ultra-light feel matters.
- Do not introduce card containers with borders, shadows, or background fills.
- Do not use default browser link blue.
- Do not add gradients to ordinary UI components.
- Do not use multiple filled buttons in close proximity.
- Do not introduce product screenshots, dashboard frames, or lifestyle imagery into the hero.
- Do not turn the page into a dense SaaS grid; it should stay sparse and editorial.
