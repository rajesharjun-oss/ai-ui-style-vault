# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Rhythm

- Use full-bleed sections on a pure black canvas.
- Center content at about `1280px` max width.
- Hero layout is an asymmetric two-column split: giant headline/body/CTA on one side, particle visual on the other.
- Alternate later sections with text-left/visual-right and visual-left/text-right rhythm.
- Keep vertical gaps between `60px` and `120px`.
- Use one or two major elements per viewport.
- Avoid card grids, pricing tables, dense dashboards, and multi-column feature walls.

## Imagery

- The primary image is a procedural particle constellation, not a screenshot.
- Particles should be tiny outlined triangles with saturated chromatic color.
- Team portraits can appear as large rounded rectangles.
- Avoid stock photography, lifestyle imagery, product screenshots, 3D renders, and generic illustration.
- Gradients belong only in brand marks or particle visuals, not core UI controls.

## Visual Hierarchy

- Main hierarchy: display type scale.
- Secondary hierarchy: white versus gray text.
- Accent hierarchy: violet CTA and amber label/link details.
- Spatial hierarchy: large empty black regions.
- Avoid borders, dividers, shadows, and background panels.
