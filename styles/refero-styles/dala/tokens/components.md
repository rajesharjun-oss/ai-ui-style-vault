# Components

## Primary Action Button

- **Role:** The singular filled CTA.
- **Style:** `#8052ff` background, white text, about `24px` radius, compact vertical padding, uppercase label.
- **Type:** PPNeueMontreal `14px`, weight `400` or `600`, positive tracking around `0.025em`.
- **Rule:** Use one primary filled button per view or section. Do not use other saturated fills for buttons.

## Ghost Text Button

- **Role:** Secondary nav or inline action.
- **Style:** No background, no border, white or ash-gray text.
- **Type:** PPNeueMontreal `14px`, weight `400`.
- **Behavior:** Let hierarchy come from text color and type treatment, not containers.

## Logo Lockup

- **Role:** Header brand mark and wordmark.
- **Style:** Small angular/triangular mark in violet with a possible teal gradient stop, paired with a white wordmark.
- **Rule:** For original work, create a new mark; do not reuse Dala's logo.

## Team Member Card

- **Role:** Portrait, name, role, and social links.
- **Style:** No background, border, or shadow. Large rounded-rectangle portrait, role label above or near name, simple inline social icons.
- **Type:** Role label can use uppercase violet text; name uses larger white display text.

## Carousel Navigation Dot

- **Role:** Slide indicator.
- **Style:** Small active dot in `#8052ff`, minimal spacing, no outer container.
- **Rule:** Keep the indicator almost invisible until needed.

## Hero Constellation Visualization

- **Role:** Signature hero/brand imagery.
- **Style:** Dense field of tiny triangular particles in violet, amber, teal, magenta, blue, and related chromatic tones.
- **Composition:** Particles form an organic brain/cloud/constellation shape, with lower-density particles drifting around it.
- **Rule:** Build an original particle visualization. Do not copy source screenshots or exact shapes.

## Section Headline Block

- **Role:** Oversized headline and body composition.
- **Style:** Two-column asymmetric layout on black. Large white headline on one side, ultra-light body copy on the other or below.
- **Details:** Small uppercase amber labels can introduce supporting copy.
- **Rule:** Do not wrap content in cards. The text floats on black.

## Navigation Bar

- **Role:** Minimal top site navigation.
- **Style:** Transparent on black, logo left, a few uppercase links, violet CTA on the right.
- **State:** Inactive links use ash gray; active/hover uses white.
- **Rule:** No blur, border, sidebar, or mega-menu.

## Ambient Particle Field

- **Role:** Low-density background atmosphere.
- **Style:** Small outlined triangles in saturated chromatic colors, used sparingly outside the main hero.
- **Rule:** Particles support the main visual; they should not make the page noisy.
