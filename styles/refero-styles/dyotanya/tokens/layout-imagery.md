# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Warm Paper full-page canvas.
- Content wrapper around 1200px.
- Minimal navigation, often a circular top-right menu trigger.
- Full-width hero statement with flowing text.
- Staggered portfolio cards rather than rigid equal columns.
- Oversized type and SVG lines may break the strict content frame.
- Use 80px section gaps.
- Avoid hard section dividers.

## Hero Recipe

1. Warm Paper background.
2. Minimal top navigation.
3. 80px serif statement with roman and italic emphasis.
4. Inline circular portrait or project image.
5. Thin black SVG line weaving between words.
6. Parenthetical Manrope caption.
7. Large pill CTA below or nearby.

## Imagery

- Use circular portraits and circular project thumbnails.
- Keep photography muted or desaturated.
- Images should feel personal and portfolio-specific.
- Do not use SaaS mockups or generic stock scenes.
- Do not use full-bleed hero photography.

## Decorative Motif

The signature motif is a 1.5px black hand-drawn SVG line. Use it as a layout connector between text fragments, images, cards, and section markers. Curves should feel human, not mathematically perfect.
