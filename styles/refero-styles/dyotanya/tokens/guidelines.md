# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Warm Paper as the base canvas.
- Use Simeiz-style serif for all display text above 20px.
- Mix roman and italic words inside headings.
- Use Manrope for functional UI.
- Use 3000px pill radius for large capsule actions.
- Use 1.5px Dusty Sky borders on cards and inputs.
- Use hard offset shadows on cards and buttons.
- Use inline black SVG squiggles as connective layout material.
- Use circular portraits and project images.

## Do Not

- Do not use pure white as the page background.
- Do not use soft drop shadows, blur, glow, or glass.
- Do not use Coral Link as a fill or border color.
- Do not use Manrope for large display headings.
- Do not use sharp corners on interactive elements.
- Do not apply multiple accent colors to the same component.
- Do not make the hero a rigid left-text and right-image layout.

## Accessibility Notes

- Keep coral links underlined or otherwise identifiable beyond color.
- Make focus states visible on dusty-blue bordered inputs and buttons.
- Do not rely on SVG line decorations to communicate essential information.
- Ensure 80px display text reflows cleanly on mobile.
