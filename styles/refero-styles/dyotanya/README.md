## Core Signature

Dyotanya feels like a designer sketchbook on warm paper. It combines huge thin serif headlines, mixed roman and italic emphasis, hand-drawn black SVG squiggles, dusty-blue borders, coral inline links, circular portraits, and hard offset shadows that make cards look pasted onto the page.
