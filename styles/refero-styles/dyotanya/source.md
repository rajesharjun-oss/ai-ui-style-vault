## Source Summary

The source describes Dyotanya as a warm off-white editorial sketchbook. Its strongest identifiers are oversized Simeiz serif headings, mixed roman and italic words, Manrope for functional UI, dusty blue borders, coral link text, circular image crops, hand-drawn black SVG lines, and hard offset shadows instead of blur-based elevation.
