# Guidelines

## Do

- Use only black, white, and muted gray.
- Use PolySans or a similar single sans family.
- Use regular weight throughout.
- Make display type huge, tight, and confident.
- Use hairline dividers for structure.
- Use square photography with intentional overlap.
- Keep labels uppercase and widely tracked.
- Keep copy sparse and editorial.
- Let negative space carry the layout.

## Don't

- Do not add a colored accent or CTA.
- Do not use gradients, bokeh, glows, or decorative effects.
- Do not use shadows for depth.
- Do not create rounded cards or soft panels.
- Do not use multiple font families.
- Do not introduce playful icons, emoji, mascots, or illustrations.
- Do not make the page dense with filters, dashboards, or form controls.
- Do not over-explain the work with long paragraphs.
