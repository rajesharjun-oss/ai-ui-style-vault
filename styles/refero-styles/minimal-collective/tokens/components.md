# Components

## Minimal Top Navigation

- Surface: transparent or Black.
- Text: White for primary links, Ash for secondary links.
- Type: PolySans, `13px`, uppercase, tracking `0.10em`.
- Layout: logo or wordmark left, sparse links right or corner-pinned.
- Divider: optional Line hairline.

## Giant Hero Headline

- Font: PolySans regular.
- Size: `120px` to `180px` desktop.
- Line height: `0.82` to `0.86`.
- Tracking: `-0.06em` to `-0.07em`.
- Color: White.
- Keep text short and architectural.

## Overlapping Editorial Image Stack

- Images: square or severe rectangle.
- Radius: `0px`.
- Shadow: none.
- Position: overlap by composition, not by messy collage.
- Caption: Ash, small uppercase label.

## Ghost Pill Button

- Fill: transparent.
- Border: `1px solid` White.
- Text: White.
- Radius: `999px`.
- Padding: `10px 18px`.
- Type: PolySans, `13px`, uppercase, tracking `0.10em`.
- Hover: invert to White fill and Black text, or increase border opacity.

## Category Badge

- Fill: transparent.
- Border: White or Line.
- Radius: `999px`.
- Text: White or Ash.
- Type: `11px`, uppercase, tracking `0.12em`.
- Use for project type, year, or service label.

## Case Study Row

- Background: Black.
- Divider: Line hairline.
- Title: White, `48px`, tight tracking.
- Metadata: Ash uppercase label.
- Image: square crop, no radius.
- Link: ghost pill or understated text link.

## Image Mosaic

- Use two to four images.
- Keep edges square and alignments intentional.
- Overlap sparingly.
- Avoid random scrapbook effects.

## Hairline Divider

- Height or width: `1px`.
- Color: Line or White at low opacity.
- Use between case studies, nav/footer, and content bands.

## Corner Footer Navigation

- Surface: Black.
- Links: small uppercase White and Ash.
- Layout: corner-pinned groups or sparse columns.
- Divider: Line hairlines.
- No large decorative footer blocks.
