# Typography

## Font Stack

- Primary: `PolySans`
- Fallback: `Suisse Int'l`, `Neue Haas Grotesk`, `Inter`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Weights: `400` only

## Scale

| Purpose | Size | Line height | Weight | Tracking | Token |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Micro label | `11px` | `1.1` | `400` | `0.12em` | `--text-micro` |
| Navigation | `13px` | `1.1` | `400` | `0.10em` | `--text-nav` |
| Body | `16px` | `1.45` | `400` | `-0.01em` | `--text-body` |
| Caption | `14px` | `1.35` | `400` | `0.02em` | `--text-caption` |
| Heading | `48px` | `0.98` | `400` | `-0.04em` | `--text-heading` |
| Display | `120px` | `0.86` | `400` | `-0.06em` | `--text-display` |
| Display huge | `180px` | `0.82` | `400` | `-0.07em` | `--text-display-huge` |

## Rules

- Use one font family and one regular weight.
- Let size and placement create hierarchy.
- Use tight negative tracking on giant headlines.
- Use wide uppercase tracking for labels, nav, badges, and metadata.
- Keep paragraphs short. The voice is editorial and sparse.
