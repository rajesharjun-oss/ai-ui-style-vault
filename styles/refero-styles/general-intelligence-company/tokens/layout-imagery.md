# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Alternate atmospheric illustrated bands with clean content sections.
- Let the hero occupy a full viewport when the illustration is central.
- Place the floating frosted nav pill near the top center.
- Anchor a frosted editorial card over the hero when the page needs a strong headline.
- Keep constrained content around 1200px max width.
- Use 64px to 96px vertical space for major editorial sections.
- Use two-column 6 plus 6 layouts for explanatory text and diagram cards.
- Avoid dense dashboards and packed grids.

## Imagery

Use hand-painted digital illustrations with visible atmosphere and texture. Good subjects include moonlit skylines, wildflower fields, warm landscapes, and a rare pixel-art flourish.

Avoid:

- Stock photography.
- Product screenshot collages.
- Glossy 3D objects.
- Flat generic vector art.
- Heavy abstract gradients.

## Iconography

Use minimal line icons only. Keep arrows, sun/landscape marks, and small UI glyphs simple and quiet.
