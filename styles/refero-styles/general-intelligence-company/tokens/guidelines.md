# Guidelines

## Do

- Use the display serif for headings 27px and above.
- Keep display weights at 400 or 500.
- Use `#dee2de` as the recurring card and section border.
- Keep buttons compact, thin, and outlined.
- Use Signal Blue only as a border, link, or small highlight.
- Let the base canvas be `#fefffc`.
- Use generous vertical spacing so each section has one clear idea.
- Use painted illustration as structural rhythm, not decoration sprinkled everywhere.

## Do Not

- Do not use pure black for text.
- Do not add shadows to buttons.
- Do not add new accent colors.
- Do not make the display serif heavy.
- Do not use transparent body text.
- Do not use pill radii everywhere.
- Do not fill primary buttons with blue.
- Do not use stock photos, glossy 3D, or generic abstract blobs.
