# Components

## Frosted Navigation Pill

Floating top-center navigation with 50px radius, translucent white fill, backdrop blur, a 1px border, compact sans links, and one small outlined CTA.

## Primary Outlined CTA

Transparent background, `#41a1cf` border and text, 8px radius, compact padding, and optional small arrow icon. Never fill this button blue.

## Secondary Outlined Button

Transparent background with `#282834` border and text. Use beside the primary CTA when hierarchy needs to stay quiet.

## Filled Dark Button

`#1f1f29` fill, white text, `#282834` border, 8px radius. Use sparingly for footer or final conversion contexts.

## Ghost Text Link

No border, no fill. Use charcoal or twilight text with an optional arrow. Best for editorial cross-links and tertiary actions.

## Frosted Hero Overlay Card

24px radius card over illustration. Use translucent background, backdrop blur, generous padding, and serif heading text.

## White Content Card

`#ffffff` surface, 12px radius, `#dee2de` border, soft shadow, and 16px padding for text cards.

## Atmospheric Illustration Card

Large 24px radius panel with hand-painted art or rare Cerulean color. Use it as a section-level punctuation mark.

## Diagram Card

16px radius, semitransparent white, soft border/shadow, compact padding, and minimal line-art diagrams.

## Input Field

Linen fill, charcoal text, bottom border only, flat editorial form feel.

## Cookie Banner

Small white banner with compact text and quiet text actions. Avoid loud color or icons.

## Footer

Paper-white editorial band with a large serif statement and compact sans navigation links.
