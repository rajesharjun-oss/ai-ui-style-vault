# Typography

## Display Font

Name: `ppmondwest`

Fallbacks: Fraunces, Recoleta, GT Sectra, serif

Role: editorial headings and brand voice.

Rules:

- Use for headings 27px and above.
- Keep weights at 400 or 500.
- Use tight negative tracking.
- Disable ligatures where supported with `"liga" 0`.
- Avoid 600 or 700 on the display serif.

## Primary UI Font

Name: `af`

Fallbacks: Inter, Geist, Sohne, system-ui, sans-serif

Role: body copy, buttons, nav, labels, inputs, captions, and small headings.

Rules:

- Use 400 for body.
- Use 500 for labels, controls, and compact nav.
- Use slight negative tracking for dense UI.
- Use 1.5 line height for readable body copy.
