# AI Implementation Prompt

Build a light editorial interface in the General Intelligence Company style.

Use a warm parchment canvas `#fefffc`, clean paper-white content blocks, and quiet green-tinted hairline borders `#dee2de`. The page should feel like a literary AI research journal: spacious, deliberate, and calm.

Typography is the identity. Use a custom display serif equivalent to `ppmondwest` for headings 27px and above, with weight 400 or 500, tight negative tracking, and 1.1 line height on large display text. Use a compact neutral sans equivalent to `af` for body, labels, nav, and controls. Keep body copy around 16px with 1.5 line height.

Use only one saturated accent in functional UI: Signal Blue `#41a1cf`, mostly as an outlined button border, link color, or small action highlight. Do not fill buttons with blue. Use Cerulean `#0081c0` only for rare atmospheric illustrated surfaces.

Core components to include:

- Floating frosted nav pill with backdrop blur, 50px radius, short nav links, and one outlined CTA.
- Editorial hero with full-bleed or large-radius painted illustration and a frosted overlay card.
- White content cards with 12px radius, `#dee2de` border, and soft layered shadow.
- Compact outlined CTAs with 8px radius and 1px borders.
- Diagram cards with 16px radius, translucent white, thin border, and subtle shadow.
- Paper-form inputs with linen fill and bottom border.
- Footer as a calm editorial colophon with a large serif statement.

Layout should alternate illustrated sections and white content sections. Use a 1200px max width for constrained content, 32px to 64px section gaps, and 64px to 96px vertical breathing room for major bands. Keep each section focused on one idea.

Avoid generic SaaS gradients, heavy shadows, sharp black text, bright CTA fills, stock photos, 3D objects, crowded dashboards, and extra accent colors.
