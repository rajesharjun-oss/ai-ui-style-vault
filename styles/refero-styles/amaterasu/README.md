## Style Summary

Amaterasu is a celestial teal sanctuary: a dark cinematic interface built around a full-bleed teal-lit portrait, polar-white text, and one structural violet ink. The UI stays thin and clinical: outlined pill controls, whisper-light display type, wide-tracked all-caps labels, atmospheric teal gradients, and no shadow elevation.

Use this style when an AI needs to build a dark AI or research product page that feels quiet, premium, clinical, and cinematic rather than dashboard-heavy.

## What To Borrow

- Full-bleed dark portrait or atmospheric teal hero image.
- Cosmic Violet `#1b2978` as the only structural color.
- Polar White `#ffffff` for primary text on dark surfaces.
- TWKLausanne-200 style display type at 50-80px with negative tracking.
- NeoSans-style uppercase labels at 10-12px with very wide tracking.
- Thin outlined pill CTAs rather than filled buttons.
- 40-60px of quiet negative space around text blocks.
- Radial cyan glow gradients only as atmosphere.
- Zero box shadows.

## Avoid

- Filled heavy CTA blocks.
- Teal as a button, link, or text color.
- Card shadows or glowing modals.
- Busy SaaS dashboard layouts.
- Bold hero typography.
- Multicolor UI accents.
- Warm beige or friendly pastel surfaces.
