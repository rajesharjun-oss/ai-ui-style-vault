# Components

## Full-Bleed Hero

- Background: dark cinematic portrait or teal-lit scene.
- Height: at least 90vh.
- Text: polar white.
- Display: 50-80px TWKLausanne-200 style.
- Label: 10px uppercase, wide tracking.
- CTA: outlined pill.
- Overlay: only a subtle darkness layer if needed for text contrast.

## Outlined Primary Action

- Background: transparent.
- Border: 1px solid `#1b2978`.
- Text: `#1b2978` on light surfaces, white on dark if contrast requires.
- Radius: 9999px.
- Padding: 10px 18px.
- Font: TWKLausanne-250 or NeoSans label depending on scale.
- Hover: subtle violet fill or border opacity shift.

Avoid a heavy filled CTA as the default.

## Label Navigation

- Font: NeoSansPro-Medium style.
- Size: 10px.
- Text transform: uppercase.
- Tracking: 0.16em to 0.32em.
- Color: white on dark hero, silver slate for secondary states.
- Layout: sparse, with generous gaps.

## Atmospheric Panel

- Background: cyan glow radial gradient or violet wash.
- Text: cosmic violet or obsidian on light gradient, polar white on dark gradient.
- Shape: no shadow.
- Use as a mood field, not as a generic card.

## Light Section

- Background: polar white.
- Heading: cosmic violet.
- Body: obsidian.
- Accent lines: violet or silver slate hairlines.
- Keep composition sparse and clinical.

## Line Icon

- Stroke: cerulean mist or silver slate.
- Stroke width: thin, around 1.5px.
- Fill: none.
- Use only when the icon supports navigation or explanation.

## Footer

- Background: obsidian or deep violet.
- Text: polar white.
- Labels: wide-tracked uppercase.
- CTA: outlined pill.
- No dense sitemap block.
