## Color Behavior

The UI has two real inks: Polar White and Cosmic Violet. Teal is environmental light, not an interactive color.

Use Cosmic Violet for:

- Outlined CTA borders.
- CTA text on light surfaces.
- Heading text on white sections.
- Thin structural rules.

Use Polar White for:

- Hero text.
- Navigation on dark imagery.
- Text on violet or obsidian panels.

Use Aurora Teal and Cyan Glow for:

- Background washes.
- Hero image atmosphere.
- Decorative panels.

## Forbidden Color Moves

- Do not use teal for buttons or links.
- Do not add a multicolor status system.
- Do not use warm beige surfaces.
- Do not use bright SaaS blues.
- Do not apply gradient fills to controls.
