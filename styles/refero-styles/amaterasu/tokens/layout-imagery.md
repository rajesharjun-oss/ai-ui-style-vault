# Layout And Imagery

## Page Structure

1. Full-bleed dark teal hero with portrait or atmospheric image.
2. Sparse navigation with wide-tracked labels.
3. Whisper display headline and outlined pill action.
4. Atmospheric gradient section or light clinical section.
5. Minimal line-icon or statement blocks.
6. Dark footer with pill CTA.

## Grid

- Content rail: around 1200px.
- Copy width: around 560px.
- Hero text: placed inside 40-60px negative space.
- Section rhythm: 96-120px.
- Avoid dense multi-card layouts.

## Imagery Rules

Use:

- Teal-lit portraits.
- Dark cinematic clinical scenes.
- Atmospheric close-ups.
- Soft directional light.
- Sparse line illustrations.

Avoid:

- Stock dashboard screenshots.
- Bright product grids.
- Generic abstract AI blobs.
- Warm lifestyle photography.
- Multi-color gradient collages.

## Composition

The composition should feel like a quiet dark room with carefully placed text. Let negative space and light direction do more work than cards, badges, or icons.
