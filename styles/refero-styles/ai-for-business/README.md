# AI for Business

Source: Refero Styles  
URL: https://styles.refero.design/style/ee403055-480e-4bd4-9216-07c9ae2dde2e  
Reference site: https://www.dayos.com  
Captured: 2026-07-28

AI for Business is a brutalist editorial showroom on warm gray: giant compressed uppercase headlines, flat white and black content blocks, generous rounded cards, a floating pill nav, and tiny mint/yellow accents. The style feels physical and material, especially when paired with 3D object renders.

Use this folder as an AI implementation brief. The most useful files are:

- `DESIGN.md` for the full style interpretation.
- `implementation-prompt.md` for a copy-ready prompt to hand to an AI builder.
- `style.json` for structured metadata.
- `code/css-variables.css` for CSS custom properties and component recipes.
- `code/tailwind-v4.css` for Tailwind v4 theme tokens.
- `code/design-tokens.json` for machine-readable tokens.

Core cues:

- Canvas: `#e5e5e5` Warm Canvas, never pure white.
- Card surface: `#ffffff` Paper White.
- Inverted blocks: `#000000` Carbon Black.
- Body secondary: `#444444` Slate.
- Accent: `#d1ffca` Mint Chip for tags and link backgrounds.
- Micro accent: `#fff100` Voltage Yellow for email highlights and tiny sparks.
- Display: SuisseIntlCond, 700, uppercase, 48px to 130px, 0.9 line-height.
- Body/UI: SuisseIntl, 400 to 500, especially 16px/500 for body and buttons.
- Micro labels: SuisseIntlMono, 12px.
- Shape: 24px to 64px card radii, 48px nav pill, 64px tags, 4px to 8px buttons.
- Surfaces: flat, no shadows, no gradients.

This is best for AI business platforms, enterprise SaaS, consulting/productized services, use-case libraries, B2B landing pages, and editorial product showrooms.
