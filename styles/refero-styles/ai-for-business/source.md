## What To Preserve

AI for Business uses a warm gray canvas, oversized condensed display type, tactile 3D product renders, and flat black/white card surfaces. The mint and yellow are accents only. The design should feel bold and material, not soft SaaS.

The page relies on:

- Warm Canvas as the full page background.
- Paper White as card surfaces.
- Carbon Black as the structural dark.
- Mint Chip as tag and link punctuation.
- Voltage Yellow as a tiny high-energy highlight.
- SuisseIntlCond for huge uppercase display headings.
- SuisseIntl for body, nav, cards, subheads, and buttons.
- SuisseIntlMono for labels and technical metadata.
- Large deliberate radius on cards and nav containers.
- Zero shadows and zero gradients.
