# Components

## Filled Dark Button

Carbon Black fill, Paper White text, 8px radius, 16px by 24px padding, SuisseIntl 16px/500. Use for primary CTAs such as schedule demo.

## Ghost Border Button

Transparent fill, 1.5px Slate border, Slate text, 4px radius, SuisseIntl 16px/500. Use for secondary navigation actions.

## Text Link Button

No background, no border, Carbon Black text, 0px to 4px radius, underline on hover, SuisseIntl 16px/500.

## Nav Pill

Paper White or Mist Gray floating navigation container with 48px radius, centered inside an 8rem top bar.

## Standard Card

Paper White fill, 24px to 32px radius, 24px padding, no shadow, no border.

## Inverted Card

Carbon Black fill, Paper White text, 32px radius, no shadow. Use for dramatic contrast zones.

## Top Arc Card

64px top corners and 0px bottom corners. Use for hero-bottom cards and section openers.

## Mint Tag

Mint Chip fill, Carbon Black text, 64px radius, compact padding, SuisseIntlMono 12px.

## Voltage Highlight

Voltage Yellow used only for email highlights, accent dots, and small decorative bursts.

## 3D Product Render

Photorealistic concrete/wood/branded object render sitting on the warm gray canvas.

## Uppercase Display Heading

SuisseIntlCond 700 at 80px to 130px, uppercase, 0.9 line-height, -0.03em tracking.

## Secondary Heading

SuisseIntl 450 at 40px, uppercase, line-height 1.1, -0.02em tracking.

## Mono Label

SuisseIntlMono 12px, letter-spacing -0.03em. Use for metadata, slide indicators, and system labels.
