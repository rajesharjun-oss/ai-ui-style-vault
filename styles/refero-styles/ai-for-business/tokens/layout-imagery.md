# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a full warm-gray canvas.
- Center content at about 1200px.
- Hero: split layout with huge headline left and 3D product render right.
- Navigation: 8rem top bar with centered floating 48px-radius pill.
- Sections: alternate warm-gray canvas, white cards, and black inverted blocks.
- Use-case library: 2-3 column card grid.
- Footer: compact dark band.
- Vertical rhythm: 80px section gaps.

## Imagery

Use:

- Photorealistic 3D product renders.
- Textured concrete cubes.
- Wood-grain material.
- Branded labels on physical objects.
- Colored geometric protrusions used as small physical accents.

Avoid:

- Lifestyle photography.
- Stock imagery.
- Abstract gradients.
- Decorative illustrations.
- Soft app-like shadows.
