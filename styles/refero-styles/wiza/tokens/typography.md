# Typography

## Families

| Role | Font | Fallback | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Britti Sans | Plus Jakarta Sans, General Sans | Headings from 24px to 64px |
| UI | Inter | IBM Plex Sans, system-ui | Body, nav, buttons, inputs, badges, tables |

## Type Scale

| Token | Size | Weight | Line height | Font |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caption | 12px | 500 | 1.3 | Inter |
| Body Small | 14px | 400 or 500 | 1.4 | Inter |
| Body | 16px | 400 or 500 | 1.5 | Inter |
| Subheading | 24px | 500 | 1 | Britti Sans |
| Heading Small | 32px | 500 | 1 | Britti Sans |
| Heading | 40px | 500 | 1 | Britti Sans |
| Heading Large | 56px | 500 | 1 | Britti Sans |
| Display | 64px | 500 | 1 | Britti Sans |

## Rules

- Britti Sans uses weight 500 only.
- Britti Sans line-height should be exactly 1.
- Inter is the workhorse for dense SaaS UI.
- Keep UI text compact from 12px to 16px.
- Avoid large body copy blocks in purple.
