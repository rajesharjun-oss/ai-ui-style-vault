# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Centered 1200px container.
- 64px to 80px gaps between sections.
- Floating white navigation bar above the hero.
- Full-bleed white-to-lavender hero gradient.
- Centered hero headline, subtext, and dual CTA row.
- Product screenshot frame below, slightly breaking the container edge.
- Alternating white and light lavender sections.
- Centered heading blocks followed by two-column or three-column grids.

## Hero Recipe

1. Lavender gradient wash background.
2. Floating white nav with 8px radius.
3. Britti Sans display headline in Deep Iris.
4. Inter subtext in Slate.
5. Filled Deep Iris button plus matching ghost button.
6. White product screenshot frame with contact data table.

## Imagery

- Use product screenshots and data tables.
- Use profile cards, avatars, company logos, and status dots.
- Use simple purple icon tiles.
- Use monochrome trust logos.
- Avoid lifestyle photos, stock imagery, rounded feature photos, and people-in-office scenes.

## Gradient System

Use gradients as atmosphere, not as a direct text background. Content should sit on white or Paper surfaces when it overlaps lavender gradients.
