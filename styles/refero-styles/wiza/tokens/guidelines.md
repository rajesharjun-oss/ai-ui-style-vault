# Guidelines

## Do

- Use 8px radius for rectangular UI surfaces.
- Use 1440px radius for pills, tags, rating badges, and capsule nav items.
- Keep Britti Sans headings at line-height 1.
- Use Deep Iris for headlines and filled buttons.
- Pair hero filled buttons with matching ghost buttons.
- Use a pill tag above section headings.
- Put lavender gradients behind white content surfaces.
- Keep Inter at 12px to 16px for data-dense UI.

## Do Not

- Do not use Royal Amethyst for body text.
- Do not use 4px or 12px radius for cards.
- Do not loosen Britti Sans line-height above 1.
- Do not introduce non-violet accent colors.
- Do not use heavy shadows.
- Do not center multi-line body paragraphs.
- Do not put dark text directly on gradients.
- Do not rely on lifestyle photography.

## Accessibility Notes

- Keep body text on white or Paper surfaces, not directly on gradients.
- Focus rings should be visible but not overly glowing.
- Data table rows need keyboard and screen-reader-friendly structure.
- Do not rely only on status dot color in data rows.
