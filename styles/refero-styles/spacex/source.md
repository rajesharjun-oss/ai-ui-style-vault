## Notes

This bundle summarizes the Refero style page into implementation-ready guidance. It is not a verbatim archive. The style is a cinematic aerospace system: black canvas, cool off-white typography, uppercase technical tracking, outlined controls, and full-viewport imagery as the main design material.

## What To Preserve

- Pure black canvas for all sections.
- Star White foreground as the only strong UI color.
- D-DIN-style industrial sans typography.
- Uppercase tracking around 0.09em to 0.10em for nav, labels, body text, and buttons.
- 48px all-caps section headlines with tighter 0.02em tracking.
- Transparent ghost outline buttons, never filled buttons.
- Text floating directly on black or full-bleed photography, never inside cards.
- Single-column sections governed by one large aerospace image.
