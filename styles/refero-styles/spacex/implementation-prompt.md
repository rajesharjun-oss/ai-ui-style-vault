# Implementation Prompt

Build a polished SpaceX-style interface: a cinematic aerospace page with an absolute black canvas, cool off-white industrial typography, transparent ghost controls, and full-bleed mission photography.

Use Void Black (`#000000`) as the background for every section. Use Star White (`#f0f0fa`) for headings, body copy, icons, navigation, button borders, and button text. Use Dim Steel (`#545457`) and Dark Gunmetal (`#404040`) only for secondary outlines, muted labels, and barely visible dividers. Do not introduce any chromatic accent color.

Typography should use D-DIN or a close industrial fallback such as Barlow. All nav links, labels, body copy, and buttons should be uppercase or technical-feeling with 0.09em to 0.10em tracking. Body text should be 13px, weight 400, line-height around 1.7, max-width around 450px. Section headlines should be 48px, uppercase, bold, line-height 1 to 1.25, and tracked at 0.02em.

Layout should be single-column and full-bleed. Use a transparent top nav over black or imagery. Each major section should use one large aerospace image across the full viewport. Put text directly on the dark area of the image or on black, anchored in the left third of the viewport. Do not use cards, panels, tinted text backgrounds, sidebars, image grids, or alternating light sections.

Buttons must be ghost outlines: transparent fill, 1px Star White border, 4px radius, uppercase Star White text, 12px to 13px, 700 weight, 0.10em tracking, and about 10px 20px padding. Secondary buttons can use Dim Steel. Filled button variants should not exist.

Before finishing, check that:

- Every page surface is black or full-bleed image.
- There is no accent color.
- There are no filled CTAs.
- Text is not placed inside cards or panels.
- Small typography is uppercase, tracked, and technical.
- Imagery is full-bleed and unframed.
- No shadows, gradients, glows, or blur effects were added.
