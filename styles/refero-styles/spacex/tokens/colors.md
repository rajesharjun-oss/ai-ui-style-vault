# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Void Black | `#000000` | `--color-void-black` | Page canvas and every section background |
| Star White | `#f0f0fa` | `--color-star-white` | Primary foreground for text, icons, nav, buttons, and borders |
| Dim Steel | `#545457` | `--color-dim-steel` | Secondary outlines, muted labels, quiet dividers |
| Dark Gunmetal | `#404040` | `--color-dark-gunmetal` | Low-emphasis separators and barely visible structural lines |

## Color Behavior

There is no accent color. Photography may contain fire, Earth, Mars, metal, and sky tones, but the UI itself stays monochrome.

## Pairings

- Primary text: Star White on Void Black.
- Primary outline: Star White border on transparent.
- Secondary outline: Dim Steel border on transparent.
- Dividers: Dark Gunmetal on black.

## Avoid

- Filled CTA colors.
- Blue links.
- Warm accent colors.
- Gradient UI.
- Tinted cards or panels.
