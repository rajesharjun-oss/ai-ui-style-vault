# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

Use a single-column full-bleed page flow. No grid, no cards, no sidebars, and no alternating light/dark bands. Every section is either pure black or a full-viewport aerospace image.

Suggested order:

1. Transparent navigation over black or imagery.
2. Full-bleed hero photograph.
3. Left-third text overlay.
4. Ghost outline action.
5. Additional full-bleed image sections.
6. Optional launch selector or compact mission utility in the nav.

## Composition

Text blocks should be left-aligned and constrained to around 400px to 500px. Use generous negative space and let the image fill most of the viewport.

## Imagery

Use cinematic aerospace imagery:

- rockets on launchpads
- booster landings
- spacecraft in darkness
- Earth from orbit
- Mars or planetary surfaces
- rocket plumes
- engineering hardware under hard light

Images should be full-bleed, raw-edged, and unframed.

## Avoid

- Icon systems.
- Product mockups.
- Illustration packs.
- Rounded image cards.
- Multi-image grids.
- Text backing panels.
- Decorative overlays.
