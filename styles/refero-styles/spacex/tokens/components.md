# Components

## Ghost Outline Button

- Transparent background.
- 1px Star White border.
- 4px radius.
- Star White uppercase text.
- D-DIN-style font.
- 12px to 13px.
- 700 weight.
- 0.10em tracking.
- Padding around 10px 20px.
- Optional right arrow in the same color.

## Secondary Outline Button

- Transparent background.
- 1px Dim Steel border.
- Dim Steel uppercase text.
- Same dimensions and type treatment as the primary ghost button.

## Pill Selector

- 32px radius.
- 1px outline.
- Uppercase tracked text.
- Use for compact selectors, toggles, or launch dropdowns.
- Do not use as a general marketing CTA style.

## Top Navigation Bar

- Transparent background.
- No shadow, blur, or filled strip.
- White wordmark on the left.
- Uppercase nav links at 10px to 12px.
- 700 weight.
- 0.09em to 0.10em tracking.
- Compact launch selector at the far right.

## Full-Bleed Hero Section

- Viewport-width aerospace photograph.
- No image border, radius, or frame.
- Text block directly on image.
- Text block positioned in the left third.
- Headline at 48px all caps.
- Body at 13px with wide tracking.
- Ghost outline CTA below.

## Text-Over-Image Block

- Same as hero, used for secondary sections.
- One photograph owns the full section.
- No panel behind text.
- No card or tinted container.

## Section Headline

- D-DIN-Bold-style.
- 48px.
- 700 weight.
- Uppercase.
- Star White.
- 0.02em tracking.
- Line-height 1 to 1.25.

## Body Paragraph

- D-DIN-style.
- 13px.
- 400 weight.
- Star White.
- 0.10em tracking.
- Line-height around 1.7.
- Max width around 450px.

## Launch Selector Widget

- 1px Star White border.
- 4px radius.
- Transparent fill.
- Uppercase tracked label.
- Small caret or chevron.
- Lives in the top-right navigation.
