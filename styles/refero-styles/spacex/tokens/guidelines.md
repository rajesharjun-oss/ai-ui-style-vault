# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Void Black as the canvas for every section.
- Use Star White as the primary UI foreground.
- Use uppercase D-DIN-style text with strong tracking for small UI and body copy.
- Use 48px uppercase bold headlines with 0.02em tracking.
- Use transparent ghost outline buttons for every action.
- Let full-bleed photography carry the composition.
- Place text blocks in the left third of the viewport.
- Keep sections single-column and image-dominant.

## Don't

- Do not introduce chromatic accent colors.
- Do not use filled buttons.
- Do not put text inside cards, panels, or tinted containers.
- Do not use structural radius above 4px except for 32px selectors.
- Do not use humanist, warm, or editorial typefaces.
- Do not add shadows, gradients, glows, or blur effects.
- Do not fragment imagery into card grids.

## Accessibility Notes

- Text over photography must sit on genuinely dark image regions.
- If an image does not provide enough contrast, choose a better crop rather than adding a panel.
- Keep focus outlines visible on ghost controls.
- Provide descriptive alt text for mission-critical imagery.
