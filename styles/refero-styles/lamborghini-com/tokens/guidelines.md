# Guidelines

## Do

- Use uppercase condensed typography for navigation, CTAs, labels, and model names.
- Use 0px radius on all buttons, tags, panels, image crops, and controls.
- Reserve Giallo for the single most important action, active indicators, and tiny brand accents.
- Use full-bleed automotive photography or video as the main visual event.
- Keep navigation and footer black.
- Use Silver for captions, metadata, inactive states, and secondary copy.
- Build hierarchy with scale, contrast, media, and dividers.
- Use white or concrete editorial sections as sharp relief between dark product moments.

## Don't

- Do not use rounded corners, pills, bubbly controls, or soft cards.
- Do not add pastel colors or a second accent hue.
- Do not use gradients except black image overlays for legibility.
- Do not use soft shadows, glow effects, or floating SaaS panels.
- Do not use abstract illustrations when product media is available.
- Do not lowercase nav items or primary CTA labels.
- Do not crowd the hero with multiple text blocks.
- Do not make every section yellow; yellow should stay rare and decisive.
