# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Model

- Start with a full-bleed black hero.
- Keep the top navigation black, square, and horizontally organized.
- Use full-width product bands for model showcases.
- Use edge-to-edge black spec strips for technical metrics.
- Use white or Concrete editorial bands for news, stories, ownership information, or brand copy.
- Contain text blocks where useful, but do not place the whole site inside a generic centered card stack.
- Prefer two-column or equal-column grids with hard edges and clean dividers.

## Imagery

- Use real automotive or premium hardware photography and video.
- Favor low, wide, dramatic product angles.
- Use night road, studio, or track lighting with deep black shadows.
- Keep the vehicle or product clearly visible and dominant.
- Image crops should be square-corner rectangles or full-bleed.
- Avoid stock lifestyle scenes, illustrations, playful mascots, blurred decorative photos, and abstract gradients.

## Motion

- Motion should feel mechanical and precise.
- Use subtle image scale, sharp opacity changes, and controlled slide transitions.
- Avoid bouncy easing, playful springs, confetti, or soft decorative movement.
