# Components

## Top Black Navigation

- Height: `80px`.
- Fill: Nero.
- Text: Bianco and Silver.
- Layout: logo left, uppercase model links centered, utility icons right.
- Type: LamboType or condensed fallback, `13px` to `14px`, weight `400`, tracking `0.04em`.
- Shape: no radius, no shadow.

## Yellow Primary CTA

- Fill: Giallo.
- Text: Nero.
- Radius: `0px`.
- Padding: `12px 24px`.
- Type: `14px`, weight `700`, uppercase, tracking `0.05em`.
- Use only for the primary action on a screen.

## Outline Ghost Button

- Fill: transparent.
- Border: `1px solid` Bianco on dark or Nero on light.
- Text: Bianco on dark or Nero on light.
- Radius: `0px`.
- Padding: `12px 24px`.
- Type: `14px`, uppercase, tracking `0.05em`.

## Full-Bleed Hero Video Or Image

- Width: `100vw`.
- Height: `80vh` to `100vh`.
- Media: dramatic car or performance product imagery.
- Overlay: black overlay or bottom gradient only when needed for legibility.
- Text: large uppercase headline placed over a dark region.
- Shape: no frame, no card, no rounded crop.

## Hero Display Text

- Font: LamboType or condensed fallback.
- Weight: `300` or `700`.
- Size: `64px` to `80px` on desktop.
- Line height: `1`.
- Color: Bianco.
- Placement: usually left aligned, never in a floating card.

## Model Tile

- Media: full-bleed car photo.
- Overlay: black gradient at bottom only for text legibility.
- Title: `32px` to `64px`, weight `700`, Bianco.
- Actions: one Yellow CTA and one ghost button.
- Hover: optional slight media scale, no shadow.

## Vehicle Spec Strip

- Background: Nero.
- Layout: equal columns.
- Numbers: large Bianco type.
- Labels: Silver uppercase captions.
- Dividers: `1px` Graphite vertical rules.
- Use for speed, acceleration, power, range, or technical proof points.

## News Or Story Card

- Surface: Bianco or Concrete.
- Image: square-corner rectangle.
- Metadata: Silver, `13px`, uppercase.
- Title: Nero, `24px`, weight `400`.
- Decoration: no shadow, no radius.

## Configurator Panel

- Surface: Nero or Concrete.
- Layout: split product render and controls/spec summary.
- Controls: rectangular, compact, uppercase.
- Dividers: Graphite lines.
- Avoid playful swatches or rounded option pills.

## Footer Mega Block

- Surface: Nero.
- Links: uppercase Bianco or Silver.
- Legal text: Silver, `13px`.
- Layout: strong columns and hard dividers.

## Icon Button

- Size: `40px` square.
- Fill: transparent.
- Icon: Bianco stroke on black surfaces.
- Border: none by default.
- Hover: opacity or color shift only.
