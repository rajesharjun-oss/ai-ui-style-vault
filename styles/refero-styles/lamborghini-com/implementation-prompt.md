# AI Implementation Prompt

Build a premium automotive interface inspired by Lamborghini.com.

Use a black-first visual system with pure black sections, white text, concrete editorial blocks, silver metadata, graphite dividers, and one yellow action color. Treat the page as a dramatic product showroom, not a SaaS dashboard.

Requirements:

- Use full-bleed car or product imagery/video as the main hero event.
- Add an 80px black navigation bar with logo left, uppercase model/category links centered, and utility icons right.
- Use a condensed uppercase type system. Prefer LamboType when licensed; otherwise use Arial Narrow, Inter Condensed, or another narrow geometric sans.
- Keep all corners at 0px for buttons, images, cards, inputs, tiles, and panels.
- Use yellow only for the primary CTA, active micro states, small icon accents, and occasional brand punctuation.
- Use white or concrete sections for editorial/news content, but keep the major product moments black.
- Add a black spec strip with large white numbers, silver uppercase labels, and thin graphite vertical dividers.
- Use ghost buttons with transparent fill, one-pixel borders, uppercase labels, and square corners.
- Avoid rounded cards, pastel colors, decorative blobs, soft shadows, generic marketing gradients, playful illustrations, and emoji.

Hero recipe:

Create a full viewport media hero with a black navigation bar, a low wide vehicle image, a large uppercase white headline, silver supporting copy, one yellow rectangular CTA, and one ghost CTA. Keep the hero uncluttered and make the product image the emotional center.

Component recipe:

For model tiles, use a full-bleed image, bottom black overlay for text legibility, a 32px to 64px uppercase model name, a short silver descriptor, and a two-button CTA row. No card border, no radius, and no shadow.
