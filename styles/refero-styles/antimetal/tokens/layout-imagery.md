# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Center content in a 1200px max-width container.
- Use generous 120px section gaps.
- Build editorial two-column moments for content sections.
- Use dashed separators and mono section labels to create rhythm.
- Avoid dense dashboards and decorative card grids.

## Imagery

- Prefer restrained technical or editorial media.
- Use video thumbnails, product stills, pull quotes, and text-first sections.
- Keep imagery desaturated and calm.
- Avoid colorful mascot or illustration-heavy treatment.

## Direction

The page should feel like a quiet engineering research journal for a high-trust product. Let typography and negative space do most of the work.
