# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Test Signifier weight 400 for all headings.
- Use dashed 1px low-opacity borders for internal separators.
- Pair the dark pill CTA with a ghost secondary button.
- Number major sections with tracked Geist Mono markers.
- Keep the main canvas warm gray and surfaces cream.
- Use chartreuse as a single small accent.
- Keep cards nearly rectangular and buttons fully pill-shaped.

## Do Not

- Do not bold headings.
- Do not use solid borders for content separators.
- Do not use pure white or pure black as dominant surfaces.
- Do not make chartreuse a large background.
- Do not add heavy shadows.
- Do not use loud blues or gradients.
- Do not make the page look like a generic SaaS dashboard.

## AI Builder Checks

- Does the serif carry the hierarchy without bold weight?
- Are separators dashed instead of solid?
- Is chartreuse used sparingly?
- Does the page feel like an editorial technical journal?
- Are cards restrained and nearly flat?
