# Components

## Dark Pill Button

- Role: primary CTA.
- Background: `#1a1614`.
- Text: warm cream or `#f7f6f3`.
- Radius: 9999px.
- Font: Geist 14px, weight 500.
- Use once per viewport.

## Ghost Outlined Button

- Transparent fill.
- Solid outline in Bistre with high opacity.
- Paired with the dark pill CTA.
- Keep it quieter than the primary action.

## Liquid-Glass Navigation Capsule

- Radius: 82px.
- Warm translucent fill based on Quill/Parchment.
- Use backdrop blur and subtle inset depth.
- Layout can place nav links, wordmark, account link, and CTA inside one capsule.

## FAQ Accordion Row

- Cream surface.
- Dashed 1px separators.
- Test Signifier 24px question text.
- Geist 16px expanded body text.
- No heavy card shell.

## Section Header

- Top marker: Geist Mono 10px to 11px, uppercase, tracked.
- Main heading: Test Signifier 36px to 48px, weight 400.
- Optional descriptor: Geist 20px in Graphite.

## Pull Quote

- Large regular serif quote.
- Quiet attribution below.
- No boxed background.

## Video Thumbnail

- Restrained still image.
- Centered circular play control.
- Small dark timestamp chip.
- Minimal radius.
