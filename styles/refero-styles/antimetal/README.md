## Use This When

Use this style when a product should feel serious, editorial, technical, and quietly premium without adopting a cold enterprise dashboard language. It works well for infrastructure SaaS, research tools, engineering products, pricing optimization, technical content pages, and product sites that need calm authority.

The key behavior is restraint: warm gray canvas, cream surfaces, a regular-weight serif for all headings, Geist for body/UI, tracked monospace section markers, dashed dividers, and one tiny chartreuse accent. Nothing should shout.
