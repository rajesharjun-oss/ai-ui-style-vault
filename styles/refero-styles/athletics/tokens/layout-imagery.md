# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a full-bleed dark layout with no visible page container.
- Hero is centered and sparse: brand mark above oversized headline.
- Alternate between two-column editorial blocks and full-width service displays.
- Give each section 128px to 144px of vertical air.
- Keep content density low.
- Use service index markers such as A, B, C, and D as a navigational/specimen-sheet device.
- Keep navigation minimal: wordmark left, links right, no chrome.

## Section Patterns

- Hero: Obsidian background, image-filled mark, 116px serif headline.
- Two-column opener: label/headline left, constrained body right, Charcoal background.
- Service index: giant serif service names with letter markers.
- Specialty block: white pill tags on Charcoal.
- Contact form: underline-only inputs and white pill button.
- Case study: full-bleed moody photography, no decorative frames.

## Imagery

- Use moody editorial photography with low-key lighting.
- Use tightly cropped image fills inside the brand mark.
- Let saturated color appear only in photography.
- Avoid decorative illustration, abstract graphics, icon-heavy pages, and photos used as filler.
- Reserve imagery for hero moments and case studies.
