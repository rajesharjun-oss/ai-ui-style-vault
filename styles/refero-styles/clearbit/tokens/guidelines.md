# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Midnight Ink for primary text and headings.
- Reserve Electric Blue and Cobalt Surface for active UI, focus, check icons, and the main action.
- Use Lavender Wash and Frost Border for section separation and structure.
- Set headings in InterVar weight 500 or 600 with positive tracking.
- Keep cards at 12px radius and buttons or inputs at 8px radius.
- Show the product through real UI mockups instead of abstract graphics.
- Keep major vertical rhythm around 64px.
- Use 8px to 20px for local spacing between related elements.

## Do Not

- Do not add resting drop shadows to cards or panels.
- Do not introduce another accent color.
- Do not use negative letter spacing on display text.
- Do not set body text below 14px.
- Do not use weight 700 for long phrases.
- Do not use pure black for long paragraphs.
- Do not break the radius ladder.
- Do not use heavy gradients, textured backgrounds, or decorative renders.
