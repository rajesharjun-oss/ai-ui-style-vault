# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a centered container with a 1200px max width.
- Alternate full-bleed white and Lavender Wash sections.
- Start with a simple nav, a centered hero text stack, and a restrained customer or logo row.
- Follow with alternating two-column feature sections.
- Place copy on one side and product cards on the other.
- Offset product cards slightly so they feel suspended, but keep the grid clean.

## Imagery

Use product-screenshot-as-hero language:

- Enrichment tables.
- Company profile cards.
- Attribute rows.
- Score badges.
- Slack-style data records.
- Customer logo cards.
- Data table modules.

Avoid:

- Stock photos.
- Lifestyle imagery.
- Abstract 3D scenes.
- Decorative gradients.
- UI-free hero art.

Brand colors may appear inside embedded product mockups, such as a customer logo or app mark. Keep the surrounding page palette restrained.
