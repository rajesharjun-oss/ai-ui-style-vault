# Components

## Top Navigation Bar

White header with brand at left and a single action at right. Keep it light, flat, and low-chrome.

## Filled Primary Button

- Background: `#127ee3`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 8px
- Font: InterVar 16px, weight 500
- Padding: 12px 20px
- Focus: Electric Blue ring with blue glow

## Outlined Ghost Button

- Background: transparent
- Border: 1px `#e1e9f0`
- Text: `#091135`
- Radius: 8px
- Padding: same as filled button

## Hero Headline Block

Centered text on a white background. Use a 56px or 64px InterVar heading with positive tracking and an 18px Slate subhead.

## Product Showcase Card

White card on Lavender Wash with 12px radius, 24px padding, 1px Frost Border, and no resting shadow. Use it for real product UI snippets: records, attribute lists, data tables, score badges, and logos.

## Data Record Row

Two-column row with Slate labels and Midnight Ink values. Use Electric Blue checkmarks as small signals. Prefer whitespace over row dividers.

## Section Eyebrow

Small 14px InterVar label above a section title. Use Midnight Ink and light positive tracking.

## Lavender Pill Badge

Lavender Wash background, Midnight Ink text, 9999px radius, 8px 16px padding.

## Score Badge

White circular chip, about 40px diameter, Frost Border, Electric Blue number, 18px weight 600.

## Brand Data Card

White product card with a brand mark and structured account data. Brand colors are allowed only inside the mock product content.

## Lavender Section Background

Full-bleed pale lavender band used behind product cards. Keep transitions soft and do not add heavy borders.
