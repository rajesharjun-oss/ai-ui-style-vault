# Colors

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight Ink | `#091135` | `--color-midnight-ink` | Primary text, headings, navigation, and links |
| Electric Blue | `#0f77ff` | `--color-electric-blue` | Focus rings, checkmarks, outlines, active edges |
| Cobalt Surface | `#127ee3` | `--color-cobalt-surface` | Filled primary action and rare blue surface emphasis |
| Slate | `#36394a` | `--color-slate` | Secondary text, labels, metadata, helper copy |
| Mist | `#b1bbcd` | `--color-mist` | Soft neutral and glow support |
| Frost Border | `#e1e9f0` | `--color-frost-border` | Hairline borders, card edges, dividers, inputs |
| Lavender Wash | `#f5f3ff` | `--color-lavender-wash` | Pale product section background |
| Paper | `#ffffff` | `--color-paper` | Page canvas and card surfaces |
| Graphite | `#000000` | `--color-graphite` | Sparse icon fills only |

## Color Rules

- Let Midnight Ink do the main work.
- Keep Electric Blue and Cobalt Surface rare and functional.
- Use Lavender Wash for section separation.
- Keep product cards white.
- Use Graphite only for tiny icon details, not paragraphs.
- Do not add extra accent colors outside brand logos embedded inside product mockups.
