## Capture Notes

The Refero page describes Clearbit as a restrained light product system built from white paper, pale lavender zones, navy text, and a narrow blue accent vocabulary. The style is not image-led in a lifestyle sense; it uses product screenshots, customer cards, enrichment rows, and score badges as the visual material.

The AI-useful pieces to preserve are:

- Single-font InterVar system with positive tracking that opens as type gets larger.
- Midnight navy text instead of black for long-form reading.
- One blue accent family for actions, focus, checkmarks, and small edges.
- White cards floating over Lavender Wash sections with 1px Frost Border.
- No resting drop shadows; use tint, border, and active focus ring.
- Hero and feature sections should show real UI records, tables, and product cards.
