# Guidelines

## Do

- Use weight 460 for all headlines at 28px and above.
- Set body text to Ink Charcoal on Warm Parchment.
- Use Midnight Wine as the single primary action fill.
- Reserve Royal Violet exclusively for inline links.
- Float product cards at 16px radius over photographic backgrounds with no drop shadow.
- Apply backdrop blur to the sticky header when content scrolls underneath.
- Keep display letter spacing tight and body text tracking neutral.

## Do Not

- Do not use weight 700 or higher for headlines.
- Do not place white surfaces directly on a white page canvas.
- Do not add drop shadows to cards.
- Do not introduce blue, green, or red accent colors.
- Do not use violet as a button fill, badge, or icon stroke.
- Do not center body copy longer than two lines.

## AI Implementation Notes

- If the layout feels flat, improve the photography or card compositing before adding shadows.
- If it feels too colorful, remove every accent except Midnight Wine, Royal Violet, and Lilac Mist.
- If it feels too generic SaaS, add editorial photo framing and lighter headline weights.
- If a card disappears, place it on parchment or over imagery rather than adding shadow.
