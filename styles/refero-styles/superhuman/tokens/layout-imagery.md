# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a 1200px max-width centered content rhythm.
- Keep Warm Parchment as the default page background.
- Use full-bleed hero photography for emotional weight.
- Float translucent product cards over the image.
- Use a top announcement pill and sticky blurred nav.
- Use white suite cards and tab strips on parchment.
- Include one Deep Lagoon dark feature band for contrast.
- Use a Midnight Wine footer.

## Section Patterns

- Announcement: maroon pill strip at top.
- Hero: cinematic photo, headline, CTA stack, floating product cards.
- Trust: row of square logo cells.
- Suite: tab strip with product cards.
- Feature band: Deep Lagoon full-bleed 2-column story.
- Gradient banner: atmospheric background with product CTA.
- Footer: maroon multi-column link layout.

## Imagery

- Use golden-hour editorial photography.
- Use product UI mockups floating over photographs.
- Use monochrome customer logos.
- Use atmospheric gradients only for dedicated visual banners.
- Avoid stock SaaS illustration, cold dashboards, and image-free hero sections.
