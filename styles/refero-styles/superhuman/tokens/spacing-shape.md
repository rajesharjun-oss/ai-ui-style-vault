## Elevation

- Drop shadows: none.
- Subtle inset: `rgb(113, 76, 182) 0px 0px 0px 1px inset`.
- Header scroll treatment: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` plus Soft Mist bottom border.
- Floating cards rely on photography and translucency for depth.
