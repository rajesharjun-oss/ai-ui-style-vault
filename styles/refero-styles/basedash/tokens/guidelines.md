# Guidelines

## Do

- Use a pure black canvas.
- Make the primary CTA a white filled button with black text.
- Keep the navigation transparent and minimal.
- Use product screenshots and dashboard mockups as the main imagery.
- Use violet and mint only as data, status, focus, or chart accents.
- Use Inter for all functional UI.
- Use the display serif only for large editorial headings.
- Keep cards nearly black and softly rounded.

## Do Not

- Do not fill CTAs with violet or mint.
- Do not use bright gradients as page backgrounds.
- Do not add lifestyle photography.
- Do not use heavy card shadows.
- Do not turn every section into a bordered card.
- Do not use the serif for tables, forms, buttons, or nav.
- Do not add extra accent colors without a product-data reason.

## Accessibility Notes

- White on black is high contrast, but muted ash text needs size and contrast checks.
- Keep focus states visible with Lavender Pulse or Ghost White outlines.
- Do not rely on violet and mint alone to communicate status.
- Preserve keyboard-visible nav and button states.
