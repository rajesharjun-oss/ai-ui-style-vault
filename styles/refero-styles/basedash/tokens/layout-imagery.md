# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Full page on `#000000`.
- Max-width content wrapper around 1200px.
- Transparent top navigation.
- Centered hero stack.
- Product mockup beneath the hero, allowed to feel wider than the text column.
- Alternating centered text sections and restrained card grids.
- Two-column testimonial layouts.
- Dense integration logo grid.

## Hero Recipe

1. Transparent nav.
2. Small announcement pill.
3. 48px serif headline.
4. Muted Inter subhead.
5. White filled CTA plus ghost secondary link.
6. Trust line.
7. Dark dashboard mockup with violet and mint chart accents.

## Imagery

- Product UI is the image language.
- Dashboard screenshots should be dark, data-rich, and calm.
- Use chart lines, status pills, tables, and command panels.
- Avatars are acceptable only in testimonials.
- Avoid lifestyle photos, decorative illustrations, and abstract glowing backgrounds.

## Iconography

- Minimal geometric line icons.
- Sizes around 16px to 20px.
- Use white or Steel Gray.
- Keep icons secondary to type and product visuals.
