## Source Summary

The source describes Basedash as a dark data-terminal style with a pure black canvas, barely lifted near-black cards, white text, white pill CTAs, tight Inter typography, and a display serif used only for high-impact editorial headings. Violet and green accents are used as data colors rather than decoration.
