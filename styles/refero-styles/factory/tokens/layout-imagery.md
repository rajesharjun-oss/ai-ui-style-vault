# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Rhythm

- Use a full-bleed `#101010` canvas as the persistent page background.
- Keep content centered at about `1200px` max width.
- Hero can use a two-column layout: text/actions on the left, large dashboard/product frame on the right.
- Keep section gaps around `96px` or larger.
- Grid cards should have about `24px` gaps and should never feel crowded or touching.
- Avoid sidebars or complex nav structures for marketing surfaces.

## Imagery

- Use product UI and dashboard frames as the primary visual asset.
- Prefer terminal panels, metric tiles, data charts, logs, and windowed product previews.
- Avoid lifestyle photography, people, abstract gradients, and decorative 3D renders.
- Light cards may carry subtle grain/noise texture, but not illustrative decoration.
- Trust strips should use monochrome wordmarks in warm gray.

## Figure/Ground Strategy

- Main background: black canvas.
- Main figure: light card, bright product panel, or product screenshot frame.
- Secondary hierarchy: hairline borders and surface contrast.
- Avoid soft elevation. The design should feel flat, precise, and instrument-like.
