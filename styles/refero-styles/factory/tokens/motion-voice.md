# Motion And Voice

## Motion

- Use short, mechanical transitions around `150ms` to `200ms`.
- Preferred easing: `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`.
- Transition color, background-color, border-color, and stroke together.
- Use named motion sparingly: a slow logo marquee or a single dashboard entrance is enough.
- Avoid spring physics, parallax, decorative scroll effects, and bouncy marketing motion.

## Voice And Type Treatment

- Geist carries the calm product/marketing voice.
- Geist Mono carries the terminal/instrument voice.
- Use Mono for labels, status tags, headers, units, and data-adjacent copy.
- Use normal-weight type and tight tracking rather than boldness for authority.
- The system should feel like a tool surface first and a marketing page second.

## Similar Design Neighborhood

- Linear-like monochrome dark productivity surface with one restrained accent.
- Vercel-like figure/ground contrast and Geist-family restraint.
- Resend-like warm terminal feel with compact radii and Mono labels.
- Cursor-like developer product frame as hero evidence.
- Railway-like instrument-panel sensibility with live metrics and sparse color.
