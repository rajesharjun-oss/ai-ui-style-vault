# Colors

## Accent

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Signal Orange | `#ee6018` | `--color-signal-orange` | Live status, build-state indicators, warning marks, chart strokes, and small graphic details. |
| Metric Green | `#a0ca92` | `--color-metric-green` | Positive metric states, trend lines, success indicators, chart strokes, and small graphic details. |

## Neutrals

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Obsidian Canvas | `#101010` | `--color-obsidian-canvas` | Page background, hero base, footer base, and primary dark canvas. |
| Carbon Lift | `#1d1a18` | `--color-carbon-lift` | Raised dark surfaces, nav wells, inline button fills, and subtle panel elevation. |
| Ash Stroke | `#3d3a39` | `--color-ash-stroke` | Hairline borders, ghost button outlines, dividers, and separators. |
| Graphite Mid | `#4d4947` | `--color-graphite-mid` | Chart bodies, secondary surfaces, and neutral data visualization fills. |
| Warm Granite | `#8a8380` | `--color-warm-granite` | Muted body copy, inactive labels, and secondary text. |
| Pale Stone | `#b8b3b0` | `--color-pale-stone` | Tertiary text, section eyebrows, column labels, and subdued support copy. |
| Bone | `#eeeeee` | `--color-bone` | Primary text, light card surfaces, and the main bright figure on the dark canvas. |
| Chalk | `#fafafa` | `--color-chalk` | High-emphasis light fill, auth buttons, and elevated light surface top layer. |

## Surface Levels

| Level | Name | Value | Purpose |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 0 | Obsidian Canvas | `#101010` | Page base, hero, footer, and all non-card sections. |
| 1 | Carbon Lift | `#1d1a18` | Nav well, inline controls, subtle dark elevation. |
| 2 | Bone Card | `#eeeeee` | Light cards on dark ground, the signature contrast move. |
| 3 | Chalk Elevated | `#fafafa` | Light button fill and highest light surface. |
