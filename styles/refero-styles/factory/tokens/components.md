# Components

## Top Navigation Bar

- **Role:** Persistent header across the product/marketing surface.
- **Style:** Transparent over `#101010`, about `64px` tall, monochrome links, compact neutral auth controls.
- **Type:** Geist or Geist Mono, uppercase labels where the surface should feel technical.
- **Rule:** Keep it uncluttered. No mega-menu or decorative nav treatment.

## Dark Filled Button

- **Role:** Primary interactive control in dark contexts.
- **Style:** `#1d1a18` or similar dark neutral fill, `#eeeeee` text, `3px` radius, compact horizontal padding.
- **Rule:** Do not fill primary buttons with orange or green.

## Light Filled Button

- **Role:** High-emphasis auth or nav trigger.
- **Style:** `#fafafa` fill, `#101010` text, `3px` radius, compact padding.
- **Rule:** Use sparingly, often once in the nav or as the clearest high-contrast control.

## Ghost Text Link

- **Role:** Secondary navigation, in-text links, "read more" actions.
- **Style:** Transparent fill, `1px #3d3a39` border, flat or very low radius, light text.
- **Behavior:** Hover should shift text and border color rather than adding a fill.

## Light Surface Card

- **Role:** Featured panels, CTAs, case studies, or primary figure on dark ground.
- **Style:** `#eeeeee` background, `10px` radius, `24px` padding, no shadow.
- **Content:** Dark text, optional subtle grain/noise texture.
- **Rule:** This is the main hierarchy tool. Use intentionally.

## Dashboard Frame

- **Role:** Product hero preview.
- **Style:** Dark app frame with `10px` radius, window-chrome details, metric grid, and thin dividers.
- **Details:** Use small status dots, compact headers, and live-data chart strokes in orange or green.

## Metric Tile

- **Role:** Data cell inside dashboards or analytics panels.
- **Style:** No heavy fill, hairline divider, about `20px` padding.
- **Type:** Geist Mono uppercase label, Geist `36px` value, compact tracking.
- **Data color:** Orange for warning/negative/live status; green for positive trend.

## CTA Section Card

- **Role:** Bottom conversion closer.
- **Style:** Light card around `480px` wide, `10px` radius, `24px` padding, dark text, neutral dark CTA.
- **Accent:** A small orange dot or marker can lead the eyebrow, but not dominate the card.

## Status Pulse

- **Role:** Inline live indicator.
- **Style:** Small `6px` filled circle in `#ee6018`, optionally with a thin stroke.
- **Behavior:** Static is fine; if animated, keep it restrained and functional.

## Logo Strip

- **Role:** Trust/social proof band.
- **Style:** Single monochrome row on `#101010`, warm-gray wordmarks, no cards, no dividers.
- **Spacing:** Use generous vertical breathing room around the strip.

## Feature Card Row

- **Role:** Product capability sections.
- **Style:** Dark cards implied by `1px #1d1a18` border, `10px` radius, `20px` padding, minimal/no fill.
- **Footer:** Ghost link or compact text link.

## Footer

- **Role:** Link columns and brand closure.
- **Style:** `#101010` background, large vertical padding, Mono uppercase headings, warm-gray links.
- **Rule:** Use column gaps instead of dividers for separation.
