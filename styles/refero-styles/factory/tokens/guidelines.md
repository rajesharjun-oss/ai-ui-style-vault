# Guidelines

## Do

- Keep the canvas `#101010` across the experience.
- Use light cards (`#eeeeee`) as the only major bright objects.
- Use Geist `400` for nearly everything.
- Use weight `500` only for dense emphasis, footer headings, or key CTAs.
- Apply negative letter spacing proportionally to size.
- Reserve `#ee6018` for live status, build-state indicators, and accent strokes in charts.
- Reserve `#a0ca92` for positive metrics, trends, and system-state marks.
- Use `3px` radius on buttons and nav, `10px` on cards, and `20px` on the largest panels.
- Build depth through contrast, spacing, and hairline borders.
- Use Geist Mono `12px` uppercase for eyebrows, status labels, and column headers.

## Don't

- Do not introduce additional accent colors.
- Do not use bold heading weights.
- Do not use orange or green as large button backgrounds, card surfaces, or major text fills.
- Do not use line-height above `1.5`.
- Do not add drop shadows, glows, or blurs to cards, buttons, or modals.
- Do not mix in serif typefaces or decorative display fonts.
- Do not make the design soft with pill buttons or very large radii.
- Do not use decorative gradients; color should read as data, not ornament.
