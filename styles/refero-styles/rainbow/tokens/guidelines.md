# Guidelines

## Do

- Use near-white backgrounds and white cards.
- Use black for the main filled CTA.
- Use Inter for the entire interface.
- Keep wallet data compact and easy to scan.
- Use 20px card radius and 12px control radius.
- Use 9999px pills for badges, filters, and token chips.
- Use rainbow color only in tiny accent moments.
- Make touch targets at least 40px high.
- Use soft shadows and soft borders.

## Don't

- Do not turn the entire interface into a rainbow gradient.
- Do not use multiple filled CTA colors.
- Do not use harsh black borders around every card.
- Do not add heavy shadows or glassmorphism.
- Do not use serif or monospace fonts.
- Do not make desktop layouts feel like enterprise dashboards.
- Do not overload token rows with too much metadata.
