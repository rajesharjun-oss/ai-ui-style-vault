# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Grammar

Rainbow favors mobile-first wallet composition: centered phone mockups, compact app panels, friendly rounded cards, and short action-focused sections.

## Common Layouts

- Hero with large friendly headline, black CTA, app-store buttons, and phone mockup.
- Wallet balance card with token list below.
- Token rows grouped into rounded white panels.
- Feature cards with tiny pastel badges and app icons.
- Onboarding checklist with a single black action.
- Segmented tabs for wallet sections like tokens, NFTs, and activity.

## Imagery

- Use phone screenshots, app mockups, wallet cards, token icons, NFT previews, and tiny app illustrations.
- Keep mockups bright and legible.
- Avoid enterprise dashboard screenshots, stock photography, dark cinematic scenes, and dense data tables.

## Motion

- Keep transitions short and friendly: `150ms` to `250ms`.
- Use small press/hover feedback on buttons and cards.
- Use gentle spring only for mobile-app-like interactions.
- Avoid dramatic parallax or cinematic transitions.
