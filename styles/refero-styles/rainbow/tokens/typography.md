# Typography Tokens

## Fonts

| Font | Token | Role | Substitute |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inter | `--font-inter` | All UI, body, headings, labels, buttons. | system sans |

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Line Height | Weight | Token |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| caption | `12px` | `1.33` | `500` | `--text-caption` |
| label | `13px` | `1.38` | `600` | `--text-label` |
| body-sm | `14px` | `1.43` | `400` | `--text-body-sm` |
| body | `16px` | `1.5` | `400` | `--text-body` |
| balance | `32px` | `1.1` | `700` | `--text-balance` |
| heading-sm | `24px` | `1.2` | `700` | `--text-heading-sm` |
| heading | `40px` | `1.1` | `700` | `--text-heading` |
| display | `64px` | `1.0` | `800` | `--text-display` |

## Font Rules

- Use Inter everywhere.
- Keep wallet rows compact around `14px`.
- Use bold weight for balances and CTA labels.
- Keep letter spacing normal.
- Avoid serif fonts, monospace, and ornate display faces.
