# Guidelines

## Do

- Use `#e42b0c` only for primary action and conversion signals.
- Use the Sunset Flux gradient only for the nav conversion button.
- Apply 8px radius uniformly across cards, buttons, inputs, tags, nav, and images.
- Use Slate 900 for display and heading text.
- Set body copy at 16px to 18px with 1.5 line height.
- Set large display type at 48px to 72px with tight negative tracking.
- Keep shadows small and sparse.
- Use product UI, metrics, logo grids, and operational visuals.
- Keep the page monochrome until an action needs vermillion.

## Do Not

- Do not use 4px, 6px, 12px, or pill radii for core UI.
- Do not introduce a second saturated color.
- Do not use vermillion as decorative body text or random illustration fill.
- Do not set display text at weight 700.
- Do not set body text below 14px.
- Do not use heavy shadows or glassmorphism.
- Do not use lifestyle photography or abstract 3D renders.
- Do not place Carbon panels without a clear emphasis reason.

## Accessibility Notes

- Slate 900 on Paper is strong for primary reading.
- Slate 600 is appropriate for supporting copy but should not carry critical tiny text.
- Vermillion buttons need white text and enough surface space.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus, preferably with a crisp dark border.
