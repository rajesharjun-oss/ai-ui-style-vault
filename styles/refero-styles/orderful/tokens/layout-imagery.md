# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Pattern

- Sticky white nav with Frost divider.
- 1200px centered container.
- Split hero on Vellum canvas.
- Left hero text plus single Vermillion CTA.
- Right product visual tile with dark panels and connector path.
- Full-width Paper logo strip.
- Vellum feature sections with centered display headings.
- Three-column feature card grid.
- Testimonial section with quote, customer visual, and stat row.
- Alternating Paper and Vellum bands.

## Content Width

- Page max width: 1200px.
- Hero split: about 45% text, 55% visual.
- Lede text: about 480px to 640px.
- Grid gap: about 32px.

## Imagery

Use:

- Product UI mockups.
- Trading partner brand grids.
- Vermillion connector lines and nodes.
- Grayscale trust logos.
- Tight 64px customer headshots.
- Metrics and award badges.
- Simple geometric icons.

Avoid:

- Lifestyle photography.
- Environmental portraits.
- Abstract gradients.
- 3D renders.
- Decorative saturated colors beyond vermillion.

## Motion

Keep interaction restrained:

- Button hover can deepen Vermillion.
- Card hover can darken border or add the subtle shadow.
- Connector path can animate if subtle.
- No playful bounce or dramatic parallax.
