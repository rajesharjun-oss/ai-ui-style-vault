# Typography

## Primary Typeface

Telegraf is the workhorse typeface.

- Use for: display, headings, body, nav, buttons, cards, icons, inputs, and footer.
- Substitute: Inter, Sohne, Suisse Int'l, or system sans-serif.
- Weights: 100, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700.
- Preferred headline range: 300 to 500.
- Avoid display text at weight 700.

## Secondary Typeface

ModernGothic is a narrow secondary register.

- Use for: selected 14px body or link contexts.
- Weight: 300.
- Substitute: Inter at light weight.
- Keep it sparse.

## Notes

- Use uppercase micro labels for industrial-eyebrow moments.
- Keep large text tight and precise.
- Use 16px to 18px for body copy.
- Do not go below 14px except for uppercase micro labels.
