# EPIC Agency Style Reference

## North Star

EPIC agency feels like a dark celestial command center for a creative studio. The interface is dramatic and cinematic, but it still uses crisp utility typography, compact controls, and clear navigation.

The main visual tension is between expressive serif display text and sharp, functional Inter UI text. Keep the drama in the hero, large statements, and celestial imagery. Keep everything else controlled, compact, and easy to scan.

## Color

The palette is dark violet with pale lavender text and small signal accents.

- `Void` `#110329`: deepest violet-black page canvas.
- `Midnight Plum` `#1b0642`: panels, overlays, hero depth, and secondary dark surfaces.
- `Cosmic Violet` `#532fbc`: brand energy, active states, luminous depth.
- `Astral Blue` `#2d4fa8`: cool contrast for orbital or digital details.
- `Lunar Lilac` `#cfc1ff`: primary readable text on dark surfaces.
- `Nebula Lavender` `#dbc9ff`: soft headings, supporting text, atmospheric glows.
- `Star Mist` `#f4efff`: brightest foreground text and tiny high-contrast marks.
- `Galactic Gold` `#bc994e`: premium signal accent, separators, selected details.
- `Ember` `#e3562d`: urgency, warm highlight, error-like signal.
- `Signal Green` `#3c8900`: success, launch, confirmation, positive status.

Use gold, ember, and green as tiny punctuation. The style becomes noisy if those colors become large surfaces.

## Typography

Use a split type system:

- Display family: `Sang Bleu`, with Georgia and serif fallbacks.
- Functional family: `Inter`, with system-ui and sans-serif fallbacks.

The display voice should feel editorial and grand. The UI voice should feel precise and contemporary.

Recommended roles:

- Micro: 10px to 11px, Inter, 500 to 600, uppercase tracking for labels.
- Caption: 13px, Inter, 400 to 500.
- Body: 14px to 16px, Inter, line-height 1.45 to 1.65.
- Card title: 18px, Inter 500 or Sang Bleu when the card is editorial.
- Statement: 42px, Sang Bleu, tight line-height.
- Hero: 80px, Sang Bleu, tight tracking and line-height.
- Monumental display: 120px, Sang Bleu, used only for first-screen impact.

Do not use Sang Bleu for nav, form labels, dense body copy, or operational UI.

## Spacing And Layout

Density is compact. Use a 4px base unit, but allow large theatrical gaps in hero and portfolio sections.

- Max content width: about 1200px.
- Hero sections can use 80px to 120px vertical rhythm.
- UI clusters should use tight 4px to 12px spacing.
- Cards and panels can use 20px to 32px internal padding.
- Navigation should feel compressed and intentional.

Layout should often feel orbital or staged:

- Full dark canvas.
- Hero statement offset against a celestial or 3D focal object.
- Compact nav with small uppercase or semi-bold labels.
- Floating action clusters, small status chips, and premium accent details.
- Project/service sections in dark cards or framed rows.

## Shape And Elevation

The system mixes rounded action controls with sharper menu geometry.

- Buttons and primary CTAs: 20px to 30px radius.
- Tags and chips: pill or softly rounded.
- Panels and cards: 10px to 20px radius depending on scale.
- Menu items may be square with 0px radius when the layout needs a technical feel.

Elevation should feel like depth in space, not standard SaaS card shadows. Use glows, layered dark panels, gradients inside dark ranges, and subtle borders.

## Components

### Hero Statement

Use Sang Bleu at large sizes with tight line-height. Pair it with a dark celestial visual, not a generic abstract gradient.

### Primary CTA

Use a rounded control with high contrast foreground text. It can use violet, gold, or light text depending on the surface, but keep the action restrained and premium.

### Compact Navigation

Use Inter, small sizes, and tight spacing. Navigation can sit directly on the dark canvas without a heavy bar.

### Signal Chips

Use small rounded labels for status, categories, launch markers, and awards. Accent colors should be tiny and precise.

### Celestial Media Block

Use orbital diagrams, star fields, luminous planets, or 3D objects as the main visual anchor. The media should feel custom and art-directed.

### Dark Portfolio Card

Use dark panels, soft radius, pale lavender text, and small accent marks. Avoid plain white cards.

## Imagery

Use celestial, orbital, and cosmic strategy imagery:

- 3D planets, moons, orbit paths, glowing celestial forms.
- Technical star maps and dark diagrams.
- Premium editorial project imagery set inside dark frames.
- Fine grain, glow, and layered atmospheric depth.

Avoid generic stock people, flat SaaS illustrations, cheerful pastel graphics, or all-white product screenshots unless they are framed intentionally against the dark canvas.

## Do

- Use the violet-black canvas as the dominant surface.
- Reserve Sang Bleu for large display moments.
- Keep Inter for all functional UI.
- Use lavender text for comfortable dark-mode readability.
- Use gold, ember, and green only as small signal accents.
- Use celestial imagery as the first-viewport anchor.
- Keep navigation compact and confident.

## Do Not

- Do not turn the accent colors into broad backgrounds.
- Do not use Sang Bleu for dense body text.
- Do not make the design a standard card-heavy SaaS layout.
- Do not introduce bright rainbow gradients.
- Do not use white as a dominant page section.
- Do not over-round every surface into soft consumer UI.
