# Colors

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Void | `#110329` | `--color-void` | Dominant dark violet-black canvas |
| Midnight Plum | `#1b0642` | `--color-midnight-plum` | Panels, overlays, depth surfaces |
| Cosmic Violet | `#532fbc` | `--color-cosmic-violet` | Brand energy, active states, luminous depth |
| Astral Blue | `#2d4fa8` | `--color-astral-blue` | Cool contrast, orbital detail, digital signal |
| Lunar Lilac | `#cfc1ff` | `--color-lunar-lilac` | Primary readable text on dark |
| Nebula Lavender | `#dbc9ff` | `--color-nebula-lavender` | Soft headings, support text, glows |
| Star Mist | `#f4efff` | `--color-star-mist` | Bright foreground and tiny high-contrast marks |
| Galactic Gold | `#bc994e` | `--color-galactic-gold` | Premium accent, selected detail, separator |
| Ember | `#e3562d` | `--color-ember` | Warm signal, urgent highlight |
| Signal Green | `#3c8900` | `--color-signal-green` | Success, launch, positive status |

## Guidance

- Keep `Void` and `Midnight Plum` dominant.
- Use lavender tones for readability.
- Use gold, ember, and green as small status or premium marks only.
- Avoid large white sections and bright rainbow gradients.
