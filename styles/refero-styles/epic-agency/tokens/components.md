# Components

## Hero Statement

- Family: Sang Bleu
- Size: 80px to 120px on desktop
- Color: `#f4efff`, `#dbc9ff`, or `#cfc1ff`
- Pair with a celestial or orbital media object.

## Primary CTA

- Radius: 20px to 30px
- Text: Inter, compact, 13px to 14px
- Background: violet, dark plum, gold detail, or light text depending on surface
- Use sparingly and keep the label concise.

## Compact Navigation

- Family: Inter
- Size: 10px to 14px
- Weight: 500 to 600
- Color: lavender or star mist
- Background: transparent or dark overlay
- Keep the nav compressed and precise.

## Signal Chip

- Radius: pill or softly rounded
- Text: Inter, 10px to 13px
- Use gold, ember, green, or violet as small marks.

## Celestial Media Block

- Visual role: hero anchor
- Content: 3D planet, orbital map, star field, moon, or cosmic diagram
- Surface: dark, layered, luminous
- Avoid generic screenshots as the primary visual.

## Dark Portfolio Card

- Background: `#1b0642` or layered dark violet
- Text: lavender and star mist
- Radius: 10px to 20px
- Border: subtle violet or translucent lavender
- Accent: tiny gold or violet detail

## Footer

- Background: `#110329`
- Text: lavender or muted violet
- Links: compact Inter
- Keep it dark and restrained.
