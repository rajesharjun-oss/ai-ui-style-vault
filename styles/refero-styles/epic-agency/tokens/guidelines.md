# Guidelines

## Do

- Use a violet-black canvas as the dominant surface.
- Put the strongest personality into the hero display text and celestial media.
- Use Sang Bleu only for large editorial moments.
- Use Inter for body, nav, captions, forms, and buttons.
- Keep gold, ember, and green as precise signal accents.
- Use dark layered panels with subtle glow and fine borders.
- Keep navigation compact and intentional.

## Do Not

- Do not use Sang Bleu for dense body copy.
- Do not turn every section into a rounded card.
- Do not use broad gold, green, or ember backgrounds.
- Do not introduce rainbow gradients or bright pastel palettes.
- Do not make the page mostly white.
- Do not use generic stock imagery as the hero.
- Do not let decoration overpower the content hierarchy.
