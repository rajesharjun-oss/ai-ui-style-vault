# Typography

## Families

- Display: `"Sang Bleu", Georgia, serif`
- UI and body: `"Inter", system-ui, sans-serif`

## Roles

| Role | Size | Family | Weight | Line Height | Use |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| Micro | 10px to 11px | Inter | 500 to 600 | 1.1 to 1.3 | Menu labels, tiny status, technical tags |
| Caption | 13px | Inter | 400 to 500 | 1.45 | Metadata and supporting copy |
| Body | 14px to 16px | Inter | 400 | 1.45 to 1.65 | Body text and descriptions |
| Card Title | 18px | Inter or Sang Bleu | 500 or 400 | 1.2 to 1.35 | Card headings |
| Statement | 42px | Sang Bleu | 400 | 0.95 to 1.05 | Section statements |
| Hero | 80px | Sang Bleu | 400 | 0.9 to 1.0 | First-screen headline |
| Monumental | 120px | Sang Bleu | 400 | 0.85 to 0.95 | Rare oversized display moment |

## Guidance

- Use Sang Bleu only when the size is large enough to matter.
- Use Inter for all operational UI.
- Keep display line-height tight.
- Use uppercase tracking for tiny labels when needed.
- Do not set dense paragraphs in Sang Bleu.
