# Typography

## Families

| Family | Token | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Satoshi | `--font-satoshi` | Display headings, nav, buttons, UI labels, editorial product copy |
| Space Mono | `--font-space-mono` | Market data, tickers, terminal labels, code, numeric tables |

## Scale

| Name | Size | Line Height | Weight | Tracking | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Caption | `12px` | `1.45` | `500` | `0.08em` | Uppercase labels and metadata |
| Data | `13px` | `1.5` | `500` | `0.03em` | Tickers, prices, table numerics |
| Body Small | `14px` | `1.6` | `400` | `0` | Dense UI copy and card text |
| Body | `16px` | `1.65` | `400` | `0` | Main body copy |
| Lead | `20px` | `1.55` | `400` | `-0.01em` | Hero support and section intros |
| H3 | `28px` | `1.18` | `600` | `-0.03em` | Panel and section headings |
| H2 | `44px` | `1.08` | `600` | `-0.05em` | Major section headings |
| H1 | `72px` | `0.96` | `700` | `-0.07em` | Hero title |
| Display | `104px` | `0.9` | `700` | `-0.08em` | Oversized campaign or product moment |

## Rules

- Use Satoshi for clear institutional confidence.
- Use Space Mono only where the content is technical, tabular, or market-like.
- Keep hero headings tight and large; keep dashboard labels small.
- Do not set paragraphs in Space Mono.
