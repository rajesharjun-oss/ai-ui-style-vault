# Guidelines

## Do

- Use black and abyss surfaces as the main canvas.
- Reserve cyan and blue beams for key focus areas, charts, and hero depth.
- Make hero typography oversized, tight, and institutional.
- Use pill geometry for CTAs, nav chips, tags, and ticker rails.
- Pair Satoshi for product confidence with Space Mono for trading and terminal credibility.
- Put translucent panels over glow; avoid flat bright backdrops.
- Use green for gain/live/liquidity and red for risk/loss only.
- Keep dashboards dense but calm with thin dividers and compact labels.
- Use luminous maps, nodes, and networks as infrastructure imagery.

## Don't

- Do not introduce warm oranges beyond controlled gold metric emphasis.
- Do not use the aurora gradient as a full-page fill.
- Do not use Space Mono for long body copy.
- Do not create light theme sections.
- Do not round major cards beyond the controlled card radius.
- Do not make the design feel like a generic blue-white CRM.
- Do not overanimate dashboards; motion should feel like live data.
- Do not rely on lifestyle photography as the main visual system.
