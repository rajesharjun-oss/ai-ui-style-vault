# Integrated Biosciences - Style Reference

Integrated Biosciences feels like a darkroom laboratory: fluorescent green data, matte-black scientific panels, monochrome microscopy, and thin ruled structures. The style is sparse, technical, and quietly confident.

## Visual Thesis

- Use black and graphite as the lab environment.
- Use lime as the single living signal color.
- Make lines, borders, and uppercase labels do the organizational work.
- Avoid decorative styling, rounded cards, and soft marketing warmth.
- Let imagery feel like research evidence: grids, specimens, instrumentation, cells, or charts.

## Core Ingredients

- Canvas: `#000000` and `#0c0c0c`.
- Accent: Bio Signal `#cef79e`, used with restraint.
- Type: one Aspekta-like geometric sans across all text.
- Shape: square corners everywhere, including buttons and image containers.
- Grid: columns, rules, and vertical rhythm based on scientific documentation.
- Motion: minimal opacity and transform changes only.

## Page Composition

Start with a sparse nav, then a black hero with a large uppercase or sentence-case display statement. Use thin divider lines to structure the page. Alternate between dark feature panels, evidence-style image blocks, diagram rows, and compact research cards. Leave deliberate empty space.

## Color Behavior

The whole style depends on color restraint. Lime should appear as:

- primary CTA text or fill
- live data highlight
- dot, marker, or diagram signal
- small scientific emphasis
- selected interactive state

Do not introduce extra accent colors. Do not make rainbow charts. This is a one-signal system.

## Type Behavior

Aspekta is the whole voice: display, labels, body, and controls. Use weight 400 for almost everything. Use uppercase labels with 0.08em to 0.16em tracking. Do not swap in a serif, condensed display face, or playful monospace.
