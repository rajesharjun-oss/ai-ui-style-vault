# Guidelines

## Do

- Use black and dark graphite as the main environment.
- Use Bio Signal lime only for meaningful signal.
- Keep all cards, buttons, plates, and inputs square.
- Use 1px rule lines to structure the page.
- Use a single Aspekta-like sans family throughout.
- Use uppercase tracked labels for scientific metadata.
- Prefer flat surfaces over depth effects.
- Use evidence-style imagery such as microscopy, cells, assay diagrams, charts, and lab hardware.

## Do Not

- Do not add rounded SaaS cards.
- Do not add gradients, glows, or soft blur decoration.
- Do not introduce extra accent colors.
- Do not use shadows as the primary surface separator.
- Do not use stock lifestyle photography.
- Do not mix in serif display type.
- Do not overuse lime on large fills or long paragraphs.
- Do not make the interface playful, warm, or consumer-finance-like.
