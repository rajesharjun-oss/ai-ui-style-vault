# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a strict max-width container around 1240px.
- Let sections breathe with 80px to 120px vertical gaps.
- Use thin horizontal and vertical rules to divide space.
- Favor two-column scientific editorial layouts.
- Use card rows, data grids, and evidence plates instead of rounded marketing modules.
- Use black hero sections with high-contrast display text.
- Keep navigation sparse and technical.

## Imagery

- Use scientific evidence imagery: microscopy, cellular textures, assay visuals, molecule diagrams, graphs, and lab equipment.
- Crop images as square or rectangular plates with no radius.
- Keep images cool, monochrome, green-tinted, or low saturation.
- Pair imagery with captions, labels, or rule-line metadata.
- Avoid abstract gradient blobs and generic team photos.

## Motion

- Use minimal transitions: opacity, color, and slight transform.
- Transition duration: 180ms to 240ms.
- Avoid bouncy or playful easing.
- Scientific interfaces should feel measured and exact.
