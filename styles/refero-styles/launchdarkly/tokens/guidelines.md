# Guidelines

## Do

- Use 30px radius for buttons, tags, tabs, and cards.
- Use 60px radius for the floating nav pill.
- Use Signal Violet for the second line or key phrase in hero headlines.
- Render embedded product screenshots as pure white panels on dark canvas.
- Use the 179deg Voltage Blue to Signal Violet gradient for ambient glows.
- Set body text at 16px to 18px for readability on dark.
- Use mono font for SDK names, code, and technical identifiers.
- Keep feature lists left-aligned outside hero blocks.

## Don't

- Do not use square corners or small 4px to 8px radii on user-facing components.
- Do not use drop shadows for normal elevation; use glow instead.
- Do not introduce additional accent colors.
- Do not use white or gray for primary action buttons.
- Do not center-align body copy or feature lists.
- Do not use display headline line-height above 1.5.
- Do not put dark product screenshots on the page.
