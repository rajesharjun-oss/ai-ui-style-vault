# Guidelines

## Do

- Use 100px border radius for buttons, nav items, and pills.
- Set display headlines at weight 400 with tight negative letter spacing.
- Reserve Iris Glow for icons, eyebrow labels, links, and active states.
- Place the NeueMachinaInktrap eyebrow label above every major section headline.
- Layer at least one gradient on every page background.
- Set max content width to 1280px and keep backgrounds full bleed.
- Use FrameGothic at 14px to 18px for body and feature text in Smoke or Ash.
- Frame product imagery in 10px-radius containers with a 1px Twilight border.

## Don't

- Do not use weight 600 or 700 for display headlines.
- Do not introduce additional accent colors.
- Do not use flat black as a full-page background.
- Do not add card backgrounds or heavy borders to feature columns.
- Do not use serif fonts or decorative typefaces.
- Do not add drop shadows to buttons or nav items.
- Do not center-align body paragraphs.
- Do not use the eyebrow label style for anything except section identifiers.
