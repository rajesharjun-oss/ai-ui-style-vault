# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Forest Depths for CTAs, dark sections, primary text, and brand-defining surfaces.
- Use Lime Pulse only for sale badges, new tags, highlight pills, and tiny active emphasis.
- Use 1000px radius for buttons, badges, tags, and pill controls.
- Use thin heading weights at 32px and larger.
- Slightly tighten tracking at 24px and larger.
- Use Seed Sans Mono for product codes, formula references, and ingredient specs.
- Keep section backgrounds mostly Snow White or Forest Depths.

## Do Not

- Do not use heavy shadows or elevation.
- Do not use gradients as page or section backgrounds.
- Do not use pure white as the main canvas.
- Do not introduce loud saturated accents outside the green/lime system.
- Do not make cards glossy, thickly bordered, or dashboard-like.
- Do not set display headlines in 600+ weights.

## AI Implementation Notes

- If an implementation lacks Seed Sans, use Inter with 300, 400, and 500 weights.
- If using product images, keep them isolated and clinical rather than lifestyle-heavy.
- If a screen starts to feel too empty, add ingredient/spec content, small mono labels, or quiet section rhythm rather than decoration.
- If a screen starts to feel too flat, use scale, whitespace, and alternating Forest Depths sections rather than shadows.
