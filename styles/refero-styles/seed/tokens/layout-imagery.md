# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a 1200px max-width container.
- Keep the default page canvas Snow White.
- Alternate light educational sections with Forest Depths product or CTA bands.
- Use sticky navigation with a calm product-brand rhythm.
- Give hero sections enough air for thin display type to breathe.
- Use compact product metadata rather than big loud labels.

## Section Patterns

- Hero: thin display headline, concise science-backed copy, pill CTA group, clinical product imagery.
- Product band: Forest Depths background, embedded product cards, Snow White text, Lime Pulse badges.
- Education section: Snow White background, airy grid, soft dividers, thin headings.
- Formula/spec section: mono labels, clear lists, restrained spacing, minimal borders.
- Checkout or subscription UI: pill options, green primary action, quiet validation states.

## Imagery

- Use isolated product packaging, capsule details, ingredient textures, soft lab-like closeups, or hands in neutral lighting.
- Keep backgrounds warm and nearly white.
- Prefer calm crop, high detail, and low visual noise.
- Avoid moody black photography, loud lifestyle scenes, decorative gradients, and cartoon botanicals.
