# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use full-bleed dark sections.
- Center content inside a 1200px max-width.
- Hero: split roughly 55/45, text left, boarding-pass card right.
- Section rhythm: 120px vertical gaps.
- Feature sections: stamped mono heading above rows.
- Comparison block: four-column card grid connected by route-line markers.
- Footer: coordinate stamp closing gesture.

## Imagery

Use:

- One atmospheric twilight sky hero image.
- Purple and lavender clouds over a glowing horizon.
- Minimal line glyphs in Almost White.
- Violet radial bloom cards.
- Route-line graphics and circular node markers.

Avoid:

- Product screenshots after the hero.
- Lifestyle stock photos.
- Repeated photography.
- Multi-column body text.
- Extra accent colors.
