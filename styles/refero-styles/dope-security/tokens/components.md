# Components

## Hero Boarding Pass

Full-viewport atmospheric hero. Left column uses GrandSlang italic plus Whyte Inktrap security headline text. Right column uses a translucent 19.2px boarding-pass card with pink wash, low-opacity Almost White border, label, origin/destination row, barcode, and two huge pill CTAs.

## Stamped Section Heading

Whyte Inktrap Mono 74px, uppercase, 0.2em tracking, Soft White. Use as a full-width section signpost with no underline or extra ornament.

## Filled Action Button

Near Black background, 1px Almost White border, 8px radius, 16px padding, Almost White text in Whyte Inktrap 16px/400.

## Ghost Pill Button

Pink Wash fill, no border, 1584px radius, 20px by 32px padding, Almost White text. Use inside cards and hero pass areas.

## Compact Outlined Button

Almost White at 8 percent fill, 1px Almost White border, 6px radius, 9px by 15px padding. Use in compact toolbars and rows.

## Frosted Nav Bar

Fixed top bar with Frosted Nav background, backdrop blur(10px), 1px Ash bottom border, brand left, tracked uppercase nav center, and two bordered actions right.

## Comparison Card

19.2px radius, full-bleed gradient or violet bloom background, 40px padding, no border, no shadow, route-line markers between cards.

## Feature Row Card

Transparent row, 19.2px radius, no visible container, left link, right Whyte body copy. Spacing creates the boundary.

## Violet Bloom Card

Signal Violet or violet radial bloom, 19.2px radius, 40px padding. Use one or two times per page maximum.

## Coordinate Footer

Dark footer with plus icon, product labels, live-like coordinate stamp, and heart icon in Whyte Inktrap 14px.

## Hairline Divider

0.5px or 1px Almost White stroke at 10 to 20 percent opacity. Use instead of shadows.
