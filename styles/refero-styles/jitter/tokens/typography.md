# Typography

## Families

- Display/headings: `"TWK Lausanne", "Inter Tight", "Switzer", "General Sans", system-ui, sans-serif`
- Body/UI: `"Inter", system-ui, sans-serif`

## Roles

| Role | Size | Family | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Use |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| UI Label | 12px to 14px | Inter | 500 | 1.1 to 1.5 | 0 | Nav, badges, small controls |
| Body | 15px to 18px | Inter | 400 to 500 | 1.4 to 1.6 | -0.017em to -0.023em | Paragraphs and card copy |
| Inter Subhead | 26px | Inter | 600 to 800 | 1.2 | -0.02em | Medium text tier only |
| Section Heading | 40px to 48px | TWK Lausanne | 700 to 800 | 1.0 to 1.2 | about -0.03em | Feature headings |
| Hero | 80px | TWK Lausanne | 750 to 800 | 0.95 | -0.032em | First-screen display |
| Monumental | 200px | TWK Lausanne | 800 | 0.85 | -0.044em | Rare oversized display |

## Guidance

- Use TWK Lausanne for display and major headings.
- Use Inter for nav, body, cards, forms, badges, and buttons.
- Do not set display text above 26px in Inter.
- Keep display line-height compressed.
- Keep headings tightly tracked and engineered.
