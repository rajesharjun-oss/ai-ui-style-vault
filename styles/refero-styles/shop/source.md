## What To Preserve

Shop uses a white commerce canvas, soft blue-gray product surfaces, large rounded cards, pill controls, and product photography. The style is clean but not sterile because product imagery, 28px cards, and the violet search action create character.

The page relies on:

- Pure White as the full page background.
- Mist Blue and Product Mist for soft retail surfaces.
- Ink Black for titles, nav labels, and high-confidence actions.
- Graphite and Ash for commerce metadata and secondary copy.
- Shop Violet for the primary search/action moment.
- Checkout Green only for payment/trust/status states.
- GTStandard-MRegular as the full interface typeface.
- 9999px pills for search, category tabs, and CTA controls.
- 28px product cards, usually flat.
- Literal product photography, merchant logos, app screenshots, and product grids.
