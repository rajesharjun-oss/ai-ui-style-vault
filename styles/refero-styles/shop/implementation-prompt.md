# Implementation Prompt

Build a clean commerce-discovery interface in the Shop style.

Use Pure White (`#ffffff`) as the main page canvas. Use Mist Blue (`#f4f8fa`) for soft commerce sections, Product Mist (`#f0f4f5`) and Soft Gray (`#f5f5f5`) for product card wells, Ink Black (`#171717`) for primary text, Graphite (`#666666`) for body/merchant metadata, Ash (`#999999`) for tertiary labels, and Hairline (`#d9d9d9`) for subtle borders. Use Shop Violet (`#5433eb`) only for the primary search/action control and active states. Use Checkout Green (`#008060`) only for checkout, trusted payment, or success messaging.

Use GTStandard-MRegular or a Shopify Sans/Inter/system fallback for all text. Keep most text weight 400. Use 600 only for short UI emphasis. Display headings can reach 80px with -0.02em tracking. Product names are about 18px with slight negative tracking. Body copy is 16px/1.5. Labels are 12px to 13px with slight positive tracking.

Use a 4px base grid, 1200px max width, 64px to 96px section gaps, 20px to 28px card padding, and 12px to 20px element gaps. Shape is a core signature: 9999px for search bars, category pills, buttons, and tabs; 28px for product cards; 20px for lower cards; 12px for icon containers. Default product cards are flat. Use light shadow only for floating search or hover.

Expected components:

- Header nav: white or Mist Blue, wordmark left, compact links, optional download CTA, no heavy border.
- Search bar: Pure White fill, 9999px radius, subtle Hairline border or floating shadow, left icon, Graphite placeholder, violet submit button.
- Violet search button: Shop Violet fill, white icon/text, 9999px radius.
- Category pill: Pure White or Product Mist fill, Ink Black text, 9999px radius, active state with black fill or violet indicator.
- Product card: Product Mist or Soft Gray fill, 28px radius, bright product photography, product name, merchant, and price metadata.
- Product grid: 3 or 4 columns on desktop, wide gutters, photography-dominant cards.
- Mobile app mockup: product feed, tracking, checkout, or search UI with white/mist panels and violet action control.
- Trust row: merchant logos, payment marks, or app stats in black/gray, green only for verified checkout.
- Promo band: Mist Blue background, big heading, product collage, one violet or black pill CTA.

The interface should feel fast, consumer-friendly, clean, and shopping-native. Avoid enterprise SaaS density, abstract graphics, second accent colors, heavy universal shadows, dark hero sections, and product cards with square corners.
