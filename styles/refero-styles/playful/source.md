## Source Summary

The source describes Playful as a light, warm-paper product style. Its identifiers are Oat Canvas background, Hot Magenta as the only chromatic accent, Inter-only typography, oversized italic display headlines, rounded card and pill geometry, deep diffused shadows, and illustration-led app tiles instead of photography.
