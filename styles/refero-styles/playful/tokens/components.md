# Components

## Primary Pill CTA

- Fill: Hot Magenta.
- Text: white or black, selected for contrast context.
- Font: Inter 600, 16px.
- Radius: 99px.
- Padding: 14px 24px.
- Shadow: none.

## Email Input Composite

- Wrapper: Paper White, 99px radius.
- Input fill: Oat Canvas.
- Input border: 1px solid Charcoal.
- Placeholder: Inter 400, 16px, muted gray.
- CTA: Hot Magenta pill placed inside the same wrapper.
- The whole unit should read as one control.

## Editorial Display Headline

- Font: Inter.
- Weight: 700 or 900.
- Style: italic.
- Size: 70px to 79px.
- Line height: 1.
- Color: Ink Black or Soft Ink.
- Alignment: centered.

## App Tile Card

- Surface: Paper White.
- Radius: 44px.
- Shadow: card shadow stack.
- Size: roughly 140px to 180px square.
- Content: centered soft 3D illustration.
- Arrangement: slightly tilted, overlapping row.

## FAQ Accordion Card

- Surface: Paper White.
- Radius: 44px.
- Optional warm border.
- Shadow: card shadow stack.
- Question: Inter 600 italic, 16px to 18px.
- Icon: Stone stroke chevron.
- Stack gap: 12px to 16px.

## Category Navigation Bar

- Full-width black band.
- Text: white Inter 500, 14px to 16px.
- Direction: horizontal scroll.
- Item gap: 24px to 32px.
- No extra border or hover fill.

## Logo Wordmark

- Hot Magenta wordmark.
- Inter 700 italic.
- Paired with small rounded-square geometric mark.

## Gradient Highlight Section

- Full-width soft gradient.
- Blend Hot Magenta through lavender-pink into Oat Canvas.
- No hard edge.
