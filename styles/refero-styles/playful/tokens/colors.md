# Colors

## Brand

| Name | Value | Token | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hot Magenta | `#ff2e95` | `--color-hot-magenta` | Primary CTA, logo mark, links, accent strokes |

## Neutrals

| Name | Value | Token | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink Black | `#000000` | `--color-ink-black` | Main heading text, button text, heavy borders, dark nav band |
| Oat Canvas | `#f6f2ee` | `--color-oat-canvas` | Page background and warm cream surface |
| Soft Ink | `#111111` | `--color-soft-ink` | Body text and headings on light surfaces |
| Slate | `#0f172a` | `--color-slate` | Secondary copy and muted body text |
| Charcoal | `#414040` | `--color-charcoal` | Form states, badge text, neutral UI feedback |
| Stone | `#848383` | `--color-stone` | Icon strokes, helper text, subtle dividers |
| Paper White | `#ffffff` | `--color-paper-white` | Card surfaces and elevated panels |
| Warm Mist | `#e8e5e0` | `--color-warm-mist` | Hairline borders and dividers |
| Sand | `#e2dcd6` | `--color-sand` | Secondary borders on warm surfaces |
| Driftwood | `#c3c1bf` | `--color-driftwood` | Muted fills and shadow-adjacent tones |
| Midnight | `#202126` | `--color-midnight` | Deep heading variant |

## Application Notes

- Hot Magenta is the only chromatic accent.
- Oat Canvas should be the full-page background.
- Paper White cards must sit on Oat Canvas, not on white.
- Use Soft Ink or Slate for body text to avoid harsh black-on-white reading.
- Stone is the default icon and helper-text color.
