# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Centered max-width container around 1200px.
- Major vertical sections separated by about 113px.
- Hero is a centered stack: display headline, subtext, email CTA composite, app tile row.
- A full-width black category nav band cuts across the warm page.
- A soft magenta gradient highlight section appears mid-page.
- FAQ uses two columns: heading and label on the left, accordion stack on the right.
- Final CTA returns to centered composition.

## Hero Recipe

1. Oat Canvas background.
2. Hot Magenta logo in header.
3. Oversized Inter italic display headline.
4. Quiet Slate subtext, centered, max width around 560px.
5. White pill email composite with Hot Magenta CTA.
6. Overlapping, tilted app tile cards with soft 3D illustrations.

## Imagery

- Use illustration, not photography.
- Use soft 3D icons and rounded mascot-like objects.
- Saturated illustration colors can live inside app tiles.
- Keep UI icons as gray stroke icons.
- Avoid lifestyle imagery, realistic portraits, and device mockup hero scenes.

## Visual Rhythm

The page alternates between quiet cream sections and one energetic magenta gradient wash. The app tiles provide playful detail, but the main page should still feel spacious and editorial.
