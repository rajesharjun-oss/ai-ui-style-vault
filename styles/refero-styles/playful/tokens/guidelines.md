# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Oat Canvas for all full-page surfaces.
- Use Hot Magenta only for the primary CTA, logo, links, and small accent strokes.
- Use heavy italic Inter for display headlines.
- Use 44px card radius and 99px button/input radius.
- Apply the card shadow only to cards, app tiles, and FAQ rows.
- Pair email inputs with the CTA inside one white pill container.
- Center hero compositions.
- Keep icons stroke-based in Stone.

## Do Not

- Do not use upright bold display headlines.
- Do not introduce extra accent colors.
- Do not use pure white for full-page backgrounds.
- Do not use 0px to 4px radii on cards or buttons.
- Do not stack more than two type weights on one screen.
- Do not place white cards on white.
- Do not use drop shadows on text, buttons, icons, or inputs.
- Do not make photography the primary image language.

## Accessibility Notes

- Check text contrast when using white text on Hot Magenta.
- Do not rely only on magenta for link recognition; add underline or clear affordance when needed.
- Keep the email composite keyboard navigable as separate input and button controls.
- Ensure large italic display type wraps cleanly on mobile.
