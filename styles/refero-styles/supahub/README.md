## Style Summary

Supahub is a sunlit feedback-platform style: mostly white space, one vivid violet for action, rounded tactile product surfaces, and decorative violet, pink, and amber gradient orbs behind product mockups. Bricolage Grotesque handles confident display statements while Inter keeps the product UI, captions, buttons, and body copy clear.

Use this style when an AI needs a friendly SaaS product page for feedback boards, feature requests, testimonials, changelogs, or customer-facing product loops.

## What To Borrow

- Voltage Violet `#862fe7` as the single primary action color.
- White canvas with fog, lavender, and mint support surfaces.
- Bricolage Grotesque display headlines at 48-56px with tight tracking.
- Inter for all dense UI and body copy.
- 12px buttons and small surfaces.
- 24px testimonial cards.
- 9999px avatars, tags, badges, and social proof stacks.
- Gradient orbs behind product mockups, never behind text or buttons.
- Testimonial walls as content wallpaper.
- Product preview cards with light chrome and one soft violet shadow.

## Avoid

- Promoting pink, amber, or mint to primary CTA colors.
- Using Bricolage Grotesque below 20px.
- Letting display headlines stretch across the full 1200px rail.
- Heavy card shadow systems.
- Enterprise-gray dashboard density.
- Purple gradients as full-page backgrounds.
- Decorative orbs behind readable text.
