## Color Behavior

Voltage Violet is the only primary action color. The rest of the palette provides atmosphere, surface separation, and testimonial/product texture.

Use Voltage Violet for:

- Primary CTA fills.
- Active nav states.
- Key accent labels.
- Section eyebrow text.
- High-emphasis badges.

Use pink, amber, lavender, and mint for:

- Decorative gradient orbs.
- Soft highlight fields.
- Product mockup atmosphere.
- Supporting cards and badges.

## Gradient Orbs

Use orbs behind product mockups only:

- Violet orb: transparent edges with rgba violet around 0.4 opacity.
- Pink orb: transparent edges with rgba pink around 0.3 opacity.
- Amber orb: transparent edges with rgba amber around 0.4 opacity.

Do not place gradient orbs behind text or buttons.

## Forbidden Color Moves

- No pink primary CTA.
- No amber primary CTA.
- No mint navigation system.
- No full-page purple gradient.
- No violet text on low-contrast lavender without checking readability.
