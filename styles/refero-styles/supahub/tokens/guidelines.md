# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Voltage Violet as the single primary action color.
- Keep the page mostly white and airy.
- Use Bricolage Grotesque for confident display copy.
- Use Inter for all dense and functional UI copy.
- Keep display headlines inside a 680-720px column.
- Use gradient orbs behind product previews only.
- Use rounded testimonials and avatar stacks for social proof.
- Use lavender, mint, pink, and amber as atmosphere, not competing actions.

## Do Not

- Do not use Bricolage Grotesque below 20px.
- Do not let Inter do hero display duty.
- Do not promote pink, amber, or mint to the primary CTA role.
- Do not place gradient orbs behind text or buttons.
- Do not use heavy shadows for all cards.
- Do not stretch hero text across the full 1200px rail.
- Do not make the page feel like a dense enterprise dashboard.

## Implementation Priorities

1. Set the white canvas and Midnight Ink text.
2. Establish Voltage Violet primary actions.
3. Add a constrained Bricolage hero headline.
4. Build CTA pair and product preview.
5. Place a gradient orb behind the product preview.
6. Add testimonial and social proof systems.
7. Use lavender or fog bands for rhythm.

## Common Failure Modes

- The design becomes generic when violet is not the clear action color.
- It becomes noisy if every support color becomes an accent.
- It loses brand rhythm if Bricolage appears in tiny UI.
- It becomes hard to read if gradient orbs sit behind text.
