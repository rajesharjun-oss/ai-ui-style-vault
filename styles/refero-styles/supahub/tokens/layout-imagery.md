# Layout And Imagery

## Page Structure

1. White top nav with product logo, links, and CTA.
2. Hero with eyebrow, constrained display headline, support copy, CTA pair.
3. Floating product preview with gradient orb behind it.
4. Feature band in lavender, fog, or mint.
5. Product explanation sections.
6. Testimonial wall.
7. Avatar social proof strip.
8. Final CTA.

## Grid

- Max width: 1200px.
- Hero text width: 680-720px.
- Feature sections: 2 columns desktop, 1 column mobile.
- Testimonial walls: 2-3 columns desktop.
- Product previews: centered or offset inside the content rail.

## Imagery Rules

Use:

- Product preview cards.
- Feedback board screenshots.
- Rounded testimonial cards.
- Avatar stacks.
- Gradient orbs behind mockups.
- Soft feature illustrations if they use the palette.

Avoid:

- Stock photography as the main hero.
- Full-page purple gradients.
- Orbs behind text.
- Dense dashboard grids without white space.
- Hard brutalist product cards.

## Gradient Atmosphere

Orbs should bleed from transparent to color and back to transparent. Keep opacity around 0.3-0.4 so the effect feels like light, not a solid blob.
