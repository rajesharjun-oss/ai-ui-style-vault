# Implementation Prompt

Build a premium dark fintech interface in the Origin Financial style.

Use a full-bleed near-black canvas (`#0f1011`) with deep black sections and restrained gray surfaces. Center the primary experience around oversized Lyon-style serif headlines in white or cloud white at weight 300, line-height around 0.9, and no bold styling. Pair the headline with quiet Swiss-style sans body text and Roboto Mono uppercase labels.

Use color sparingly but boldly. Reserve violet, cyan, pink, and periwinkle for full category cards, product visualizations, chart lines, or data signals. Do not scatter accent colors through small links or decorative borders. The main action should be a white button with black text, 8px radius, and a small trailing arrow.

Use 30px radius for major feature cards and category tiles, 16px for product/device panels, 8px for inputs and buttons, and pill radius for badges. Prefer color-step surface separation over shadows. If using shadow, use it only once for a high-value CTA or raised product element.

Layout should feel like a calm gallery: centered hero, generous 80px section gaps, max-width 1200px content, sticky glass top nav, 3-column category grids, tilted phone mockups, and occasional silver inverted stat cards. Avoid generic SaaS cards, bouncy motion, decorative gradients, and body copy in pure white.
