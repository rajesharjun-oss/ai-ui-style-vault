# Origin Financial - Style Reference

Origin Financial feels like a midnight gallery for personal wealth: black room, museum lighting, hushed serif headlines, and luminous category panels. The design makes finance feel calm, premium, and emotionally legible.

## Visual Thesis

- Dark fintech, but less trading-terminal and more private gallery.
- Oversized serif typography carries the brand emotion.
- Color appears as large semantic panels, not as decoration.
- White primary actions are the brightest and most certain elements on the page.
- Data accents use clean cyan and violet rather than harsh green/red finance cues.

## Core Ingredients

- Canvas: full-bleed near-black, usually `#0f1011`, with deeper black pockets.
- Typography: three voices - Lyon-style display serif, neutral Swiss sans, and Roboto Mono labels.
- Shape: soft rectangles, 8px controls, 16px devices, 30px feature cards, pill chips.
- Imagery: cloud/sky atmosphere and tilted iPhone product mockups on dark stages.
- Motion: restrained fades and long atmospheric reveals, no playful bounce.

## Page Composition

Start with a centered hero. Place a huge serif headline in white or cloud white. Add a compact AI prompt or CTA input below it, then move into product and category sections. Break the page into large dark modules, occasional silver inverted panels, and chromatic feature tiles.

## Color Behavior

The palette is not a normal accent system. Most of the page stays black, gray, silver, and white. Violet, cyan, pink, periwinkle, and iris tones should show up as:

- category card backgrounds
- chart lines or small data highlights
- product UI visualization moments
- soft panel fills for financial categories

Do not use these colors for lots of small text links, borders, or random badges.

## Type Behavior

Headlines use a Lyon-style display serif at weight 300 with tight line-height. UI and body use a low-contrast Swiss sans. Labels, data, badges, and tiny uppercase elements use Roboto Mono.

The strongest Origin Financial move is the contrast between a huge soft serif statement and very precise mono labels.
