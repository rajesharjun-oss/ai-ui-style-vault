# Typography

## Display Serif

- Family: Lyon Display
- Substitute: DM Serif Display or Playfair Display
- Weight: 300 only
- Sizes: 38px, 80px, 96px
- Line-height: 0.9 to 1
- Tracking: 0
- Use for hero headlines, large section statements, and feature-card titles.

Never bold the display serif. The style depends on light, oversized serif type.

## Sans UI And Body

- Family: Suisse Intl or Suisse-style sans
- Substitute: Inter or Geist Sans
- Weights: 300 and 400
- Sizes: 12px, 14px, 16px, 18px
- Line-height: 1.41 to 2
- Use 300 for airy subheads and 400 for controls.

## Mono Labels

- Family: Roboto Mono
- Weights: 400 and 500
- Sizes: 10px, 11px, 12px, 16px
- Line-height: 1.35 to 2
- Small uppercase tracking: 0.016em to 0.021em
- Use for badges, data labels, tiny nav metadata, category tags, and interface numerals.
