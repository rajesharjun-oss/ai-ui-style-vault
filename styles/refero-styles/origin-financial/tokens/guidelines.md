# Guidelines

## Do

- Use a near-black full-page canvas with subtle dark surface steps.
- Make the hero headline oversized, centered, serif, and weight 300.
- Keep display line-height tight, around 0.9 at 96px.
- Use Roboto Mono uppercase labels for badges, data, and category metadata.
- Reserve chromatic colors for big category panels or data signals.
- Use white-on-black or black-on-white for primary action clarity.
- Use 30px radius on category cards and stat blocks.
- Use 8px radius on buttons, inputs, and nav controls.
- Use muted Ash or Fog for body copy.

## Do Not

- Do not bold the serif display face.
- Do not add shadows to every card.
- Do not use chromatic colors for small text under 18px.
- Do not set body copy in pure white.
- Do not break the three-typeface system.
- Do not use decorative gradients on buttons, cards, or text.
- Do not make Cyan Signal a general-purpose brand fill.
- Do not add playful spring motion, parallax, or overshoot animations.
