# Components

## Primary CTA Button

- Fill: `#ffffff`
- Text: `#000000`
- Radius: 8px
- Padding: 12px 18px
- Font: Suisse Intl, 16px, 400
- Add a small trailing arrow icon.
- Treat this as the brightest action on the screen.

## Ghost Outline Button

- Transparent fill
- 1px white border
- White text
- Radius: 8px
- Padding: 12px 18px
- Use for nav groups such as products, employers, and resources.

## Nav Glass Button

- Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`
- Border: 1px solid white
- Radius: 8px
- Padding: 9px 12px
- Backdrop filter: blur(24px)
- Use in sticky navigation only.

## Pill Badge Button

- White 20% fill
- White border
- Radius: 9999px
- Tiny padding
- Icon-only or very short text.

## Pill Chip Label

- Background: white at 12%
- Border: white at 15%
- Radius: 9999px
- Padding: 10px 32px
- Text: Roboto Mono, 10-11px, uppercase, `#fafafa`

## Feature Category Card

- Background: one chromatic category color
- Radius: 30px
- Padding: 32px
- Title: Lyon Display, 38px, weight 300
- Description: Suisse Intl, 16-18px
- Use white or near-white foregrounds.

## Stat Or Inverted Card

- Background: `#cacaca`
- Radius: 30px
- Padding: 32px
- Text: dark neutral
- Use sparingly, usually one or two per page.

## Phone Mockup Module

- Background: `#2e2e2e`
- Radius: 16px
- Padding: about 90px when the screen is wide
- Place tilted iPhone app renders on dark canvas with controlled glow.

## AI Prompt Input

- Background: `#000000`
- Text: `#fafafa`
- Radius: 8px
- Padding: 8px top/bottom and about 22px left
- Font: Suisse Intl, 14-16px
- Include a circular submit button with a white 20% fill and arrow icon.
