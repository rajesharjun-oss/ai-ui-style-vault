# Components

## Gate Pill Button

Cream fill, 100px radius, 17px horizontal padding, DegularDisplay-Bold 16px, 0.05em tracking, Pure Black text. No shadow.

## Outlined Display Button

Transparent fill, 2px to 3px Hi-Vis Yellow border, Hi-Vis Yellow text, 100px radius, DegularDisplay-Bold 16px. Do not fill this button yellow.

## Underline Text Link

Bone White text on violet, 1px underline, Bergen Mono 12px, line-height 0.80. Use for age-decline or tertiary disclaimers.

## Hero Display Headline

ObviouslyVariable 800-900, 184px to 341px, line-height 0.80 to 0.85, tracking 0.02em. Use Hi-Vis Yellow and Buttery Yellow.

## Brand Wordmark

Centered wordmark in ObviouslyVariable 800 at 30px, Hi-Vis Yellow. The wordmark is the brand mark.

## Mascot Illustration

Flat cartoon character with 2px to 3px Pure Black outline and 2-3 flat fills. It can overlap or peek through display type.

## Confetti Card

Single-color flat card in Bubblegum Pink, Matcha Cream, Magenta Punch, or Firecracker Red. Use 6px radius, 17px padding, no shadow, no border.

## Dark Text Card

Bone White fill, 6px radius, 17px padding, Ink Black text, optional 1px Ink Black border.

## Color Swatch Card

Solid fill, 6px radius, 17px padding, label and hex set in DegularDisplay-Bold or Bergen Mono.

## Top Nav Bar

Transparent over the violet stage. Centered wordmark, very little navigation chrome.

## Mono Label Tag

Bergen Mono 12px, line-height 0.80, optional 1px border, stamped-label behavior.

## Footer Block

Solid brand or accent color block with Bergen Mono 12px and Bone White text.
