## Color Rules

- Dusk Violet is the page canvas.
- Hi-Vis Yellow is the primary accent, but mainly as outline and display text.
- Bone White is a card and cream pill surface, not a page background.
- Accent colors are paint swatches, not semantic status colors.
- Use one or two accent colors per composition.
- Do not use shadows or gradients to create depth.
