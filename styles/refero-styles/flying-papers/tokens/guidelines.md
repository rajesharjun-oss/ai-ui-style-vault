# Guidelines

## Do

- Use the Dusk Violet canvas across the whole page.
- Use hero display type between 184px and 341px.
- Set display type in ObviouslyVariable 800-900.
- Use line-height 0.80 to 0.85 for giant headlines.
- Use Hi-Vis Yellow for outlined action borders.
- Pair one giant headline with one mascot and one inline action.
- Keep cards at exactly 6px radius.
- Keep buttons and tags at 100px radius.
- Use flat color fills instead of shadows.
- Use Bergen Mono micro-copy at 12px with tight leading.

## Do Not

- Do not use filled CTAs as the main action.
- Do not add drop shadows, glows, or inner shadows.
- Do not use gradients.
- Do not soften the page with a cream or white background.
- Do not combine too many accent colors in one composition.
- Do not round cards beyond 6px.
- Do not enable display font contextual alternates.
- Do not turn the layout into a conventional SaaS grid.
