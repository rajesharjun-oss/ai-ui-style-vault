## Capture Notes

The Refero page describes Flying Papers as a saturated cartoon poster system on a muted violet canvas. The design is intentionally flat: borders and color contrast do the structural work, not shadows.

The AI-useful pieces to preserve are:

- Dusk Violet is the full page stage, not a section accent.
- ObviouslyVariable is used at extreme sizes for poster-scale headlines.
- Display type uses 800-900 weight, tight leading, and 0.02em tracking.
- Cards stay sharp at 6px radius.
- Buttons and tags are soft 100px pills.
- Hi-Vis Yellow is an outlined action color, not a filled CTA.
- Mascot illustrations use thick black outlines and flat 2-3 color fills.
- Confetti cards use one accent paint color at a time.
- No drop shadows, gradients, glows, or softened white page backgrounds.
