## What To Preserve

Dimension uses a matte-black shell, glassy translucent cards, 9999px pill controls, and medium-weight typography instead of loud bold type. Color is extremely restrained: white and warm grays carry the interface, while violet appears only as a gradient glow or spotlight. The hero can use a warm amber-to-cobalt atmosphere, but UI controls should stay achromatic.

The page relies on:

- Void Canvas as the main dark page background.
- Graphite as the elevated dark surface.
- Frosted Glass as a translucent overlay over dark surfaces.
- Bone for readable body text on dark surfaces.
- Ash, Slate, and Smoke for text hierarchy.
- Snow White for the primary pill CTA and key highlights.
- Dusk Violet only as a glow or gradient wash.
- DM Sans at weight 500 for the 72px hero and 16px body/UI.
- Geist for feature headings and compact product/editorial sections.
- 9999px pills for CTAs, nav links, and tag-like controls.
- 10px radius for UI controls and 24px to 42px for panels.
- No box-shadow based elevation.
