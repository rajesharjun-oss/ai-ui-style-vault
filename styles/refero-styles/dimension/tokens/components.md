# Components

## White Pill CTA

Snow White fill, Graphite text, 9999px radius, 8px vertical and 12px horizontal padding, DM Sans 14px to 16px. This is the primary action.

## Ghost Nav Button

Transparent fill, white text at reduced opacity, 1px Hairline border, 9999px radius, 6px vertical and 14px horizontal padding. Use inside the floating nav.

## Floating Frosted Nav

Graphite or translucent Graphite surface, 19px asymmetric radius, 4px backdrop blur, 1px Hairline border, compact internal link padding. Detached, glass-like, and signature.

## Hairline Ghost Button

Transparent fill, Bone or Smoke text, 1px Hairline border, 10px radius, tight 8px padding. Use for integration labels and secondary actions.

## Frosted Glass Feature Card

Frosted Glass at 10 percent opacity over Void Canvas, 24px radius, 1px translucent Hairline border, no shadow, optional 4px backdrop blur.

## Gradient Hero Panel

Warm amber/coral to cobalt atmosphere, 72px DM Sans display text, compact bullet list, white pill CTA, and product/device mockup.

## Numbered Accordion Row

Bone feature title left, Ash two-digit number right, no visible background, no card shell, 20px row rhythm.

## Bulleted Feature Row

Small monochrome icon plus 16px DM Sans Bone text, 12px gap, 16px vertical rhythm, no border or background.

## Device Mockup Frame

Large product screenshot container with 40px top corners and 0px bottom corners. Screenshot bleeds to edges.

## Status Banner Pill

Dark or translucent top announcement pill, 9999px radius, small icon, short text, arrow, and compact spacing.

## Section Header

Geist 32px/600 or 36px/500 in Bone, followed by 18px supporting text in Ash.

## Icon Glyph

Monochrome geometric icon, 16px to 20px, 1.5px stroke, optional 4px squircle container.
