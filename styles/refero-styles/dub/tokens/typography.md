# Typography Tokens

## Fonts

| Font | Token | Role | Substitute |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Satoshi | `--font-satoshi` | Display headings, hero copy, high-level marketing hierarchy. | Plus Jakarta Sans |
| Inter | `--font-inter` | Body, UI labels, nav, tables, dashboards, forms. | system sans |

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Line Height | Weight | Token |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| caption | `12px` | `1.33` | `500` | `--text-caption` |
| body-sm | `14px` | `1.5` | `400` | `--text-body-sm` |
| body | `16px` | `1.5` | `400` | `--text-body` |
| body-lg | `18px` | `1.56` | `400` | `--text-body-lg` |
| subheading | `20px` | `1.4` | `600` | `--text-subheading` |
| heading-sm | `30px` | `1.2` | `600` | `--text-heading-sm` |
| heading | `48px` | `1.08` | `700` | `--text-heading` |
| display | `72px` | `1.0` | `700` | `--text-display` |

## Font Rules

- Use Satoshi for hero and large marketing headings.
- Use Inter for every piece of product UI.
- Keep dashboard text compact around `14px`.
- Use semibold for labels and card headings.
- Avoid serif fonts and decorative display faces.
