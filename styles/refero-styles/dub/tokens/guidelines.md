# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Electric Blue for primary actions and active product states only.
- Keep sections on white or rice-paper surfaces.
- Use `1px #e5e5e5` borders for cards, tables, and controls.
- Use pill-shaped 24px CTAs and 9999px badges.
- Use Satoshi for hero/display headings.
- Use Inter for all body and product UI.
- Use product screenshots and dashboards as primary visuals.
- Keep cards shadow-free or use only a micro shadow.
- Align data and UI panels to a 12-column grid.

## Don't

- Do not add gradients as primary backgrounds.
- Do not use blue for decorative floods or every icon.
- Do not replace white surfaces with dark hero sections.
- Do not round dashboard cards beyond `16px`.
- Do not mix multiple brand accent colors in one section.
- Do not use serif fonts.
- Do not add heavy shadows or glassmorphism.
- Do not put large marketing copy inside dashboard-like cards.
