# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Grammar

Dub uses product-led SaaS composition. The structure is clear and contained: a clean hero, product mockup, social proof, feature bento, analytics panels, pricing cards, and table-like product details.

## Common Layouts

- Hero with large display headline, short subhead, pill CTA row, and product screenshot.
- Alternating white and rice-paper section backgrounds.
- 12-column feature grids.
- Bento cards with product UI fragments.
- Pricing cards with clean metrics and feature lists.
- Analytics cards, line charts, metric tiles, and compact legends.
- Table-heavy link management views.
- Logo rows treated as muted UI typography.

## Imagery

- Use product screenshots, dashboards, browser mockups, charts, tables, and link cards.
- Avoid lifestyle photography, abstract gradients, 3D mascots, and decorative illustration.
- Keep product visuals sharp, bright, and legible.

## Motion

- Use subtle `150ms` to `200ms` ease transitions.
- Add hover motion to buttons, cards, rows, and charts only.
- Avoid slow cinematic motion and large parallax effects.
