| --- | --- | --- |
| buttons | `24px` | `--radius-button` |

## Borders And Elevation

- Default border: `1px solid #e5e5e5`.
- Default card shadow: none.
- Micro shadow: `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`.
- Use shadows only for active/hover states, primary CTA polish, or product mockup emphasis.
