# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Salmond at 130px to 213px for hero and section display type.
- Set display line-height between 0.80 and 0.90.
- Use 9999px radius for buttons, badges, inputs, and icon containers.
- Use only `#faae33` as the main accent.
- Reserve `#d1255c` for heat-level badges only.
- Step depth through `#823513`, `#402011`, and `#281006`.
- Use dotted `#faae33` horizontal rules between major sections.
- Put massive type before product photography in the visual hierarchy.
- Keep product jars raw on the canvas.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce a secondary display typeface.
- Do not use box shadows, drop shadows, or glow effects.
- Do not use gradients.
- Do not frame product photography in cards.
- Do not round product images.
- Do not use 6px radius for buttons, badges, inputs, or icons.
- Do not add white, blue, green, or cool neutrals.
- Do not make body copy larger than 18px.
- Do not use body line-height above 1.4.
- Do not alternate section background colors.
