# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use full-bleed Ember Rust as the continuous page background.
- Treat every viewport like a poster.
- Place small eyebrow text above massive display headlines.
- Let a 130px to 213px headline dominate the viewport.
- Anchor product jars below, beside, or around the type.
- Use asymmetric two-column compositions.
- Use dotted gold dividers between major bands.
- Navigation should use ghost pill buttons and a centered brand mark.
- Floating circular icon controls can dock near the bottom-right.
- Keep spacing generous: 80px to 120px between sections.

## Imagery

Use:

- Studio-lit condiment jars.
- Product PNGs with transparent backgrounds.
- Raw product images sitting directly on the rust canvas.
- Faint botanical watermark layers.
- Thin gold utility icons.

Avoid:

- Lifestyle photography.
- Kitchen scenes.
- People and hands.
- Large product cards.
- Rounded product frames.
- 3D renders.
- Abstract graphics.
- Cool-toned visuals.
