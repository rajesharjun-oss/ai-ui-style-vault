# Components

## Ghost Outline Button

- Role: default nav and secondary action.
- Type: Salmond 13px, weight 500, 0.01em tracking.
- Text: `#faae33`.
- Border: `1px solid #faae33`.
- Fill: transparent.
- Radius: 9999px.
- Padding: 8px 17px.

## Filled Primary Button

- Role: hero BUY NOW and primary CTA.
- Fill: `#faae33`.
- Text: `#281006`.
- Border: `1px solid #faae33`.
- Radius: 9999px.
- Padding: 10px 20px.
- Type: Salmond 13px, weight 500.
- Use sparingly.

## Ghost Borderless Button

- Role: low-emphasis inline micro CTA.
- Fill: none.
- Border: none.
- Text: `#faae33`.
- Type: Salmond 13px, weight 500.

## Pill Badge

- Role: tag, label, product type, flavor note.
- Type: Salmond 11px to 12px, weight 500, uppercase, 0.02em tracking.
- Radius: 9999px.
- Padding: 4px to 8px vertical, 12px to 16px horizontal.
- Primary fill: `#faae33` with `#281006` text.
- Secondary fill: `#402011` with `#faae33` text.

## Alert Badge

- Role: heat level or flavor intensity.
- Fill: `#d1255c`.
- Radius: 9999px.
- Text: 11px Salmond weight 500.
- Padding: 4px 12px.
- Use rarely.

## Pill Input Field

- Role: email, search, or form field.
- Fill: transparent.
- Border: `1px solid #6b2e12`.
- Radius: 9999px.
- Type: Salmond 13px weight 500.
- Padding: 12px to 16px vertical, 16px to 20px horizontal.
- Focus: border shifts to `#faae33`.
- Avoid visible focus glow.

## Surface Card

- Role: product card, recipe card, content tile.
- Fill: `#402011`.
- Radius: 6px.
- Padding: 12px to 16px.
- Shadow: none.
- Depth comes only from color shift against `#823513`.

## Product Bottle Showcase

- Role: hero product visual.
- Raw product jar image on transparent canvas.
- No card frame.
- No background plate.
- No rounded edges.
- Anchor it below or beside the massive type.

## Botanical Watermark Layer

- Role: atmospheric spice-garden texture.
- Use very low opacity fern, leaf, or floral forms.
- Keep motifs behind content and near-invisible at first glance.
- Do not use colorful illustrations.

## Dotted Divider

- Role: section separator.
- Style: 1px dotted line.
- Color: `#faae33`.
- Span: full width.
- Use between major viewport sections.

## Iconography Set

- Role: bag, search, basket, social icons.
- Style: thin stroke around 1.5px.
- Color: `#faae33`.
- Size: 16px to 20px.
- Container: full pill/circle.
- Never multi-color.
