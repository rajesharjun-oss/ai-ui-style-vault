## Use This Style When

Use Hungry Tiger when the interface should feel like a maximalist food brand poster: hot, earthy, loud, typographic, and product-label driven. It fits condiment brands, hot sauces, snack launches, food ecommerce, game-like brand pages, and campaign microsites where massive type should dominate the viewport.

## Quick DNA

- Full-bleed rust-brown canvas.
- One dominant chromatic accent: tiger gold `#faae33`.
- Chili red `#d1255c` only for rare heat-level badges.
- Huge Salmond display type, often 130px to 213px.
- Pills for buttons, badges, inputs, and icon controls.
- 6px radius only for surface cards.
- No shadows, glows, gradients, or framed product cards.
- Product jars sit raw on the canvas; type is the real hero.
