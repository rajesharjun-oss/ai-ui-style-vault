# Implementation Prompt

Build a UI in the Hungry Tiger style.

Create a full-bleed dark rust condiment-brand page that feels like a spice-market poster. The page should be almost entirely warm brown and tiger gold, with huge condensed display type, pill controls, dotted gold dividers, and small raw product jars anchored on the canvas.

Use these hard rules:

- Page canvas: `#823513`.
- Primary accent and text on dark: `#faae33`.
- Card surface: `#402011`.
- Deepest surface and text on gold buttons: `#281006`.
- Border/muted brown: `#6b2e12`.
- Secondary warm accent: `#9f531b`.
- Heat badge only: `#d1255c`.
- Do not add white, blue, green, or cool neutrals.
- Do not add a second display typeface.
- Use Salmond for the main system.
- Use Graphikx only for 13px functional microcopy when needed.
- Hero display text should be 130px to 213px, weight 700, line-height 0.80 to 0.90.
- Display tracking should tighten to about -0.02em at the largest sizes.
- Small labels should use 0.01em to 0.02em positive tracking.
- Buttons, badges, inputs, and icon containers must be full pills.
- Cards may use 6px radius.
- Do not use shadows, glows, gradients, or elevated cards.
- Do not frame product photography.
- Do not let body copy exceed 18px or line-height 1.4.

Build a full-viewport poster hero. Put a small eyebrow in tiger gold near the top, then a massive Salmond headline taking over the viewport. Place the product jar as a small raw image below or to the side, without a card frame. Use ghost outline pill nav buttons, a filled gold BUY NOW pill, dotted gold horizontal dividers between sections, and low-opacity botanical watermarks in the background.

Keep every section on the Ember Rust canvas. Use Dark Spice cards sparingly for product or recipe tiles, and use color shifts instead of elevation.
