# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Rhythm

- Use a centered max-width around `1200px`.
- Keep the page mostly white, with quiet `#fafafb` or `#f2f2f3` section alternation.
- Use `80px` vertical section gaps.
- Hero composition can center a large serif headline and surround it with floating product artifacts.
- Feature sections can use two-column text plus UI arrangements.
- Keep content spacious, calm, and editorial.
- Avoid crowding edges or stacking too many cards in a single viewport.

## Imagery

- Use product UI fragments as the primary imagery.
- Show tables, charts, AI composers, progress rings, and analytics cards as cropped/floating artifacts.
- Avoid lifestyle photography, generic illustration, stock imagery, and abstract decoration.
- Product fragments should sit on white artifact cards with subtle elevation.
- Avatar bubbles and cursor-like details can suggest live collaboration.

## Surface Strategy

- Main canvas: Paper White.
- Secondary section: Fog White or Mist Gray.
- Accent: one Blush Peach card with Sienna Brown ink.
- Elevated artifacts: white with subtle shadow.
- Flat content cards: Mist Gray, no shadow.

## Similar Design Neighborhood

- Editorial SaaS pages with large serif headlines.
- Presentation-tool landing pages with floating product UI.
- Soft monochrome productivity sites with pill CTAs.
- Magazine-like product pages that use whitespace as structure.
