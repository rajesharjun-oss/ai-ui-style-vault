# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Signifier weight `400` at `44px`, `64px`, and `90px` for display headings.
- Use peach `#fbe1d1` at most once per page and only for editorial emphasis.
- Use `9999px` radius for buttons and `24px` radius for content cards.
- Pair filled pill buttons with ghost pill buttons.
- Use Sohne half-step weights (`430`, `450`, `480`) for subtle hierarchy.
- Apply tighter letter spacing at larger sizes.
- Keep the 4px base unit.
- Present product UI as floating artifacts around editorial text.

## Don't

- Do not introduce chromatic colors beyond the peach/brown pair.
- Do not use bold or semibold Signifier headings.
- Do not apply drop shadows to neutral or peach content cards.
- Do not use card radii below `16px` or button radii below `9999px`.
- Do not underline text links at rest.
- Do not place peach cards on dark or chromatic backgrounds.
- Do not use Sienna Brown as ordinary body text on white.
- Do not build a dense dashboard shell unless the app itself requires it.
