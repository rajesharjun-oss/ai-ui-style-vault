# Colors

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Blush Peach | `#fbe1d1` | `--color-blush-peach` | Rare editorial accent card background and warm highlight wash. |

## Neutrals

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Ink Black | `#17191c` | `--color-ink-black` | Primary text, filled button background, logo, and headline color. |
| Slate Gray | `#777b86` | `--color-slate-gray` | Links, muted helper text, secondary copy, and footer copy. |

| Smoke Gray | `#a3a6af` | `--color-smoke-gray` | Placeholder text and disabled labels. |

| Paper White | `#ffffff` | `--color-paper-white` | Page canvas, button text, elevated card surfaces, and primary white surface. |

## Surface Levels

| Level | Name | Value | Purpose |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 0 | Canvas | `#ffffff` | Default page background. |
| 1 | Card Mist | `#f2f2f3` | Quiet nested content blocks and feature cards. |
| 2 | Section Fog | `#fafafb` | Alternating section bands without strong contrast. |
| 3 | Accent Blush | `#fbe1d1` | Rare editorial accent cards. |
| 4 | Elevated White | `#ffffff` | Floating product UI artifacts with subtle elevation. |
