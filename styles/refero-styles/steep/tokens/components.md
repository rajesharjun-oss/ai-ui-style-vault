# Components

## Pill Button - Filled

- **Role:** Primary CTA such as get started or book demo.
- **Style:** `#17191c` fill, `#ffffff` text, `9999px` radius, horizontal padding around `20px`.
- **Type:** Sohne `16px`, weight `400`.
- **Rule:** Pair with a ghost pill when there is a secondary action.

## Pill Button - Ghost

- **Role:** Secondary CTA paired with the filled button.
- **Style:** Transparent fill, `1px #17191c` border, `#17191c` text, `9999px` radius.
- **Rule:** Keep it on the same baseline as the filled CTA.

## Text Link With Arrow

- **Role:** Inline navigation and low-emphasis section transitions.
- **Style:** No background, border, or radius; text in Ink Black with an arrow suffix.
- **Behavior:** Underline on hover only.

## Nav Link

- **Role:** Top navigation item.
- **Style:** Transparent top bar, `#17191c` text, Sohne `16px`, weight `400`.
- **Rule:** No nav background, separator, border, or shadow.

## Neutral Card

- **Role:** Feature block, content container, or base card surface.
- **Style:** `#f2f2f3` background, `24px` radius, no shadow, no border.
- **Rule:** This is the quiet workhorse card.

## Accent Peach Card

- **Role:** Editorial highlight, quote, or feature spotlight.
- **Style:** `#fbe1d1` background, `#5d2a1a` text/strokes, `24px` radius, no shadow.
- **Rule:** Use once per page at most to preserve impact.

## Floating Product Artifact

- **Role:** Hero and section UI fragment.
- **Style:** White surface, `20px` radius, subtle hairline and soft shadow, compact padding.
- **Examples:** Region table, activation chart, registration card, AI composer, stat card.
- **Rule:** Product artifacts can float around editorial headlines; they are not a dashboard shell.

## Input Or Composer

- **Role:** AI question input or command field.
- **Style:** White background, light hairline border, `16px` radius, `16px` padding, placeholder in Smoke Gray.
- **Action:** Right-side circular send button with Ink Black fill and white icon.

## Stat Card With Chart

- **Role:** Analytics fragment.
- **Style:** White floating artifact, compact metric, muted delta copy, minimal Sienna Brown line or radial chart.
- **Rule:** No dense gridlines or full dashboard chrome.

## Avatar Bubble

- **Role:** User presence on floating product cards.
- **Style:** Circular `40px` monogram bubble with soft tinted fill and small pointer detail.

## Tag Or Category Label

- **Role:** Lightweight category grouping.
- **Style:** No background or border; Ash Gray text, Sohne `14px`.
- **Rule:** Use typographic tags, not badges.
