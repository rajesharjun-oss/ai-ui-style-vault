# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Shell: two columns.
- Left rail: 260px to 280px Sidebar Mist.
- Sidebar header: 52px.
- Main column: Pure White conversation surface.
- Conversation width: 720px to 768px centered.
- Section gap: 24px.
- Element gap: 6px.
- Navigation: vertical sidebar list, no global top bar.

## Imagery

Use no decorative imagery:

- No photography.
- No illustrations.
- No abstract art.
- No product screenshots inside the chrome.
- No colorful avatar art.

The conversation text and user-generated content are the visual content. Icons should be monochrome and quiet.

## Composition Notes

The interface should disappear behind the content. Avoid ornamental empty states, large marketing headers, and bright callouts. The welcome heading can be 24px; everything else should stay compact and functional.
