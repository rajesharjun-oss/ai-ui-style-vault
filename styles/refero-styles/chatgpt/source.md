## What To Preserve

- Achromatic interface chrome.
- Sidebar Mist left rail and Pure White main conversation canvas.
- Graphite Ink as primary text and icon color.
- System font stack only, no custom fonts.
- Compact 6px, 10px, 16px, 24px rhythm.
- 10px radius on most controls and cards.
- Hairline borders for elevation.
- Hover Veil fill for interactive feedback.
