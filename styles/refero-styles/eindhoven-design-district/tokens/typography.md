# Typography

## Family

| Token | Family | Fallback | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--font-helvetica-now` | Helvetica Now | Inter | All text, headings, labels, buttons, captions, and body copy |

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Letter Spacing | Token |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caption | 14px | 600 | 1.47 | 0.15px | `--text-caption` |
| Body | 16px | 600 | 1.31 to 1.4 | 0.005px | `--text-body` |
| Subheading | 18px | 600 | 1.31 to 1.47 | -0.004px | `--text-subheading` |
| Body large | 19px | 400 | 1.47 | -0.017px | `--text-body-lg` |
| Heading | 23px | 600 | 1.0 to 1.15 | -0.02em | `--text-heading` |
| Heading large | 35px | 600 | 1.0 | -0.024em | `--text-heading-lg` |
| Display | 46px | 600 | 0.93 | -0.03em | `--text-display` |
| Display large | 50px | 600 | 0.93 to 1.0 | -0.05em | `--text-display-lg` |

## Rules

- Use Helvetica Now only.
- Use size, weight, and spacing for hierarchy.
- Tighten letter spacing as headings get larger.
- Keep display text confident and compact.
- Do not introduce serif, mono, handwritten, or novelty display fonts.
