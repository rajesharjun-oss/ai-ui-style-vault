# Components

## Ghost Button

- Canvas White or transparent fill.
- Ink Black text.
- 1px Ink Black border.
- 500px radius.
- Compact padding, about 1px vertical and 15px horizontal.
- Use for navigation, secondary actions, and inline links.

## Primary Action Button

- Canvas White background.
- Ink Black text.
- 1px Ink Black border.
- 500px radius.
- Generous padding: about 18px top, 21px bottom, 35px horizontal.
- Use for important actions without adding a CTA color.

## Icon Button

- Circular form.
- Canvas White background.
- 1px Ink Black border.
- 100% radius.
- Simple monochrome line icon.
- Use for menu, search, or utility controls.

## Plain Link Button

- Ink Black text.
- No background.
- No border.
- No radius.
- Use for low-hierarchy actions inside content.

## Article Card

- Transparent background.
- No border.
- No shadow.
- 0px radius.
- Rectangular image plus strong headline typography.
- Text in Ink Black.

## Gray Background Card

- Ash Gray surface.
- 0px radius.
- No border or shadow.
- Use to highlight a content block without strong elevation.

## Rectangular Image Block

- 0px radius.
- No shadow.
- No mask.
- Optional 1px black border if the image needs definition.

## Accent Color Block

- Solid Focus Red, Blush Pink, or Electric Blue.
- Use as a poster-like background or punctuation mark.
- Avoid combining multiple accents in one small component.

## Minimal Top Bar

- Canvas White surface.
- Ink Black text and icons.
- Utility icons and hamburger/menu control.
- Keep icons simple and outlined.
