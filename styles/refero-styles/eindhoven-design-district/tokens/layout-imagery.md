# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

Use a centered 1200px content rail for most sections. Let the hero break wider when typography and photography need poster scale.

Suggested order:

1. Minimal top bar with utility icons.
2. Hero with large display headline and rectangular image.
3. Plain intro text or link row.
4. Two-column text and image section.
5. Three-column article or event grid.
6. Ash Gray or vivid accent feature block.
7. Stark footer.

## Composition

Make type, image edges, and white space do the work. Avoid decorative layering. Give headings enough room to act like graphic objects.

## Imagery

Use:

- architectural details
- urban landscapes
- design objects
- exhibitions
- candid cultural scenes
- high-contrast photography

Images should be unmasked rectangles with 0px radius.

## Avoid

- Rounded photos.
- Illustration packs.
- 3D renders.
- Soft editorial washes.
- Heavy icon systems.
- Stock-like lifestyle scenes.
