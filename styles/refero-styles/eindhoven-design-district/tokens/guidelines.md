# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Ink Black and Canvas White for the core UI.
- Use Helvetica Now as the only typeface.
- Make display typography large, tight, and graphic.
- Use 1px Ink Black borders for crisp definition.
- Use 500px radius for buttons and tags.
- Keep cards and imagery at 0px radius.
- Use Ash Gray only as a subtle secondary surface.
- Use red, pink, or blue as rare decorative punctuation.

## Don't

- Do not add extra gray scales beyond Ash Gray and Silver Thread.
- Do not add shadows or soft elevation.
- Do not use gradients.
- Do not round cards or images.
- Do not use accent colors for routine buttons or app states.
- Do not mix typefaces.
- Do not use generic colorful icons.
- Do not soften the poster-like geometry.

## Accessibility Notes

- Black-on-white contrast is strong; keep accent blocks checked for readable foreground pairings.
- Use visible focus outlines on pill buttons and icon buttons.
- Do not rely on red alone to indicate critical state.
- Keep text large enough when using tight tracking.
