# Implementation Prompt

Build a polished Eindhoven Design District-style interface: a light graphic modernist poster layout with stark black-and-white contrast, Helvetica-only typography, sharp rectangular imagery, and vivid color blocks used sparingly.

Use Canvas White (`#ffffff`) as the main page surface and Ink Black (`#000000`) for primary text, borders, icons, and dark display sections. Use Ash Gray (`#e8e8e8`) only for secondary cards or section breaks, and Silver Thread (`#bfbfbf`) only for quiet lines or low-emphasis text. Use Focus Red (`#ff0000`), Blush Pink (`#ffc2eb`), and Electric Blue (`#0f26ed`) only as controlled poster-like punctuation. Do not turn them into a general UI color system.

Typography must use Helvetica Now everywhere, with Inter as fallback. Do not introduce serif, mono, or decorative display fonts. Use 600 weight for most labels, buttons, and headings. Use large tight headlines: 46px to 50px, line-height around 0.93, and negative tracking from -0.03em to -0.05em. Body text is compact, direct, and black.

Layout should use a 1200px centered content rail with occasional full-bleed hero moments. Combine large headline blocks, rectangular images, single-column text blocks, two-column text-image sections, and three-column article grids. Section gaps are around 35px and element gaps around 20px.

Components should be flat and crisp. Cards and images have 0px radius. Buttons and tags have 500px radius. Most interactive surfaces use a 1px black border. Icon buttons are circular with simple monochrome icons. Do not add shadows, gradients, soft glass effects, or rounded image treatments.

Before finishing, check that:

- The page is mostly black and white.
- Helvetica Now or a close fallback is the only typeface.
- Cards and images are sharp rectangles.
- Buttons and tags are pill-shaped.
- Accent colors appear only as limited visual punctuation.
- There are no shadows, gradients, or soft elevation styles.
