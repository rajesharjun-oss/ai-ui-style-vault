## Notes

This bundle summarizes the Refero style page into implementation-ready guidance. It is not a verbatim archive. The style is a high-contrast modernist poster system: black and white surfaces, Helvetica Now as the only typeface, large tight headings, rectangular photography, and occasional vivid color blocks.

## What To Preserve

- Canvas White and Ink Black as the dominant pair.
- Helvetica Now as the only typeface.
- Large display headings with tight letter spacing.
- 1px black borders for definition.
- 0px radius for cards and imagery.
- 500px pill radius for buttons and tags.
- Rectangular photography integrated cleanly into the grid.
- Red, pink, and blue as decorative emphasis only.
