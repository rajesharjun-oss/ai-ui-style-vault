# Guidelines

## Do

- Use `#e5e4e0` as the page canvas.
- Use Ataero Retina OB Edition as the sole typeface.
- Set display headlines at 70px to 103px, weight 400, line-height 0.80.
- Use the iridescent gradient only as one large hero sphere.
- Use 10px radius for links, buttons, and inputs.
- Use 0px radius for cards and images.
- Use 11px all-caps section labels with 0.05em tracking.
- Use 76px to 119px vertical section spacing.
- Use `#bfbebe` 1px hairlines for structure.
- Keep body copy left aligned.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce a second typeface family.
- Do not use colored fills on buttons, cards, sections, or backgrounds.
- Do not add shadows or elevation effects.
- Do not reuse the gradient outside the hero sphere.
- Do not set body text below 15px or above 18px.
- Do not center-align body paragraphs.
- Do not round card or image containers.
- Do not use lifestyle photography or stock imagery.
