# Typography

## Families

| Family | Token | Fallback | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calsans | `--font-calsans` | Space Grotesk, wide-tracked geometric sans | Display and heading type. |
| Geist | `--font-geist` | Inter, system-ui | Body, navigation, controls, forms, activity rows, badges, and metadata. |
| Helvetica | `--font-helvetica` | Arial, system-ui | Incidental legacy content only. |

## Rules

- Use Calsans for headings at 56px or larger.
- Use positive 0.056em tracking on Calsans.
- Never tighten Calsans.
- Use Geist for functional interface copy.
- Keep body copy in the 14px to 18px range.
- Use wide tracking for tiny badges and micro labels.
