# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Keep the full page dark from hero to footer.
- Use a centered 1200px content rail.
- Build the hero as a 50/50 split: Calsans headline and copy on the left, 3D visual bleeding right.
- Use 64px to 80px section rhythm.
- Alternate two-column text and visual sections with centered header plus card-grid sections.
- Use 2-column or 3-column card grids with 20px to 24px gaps.
- Use a single sticky top navigation bar, not a sidebar.

## Imagery

- Use 3D-rendered product visuals, curved tracks, flowing light ribbons, abstract geometric objects, and neon edge-lit forms on dark gradient grounds.
- Use activity feeds with avatars and status dots for flat product content.
- Avoid photography, flat illustration, light sections, and generic marketing icons.
