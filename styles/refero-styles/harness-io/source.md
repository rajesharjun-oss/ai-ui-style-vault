## What To Preserve

Harness.io uses a strict dark stack: black canvas, one-step card plates, nested dark surfaces, and light borders that cut card edges out of the darkness. The system gets its atmosphere from mint and blue restraint, not from a broad neon palette.

The page relies on:

- Void Canvas for the whole shell.
- Carbon Plate for primary cards.
- Obsidian for nested panels and code surfaces.
- Iron Edge and Steel Border for dividers and hover structure.
- Fog for light card hairlines.
- Pure White for primary text and the only filled button.
- Phosphor Mint for featured card fills and one highlight panel per viewport.
- Signal Blue and Current Blue for active links and nav states.
- Calsans with positive tracking for large headlines.
- Geist for all functional interface copy.
- 800px pill controls for primary and ghost buttons.
- Light borders, not shadows, to define elevation.
- 3D dark product visuals and activity rows as imagery.
