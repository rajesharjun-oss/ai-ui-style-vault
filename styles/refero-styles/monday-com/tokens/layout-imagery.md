# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a centered 1200px max-width content rhythm.
- Build the hero as a centered headline and CTA stack.
- Use product mockups that break the container or peek over section edges.
- Follow the hero with a monochrome logo strip.
- Alternate text-plus-product sections.
- Use Cloud backgrounds to create soft bands between Snow sections.
- Use a 50/50 violet panel for AI or marquee product features.

## Section Patterns

- Hero: centered display headline, gradient phrase, primary pill CTA.
- Trust strip: muted logos with no cards.
- Feature grid: pastel cards with icons and short labels.
- Product proof: board mockup card with rows, status pills, avatars, and soft shadow.
- AI feature: left text, right violet panel with floating mockup.
- Navigation: sticky white bar with logo, links, and dual CTAs.

## Imagery

- Use actual product UI mockups.
- Show status pills, table rows, avatars, columns, dashboards, and AI sidebars.
- Let mockups overlap or float above page edges.
- Avoid lifestyle photography, portraits, and generic stock scenes.
- The only major decorative graphic should be the prism mark or gradient headline text.
