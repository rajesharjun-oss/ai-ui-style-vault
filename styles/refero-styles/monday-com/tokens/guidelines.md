# Guidelines

## Do

- Use 160px radius for all buttons and CTAs.
- Reserve Monday Violet for primary actions and brand panels.
- Apply pastel accent backgrounds to feature cards and highlight surfaces.
- Set display headlines at 48px to 64px with weight 300 and negative tracking.
- Use 24px radius for cards.
- Use 6px radius for badges, inputs, and compact product controls.
- Apply the prism gradient only to logo marks, dividers, and loading states.
- Use gradient text only in hero headlines.
- Show product UI as the hero visual.

## Do Not

- Do not use square or 4px-radius buttons.
- Do not apply pastel accent colors to body text, borders, or icons.
- Do not use weight 300 for body copy or UI labels.
- Do not use pure black for large text blocks; use Ink.
- Do not stack too many pastel colors in adjacent cards.
- Do not use heavy drop shadows.
- Do not introduce CTA colors outside Monday Violet.
- Do not rely on lifestyle photos.

## AI Implementation Notes

- If the page feels too corporate, add pastel feature surfaces and softer product mockup composition.
- If the page feels childish, reduce the number of pastel colors per row and let violet anchor the screen.
- If hierarchy feels weak, use product mockup scale and violet panels before adding more decoration.
- If a CTA is not the primary action, keep it outlined or text-only.
