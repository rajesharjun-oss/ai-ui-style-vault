# Arva Design Reference

## Essence

Arva feels like an agricultural magazine spread translated into a website. The base is warm bone paper, not pure white. Deep forest green creates the main structural bands, while pastoral field photography provides scale and credibility. The typography pairing is the key voice: Reckless makes section headings literary and calm, while Inter keeps navigation, labels, body copy, and forms practical.

## Color System

The palette is restrained and earth-toned. Forest Ink is the backbone: header, nav, footer, section dividers, and primary action. Bone is the page canvas. Pure White is layered on top for cards, inputs, and button text. Pastel tiles, including Sky Card, Peach Card, Sage Card, and Ash Gray, create quilt-like rows for testimonials and partner content. Vivid Lime is the only high-energy color and should stay rare, mostly in a marquee or promotional strip.

## Typography

Use Inter for functional text and Reckless for editorial headlines. Inter handles nav, metadata, body copy, badges, buttons, and giant display when needed. Reckless should appear at 24px and above for headings, pull quotes, and section anchors. A light serif weight is part of the brand attitude: the type whispers with confidence instead of shouting.

## Shape And Space

The shape language is soft and oversized. Buttons use 100px radius, nav pills use 110px, inputs use 33px, cards use 20px, and hero cards can use 30px. Do not replace these with small utility radii. Spacing is comfortable, with 50px section gaps, 30px card padding, and compact 8px element gaps inside small controls.

## Layout Rhythm

Use full-bleed horizontal bands. Internal content can be centered at 1200px max-width, but the bands themselves should reach the viewport edges. A vivid lime marquee sits above a forest-green header. The hero is full viewport or near full viewport with landscape photography, centered headline, pill buttons, and a scroll cue. Below, alternate bone sections, forest interstitial sections, and pastel card grids.

## Components

- Lime marquee strip with repeating resource text and small checkmark separators.
- Forest header with centered logo, nav links, language selector, and white pill CTA.
- Full-bleed landscape hero with centered white headline and two pill buttons.
- Forest filled primary CTA with white text and 100px radius.
- Cream or ghost pill button with outline and 100px radius.
- Pastel quilt card for testimonials, partner quotes, or category content.
- Forest section banner with white serif headline and simple icon benefit grid.
- Rounded input field with moss border and forest focus state.
- Section divider header with serif headline on bone canvas.
- Forest footer with multi-column link grid and generous vertical padding.

## Implementation Direction

Start from Bone, not white. Use Forest Ink as the main section skeleton, then layer white cards and pastel tiles. Keep lime scarce. Let full-bleed agricultural photography create atmosphere. Use Reckless only for elevated editorial moments and Inter for everything operational. Avoid shadows, hard dashboard radii, random saturated colors, centered body paragraphs, and illustrated mascots.
