# Components

## Lime Marquee Strip

- Surface: Vivid Lime.
- Height: around `32px`.
- Text: Inter, `12px` to `14px`, Charcoal.
- Content: repeating resource or guide text.
- Separators: simple outline checkmark or minimal line icon.
- Placement: full-width above the forest header.

## Forest Header Bar

- Surface: Forest Ink.
- Height: about `60px`.
- Logo: centered white wordmark with small leaf-like mark.
- Left links: Inter, `15px`, white.
- Right action: Pure White pill CTA with Charcoal text.
- Shape: nav CTA radius `110px`.

## Full-Bleed Photography Hero

- Background: full-viewport agricultural landscape photo.
- Headline: centered white serif, `57px` to `80px`.
- Actions: two 100px pill buttons below headline.
- Scroll cue: small Inter label at bottom center.
- Treatment: no heavy overlay, no card over the image.

## Forest Filled Pill CTA

- Fill: Forest Ink.
- Text: Pure White.
- Radius: `100px`.
- Padding: `10px 24px`.
- Type: Inter, `14px` to `15px`, weight `500` or `600`.
- Use for primary actions.

## Cream Outline Pill Button

- Fill: transparent.
- Border: Graphite, Charcoal, or currentColor.
- Text: Charcoal on light, Pure White on photo or forest.
- Radius: `100px`.
- Padding: `10px 24px`.
- Type: Inter, `14px`.
- Use as the paired secondary action.

## Pastel Quilt Card

- Surface: Sky Card, Peach Card, Sage Card, Ash Gray, or Pure White.
- Radius: `20px`.
- Padding: `30px`.
- Content: centered logo, quote or short copy, author name, role.
- Layout: usually 3-column row.
- Shadow: none.

## Forest Section Banner

- Surface: Forest Ink.
- Text: Pure White.
- Headline: Reckless, `45px`, weight `300`.
- Body: Inter, `17px`, max `60ch`, left aligned.
- Benefit grid: 2 by 2, small circular icon containers, 30px gaps.

## Input Field

- Fill: Pure White.
- Border: Moss or Graphite.
- Radius: `33px`.
- Padding: about `12px` vertical.
- Type: Inter, `16px`.
- Focus: Forest Ink ring or border.

## Section Divider Header

- Surface: Bone.
- Headline: Reckless, `45px` to `57px`, Charcoal.
- Supporting copy: Inter, `17px`, left aligned.
- Decoration: none. Let type and spacing carry hierarchy.

## Partner Logo Card

- Surface: Pure White or pastel tile.
- Radius: `20px`.
- Padding: `30px` or more.
- Logo: centered raster or vector mark.
- Quote: Inter, `14px` to `15px`.
- Attribution: Inter, `14px`, weight `500` to `600`.

## Forest Footer

- Surface: Forest Ink.
- Links: Pure White.
- Type: Inter, `14px` to `15px`.
- Layout: multi-column link grid.
- Padding: generous, about `110px` top padding or more.
