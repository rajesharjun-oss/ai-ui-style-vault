# Typography

## Font Stacks

- Sans/UI: `Inter`, `DM Sans`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Display serif: `Reckless`, `Cormorant Garamond`, `GT Sectra`, `Source Serif Pro`, serif
- Light display serif: `RecklessLight`, `Cormorant Garamond Light`, `Source Serif ExtraLight`, serif

## Scale

| Purpose | Size | Line height | Weight | Tracking | Token |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Caption | `12px` | `1.5` | `400` or `500` | `0.025px` | `--text-caption` |
| Body small | `14px` | `1.5` | `400` | `0.025px` | `--text-body-sm` |
| Body | `16px` | `1.52` | `400` | `-0.022px` | `--text-body` |
| Subheading | `24px` | `1.24` | `300` | `-0.012px` | `--text-subheading` |
| Heading small | `37px` | `1.22` | `300` | `-0.012px` | `--text-heading-sm` |
| Heading | `45px` | `1.06` | `300` | `-0.012px` | `--text-heading` |
| Heading large | `57px` | `1.06` | `300` | `-0.022px` | `--text-heading-lg` |
| Display | `80px` | `1` | `100` or `600` | `-2.96px` | `--text-display` |

## Rules

- Use Inter for all functional interface text.
- Use Reckless only at 24px and above.
- Use RecklessLight for delicate pull quotes or editorial lead text.
- Keep body copy left aligned and readable, usually around 60ch maximum.
- Use positive tracking on small metadata and badge text.
- Avoid heavy sans-only SaaS typography; the serif/sans tension defines the mood.
