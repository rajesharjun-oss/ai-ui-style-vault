# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Model

- Stack full-bleed horizontal sections.
- Keep internal content centered at about `1200px`.
- Start with a lime marquee, forest header, and full-bleed hero photo.
- Alternate Bone editorial sections, Forest Ink interstitials, and pastel quilt card rows.
- Use centered headlines for heroes and section openers, but keep explanatory body copy left aligned.
- Keep section gaps around `50px`, with more breathing room in hero and footer.

## Imagery

- Use aerial field photography, crop rows, pasture, farmers, soil, and warm agricultural landscapes.
- Favor natural greens, golden browns, and warm light.
- Let photography fill the viewport or large bands.
- Avoid duotone filters, heavy overlays, abstract illustrations, and generic office stock.
- Use simple line icons only for checks, benefit marks, and small visual aids.

## Motion

- Motion should feel calm and editorial.
- Use marquee movement, gentle photo reveal, and soft section transitions.
- Avoid bouncy SaaS animation, noisy confetti, and heavy parallax.
