# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Bone as the page canvas for every light section.
- Use Forest Ink for nav, footer, primary action, and section bands.
- Use 100px radius for all button variants.
- Pair Reckless headlines with Inter body and UI text.
- Keep Vivid Lime rare and mainly attached to promotional strips.
- Rotate pastel tile colors across card rows in a deliberate sequence.
- Use full-bleed warm field and agricultural photography.
- Use surface shifts instead of shadows.
- Keep long body paragraphs left aligned.

## Don't

- Do not use pure white as the page background.
- Do not apply small utility radii to buttons or cards.
- Do not introduce new saturated colors beyond Forest Ink and Vivid Lime.
- Do not use Reckless below 24px or for body copy.
- Do not add box shadows or glass effects.
- Do not mix multiple pastel colors inside one card.
- Do not use abstract graphics instead of landscape photography.
- Do not make the layout feel information-dense or dashboard-like.
