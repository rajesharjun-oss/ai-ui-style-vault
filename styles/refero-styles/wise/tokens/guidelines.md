# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Wise Sans 900 for large display moments.
- Use Lime Voltage sparingly for action and active state.
- Use Forest Ink instead of pure black for dark surfaces.
- Use Charcoal instead of pure black for body copy.
- Use full pill radius for buttons, tags, nav segments, and tabs.
- Pair one filled CTA with an underlined text link.
- Use circular flags in country grids.
- Alternate Paper, Fog, Linen Mist, and Forest Ink sections.

## Do Not

- Do not set large display headlines in Inter.
- Do not use Lime Voltage as text on light backgrounds.
- Do not cluster many lime accents in one viewport.
- Do not introduce gradients, decorative blurs, or heavy shadows.
- Do not use sharp 0px to 4px corners on buttons, tags, or nav.
- Do not use pure black for normal body text.
- Do not make the system generic blue fintech.

## Accessibility Notes

- Lime text on light backgrounds has low contrast; use lime mainly as fill or on dark green.
- Make segmented controls expose active state beyond color.
- Keep country grid links keyboard-focusable and visibly underlined or outlined on focus.
- Ensure the floating QR badge does not cover essential mobile content.
