# Typography

## Families

| Role | Font | Fallback | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Wise Sans | Inter Black 900 | Hero headlines and major section openers |
| UI | Inter | system-ui, sans-serif | Body, nav, forms, labels, cards, smaller headings |

## Rules

- Use Wise Sans for 60px and larger display moments.
- Use Inter for body, labels, controls, and content cards.
- Keep large type compact with negative tracking.
- Use Charcoal for body text and Obsidian or Forest Ink for display.
- Enable `calt` if available.
