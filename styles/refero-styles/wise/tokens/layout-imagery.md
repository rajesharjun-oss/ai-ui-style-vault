# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Centered 1200px content container.
- Hero and dark sections can go full-bleed.
- Top nav is a sticky white bar with segmented pill navigation.
- Hero uses centered giant display type and a large globe illustration.
- Section rhythm alternates Paper, Fog, Linen Mist, and Forest Ink.
- Supporting content uses two-column text and visual blocks.
- Trust sections use three-column feature rows.
- Country directories use a five-column flag grid.
- QR badge floats fixed at bottom-right.

## Hero Recipe

1. Paper canvas.
2. White top nav with segmented pill switcher.
3. Massive Wise Sans display headline.
4. Primary lime CTA plus secondary underlined text link.
5. Large globe and coin illustration breaking the lower edge.

## Imagery

- Use painted 3D-style globe and gold coin illustrations.
- Use circular flag thumbnails for country and currency references.
- Photography should be minimal and high-key when used.
- Avoid abstract patterns, stock photo montages, and decorative gradients.

## Dark Section Recipe

Use Forest Ink background, rounded dark card surfaces, Lime Voltage headline text, Paper body text, and inset white functional cards for currency or amount selection.
