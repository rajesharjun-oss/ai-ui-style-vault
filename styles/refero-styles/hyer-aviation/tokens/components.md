# Components

## Filled Dark Pill Button

Deep Ink fill, Pure White text, 1000px radius, 15px 22px 16px padding, HelveticaNowDisplay 17px/700. Use for primary actions on light surfaces.

## Ghost White Pill Button

Transparent background, Pure White text, 1px white border, 1000px radius, matching padding. Use on dark sections and hero imagery.

## Circular Icon Button

100% radius, Deep Ink background, white icon, square hit area. Use for menu and compact navigation actions.

## Featured Clay Card

Clay Ember fill, Pure White text, 0px radius, 53px 59px padding. Use exactly once per page or viewport to create focal asymmetry.

## Standard White Card

Pure White fill, 0px radius, no border, no shadow. Boundary is created by negative space and nearby Pebble hairlines.

## Hero Wordmark

Large Deep Ink brand mark around 131px, weight 700, tight tracking, flush-left. Keep it architectural.

## Hero Headline

60px to 63px, weight 700, line-height 1.0, tight tracking, Deep Ink. End with a period.

## Section Headline

37px to 52px, weight 700, line-height 1.0, tight tracking, stacked into short lines.

## Feature Block

23px Deep Ink title, 18px Cool Ash body at 1.61 line-height, Pebble hairline rule, usually inside a 2x2 grid.

## Dark Content Section

Full-bleed Midnight Hull or Charcoal Deck background, white content, right-aligned column. Use for support, contact, or contrast content.

## Footer Terminal

Full-bleed Deep Ink footer with oversized white wordmark, Cool Ash nav links, legal row, and social links.

## Top Navigation

Transparent over hero, 20px links, light spacing, circular action/menu control.
