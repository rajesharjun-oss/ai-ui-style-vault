# Typography

## Families

| Family | Token | Fallback | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| HelveticaNowDisplay | `--font-helveticanowdisplay` | Neue Haas Grotesk Display, Inter, Helvetica Neue | Sole major typeface for headings, nav, body, and UI. |
| sans-serif | `--font-sans-serif` | system-ui, Inter, Helvetica Neue | Emergency fallback only. |

## Rules

- Use one type family for the whole system.
- Use weight 700 from subheadings upward.
- Use weight 400 for body copy.
- Keep body copy at 18px with line-height 1.61.
- Use extreme negative tracking at hero sizes.
- Do not use light or thin headings.
