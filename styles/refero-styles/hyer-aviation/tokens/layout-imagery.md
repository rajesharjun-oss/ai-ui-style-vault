# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use full-bleed sections rather than framed page cards.
- Alternate pale hero, white content bands, and dark midnight bands.
- Center the main content inside a 1200px rail.
- Use 80px vertical gaps.
- Make the hero asymmetric: huge wordmark left, headline right, aircraft low-center.
- Put content grids on white bands, often with rightward gravity.
- Use 2x2 feature grids with hairline rules.
- Finish with a Deep Ink footer terminal.

## Imagery

- The aircraft is the hero.
- Use a clean white private jet render or isolated aircraft photograph.
- Keep the aircraft in a premium product-showcase crop.
- Use a pale sky or white atmospheric background in the hero.
- Keep icons minimal and stroke-based.
- Avoid passengers, airports, busy skies, generic resort photography, and decorative illustration.
