# Guidelines

## Do

- Set display headlines at 60px to 187px with weight 700.
- Use tight tracking on large type.
- Use Deep Ink for primary text, filled buttons, icon strokes, and footer.
- Reserve Clay Ember for one featured card or focal offer.
- Use 1000px radius for every button and nav pill.
- Set body copy at 18px with line-height 1.61.
- Alternate white sections with Midnight Hull or Charcoal Deck bands.
- End the main hero headline with a period.
- Let one aircraft render or photo carry the hero.

## Don't

- Do not apply shadows to cards or buttons.
- Do not introduce additional accent hues.
- Do not use Deep Ink as the light-section page background.
- Do not round the featured clay card.
- Do not set body type below 18px.
- Do not use light or thin weights for headlines.
- Do not place two Clay Ember feature blocks in one viewport.
- Do not use generic lifestyle travel photography.
