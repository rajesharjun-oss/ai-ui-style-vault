# Implementation Prompt

Build a luxury aviation interface in the Hyer Aviation style.

Use a mostly monochrome palette. The primary canvas is Pure White (`#ffffff`) with Deep Ink (`#000d10`) for text, icons, filled buttons, and footer structure. Use Cool Ash (`#8e8e95`) for muted copy and nav labels, Pebble (`#d5d3d4`) for hairline dividers, and dark full-bleed bands in Midnight Hull (`#0f0f1c`) or Charcoal Deck (`#151623`). Clay Ember (`#bc7155`) is a single-use accent: one featured card or one focal offer per page. Do not add more accent hues.

Typography should feel architectural. Use HelveticaNowDisplay or a close Neue Haas Grotesk Display/Inter fallback. Headlines from 23px upward are weight 700. The hero wordmark can be 131px and the largest hero type can reach 187px with extreme negative tracking. Body copy is 18px, weight 400, line-height 1.61. Use short stacked headline lines and end the main hero statement with a period.

Use spacious 4px-based layout values: 1200px max width, 80px section gaps, 22px card padding, 16px element gaps. Actions are full pills: 1000px radius for nav pills and buttons, 100% radius for icon buttons. Cards and featured panels are hard-edged: 0px radius for white panels and the clay card. Do not use shadows; rely on surface shifts, whitespace, and Pebble hairlines.

Expected components:

- Transparent top navigation over hero with large text links and a circular menu or icon button.
- Filled dark pill button: Deep Ink background, Pure White text, 1000px radius, 17px/700 type.
- Ghost white pill button: transparent, white text and border, 1000px radius for dark/image contexts.
- Featured Clay Card: Clay Ember fill, white text, 0px radius, generous 53px 59px padding, used once.
- Feature block grid: 2x2 layout, hairline rule, 23px title, 18px Cool Ash body copy.
- Dark content band: Midnight Hull or Charcoal Deck, right-aligned content column, white copy.
- Footer terminal: Deep Ink background, huge white wordmark, muted link columns.

The hero should be dominated by a clean white private jet render or aircraft photograph against a pale sky-to-white atmosphere. Keep it isolated, premium, and product-like. Avoid lifestyle photography, airports, passengers, busy travel imagery, gradients outside the hero atmosphere, rounded cards, shadows, and loud secondary colors.
