## What To Preserve

Hyer Aviation uses a bright white canvas, near-black structural type, huge tight-tracked display headings, dark midnight content bands, and a single warm clay accent. The system is strict: one display family, headline weight 700, spacious 18px body copy, pill buttons, hard-edged feature cards, and no drop shadows.

The page relies on:

- Deep Ink for text, button fills, icons, and footer structure.
- Pure White as the primary page and card canvas.
- Midnight Hull and Charcoal Deck for dark contrast sections.
- Cool Ash for secondary copy and muted labels.
- Pebble for hairline dividers.
- Clay Ember for one featured offering or focal card.
- HelveticaNowDisplay for all major type.
- 1000px radius for buttons and nav pills.
- 0px radius for white content panels and featured cards.
- 3D or photo-real aircraft imagery as the hero object.
