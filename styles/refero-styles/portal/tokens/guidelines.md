# Guidelines

## Do

- Set display headings in Perfectly Nineties at 36-48px, weight 400, line-height 1.
- Use Signal Blue only for actions, active states, inline links, and brand icon highlights.
- Make every button a 50px pill.
- Keep Inter body text at 14-16px with line-height 1.30-1.35.
- Round cards and images generously, usually 22-30px.
- Default content sections to Ash Mist with Paper White cards.
- Use the 5px Ash Mist glow ring as the primary elevation technique.
- Use Ink Black for headings and primary borders, Graphite for body, and Smoke for metadata.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce another chromatic accent.
- Do not use Perfectly Nineties for body, captions, buttons, or labels.
- Do not use heavy drop shadows.
- Do not use sharp 0-4px corners on cards or interactive elements.
- Do not set body text below 14px.
- Do not use Inter above weight 600.
- Do not place black text directly on the chromatic gradient.
- Do not use Signal Blue for large fills, hero backgrounds, or decorative shapes.
