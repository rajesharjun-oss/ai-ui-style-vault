# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Center content in a max-width container around 1200px.
- Hero is full-bleed and edge-to-edge.
- Floating nav capsule is anchored near the top with margin, not a full-width bar.
- Device mockup overlaps the bottom of the hero.
- Below the hero, content flows on an Ash Mist canvas.
- Reading columns are 640px to 720px wide.
- Marketing sections are text-dominant and usually single-column.
- Section gaps should be generous, around 80px to 120px.

## Imagery

- The hero is the only atmospheric visual.
- Use a sunset gradient with subtle horizon or landscape silhouettes if needed.
- Use product screenshots inside white rounded device mockups.
- Avoid lifestyle photos, stock imagery, generic illustrations, and decorative image collages.
- Let the product UI, rounded frames, and serif headings do the work below the hero.

## Surfaces

| Level | Name | Value | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 0 | Page Canvas | `#f7f7f7` | Content sections below the hero |
| 1 | Card Surface | `#ffffff` | Product cards, project entries, content blocks |
| 2 | Nav Pill | `#ffffff` | Floating navigation capsule |
| 3 | Hero Atmosphere | `#7a7fd4` | Approximate middle color of the full-bleed gradient hero |

## Motion

- Keep motion soft and native.
- Use opacity, color, and slight position transitions.
- Avoid bounce, springy overshoot, parallax, and loud animation.
