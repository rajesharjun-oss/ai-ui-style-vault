# Portal - Style Reference

Portal feels like an indie magazine spread inside a native iOS shell. A twilight gradient hero supplies the atmosphere, while the rest of the page becomes quiet, white, rounded, and editorial.

## Visual Thesis

- Use one full-bleed dusk gradient hero as the only atmospheric moment.
- Switch below the hero to a pale `#f7f7f7` canvas with white cards.
- Pair retro serif display headings with tight, refined Inter UI text.
- Use Signal Blue only for functional states and action emphasis.
- Use generous radii and pale glow rings instead of heavy shadows.
- Keep marketing sections text-led and single-column.

## Core Ingredients

- Hero: vertical gradient from sky blue through violet and mauve to warm coral.
- Canvas: Ash Mist `#f7f7f7`.
- Cards: Paper White `#ffffff` with pale glow-ring elevation.
- Accent: Signal Blue `#007aff`.
- Display type: Perfectly Nineties-style serif at 36-48px.
- UI type: Inter, 10-18px, slightly tight tracking.
- Shape: 22px nav, 22-30px cards, 30-40px images, 50px buttons.

## Page Composition

Use a floating white nav capsule above a full-bleed hero. Place a serif headline and a white hero CTA over the gradient, but keep dark text on white surfaces rather than directly on the gradient. Let a rounded device mockup overlap the bottom of the hero, then continue into centered editorial sections and product UI cards on a pale light canvas.

## Color Behavior

The system is achromatic plus one blue. Signal Blue is not decorative. Use it for CTAs, active states, inline links, brand icon highlights, and product UI status moments. Do not turn it into a hero background or large surface fill.

## Type Behavior

Perfectly Nineties is the personality: use it for H1/H2 at 36-48px, weight 400, line-height 1. Inter handles body, nav, buttons, labels, badges, metadata, and product UI. Body copy should be 14-16px with a tight, iOS-like feel.
