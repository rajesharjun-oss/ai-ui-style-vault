# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Pattern

- Transparent top nav with hairline bottom border.
- Centered hero stack: line illustration, serif headline, single CTA.
- Monochrome logo strip directly on the page.
- Alternating quiet paper sections and one Ink Violet reset panel.
- Centered feature grids with simple paper cards.
- Generous vertical spacing.
- No dashboard columns or heavy app shell.

## Content Width

- Page max width: about 1200px.
- Body copy: around 60ch.
- Hero headline: centered and two lines where possible.
- Feature cards: keep grids relaxed and readable.

## Imagery

Use:

- Hand-drawn black line illustrations.
- Science, note-taking, research, and study motifs.
- Sparse doodle-like marginalia.
- Occasional Margin Yellow wash behind illustrations.
- Single small hero illustration.

Avoid:

- Photography.
- Product screenshot hero blocks.
- 3D renders.
- Dense illustration collages.
- Grid-snapped decorative marks.

## Motion

Keep motion minimal:

- Gentle hover color change on buttons.
- Border darkening on cards.
- Simple tab transitions.
- No bouncy, glossy, or theatrical motion.
