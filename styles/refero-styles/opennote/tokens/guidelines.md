# Guidelines

## Do

- Use `#fffdf8` as the main canvas.
- Use `#512906` Sepia as the only filled action color.
- Set all headlines in IowanOld or a similar serif at weight 400.
- Reserve Ink Violet for one full-bleed feature panel per page.
- Draw structure with 1px Graphite Rule borders.
- Keep cards, buttons, and tags at 10px radius.
- Use hand-drawn black line illustrations sparingly.
- Pair serif headlines with small muted sans-serif subheads.
- Let whitespace carry the page.

## Do Not

- Do not use pure white as the main canvas.
- Do not introduce bright modern accent colors.
- Do not use shadows, glows, or glassmorphism.
- Do not bold the serif headlines.
- Do not use sans-serif type for headings above 24px.
- Do not make Margin Yellow interactive.
- Do not stack multiple filled Sepia CTAs in a single viewport.
- Do not force marginalia illustrations into a strict grid.

## Accessibility Notes

- Ink Charcoal on Ivory Page is strong for primary text.
- Smoke is for secondary text and should not carry critical information at small sizes.
- Sepia with Ivory Page text is appropriate for primary action contrast.
- Keep focus states visible, preferably with a crisp border change or outline.
