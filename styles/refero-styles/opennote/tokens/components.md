# Components

## Sepia Filled CTA

Primary action button.

- Background: `#512906`.
- Text: `#fffdf8`.
- Font: SuisseIntl 14px, weight 400.
- Radius: 10px.
- Padding: 8px 16px.
- Border: none.
- Shadow: none.
- Hover: deepen the Sepia slightly, but do not lift.

## Outlined Ghost Button

Secondary action and header action.

- Background: transparent.
- Border: 1px solid `#d1d1d1`.
- Text: `#0a0a0a`.
- Font: SuisseIntl 14px.
- Radius: 10px.
- Padding: 8px 16px.
- On Ink Violet: invert text to Ivory Page and use a light hairline border.

## Tab Navigation

Text-only section navigation above feature panels.

- Inactive: Smoke text.
- Active: Ink Charcoal text.
- Active treatment: underline only.
- Gap: about 24px.
- Avoid pill backgrounds, cards, and borders.

## Ink Violet Feature Panel

The one dark reset panel.

- Background: `#242d64`.
- Text: Ivory Page.
- Padding: 40px to 64px.
- Heading: IowanOld 32px to 42px, weight 400.
- Body: SuisseIntl 16px, line-height 1.5.
- Action: outlined ghost button.

## University Logo Strip

Social proof band.

- Monochrome wordmarks.
- Directly on Ivory Page.
- Even spacing around 40px.
- No card, caption, or background panel.

## Centered Hero Block

Above-the-fold introduction.

- Small black line illustration centered above the headline.
- IowanOld 42px to 48px headline.
- One filled Sepia CTA beneath.
- Generous vertical spacing.

## Marginalia Illustration

Decorative line drawings.

- Stroke: Pressed Black or Ink Charcoal.
- Fill: none, except occasional Margin Yellow wash.
- Placement: loose, editorial, in negative space.
- Avoid overlapping body copy.

## Feature Card

Paper card on the ivory canvas.

- Background: Ivory Page.
- Border: 1px solid Graphite Rule.
- Radius: 10px.
- Padding: 24px.
- Title: IowanOld 20px to 32px.
- Body: SuisseIntl 16px, Slate Body.
- Shadow: none.

## Header Bar

Site navigation.

- Transparent background.
- Bottom border: 1px solid Graphite Rule.
- Height: about 60px to 72px.
- Center nav links: SuisseIntl 14px.
- Right action: outlined ghost button.

## Cream Band Background

Section canvas for tabbed feature areas.

- Warm paper base with subtle ruled lines.
- Hairline rules in Graphite Rule.
- Optional cool washed-blue tint, but keep it pale.
