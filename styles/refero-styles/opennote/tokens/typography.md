# Typography

## Serif Display

IowanOld owns the editorial voice.

- Use for: hero headline, section headings, feature titles, emphasis blocks, and type above 24px.
- Substitute: Source Serif Pro, Tiempos Text, PT Serif, or another old-style serif.
- Weights: 400, 500.
- Default headline weight: 400.
- Avoid: bold serif headlines.

## Sans UI

SuisseIntl carries the utility layer.

- Use for: body copy, nav, button labels, footer links, utility chrome, captions, and forms.
- Substitute: Inter, Sohne, Helvetica Neue, or system sans-serif.
- Weights: 400.
- Body size: 16px.
- Body line height: 1.5.

| Heading Small | 32px | 1.12 | IowanOld | 400 |
| Heading | 42px | 1.1 | IowanOld | 400 |
| Display | 48px | 1.08 | IowanOld | 400 |

## Pairing Rule

The serif and sans-serif are locked into separate roles. Serif is for display and emotional tone. Sans-serif is for reading, navigation, labels, and controls. Do not swap them.
