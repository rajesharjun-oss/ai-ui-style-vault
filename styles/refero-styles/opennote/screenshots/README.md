## Visual Cues To Preserve

- Warm ivory paper canvas.
- Centered serif hero headline.
- One Sepia filled CTA.
- 10px card and button radius.
- Thin graphite borders.
- Sparse black line illustration.
- One Ink Violet dark feature panel.
- No shadows or glossy elevation.
