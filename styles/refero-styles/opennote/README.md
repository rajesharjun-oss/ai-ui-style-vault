## Style Summary

Opennote is a warm paper notebook style: ivory canvas, old-style serif headlines, quiet sans-serif UI text, sepia-filled CTAs, 10px corners, hairline gray rules, and hand-drawn marginal illustrations. It feels editorial and thoughtful rather than engineered or dashboard-like.

Use this style when an AI needs a polished notes app, research assistant, education product, writing tool, knowledge workspace, or editorial AI landing page with a calm journal voice.

## What To Borrow

- Ivory Page `#fffdf8` as the page background.
- Ink Charcoal `#0a0a0a` for primary text and icon strokes.
- Graphite Rule `#e5e5e5` for all structural hairlines.
- Sepia `#512906` as the only filled CTA background.
- Ink Violet `#242d64` for one dark full-bleed feature panel.
- Margin Yellow `#ffc934` only as decorative highlighter wash.
- IowanOld or a Source Serif Pro fallback for headlines and display text.
- SuisseIntl or an Inter fallback for body, nav, buttons, labels, and footer.
- 10px radius across cards, buttons, and tags.
- 8px spacing base with generous page breathing room.
- Thin bordered paper cards with no shadows.

## Avoid

- Pure white canvas as the main page color.
- Blue or green "modern SaaS" accents.
- Filled yellow buttons or badges.
- Drop shadows, glow effects, or layered card elevation.
- Sans-serif headlines above 24px.
- Bold serif headlines.
- More than one filled Sepia CTA in a viewport.
- Grid-snapped or dense illustration placement.
