## Notes

This bundle summarizes the Refero style page into implementation-ready guidance. It is not a verbatim archive. The style is a near-white financial product system: compact grayscale UI, hairline borders, restrained shadows, one editorial serif headline, and one violet primary-action accent.

## What To Preserve

- Paper White canvas and cool grayscale UI structure.
- Violet Pulse as the only filled chromatic CTA.
- TWK Lausanne for all product UI, navigation, labels, cards, and most headings.
- Moderat Serif only for the largest 40px to 46px display headline.
- 9999px pill buttons and badges.
- 8px cards and 4px input fields.
- Hairline Ash borders as the structural grid.
- Product screenshot cards instead of lifestyle imagery.
- Soft blue-violet radial washes as atmosphere, never heavy color blocks.
