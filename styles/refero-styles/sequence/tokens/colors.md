## Color Behavior

The system is grayscale plus one violet action color and one green semantic text accent. Violet Pulse should appear as a filled primary action or active state, not as a general background wash.

## Pairings

- Main text: Graphite on Paper White.
- Body text: Iron on Paper White.
- Muted text: Steel or Smoke on Paper White.
- Border: Ash or Mist.
- Primary CTA: Paper White text on Violet Pulse with Iris Glow halo.
- Focus: Violet Pulse border with Lavender Wash ring.

## Avoid

- New chromatic colors.
- Indigo Ink as button fill.
- Large violet blocks.
- Heavy colored backgrounds.
- Multicolor charts unless product content requires them.
