# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Violet Pulse only for the main filled CTA and active accents.
- Use Ash 1px borders across cards, dividers, inputs, and tiles.
- Use TWK Lausanne for all product UI and most headings.
- Reserve Moderat Serif for one 40px to 46px h1.
- Use pill radius for buttons, nav pills, tags, and badges.
- Use 8px cards and 4px inputs.
- Keep section gaps at 80px and card padding at 24px.
- Use product UI mockups as the primary imagery.

## Don't

- Do not introduce new chromatic colors.
- Do not use Indigo Ink as a button background.
- Do not use Moderat Serif below 40px.
- Do not use large solid colored regions.
- Do not use heavy drop shadows.
- Do not set body text below 12px.
- Do not use 8px radius for buttons or tags.
- Do not use lifestyle photography or stock imagery.

## Accessibility Notes

- Keep Violet Pulse CTA contrast checked against white text.
- Use focus states that include shape or ring, not color alone.
- Keep small captions legible; avoid going below 11px except for technical chrome.
- Use meaningful alt text for product screenshots when they explain functionality.
