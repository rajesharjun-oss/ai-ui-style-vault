# Components

## Primary Action Button

- Violet Pulse background.
- Paper White text.
- 9999px radius.
- 10px 20px padding.
- TWK Lausanne 14px weight 500.
- Iris Glow halo at low opacity.
- Use once per screen as the only filled chromatic CTA.

## Secondary Action Button

- Transparent or Paper White background.
- 1px Silver border.
- Graphite text.
- 9999px radius.
- 10px 20px padding.
- Hover can shift to Bone.
- Use a thin neutral ring shadow only.

## Navigation Link

- Iron text.
- TWK Lausanne 14px weight 500.
- No underline by default.
- Active state shifts to Graphite and may add a 1px underline.

## Display Headline Block

- Moderat Serif.
- 46px.
- Weight 300.
- Line-height 1.
- -0.025em tracking.
- Graphite color.
- Pair with TWK Lausanne body text in Iron.

## Product Feature Card

- Paper White background.
- 1px Ash border.
- 8px radius.
- 24px padding.
- 24px Graphite icon top-left.
- 18px TWK Lausanne title.
- 14px Steel description.
- No resting shadow.

## Product Screenshot Card

- Paper White background.
- 1px Ash border.
- 8px radius.
- 16px padding.
- Layered thin-ring shadow.
- Internal fills use Bone.
- Active UI details can use Lavender Wash.

## Input Field

- Paper White background.
- 1px Mist border.
- 4px radius.
- 8px 12px padding.
- Placeholder Smoke.
- Focus: Violet Pulse border plus Lavender Wash ring.

## Integration Logo Tile

- Paper White background.
- 1px Ash border.
- 8px radius.
- 16px padding.
- Center monochrome logo mark.

## Status Badge

- Lavender Wash or Bone background.
- Indigo Ink or Ledger Green text.
- 11px weight 500.
- 9999px radius.
- 2px 10px padding.

## Announcement Bar

- Full-bleed Marble background.
- Graphite 13px centered text.
- 8px vertical padding.
- Inline arrow link can use Indigo Ink.

## Logo Lockup

- Graphite glyph plus Sequence wordmark.
- TWK Lausanne 18px weight 500.
- No tagline.

## Section Header

- Eyebrow: TWK Lausanne 12px weight 500 in Steel.
- Heading: 40px light TWK Lausanne in Graphite.
- Centered in hero, left-aligned in product sections.

## Customer Logo Strip

- Centered monochrome wordmarks.
- No background or border.
- 48px to 80px column gaps.
- Star rating above in muted gray.
