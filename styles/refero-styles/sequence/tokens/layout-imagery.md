# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

Use a 1200px centered container with 24px gutters. Stack sections vertically with 80px gaps and occasional Ash hairline dividers.

Suggested order:

1. Fixed top navigation.
2. Centered hero with eyebrow, serif h1, paragraph, and CTA pair.
3. Floating 3-column product screenshot grid.
4. Customer logo strip with rating.
5. Alternating product suite sections.
6. Two-column diagram and feature grid.
7. Announcement or warm Marble band.
8. Footer with grayscale links.

## Composition

The page should feel precise and product-led. Avoid airy lifestyle layouts. Use compact cards, tables, forms, and logo tiles to show product confidence.

## Imagery

Use:

- product screenshot cards
- contract UI
- billing forms
- invoice tables
- logo integration tiles
- concentric-circle diagrams
- line icons
- soft blue-violet radial washes

## Avoid

- Lifestyle photography.
- Stock imagery.
- Decorative illustration packs.
- Heavy 3D renders.
- Large colored slabs.
- Multicolor icon systems.
