## Use This When

Use this style when a finance, billing, contracts, platform, or B2B product should feel precise, premium, and operational without becoming a dashboard wall. It works well for fintech marketing, revenue tooling, API-adjacent SaaS, enterprise product pages, and product-led landing pages that need trust and polish.

The system is mostly grayscale: white canvas, near-black text, cool gray borders, compact product cards, and UI screenshots. Violet appears as the single active pulse: primary CTA, focus ring, active icon, or small brand glow.
