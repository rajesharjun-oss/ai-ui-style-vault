# Implementation Prompt

Build a warm productivity SaaS interface in the Getharvest style.

Use Cream Canvas (`#fff8f1`) as the page background. Do not use white as the page canvas. Use Paper White (`#ffffff`) for cards, inputs, product screenshots, and elevated panels. Use Ink Black (`#1d1e1c`) for primary text and icons, Ironwood (`#4a4a47`) for stronger secondary text, Warm Stone (`#615f5c`) for body copy, Driftwood (`#8e8b87`) for muted text, Bone (`#c0bbb6`) for input borders, and Mist Gray (`#d9d9d9`) for hairlines.

Use Harvest Flame (`#fa5d00`) as the only brand accent. It can fill primary CTA buttons, color links, show active nav states, mark heading underlines, and accent small icons. Do not use it for paragraphs or large background fills. Use Marigold Glow (`#fee3b5`) only as a decorative wash. Use warm shadows such as `rgba(250, 166, 0, 0.25) 6px 4px 24px 0px`; never use cool blue-gray shadows.

Typography should be warm and editorial. Use MuotoWeb or an Inter/Sohne fallback for all UI, nav, body, buttons, product cards, and headings up to 50px. Apply 0.015em positive tracking to MuotoWeb text. Use Monarch or a GT Super/Tiempos/Georgia fallback only for 72px hero-grade display headlines. Do not use Monarch for body copy or small headings.

Use comfortable spacing: 1200px max width, 64px to 80px section gaps, 32px to 40px card padding, and 16px to 24px element gaps. Use 16px radius for buttons, inputs, and images. Use 20px radius for cards. Tags may use 999px radius.

Expected components:

- Sticky cream navigation with logo left, nav links center, sign-in ghost link, and orange primary CTA right.
- Primary CTA: Harvest Flame fill, Paper White text, MuotoWeb 16px/600, 12px by 24px padding, 16px radius, subtle black lift.
- Ghost text link: orange text, no fill, no border, optional arrow, underline on hover.
- Email input: white fill, Bone border, 16px radius, Driftwood placeholder, orange focus ring.
- Feature card: cream or white fill, 20px radius, 32px to 40px padding, Ink Black icon, Warm Stone body, optional warm glow.
- Dashboard preview card: product screenshot inside Paper White, 16px radius, warm shadow.
- Section header: orange uppercase eyebrow, 34px to 48px MuotoWeb heading, Warm Stone subtitle.
- Trust row: grayscale logos and a small label, with only the number or tiny mark in orange.
- Hero: centered Monarch display headline, subtitle, email capture or CTA, warm organic orange/peach wash, and floating product screenshots.

The result should feel golden, practical, friendly, professional, and product-led. Avoid white page backgrounds, second accent colors, sharp corners, cool shadows, stock photography, abstract 3D, and saturated orange surfaces.
