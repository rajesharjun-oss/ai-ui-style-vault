# Typography

## Families

| Family | Token | Fallback | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| MuotoWeb | `--font-muotoweb` | Inter, Sohne, system-ui | Navigation, body, buttons, cards, subheadings, and most headings. |
| Monarch | `--font-monarch` | GT Super, Tiempos Headline, Georgia | Hero-grade 72px display moments only. |

## Rules

- Use MuotoWeb for almost everything functional.
- Apply 0.015em tracking to MuotoWeb.
- Use Monarch only at hero scale.
- Use MuotoWeb for all headings 50px and below.
- Do not use Monarch for body, labels, or cards.
