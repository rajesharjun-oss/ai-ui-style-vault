# Components

## Primary CTA Button

Harvest Flame fill, Paper White text, MuotoWeb 16px/600, 0.015em tracking, 12px by 24px padding, 16px radius, and subtle black lift.

## Ghost Text Link

No fill, no border, Harvest Flame text, MuotoWeb 16px/500, optional small arrow, underline on hover.

## Email Input Field

Paper White fill, 1px Bone border, 16px radius, 14px by 20px padding, Driftwood placeholder, Harvest Flame focus outline.

## Feature Card

Cream or Paper White fill, 20px radius, 32px to 40px padding, optional warm glow, Ink Black icon, 20px to 24px heading, Warm Stone body, orange link.

## Integration Logo Circle

48px Paper White circle containing a literal third-party logo. External brand colors are allowed only inside logo assets.

## Navigation Bar

Cream Canvas background, Harvest wordmark, MuotoWeb 16px Ink Black links, sign-in ghost link, and primary orange CTA.

## Dashboard Preview Card

Paper White product screenshot container, 16px radius, warm soft shadow, showing app UI like timesheets, reports, charts, or invoicing.

## Section Heading

Orange uppercase eyebrow, MuotoWeb 34px to 48px title in Ink Black, and optional Warm Stone subtitle.

## Trust Badge Logo Row

Uppercase label in Warm Stone, optional orange number highlight, and grayscale partner logos in Ink Black at reduced opacity.

## Hero Gradient Wash

Soft organic wash of Harvest Flame, Marigold Glow, and peach tones at low opacity behind product preview cards. Decorative only.

## Feature Grid

Two-column product block or three-card feature grid with cream canvas and white product cards.
