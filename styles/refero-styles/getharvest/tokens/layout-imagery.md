# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a centered 1200px max-width container.
- Keep Cream Canvas full-bleed edge to edge.
- Use a centered hero with large serif headline, subtitle, CTA or email capture, warm glow, and floating screenshots.
- Use 64px to 80px section gaps.
- Alternate centered text-only blocks with two-column text and screenshot sections.
- Use three-column feature cards for high-level benefits.
- Keep navigation slim with logo, menu, sign-in, and orange CTA.

## Imagery

- Product UI screenshots are the dominant visual.
- Use timesheets, profitability charts, reports, invoices, dashboards, and integration panels.
- Place screenshots in Paper White cards with 16px radius and warm shadows.
- Use dark geometric icons for features.
- Render partner logos in grayscale.
- Avoid stock photography, lifestyle imagery, abstract 3D renders, and cool-toned visual effects.
