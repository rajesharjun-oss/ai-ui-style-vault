## What To Preserve

Getharvest replaces generic SaaS white with a cream page canvas and then lifts product UI with white cards. Orange is the only brand accent. It handles every primary CTA, brand link, active indicator, and small emphasis. The rest of the system is warm monochrome.

The page relies on:

- Cream Canvas as the full page background.
- Paper White as product cards, input fields, and elevated panels.
- Ink Black as warm primary text and icon color.
- Warm Stone and Ironwood for hierarchy.
- Harvest Flame as the single chromatic action color.
- Marigold Glow only as a decorative wash behind product cards.
- MuotoWeb for nearly all UI and body type.
- Monarch only for the largest hero display headline.
- 16px controls and images, 20px cards.
- Warm-tinted shadows rather than gray or blue shadows.
- Product screenshots as the main visual proof.
