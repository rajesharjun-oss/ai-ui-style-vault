# Guidelines

## Do

- Use wine-dark canvas everywhere.
- Use Impossible Red for primary action, active state, and display type.
- Use Blush Highlight as the soft secondary text color.
- Make major display type huge, uppercase, and tight.
- Pair red filled buttons with white ghost buttons.
- Use 15px radius on CTAs, nav controls, and filter pills.
- Crop decorative food photos with irregular organic masks.
- Keep product cards flat.

## Do Not

- Do not use shadows, glows, or gradients.
- Do not add extra accent colors.
- Do not use red for body copy.
- Do not use a light or white page background.
- Do not use regular rectangular food photos in hero areas.
- Do not make display line-height loose.
- Do not soften the brand into a generic SaaS style.

## Accessibility Notes

- Huge display type needs responsive resizing on mobile.
- Red text on wine backgrounds should be checked for contrast when used below display scale.
- Do not rely only on color for active filter state; include underline or aria state.
- Product cards and carousel controls need visible focus states on the dark canvas.
