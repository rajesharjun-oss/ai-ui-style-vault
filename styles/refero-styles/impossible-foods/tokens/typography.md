# Typography

## Families

| Role | Font | Fallback | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | sans-meat | Druk Wide, Knockout-style condensed sans | Display, nav, labels, CTAs, product names |
| Body fallback | sans-meat | Inter, Sohne-style sans | Body copy and UI text if the custom face is unavailable |

## Weights

| Weight | Role |
| --- | --- |
| 400 | Body, secondary UI |
| 500 | Nav, buttons, toggles, labels |
| 700 | Display headlines, product names, wordmark |

## Type Scale

| Token | Size | Weight | Line height | Tracking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caption | 10px | 400 or 500 | 1.4 | 0.02px |
| Body Small | 14px | 400 or 500 | 1.4 | 0.02px |
| Body | 18px | 400 or 500 | 1.4 | 0.02px |
| Subheading | 24px | 500 or 700 | 1.15 | 0.02px |
| Heading Small | 32px | 700 | 1.1 | 0.02px |
| Heading | 48px | 700 | 0.9 | 0.02px |
| Heading Large | 103px | 700 | 0.75 | 0.03px |
| Display | 160px | 700 | 0.73 | 0.06px |

## Rules

- Major display moments should start around 103px.
- Use 160px or larger when the page needs a campaign-level statement.
- Keep display line-height tight at 0.73 to 0.75.
- Use uppercase for display, nav, CTAs, toggles, and product names.
- Do not use this display voice for long-form paragraphs.
