# Components

## Sticky Top Navigation

- Background: Butcher Black.
- Wordmark: Impossible Red, uppercase, condensed, 18px to 20px.
- Nav links: Bone White, uppercase, compact.
- Right controls: Foodservice dropdown and Find Us CTA.
- Behavior: sticky at top.

## Primary CTA Button

- Fill: Impossible Red.
- Text: Bone White.
- Font: sans-meat, 14px, weight 500, uppercase.
- Tracking: 0.02em.
- Radius: 15px.
- Padding: 10px 14px.
- Include a small right caret.

## Ghost CTA Button

- Fill: transparent.
- Border: 1px solid Bone White.
- Text: Bone White.
- Same size, radius, and typography as primary CTA.
- Use beside a filled CTA for paired actions.

## Filter Pill Toggle

- Radius: 15px.
- Padding: 10px 16px.
- Gap: 6px between pills.
- Active: Impossible Red fill and Bone White text.
- Default: transparent fill with Blush Highlight or Bone White text.

## Hero Display Block

- Canvas: Velvet Wine or Burgundy Stage.
- Eyebrow: 14px to 18px uppercase red.
- Display: 160px to 231px uppercase red.
- Weight: 700.
- Line height: 0.73.
- Tracking: 0.06px.
- CTA below the headline.

## Product Carousel Card

- Surface: Burgundy Stage.
- Border: optional 1px Butcher Black.
- Radius: 12px.
- Shadow: none.
- Product photo fills top area.
- Product name: 22px to 24px uppercase Blush Highlight or Bone White.
- CTA row: filled Learn More and ghost Find It.

## Category Tab With Number Badge

- Label: uppercase, 20px to 24px, Blush Highlight.
- Badge: 16px circle, Burgundy Stage fill, Bone White text.
- Active state: Impossible Red underline.

## Masked Food Photography

- Irregular organic mask.
- Full saturation.
- Sharp edge.
- No border or shadow.
- Use only as decorative hero imagery.

## Recipe Or Product Grid Card

- Surface: Burgundy Stage.
- Border: 1px Butcher Black.
- Radius: 12px or 38px for feature card.
- Padding: 16px to 24px.
- Image top, text and optional CTA below.
