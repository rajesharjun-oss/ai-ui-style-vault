# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Full-bleed Velvet Wine page canvas.
- Sticky black nav at the top.
- Centered hero stack with huge red display type.
- Irregular food-photo masks flanking or surrounding hero type.
- Product carousels below display sections.
- Category tabs centered above carousels.
- Four-column product grid for dense product groups.
- Section separation comes from vertical spacing only.

## Hero Recipe

1. Velvet Wine background.
2. Sticky black nav with red wordmark.
3. Red uppercase eyebrow.
4. Giant red condensed display headline.
5. Parenthetical aside as a structural second line.
6. Red primary CTA below.
7. Organic masked food images around the type.

## Imagery

- Use product packaging photography for carousel and grid cards.
- Use decorative food photography only in organic masks.
- Keep food and packaging saturated and sharp.
- Avoid lifestyle context, illustrations, 3D renders, and abstract graphics.
- Use minimal flat icons: caret arrows and location pins.

## Product Carousel

Show one primary product card with adjacent content peeking at the edge when possible. This creates overflow and abundance without making the layout dense.
