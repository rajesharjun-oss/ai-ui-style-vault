# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Canvas: full-bleed Pale Sage.
- Max width: 1200px centered.
- Hero: two-column asymmetric split.
- Photo: one side, warm and human.
- Text/stat stack: large serif headline plus Deep Forest stat card.
- Section rhythm: 56px to 80px vertical gaps.
- Feature layout: alternating two-column text and image sections.
- Navigation: minimal top bar with left logo, centered links, and right CTA pair.

## Imagery

Use:

- Teams collaborating at desks.
- People reviewing dashboards.
- Candid workspace moments.
- Warm natural lighting.
- Documentary-style composition.

Avoid:

- Staged stock photography.
- Duotone filters.
- Color overlays.
- Decorative illustration.
- Cold blue enterprise dashboard cliches.

## Composition Notes

Photos should sit inside sage or paper surfaces with 16px radius. The sage canvas acts as an editorial mat. Keep images warm and grounded; the interface should still feel like a brand site, not a raw analytics console.
