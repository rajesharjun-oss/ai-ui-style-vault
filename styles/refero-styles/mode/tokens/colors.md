# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pale Sage | `#eef2e3` | `--color-pale-sage` | Page canvas, light card surfaces, eyebrow backgrounds, hero photo mats |
| Paper White | `#fcfcfc` | `--color-paper-white` | Elevated cards, nav, modals, footer |
| Ink Black | `#000000` | `--color-ink-black` | Primary text, icon strokes, hairline borders, ghost outlines |
| Charcoal | `#242423` | `--color-charcoal` | Secondary text and muted icon fills |
| Deep Forest | `#043f2e` | `--color-deep-forest` | Brand primary, text on sage, dark sections, stat cards |
| Chartreuse Lime | `#c8f169` | `--color-chartreuse-lime` | Filled CTA, highlights, tag and badge backgrounds |
| Forest Mid | `#2a6f2b` | `--color-forest-mid` | Hover and active state for green surfaces |
| Vivid Green | `#78c51c` | `--color-vivid-green` | Data visualization support and secondary chart strokes |

## Usage

Pale Sage is the page canvas. Paper White is only for elevated surfaces. Chartreuse Lime is the only filled CTA accent. Forest Mid and Vivid Green support hover and data visualization; do not let them become additional marketing accents.
