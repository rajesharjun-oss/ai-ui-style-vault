# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Deep Forest for primary text on Pale Sage or Paper White.
- Use Grenette serif for all display and section headings at 36px and above.
- Use Chartreuse Lime as the filled CTA background with Ink Black text.
- Use 16px radius for cards, image blocks, and large surfaces.
- Use 4px radius for buttons, inputs, and tags.
- Layer surfaces with sage, forest, and chartreuse instead of shadows.
- Use uppercase Graphik 12px to 14px with 0.06em tracking for eyebrows.
- Keep photography warm, documentary, and human.

## Do Not

- Do not use Graphik for display headlines.
- Do not add box shadows to cards or buttons.
- Do not use pure white as the page background.
- Do not apply bold 600+ to body or display text.
- Do not use more than one chromatic accent per viewport.
- Do not mix in 8px, 12px, or 20px radii.
- Do not apply the serif below 36px.
- Do not use staged stock, duotone, or color overlays on images.
