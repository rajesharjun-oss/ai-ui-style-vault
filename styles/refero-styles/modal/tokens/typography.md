# Typography

## Display Typeface

- Family: Goga
- Substitute: Inter Tight or Space Grotesk
- Weights: 400 and 500
- Sizes: 20px, 21px, 24px, 28px, 30px, 36px, 40px, 42px, 48px, 54px, 64px
- Line-height: 1 to 1.5
- OpenType: `ss01`
- Use for H1-H4 and card titles.

Goga defines the brand. Do not use Inter for display headings.

## UI Typeface

- Family: Inter Variable
- Substitute: Inter
- Weights: 400 and 500
- Sizes: 12px, 14px, 16px, 20px
- Line-height: 1.25, 1.33, 1.43, 1.5
- OpenType: `cv11`
- Use for nav, buttons, body copy, labels, and footer.

## Type Scale

| Token | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `caption` | 12px | 500 | 1.33 | 0.6px | uppercase labels |
| `body-sm` | 14px | 400 | 1.43 | -0.364px | small body and links |
| `body` | 16px | 400 | 1.5 | -0.352px | body copy |
| `subheading` | 20px | 400-500 | 1.5 | -0.36px | lead and small headings |
| `heading-sm` | 24px | 400 | 1.3 | -0.312px | card titles |
| `heading` | 30px | 400 | 1.2 | -0.36px | section headings |
| `heading-lg` | 42px | 500 | 1.05 | -0.336px | large headings |
| `display` | 64px | 500 | 1 | -0.448px | hero display |

## Rules

- Use Goga for headings and card titles.
- Use Inter for body and UI chrome.
- Keep both families tightly tracked.
- Enable Goga `ss01` and Inter `cv11` where possible.
- Use uppercase 12px labels with positive tracking as a counter-rhythm.
