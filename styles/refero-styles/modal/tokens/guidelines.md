# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Lime Pulse as a fill on exactly one element per viewport.
- Enable Inter `cv11` and Goga `ss01` where available.
- Default body text to Sage 60 on Void Black.
- Render headlines in Goga with negative tracking.
- Use 12px radius for buttons and 8px for cards.
- Communicate depth through surface steps and 1px green-tinted hairlines.
- Use 32px padding on primary cards and content blocks.
- Keep section spacing generous.

## Do Not

- Do not use Lime Pulse as body text.
- Do not apply drop shadows to cards, buttons, or content blocks.
- Do not introduce blue, purple, or non-green chromatic accents.
- Do not use Phosphor White for body paragraphs.
- Do not set display headings in Inter.
- Do not use full pill radius on cards or panels.
- Do not use raw white or pure green.
- Do not add photography.
