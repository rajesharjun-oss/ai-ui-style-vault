# Implementation Prompt

Build a dark developer platform interface in the Modal style.

Use a full-bleed black canvas (`#000000`) with subtle dark surfaces: Ground Iron `#181818` for cards and filled controls, Carbon Veil `#212525` for elevated bars or selected states, and green-tinted borders such as Circuit Border `#485346`. Avoid normal white and ordinary gray UI. The whole page should feel like a terminal room lit by phosphor.

Use Lime Pulse `#7fee64` with strict restraint. It can appear as one filled CTA pill, an active status tag, the logo, a short hero phrase, or a 3D product object. Do not use it for body text, large surfaces, repeated card fills, or general decoration.

Use Goga or a tight geometric display sans for H1-H4 and card titles. Use weight 400 or 500 with negative tracking. Use Inter Variable for nav, buttons, body, labels, and footer. Body text should be Sage 60 `#8cab87`, not Phosphor White. Phosphor White `#ddffdc` is for headings, icon strokes, and button text.

Use 8px radius on cards and 12px radius on buttons. Full pill radius is only for CTA pills, tags, and link chips. Use 32px card padding, 20px element gaps, and 80px section gaps. Depth should come from surface steps and 1px hairline borders. Use shadow only on the nav bar.

Use terminal code-window panels, traffic-light dots, monospaced syntax highlights, green isometric workload icons, customer logo strips in muted green-gray, and one glowing 3D cube in the hero. Do not use photography, colorful accent palettes, heavy shadows, glassy SaaS cards, or rounded panels beyond the defined radii.
