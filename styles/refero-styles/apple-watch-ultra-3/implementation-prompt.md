# Implementation Prompt

Build in the Apple Watch Ultra 3 style: a cinematic mixed product page with pure-black hardware stages, white detail bands, SF Pro typography, huge product photography, and one filled Apple Blue buy button.

Use these rules:

- Hero and feature stage: `#000000`.
- Secondary dark surface: `#1d1d1f`.
- Carbon surface: `#111111`.
- Light detail band: `#ffffff`.
- Soft light surface and text on dark: `#f5f5f7`.
- Body/caption gray: `#86868b`.
- Dark dividers: `#424245`.
- CTA fill: `#0071e3`.
- Link on light: `#0066cc`.
- Link on dark: `#2997ff`.
- Category eyebrow: `#f56900`.
- Alternate category accents: `#8668ff` and `#00a1b3`, sparingly.
- Headline font: SF Pro Display, with Inter/system fallback.
- Body font: SF Pro Text, with Inter/system fallback.
- Hero type: 80px, weight 600, line-height 1.05, tracking -0.24px.
- Display type: 56px, weight 600, line-height 1.07, tracking -0.84px.
- Body: 14px, weight 400, line-height 1.43, tracking -0.22px.
- Buttons: 36px radius, 10px 20px padding.
- Photo cards and containers: 28px radius.
- Nav and chips: 980px radius.
- Max width: 1440px.
- Section gap: 88px to 120px.
- Card padding: 28px.
- No shadows.
- No gradients.

Design structure:

1. Slim promo banner.
2. Pill-like black navigation with Apple glyph, nav items, search, and bag icons.
3. Full-viewport black hero stage with a single floating product render.
4. Bottom-left hero text, price label, and one Apple Blue buy button.
5. Dark feature sections with huge product photos and rounded 28px photo cards.
6. White detail bands with two-column product copy and large images.
7. Orange category eyebrow labels above activity or feature text.

Avoid:

- Multiple filled blue buttons in one viewport.
- Orange buttons.
- Box shadows.
- Visible hero cards.
- Decorative gradients.
- Generic dashboards.
- Small product imagery.

The result should feel like an obsidian showroom where the hardware is the whole event.
