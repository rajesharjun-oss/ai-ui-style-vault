# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Apple Blue as the only filled chromatic CTA.
- Keep only one filled chromatic button in a viewport.
- Use 28px radius for photo cards and content containers.
- Use 36px radius for standard buttons.
- Use 980px radius for pill nav and chips.
- Set headline type in SF Pro Display at 56px to 80px with negative tracking.
- Alternate Pure Black feature stages and Paper White detail bands.
- Use Signal Orange for category eyebrows only.
- Use Platinum for body text on light sections.
- Use Frost White for text on dark sections.
- Use huge product photography without hero card chrome.

## Do Not

- Do not use drop shadows or CSS elevation.
- Do not add more than one filled chromatic button in the same viewport.
- Do not apply Signal Orange to body text or buttons.
- Do not use pure white for dark-stage paragraph copy.
- Do not add decorative gradients.
- Do not wrap hero hardware in a card.
- Do not use small product imagery.
- Do not treat category accent colors as general UI colors.
