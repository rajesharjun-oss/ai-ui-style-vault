# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use full-bleed black and white bands.
- Center content at 1440px max width.
- Make the hero full viewport or near full viewport.
- Anchor hero copy low and away from the product.
- Alternate black feature stages and white detail bands.
- Use 88px to 120px section gaps.
- Use two-column detail bands on white sections.
- Keep feature stages spacious and image-led.

## Imagery

Use:

- High-resolution product renders.
- Watch macro photography.
- Black-and-white product-in-use photography.
- Huge edge-to-edge product photos.
- Rounded photo cards with text overlays.

Avoid:

- Stock photography.
- Generic illustrations.
- Dashboard screenshots as hero visuals.
- Decorative gradients.
- Visible hero cards.

The product object is the composition.
