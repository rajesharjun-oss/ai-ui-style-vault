# Components

## Hero Stage

Full-viewport Pure Black background with product image occupying 50 to 60 percent of viewport height. No card chrome. Headline anchors bottom-left in Frost White SF Pro Display at 80px.

## Primary CTA Button

Apple Blue fill, Paper White text, 36px radius, 10px 20px padding, SF Pro Text 14px weight 600. Only one filled chromatic button per viewport.

## Ghost Price Label

Transparent background, Frost White text on dark stages, SF Pro Text 14px weight 400, 36px radius, 10px 20px padding.

## Category Eyebrow Tag

Plain Signal Orange text, SF Pro Text 17px weight 600, no background, no border, no chip chrome.

## Feature Card Dark Stage

Rounded photo card on dark sections, 28px radius, 28px internal padding, black-and-white or low-color product-in-use photography, Frost White overlay text, no shadow.

## Light Section Content Block

Paper White background, two-column layout, Signal Orange eyebrow, 56px SF Pro Display heading in Obsidian, Platinum body, optional inline link.

## Top Navigation Bar

Pure Black pill navigation, 980px radius, about 10px vertical padding, Apple glyph left, nav items center, search and bag icons right. Backdrop blur is allowed on scroll.

## Promo Banner

Slim Obsidian strip above nav, centered SF Pro Text 12px Frost White text with inline Link Blue action.

## Section Headline Dark

Large left-aligned SF Pro Display headline, 56px to 80px, Frost White, negative tracking, no subtitle.

## Inline Feature Link

Small circular icon and two-line label in SF Pro Text 17px weight 600. Use as tertiary drill-in below body copy.

## Product Spec Tile

Photo-led or hardware-detail block with 28px radius, concise copy, Platinum body, and no generic dashboard chrome.
