## Color Rules

- Apple Blue is the only filled CTA color.
- Link Blue is for light surfaces; Halo Blue is for dark surfaces.
- Signal Orange is category text only.
- Violet and teal are category accents only.
- Do not use pure white for paragraph copy on dark stages.
- Do not add extra chromatic accents.
