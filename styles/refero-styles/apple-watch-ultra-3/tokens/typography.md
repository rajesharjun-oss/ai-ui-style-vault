# Typography

## Font Roles

| Font | Role | Weights | Sizes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SF Pro Display | Headlines, display text, section openers, feature titles | 600, 700 | 19px to 80px |
| SF Pro Text | Body, nav, buttons, captions, footnotes, legal text | 400, 600 | 10px to 44px |

## Typography Rules

- Use SF Pro Display for the large dramatic language.
- Use SF Pro Text below headline scale.
- Keep negative tracking on every size.
- Use weight 600 to 700 for headings and labels.
- Use weight 400 for paragraphs, prices, and legal copy.
- Use OpenType feature `numr` when available.
