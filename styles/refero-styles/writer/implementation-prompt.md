# Implementation Prompt

Build a polished editorial enterprise-AI interface inspired by the WRITER Refero style system.

Use a near-white canvas, centered Poppins display headlines, one violet-highlighted word per headline, rounded pill controls, CanelaDeck-style serif body accents, and alternating white and dark resource sections. The result should feel like an AI newsroom for enterprise teams.

## Required Style Decisions

- Page background: Pure White `#ffffff`.
- Main text: Ink Black `#000000`.
- Dark sections: Obsidian `#27272b`.
- Structural borders: Mist `#e4e7ed`.
- Muted copy: Slate `#666666`, Ash `#bdbdbd`, and Fog `#d2d4d7`.
- Headline word highlight: Orchid Accent `#a95ef8`, exactly one word.
- Action accent: Iris Brand `#5551ff`, used sparingly.
- Soft section wash: Lavender Wash `#e4e9ff`.
- Buttons: 60px radius.
- Inputs: 72px radius.
- Cards, images, icons: 12px radius.
- No heavy shadows.

## Typography Direction

Use Poppins for display, headings, nav, labels, UI, buttons, and body. Display headlines should be 64px, weight 500, line-height 1, and -1.98px tracking. Eyebrow labels should be uppercase at 11px to 12px with wide positive tracking.

Use CanelaDeck or a similar serif only as a body-size editorial accent. Do not set the whole interface in serif.

## Layout Direction

Create:

- A centered editorial hero with an eyebrow, display headline, and pill CTA group.
- Exactly one Orchid word highlight in the main headline.
- Pill email capture input paired with a pill button.
- White feature sections with rounded cards.
- A dark Obsidian resource section with white cards.
- A grayscale trust-logo row.
- A soft privacy/announcement gradient strip if needed.

## Avoid

- Orchid Accent used in buttons, icons, body text, or broad surfaces.
- More than one or two Iris accent elements in a viewport.
- Sharp corners below 12px on cards, images, or icons.
- Body text below 14px or above 18px.
- Drop shadows on cards or buttons.
- Full black large background fills.
- New accent colors beyond Orchid, Iris, Lavender, and Cobalt.
