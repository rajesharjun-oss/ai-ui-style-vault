# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Poppins 64px weight 500 with -1.98px tracking for display headlines.
- Highlight exactly one word per display headline in Orchid Accent.
- Use 60px radius on buttons and 72px radius on email inputs.
- Use 12px radius on cards, images, and icons.
- Set eyebrow labels in uppercase Poppins 11px to 12px with wide tracking.
- Alternate Pure White and Obsidian section backgrounds.
- Use Iris Brand for only one or two high-attention elements per viewport.
- Use CanelaDeck only as a sparse body accent.
- Use Mist borders and whitespace for separation.

## Do Not

- Do not use Orchid Accent outside display headline word highlights.
- Do not use sharp corners below 12px on cards, images, or icons.
- Do not set body text below 14px or above 18px.
- Do not add drop shadows to cards or buttons.
- Do not use wide letter spacing on text larger than 14px.
- Do not introduce new accent colors.
- Do not use full-opacity black as a large background fill.
- Do not overuse Iris Brand as a generic decoration.
