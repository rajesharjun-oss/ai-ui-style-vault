# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Page canvas: Pure White.
- Max content width: 1200px.
- Hero headline width: about 800px.
- Section gap: 80px to 120px.
- White sections alternate with Obsidian resource sections.
- Controls: pill-shaped and centered around conversion moments.
- Cards: soft 12px radius with light structure.

## Imagery

Use:

- Enterprise AI workflow cards.
- Product screenshots.
- Agent result checklists.
- Grayscale trust-logo rows.
- Clean UI diagrams.
- Subtle dark promotional cards.

Avoid:

- Generic AI abstract art.
- Neon gradients across full sections.
- Colorful brand-logo rows.
- Heavy 3D renders.
- Stock business photography as the primary visual language.

## Composition Notes

Headlines and product UI should do most of the work. The style feels editorial because the white space, center alignment, and typographic scale are confident. Keep visuals useful and restrained.
