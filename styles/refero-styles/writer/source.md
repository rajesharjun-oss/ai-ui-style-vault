This bundle paraphrases the Refero style reference into implementation notes for AI-assisted UI building. It preserves the system intent, color roles, typography behavior, spacing, component recipes, layout rhythm, and visual constraints without copying the source text wholesale.

## What To Preserve

- Near-white editorial page canvas.
- Poppins display headlines with tight tracking.
- Exactly one Orchid Accent word in major headlines.
- Pill controls with 60px to 82px radii.
- CanelaDeck-style serif accent copy at body size.
- 12px radius for cards, images, and icons.
- Alternating Pure White and Obsidian sections.
- Minimal elevation and light structural borders.
