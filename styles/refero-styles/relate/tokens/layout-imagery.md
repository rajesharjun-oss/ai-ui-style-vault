# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use full-width sections with centered content capped at 1200px.
- Hero is centered and single-column.
- Place a trust badge above the headline.
- Use a huge headline with one royal-blue highlighted word.
- Place a pill CTA below the hero body copy.
- Let a pale blue/lavender wash sit behind the hero product mockup.
- Put product screenshots below the fold as large floating cards.
- Use feature sections with centered heading and description above product UI.
- Use 40px rounded feature containers with generous padding.
- Use a 4-by-2 customer logo strip.
- Footer can switch to a dark navy surface.

## Imagery

Use:

- Kanban pipeline boards.
- Deal cards with company avatars and values.
- Prospect list panels.
- Email thread panels.
- Contact detail panels.
- Monochrome customer logos.
- Small flat trust badges.

Avoid:

- Lifestyle photos.
- Decorative 3D renders.
- Heavy illustrations.
- Busy chart art.
- Non-product visual filler.
