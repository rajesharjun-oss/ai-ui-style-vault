# Guidelines

## Do

- Use `#145aff` as the main saturated brand color.
- Use Inter for headings, body, nav, and buttons.
- Set body text at 14px to 16px on the cool white canvas.
- Use pill radius for buttons, tags, and nav CTAs.
- Use soft shadows on deal cards and larger feature cards.
- Use small colored dots for pipeline status.
- Set display headings at 56px to 80px with tight negative tracking.
- Use 8px radius for inner cards and 16px to 40px for outer containers.
- Use product UI screenshots as the main imagery.
- Keep the hero centered and clean.

## Do Not

- Do not use browser-default link blue.
- Do not create heavy solid-color CTA blocks.
- Do not introduce gradients with more than two stops.
- Do not use sharp 0px corners.
- Do not set body text below 14px.
- Do not stack multiple saturated accents in one component.
- Do not use Inter weights above 600 or below 400.
- Do not use lifestyle photography, 3D renders, or large decorative illustrations.
