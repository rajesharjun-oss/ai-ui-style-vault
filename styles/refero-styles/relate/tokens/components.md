# Components

## Ghost Outline Button

- Role: secondary action and nav CTA.
- Background: `#fcfcfc` or semi-transparent white on tinted surfaces.
- Text: `#145aff` or `#020520`.
- Border: `1px solid #145aff`.
- Radius: 9999px or 100px.
- Padding: 14px 32px.

## Frosted Pill Button

- Role: primary CTA pattern when paired with hero or nav.
- Background: white or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.
- Text: `#145aff`.
- Border: subtle blue or transparent.
- Radius: 9999px.
- Avoid heavy saturated fills.

## Pipeline Column Card

- Role: Kanban CRM column.
- Background: `#ffffff`.
- Radius: 16px.
- Padding: 12px.
- Shadow: tiny black soft shadow.
- Header includes a 4px to 6px colored dot, count, and deal value.

## Deal Card

- Role: individual deal in a pipeline column.
- Background: `#ffffff`.
- Radius: 8px.
- Padding: 12px 16px.
- Shadow: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1) 0px 0px 4px -2px`.
- Content: avatar, company name, deal value, activity note, assignee, timestamp.

## Prospect List Card

- Role: feature screenshot showing prospect/contact rows.
- Background: `#ffffff`.
- Radius: 16px.
- Padding: 12px.
- Layout can include company list, email thread, and contact detail panel.

## Logo Mark Badge

- Role: nav and footer brand mark.
- Shape: rounded square, 32px to 40px.
- Fill: `#145aff`.
- Glyph: white lowercase mark.
- Wordmark: Inter 600, `#14141e`.

## Nav Link

- Type: Inter 15px weight 500.
- Color: `#14141e`.
- Hover/active: `#145aff`.
- Gap: about 16px.
- No underline by default.

## Hero Gradient Banner

- Role: soft glow behind hero/product area.
- Use a simple two-color blue/lavender wash.
- Large bottom radius around 40px to 48px.
- Keep the glow subtle and airy.

## Feature Section Card

- Background: `#fcfcfc`.
- Radius: 40px.
- Padding: 52px 72px.
- Shadow: soft multi-layer stack.
- Contains heading, description, and product UI mockup.

## Glassmorphic Container

- Background: `rgba(252, 252, 252, 0.2)`.
- Radius: 28px to 48px.
- Padding: 12px to 20px.
- Backdrop blur: around 15px.
- Use over tinted or darkened washes.

## Status Dot

- Size: 4px to 6px.
- Potential/blue: `#3b82f6`.
- Pending/orange: `#ffa64d`.
- Won/green: `#16ca2e`.
- Lost/red: `#f26052`.
- Keep status color tiny and contextual.

## Customer Logo Strip

- Two rows of four monochrome logos.
- Logo color: `#14141e` at about 60 to 70 percent opacity.
- Gap: 40px to 60px.
- Max width around 900px.

## Input Field

- Background: white on light surfaces, subtle translucent white on dark/tinted surfaces.
- Border: `1px solid #e2e8f0` or white on dark.
- Radius: 12px.
- Padding: 15px.
- Focus: `#0099ff` glow ring.

## Y Combinator Badge

- Small inline trust badge above hero.
- Orange-red square icon with `#f26052`.
- Text: Inter 14px, `#14141e`.
- Centered above the headline.
