# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink Black | `#000000` | `--color-ink-black` | Maximum contrast headings and nav borders |
| Charcoal Deep | `#070707` | `--color-charcoal-deep` | Densest display moments |
| Obsidian | `#242429` | `--color-obsidian` | Primary text, icon strokes, and hairline borders |
| Graphite | `#3e3e3e` | `--color-graphite` | Body text, metadata, and secondary lists |
| Ash Gray | `#919191` | `--color-ash-gray` | Muted location subtitles and tertiary labels |
| Parchment | `#f9f5f2` | `--color-parchment` | Page canvas, hero, footer, and breathing space |
| Gallery White | `#ffffff` | `--color-gallery-white` | Card surfaces, image borders, badge backgrounds |

## Usage

Use no chromatic accent color in the UI. The photographs provide color. Keep surfaces in Parchment and Gallery White, and keep structure in Obsidian hairlines.
