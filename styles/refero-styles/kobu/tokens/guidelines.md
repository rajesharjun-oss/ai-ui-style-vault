# Guidelines

## Do

- Make the wordmark large enough to act as the hero element.
- Use Fira Mono with wide tracking for every badge, label, nav item, category, price, and metadata element.
- Place property images full-bleed with 0px radius.
- Let photography be the only color.
- Keep UI surfaces in Parchment and Gallery White.
- Use 10px to 15px gaps inside card groups.
- Use 60px to 80px between sections.
- Right-align prices and View All links.
- Use 1px Obsidian hairlines for structure.

## Do Not

- Do not add colored CTA buttons, accent fills, or gradients.
- Do not use shadows or elevation.
- Do not round images, cards, buttons, or nav.
- Do not use badge radius above 5px.
- Do not use weights above 500.
- Do not add filled icons, illustrations, or decorative graphics.
- Do not introduce a new neutral or chromatic UI color.
- Do not use any type pairing besides Gill Sans-like humanist sans plus Fira Mono-like mono.
