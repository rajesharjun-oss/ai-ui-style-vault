# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Page canvas: Parchment.
- Max width: 1440px.
- Navigation: full-width top bar with bottom hairline.
- Hero: massive wordmark as the visual anchor.
- Content: image-first property cards.
- Section gap: 60px to 80px.
- Card internal gap: 10px to 15px.
- Footer: minimal Parchment band with a top hairline.

## Imagery

Use:

- Boutique hotel exteriors.
- Villas and retreats.
- Architecture and landscape photography.
- Warm interior detail shots.
- Tropical foliage against concrete or stone.
- Natural editorial framing.

Avoid:

- Image overlays.
- Duotone treatments.
- Vignettes.
- Decorative masks.
- Illustrations.
- Abstract graphics.
- Product-style thumbnail framing.

## Composition Notes

Images should feel like artifacts placed on paper. Avoid framing them as digital cards. Let whitespace between images and text create the gallery-catalogue rhythm.
