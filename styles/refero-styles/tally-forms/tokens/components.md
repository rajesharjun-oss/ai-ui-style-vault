# Components

## Primary Blue Button

- Background: `#0b6bcb`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 7px
- Font: Inter 16px/500
- Padding: 8px 14px
- Use for create, start, publish, continue, and sign-up actions.

## Secondary Button

- Background: Paper or transparent
- Border: 1px solid `#e9e9e7`
- Text: `#37352f`
- Radius: 7px
- Hover: Mist Gray
- Use for secondary actions.

## Form Builder Card

- Background: Paper
- Border: 1px solid Line Gray
- Radius: 10px
- Padding: 24px
- Shadow: soft card shadow
- Content: block rows, icons, form fields, editor text.

## Form Input

- Background: Paper
- Border: 1px solid Line Gray
- Radius: 7px
- Text: Graphite Ink
- Placeholder: Soft Gray
- Focus: Action Blue ring or border.

## Template Card

- Background: Paper
- Border: 1px solid Line Gray
- Radius: 10px
- Shadow: subtle
- Content: form preview, category tag, short description.

## Tag Or Status Badge

- Radius: 7px
- Background: Paper or Warm Paper
- Border: 1px solid Line Gray
- Text: Soft Gray or Graphite Ink
- Selected: Blue Tint background with Action Blue text.

## Doodle Cluster

- Colors: Sun Yellow, Electric Magenta, Lime Pop, Soft Purple, Warm Orange
- Use near hero, empty states, and celebratory surfaces.
- Do not place doodle colors inside functional controls.

## Response Graph Card

- Background: Paper
- Border: 1px solid Line Gray
- Radius: 10px
- Chart accent: Action Blue
- Labels: Soft Gray
- Keep chart simple and friendly.

## Editor Sidebar

- Background: Warm Paper or Paper
- Divider: Line Gray
- Selected state: Blue Tint or Action Blue marker
- Rows: compact 8px rhythm.

## Mobile Menu

- Background: Paper
- Radius: 10px to 12px
- Text links: Graphite Ink
- CTA: Action Blue
- Avoid heavy overlay effects.
