# MindMarket

MindMarket is a warm illustrated editorial style system: cream-paper canvas, massive Inter headlines, floating white pill navigation, sticker-soft rounded cards, and flat paper-cut character art in green, blue, coral, and yellow.

- Source: https://styles.refero.design/style/9130ad37-bf80-458f-b808-ac0ef6a8d1e9
- Reference site: https://mindmarket.com
- Captured: 2026-07-28
- Theme: light
- Best for: market research brands, insight products, friendly agencies, editorial service sites, illustrated landing pages

## Use This When Building

Use Cream Paper as the page canvas, not white. Let Inter at enormous scale carry the brand voice, then support it with flat paper-cut illustrations and very rounded white cards. Keep UI chrome minimal and soft: 50px radii for nav, cards, and buttons, small chromatic icon dots inside CTAs, and green as a structural accent rather than body text.
