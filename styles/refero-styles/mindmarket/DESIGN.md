# MindMarket - Style Reference

MindMarket feels like a warm storybook printed on cream paper. The design pairs huge geometric type with friendly paper-cut characters, using large radii and soft surface contrast instead of shadows.

## Visual Thesis

- Use a warm cream background as the structural canvas.
- Use Inter at extreme display scale as the signature voice.
- Make nav, cards, and buttons sticker-soft with 50px radii.
- Keep white as an elevated surface, not the page background.
- Use green as a brand-structural accent in borders, nav strokes, and small UI highlights.
- Use blue, coral, and yellow decoratively in illustration and small action details.
- Avoid shadows and gradients; use cream-to-white surface shifts for depth.

## Core Ingredients

- Canvas: Cream Paper `#f5f1e4`.
- Primary text: Ink Black `#2c2e2a`.
- Elevated surface: Pure White `#ffffff`.
- Brand accent: Fresh Grass `#8ed462`.
- Illustration accents: Sky Pop, Coral Pop, Sunshine Pop.
- Type: Inter only, from 9px labels to 144px display.
- Shape: 50px nav/cards/buttons, 63.75px illustration containers, 10px micro-elements.

## Page Composition

Use a floating white pill nav above a giant headline hero. Place character illustration art directly on the cream canvas, often bleeding from the bottom edge of the hero into the next section. Use white rounded content cards for service descriptions or insight blocks, then close with a vivid yellow footer accent band.

## Color Behavior

Color is decorative and brand-structural, not a strict semantic state system. Fresh Grass ties the interface together, while blue, coral, and yellow live mostly in illustration and single-purpose accents. Do not turn every state into a color system.

## Type Behavior

Inter is the whole system. The display move is enormous: 140-144px, weight 500, line-height 0.95, and -0.06em tracking. Smaller section headings drop to 53-81px but should remain bold in scale, not in weight.
