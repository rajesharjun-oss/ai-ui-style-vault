# Components

## Floating Pill Navigation Bar

- Background: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 50px
- Floats above cream canvas with generous margins.
- Contains logo container, nav links, menu icon button, and CTA.
- Nav links: Inter 15px, weight 500, Ink Black.
- Horizontal padding inside items: 17px to 20px.
- Treat the whole nav as one soft pill.

## Brand Logo Container

- Small rounded square.
- Radius: 10px to 20px.
- Contains MindMarket mark.
- Pair with wordmark in Inter 15px to 17px, weight 500, Ink Black.

## Primary CTA Button

- Light or white pill treatment.
- Radius: 50px.
- Padding: about 11px vertical, 20px horizontal.
- Text: Inter 15px, weight 500, Ink Black.
- Include a small colored circular icon at the right edge.
- Do not rely on a filled chromatic background for the global CTA.

## Menu Toggle Button

- Circular button around 40px.
- Fill: Fresh Grass.
- Icon: dark menu glyph.
- Position at the right edge of nav before CTA.

## Content Card

- Background: Pure White.
- Radius: 50px to 64px.
- Padding: 21px.
- Shadow: none.
- Optional hairline border.
- Contains heading, body copy, and CTA.

## Service Card Link

- White card with service content.
- Uses a Coral Pop filled pill as a service-level action.
- Radius: 50px.
- Use coral sparingly, not as the universal action color.

## Hero Display Block

- Background: Cream Paper.
- Headline: Inter 140px to 144px, weight 500, Ink Black, -0.06em tracking, line-height 0.95.
- Subheadline: 17px to 20px.
- No card wrapper.
- Illustration can bleed in from below.

## Illustration Hero Panel

- Flat paper-cut characters.
- Color fills: Fresh Grass, Sky Pop, Coral Pop, Sunshine Pop, purple, white.
- Art sits directly on cream canvas.
- No frames, masks, or photographic backgrounds.

## Inline Text Link

- Text: Ink Black or Stone Gray.
- Font: Inter 15px to 18px, weight 400.
- Underline or border-bottom in matching neutral.
- Avoid colorful link state changes.

## Footer Accent Block

- Background: Sunshine Pop.
- Full-width closing band.
- Use generous padding.
- Treat it as a warm page close, not a CTA panel.
