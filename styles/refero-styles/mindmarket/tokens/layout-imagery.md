# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Full-bleed cream sections.
- Floating white pill nav above content.
- Hero uses a centered or left-aligned giant headline stack.
- Illustration can bleed from the bottom of the hero into the next section.
- Content cards should be 600px to 700px wide for readability.
- Illustration-led sections can let art take around 60% of width.
- Text-card sections can use white cards around 50% width.
- Vertical spacing: 80px to 120px.
- Avoid sidebars, dense grids, and pricing-table layouts.

## Imagery

- Illustration only.
- Flat paper-cut character art.
- Bold outlines and vivid flat fills.
- Human figures can be exaggerated, dynamic, and overlapping.
- Art sits directly on the cream canvas.
- No photography.
- No product screenshots.
- No 3D renders.
- No gradient backgrounds.

## Surfaces

| Level | Name | Value | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 0 | Cream Paper | `#f5f1e4` | Dominant warm page canvas |
| 1 | Pure White | `#ffffff` | Elevated cards, nav, inset blocks |
| 2 | Sandstone | `#e0dbce` | Recessed or secondary surface |

## Motion

- Keep movement gentle and illustration-friendly.
- Use simple fades or subtle position shifts if needed.
- Avoid technical dashboard motion, parallax, and loud animation.
