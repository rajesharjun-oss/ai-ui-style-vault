# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Cream Paper as the page canvas.
- Reserve white for elevated cards and floating nav.
- Use 50px radius for cards, buttons, and nav containers.
- Use 63.75px radius for illustration containers.
- Use 10px radius for small chips and inline micro-elements.
- Set display headlines at 140-144px Inter with tight tracking.
- Use Fresh Grass as structural accent in borders, nav strokes, and toggles.
- Use Ink Black for primary text, borders, and icons.
- Embed a small chromatic circle icon in CTA buttons.
- Float the nav as one white pill bar.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce a second typeface.
- Do not use accent colors as functional success, error, or warning states.
- Do not use sharp 0-4px corners on cards or buttons.
- Do not place white cards on white backgrounds.
- Do not use shadows or gradients for elevation.
- Do not use more than one chromatic accent per UI component.
- Do not reduce primary display type below 53px.
- Do not rely on photography or screenshots.
