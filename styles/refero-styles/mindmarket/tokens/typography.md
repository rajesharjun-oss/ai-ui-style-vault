# Typography

## Typeface

- Family: Inter
- Substitute: Inter from Google Fonts, system sans as fallback
- Weights: 400 and 500
- Sizes: 9px, 15px, 17px, 18px, 20px, 30px, 53px, 81px, 140px, 144px
- Line-height: 0.95 to 2, with display at 0.95 to 1.20 and body around 1.5
- Letter spacing: -0.06em at 81px and above, -0.04em at 53px, normal at body sizes

Inter is the whole system. Do not introduce a serif or custom display typeface.

## Type Scale

| Token | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `body-sm` | 15px | 400-500 | 1.5 | 0 | small body and nav |
| `body-lg` | 18px | 500 | 1.5 | 0 | larger body |
| `subheading` | 20px | 500 | 1.25 | 0 | support text |
| `heading-sm` | 30px | 500 | 1.2 | 0 | card heading |
| `heading` | 53px | 500 | 1.15 | -2.12px | section heading |
| `heading-lg` | 81px | 500 | 1.2 | -4.86px | large section heading |
| `display` | 140px | 500 | 0.95 | -8.4px | hero display |
| `display-lg` | 144px | 400 | 0.95 | -8.64px | alternate hero display |

## Rules

- Use Inter for every text element.
- Use extreme display scale for primary page headings.
- Do not shrink primary page headings below 53px.
- Use weight 500 for display authority.
- Use scale and tight tracking, not extra font families, for personality.
