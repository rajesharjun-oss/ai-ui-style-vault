# Implementation Prompt

Build a bright fintech landing page or app surface in the Fold style.

Use Fog White `#f0f1f5` as the full-page canvas and Pure White `#ffffff` as the elevated card surface. Use Midnight Navy `#20294c` for primary text, hero headlines, and filled button backgrounds. Use Cornflower Steel `#375390` for borders, icon strokes, secondary surfaces, and structural details. Keep Electric Blue `#459af8` to inline links, active nav text, and one small accent dot per screen.

Set the hero headline in GT Walsheim Pro or a rounded geometric sans. Use 100px, weight 700, line-height around 0.91, and -0.125em tracking. This poster headline is the signature. Do not dilute it with light weights or small display sizes. Use 18px body copy in Dusk for support text.

Use 12px radius for cards and 9999px radius for all interactive pills, including buttons, tags, and nav links. Keep this shape vocabulary simple. Use 24px card padding, 48px section gaps, and navy-tinted shadows such as `rgba(32, 41, 76, 0.12)`.

Use crisp 3D-style object illustrations such as pizza, popcorn, phone, shoe, mango, notebooks, or app objects. Objects should float against the Fog White canvas without frames, gradients, or photographic backgrounds. Use thin-line map drawings and bank connection cards for product features.

Avoid pure black text, gray shadows, gradients, yellow used repeatedly, square controls, centered long body paragraphs, and more than three nested surface levels.
