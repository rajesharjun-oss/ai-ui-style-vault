# Guidelines

## Do

- Use 100px weight 700 with -0.125em tracking for the hero headline.
- Use navy-tinted shadows such as rgba(32, 41, 76, 0.12).
- Use 12px radius for cards and 9999px for interactive pills.
- Set the page background to Fog White and card surfaces to Pure White.
- Use Cornflower Steel for icons, borders, and structural elements.
- Reserve Midnight Navy for text and filled buttons.
- Keep Electric Blue to links and one small accent dot per screen.
- Use 48px section gaps and 24px card padding.

## Do Not

- Do not use Voltage Yellow across more than one card per page.
- Do not set major headlines at weight 400.
- Do not introduce extra border-radius tokens.
- Do not use pure black for text.
- Do not add gradients to backgrounds or buttons.
- Do not center long body paragraphs.
- Do not stack more than three surface levels.
- Do not use neutral gray shadows.
