# Typography

## Primary Typeface

- Family: GT Walsheim Pro
- Substitute: Inter, Switzer, Satoshi, or General Sans
- Weights: 400, 600, 700
- Sizes: 10px, 14px, 16px, 17px, 18px, 20px, 24px, 26px, 32px, 64px, 100px
- Line-height: 0.85 to 1.75
- Letter spacing: -0.125em at 100px, -0.040em at 64px, -0.030em at 24-32px, positive tracking for 10-14px labels

## Secondary Typeface

- Family: GT America
- Substitute: Inter, Sohne, NB International Pro
- Weights: 400 and 500
- Sizes: 24px and 42px
- Use sparingly for compressed display or heading moments.

## Type Scale

| Token | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `tag` | 10px | 600 | 1.2 | 0.4px | category pills |
| `caption` | 14px | 400 | 1.43 | 0.35px | captions and labels |
| `body-sm` | 16px | 400 | 1.5 | 0 | small body |
| `body` | 18px | 400-600 | 1.41 | -0.54px | body copy |
| `subheading` | 24px | 600 | 1.33 | -0.72px | card headings |
| `heading-sm` | 32px | 700 | 1.18 | -0.96px | section title |
| `heading` | 42px | 500 | 1 | -1.68px | compressed heading |
| `heading-lg` | 64px | 700 | 0.85 | -2.56px | H2 |
| `display` | 100px | 700 | 0.91 | -12.5px | hero poster headline |

## Rules

- Use the 100px weight-700 hero headline as the signature.
- Do not set major headlines at weight 400.
- Use GT Walsheim Pro for most UI and copy.
- Keep body paragraphs left-aligned if they run longer than two lines.
