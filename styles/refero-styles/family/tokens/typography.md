# Typography

## Font Families

| Role | Family | Fallback |
|------|--------|----------|
| Display | `Family` | `Inter`, ui-sans-serif |
| Body and UI | `Inter` | ui-sans-serif, system-ui |

## Scale

| Token | Size | Weight | Line height | Tracking | Use |
|-------|------|--------|-------------|----------|-----|
| Micro | 12px | 400-600 | 19px | -0.01px | Tiny labels, utility text |
| Caption | 15px | 600 | 22px | -0.14px | Pills, badges, compact labels |
| Body | 17px | 400 | 26px | -0.22px | Main copy |
| Subheading | 19px | 400 | 27px | -0.3px | Secondary headings |
| Heading | 23px | 500 | 25px | -0.44px | Card headings |
| Heading Large | 44px | 400-500 | 53px | -0.88px | Section headings |
| Display | 68px | 500 | 75px | -2.1px | Hero headline |

## Rules

- Use the custom display face for hero and display text whenever available.
- Use Inter for everything below major headings.
- Keep display tracking tight and confident.
- Keep body copy warm, calm, and readable at 16px to 17px.
- Avoid generic system display headings.
