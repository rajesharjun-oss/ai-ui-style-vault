## Color Behavior

- The page background is always warm cream, not cold white.
- Use stone surfaces or inset borders to separate card zones.
- Keep orange for text links and small callouts.
- Keep blue for inline action text, not primary filled buttons.
- Let illustrations carry most of the bright color.
