# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Center content in a 1200px max-width container.
- Use generous section gaps between 80px and 120px.
- Keep feature grids readable and spacious.
- Use grouped card clusters on `#f2f0ed` when a section needs depth.
- Avoid dense dashboards and crowded product chrome.

## Imagery

- Use flat hand-drawn illustration clusters.
- Place abstract mascots and confetti-style shapes around hero text.
- Pull fills from blue, orange, green, yellow, pink, and violet accents.
- Use thin charcoal strokes.
- Keep the canvas cream and the illustrations flat.
