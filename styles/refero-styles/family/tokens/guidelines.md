# Guidelines

## Do

- Use the custom Family typeface for display and hero headings.
- Use 10px radius for cards and nav surfaces.
- Use 32px or 9999px only for pill-style controls.
- Build cards with 1px inset borders in `#f2f0ed`.
- Use `#ff3e00` for demo links and short accent text.
- Set body text in Inter at 16px to 17px.
- Use `#fbfaf9` as the base for full-bleed sections.
- Use flat illustration clusters to add personality.

## Do Not

- Do not use heavy drop shadows.
- Do not use `#0086fc` as a filled CTA.
- Do not introduce gradients.
- Do not use Inter at display sizes when the Family face is available.
- Do not separate surfaces with white-on-white.
- Do not add decorative effects to pill buttons.
- Do not use `#ff2b3a` outside destructive or error states.

## AI Builder Checks

- Does the page feel like warm paper rather than glass or chrome?
- Are cards defined by inset borders instead of floating shadows?
- Are colorful accents mostly in illustrations and links?
- Is the main CTA a dark pill?
- Are the hero illustrations framing the text rather than crowding it?
