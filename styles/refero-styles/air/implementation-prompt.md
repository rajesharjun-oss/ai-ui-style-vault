# Implementation Prompt

Create an original dark creative-operations or asset-library interface inspired by the Air Refero style.

Use a full-bleed dark photographic canvas with cloud or sky imagery and translucent glass-like sculptural forms. Use `#ffffff` for text and borders on dark surfaces, `#f5f5f5` for cards and inputs, `#1b1b1b` for text and borders on light surfaces, `#426188` for atmospheric heading text on dark surfaces, and `#2b7fff` only for links or short inline emphasis.

Use Control or a restrained sans substitute such as Inter or system-ui for most UI at weight 500. Use Control Compressed or Anton/Druk Wide only for huge uppercase display statements. Use Control Cursive or Caveat/Reenie Beanie for one italic emphasis word inside a headline.

Build these elements:

- Minimal top nav, about 72px high, with left links and right ghost actions.
- Ghost nav buttons with transparent fill, 1px white border, white text, 8px radius, and 10px 16px padding.
- Full-viewport photographic hero with centered headline and one cursive emphasis word.
- Haze cards using `#f5f5f5`, 12px radius, 20px padding, no border, and no shadow.
- Text inputs with Haze fill, Ink text, low-alpha black border, 4px radius, and 10px padding.
- Image cards with 11px to 14px radius and no shadow.
- Monochrome logo bar on dark canvas.
- Feature sections with centered heading, 48px section gap, and 2-column or 3-column grids.

Do not copy Air brand assets, exact screenshots, page copy, or proprietary layouts. Generate a new media-library or creative-workflow screen that borrows only the mood, token relationships, and component language.
