# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Patterns

- Full-bleed dark photographic hero with minimal nav.
- Centered headline and short supporting copy over atmospheric imagery.
- 1150px max-width for text, cards, and grids.
- Full-viewport atmospheric sections can break out of the max-width.
- Feature sections use centered header plus 2-column or 3-column grid.
- Haze cards appear as light islands on the dark canvas.
- Logo bars are monochrome on dark background.

## Imagery

- Use moody sky and cloud photography.
- Use translucent glass-like sculptural 3D forms.
- Use product screenshots in Haze cards or image grids.
- Avoid generic illustration systems.
- Avoid decorative gradients and heavy overlays.
- Keep image-heavy sections open and spacious.

## Responsive Notes

- On mobile, reduce large display headlines dramatically while preserving uppercase impact.
- Stack grids vertically and keep Haze card padding at 20px.
- Keep nav actions compact with 8px radius.
- Preserve full-bleed hero photography.
- Use fewer glass/photographic objects around text to maintain readability.
