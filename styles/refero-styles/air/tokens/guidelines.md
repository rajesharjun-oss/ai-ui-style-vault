# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Control at weight 500 for all body, nav, button, and link text.
- Set all buttons and interactive elements to 8px radius.
- Apply 4px radius to form inputs only.
- Use Haze for card surfaces and inputs on dark backgrounds.
- Use Whiteout for borders and text on dark surfaces.
- Use Ink for text and borders on light surfaces.
- Break headlines with one italic word in Control Cursive against upright Control TNT or Control.
- Let full-bleed photography and glass forms carry the emotional weight.

## Don't

- Do not use solid filled colored buttons.
- Do not apply shadows or elevation to cards.
- Do not use more than one saturated accent color per page.
- Do not set body text below 16px or above weight 500.
- Do not use rounded pill shapes except for toggle or filter controls.
- Do not introduce new radii outside 4px, 8px, 11px to 14px, and 12px.
- Do not use gradients.
- Do not make the UI busier than the photography.
