# Colors

## Accent

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Twilight Blue | `#426188` | `--color-twilight-blue` | Heading text on dark backgrounds, desaturated and atmospheric rather than vivid |
| Signal Blue | `#2b7fff` | `--color-signal-blue` | Blue text accent for links, tags, and emphasized short phrases. Do not use for buttons or backgrounds |

## Neutrals

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Black Void | `#000000` | `--color-black-void` | Base background behind photographic sections, footer, and dark UI moments |
| Ink | `#1b1b1b` | `--color-ink` | Body and heading text on light surfaces, button borders on light cards |
| Haze | `#f5f5f5` | `--color-haze` | Card surfaces, input fields, and subtle button fills on dark backgrounds |
| Whiteout | `#ffffff` | `--color-whiteout` | Primary text on dark surfaces, nav button borders, hairline strokes, and card surfaces over photographic backgrounds |

## Rules

- Signal Blue is for links and short text emphasis only.
- Do not create blue buttons or blue backgrounds.
- Do not use gradients; depth comes from photography and flat surface contrast.
- Use Whiteout on dark surfaces and Ink on light surfaces.
