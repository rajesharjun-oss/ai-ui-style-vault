# Components

## Ghost Navigation Button

- Role: Primary nav CTA on dark canvas.
- Background: transparent.
- Border: `1px solid #ffffff`.
- Text: `#ffffff`.
- Radius: `8px`.
- Padding: `10px 16px`.
- Font: Control `14px`, weight `500`.

## Solid Light Button

- Role: Secondary action on light cards.
- Background: `#f5f5f5`.
- Text: `#1b1b1b`.
- Border: `1px solid #1b1b1b`.
- Radius: `8px`.
- Padding: `8px 16px`.
- Do not use saturated fills.

## Pill Toggle Button

- Role: Segmented control or filter chip.
- Shape: full pill.
- Background: semi-transparent dark fill.
- Text: `#000000` on light or `#ffffff` on dark.
- Use only for toggles and filters.

## Underline Link

- Role: Inline text link.
- Background: transparent.
- Text: `#2b7fff` or `#1b1b1b`.
- Underline: 2px bottom line.
- Padding: `0 8px`.
- No filled hover state.

## Haze Card

- Role: Content card on dark background.
- Background: `#f5f5f5`.
- Text: `#1b1b1b`.
- Radius: `12px`.
- Padding: `20px`.
- Border: none.
- Shadow: none.

## Image Card With Radius

- Role: Media container for photo grids and product screenshots.
- Background: transparent.
- Radius: `11px` to `14px`.
- Padding: `0`.
- Shadow: none.
- Use object-fit cover imagery.

## Text Input

- Background: `#f5f5f5`.
- Text: `#1b1b1b`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)`.
- Radius: `4px`.
- Padding: `10px`.
- Font: Control `16px`, weight `500`.

## Logo Bar Row

- Role: Client or partner logo grid.
- Background: dark canvas.
- Logos: white or semi-transparent white.
- Gap: `32px` columns and `48px` rows.
- Use one or two rows.

## Full-Bleed Photographic Section

- Role: Hero and atmospheric sections.
- Background: full viewport sky/cloud/glass photography.
- Text: Whiteout or Twilight Blue.
- No visible container.
- No border.
- No gradient overlay unless required for readability.

## Compressed Display Headline

- Font: Control Compressed.
- Size: `259px`.
- Weight: `900`.
- Line height: `0.85`.
- Transform: uppercase.
- Color: `#ffffff`.
- Bleeds to viewport edges.

## Dual-Style Headline

- Combine upright Control or Control TNT with one word in Control Cursive.
- Cursive word: `32px` to `56px`, italic, weight `400`.
- Keep the rest grounded and restrained.

## Feature Section Header

- Subheading: Control `20px`, weight `500`.
- Main title: Control Cursive or Control TNT at `32px` to `56px`.
- Alignment: centered on dark background.
- Gap: `48px` above and below.
