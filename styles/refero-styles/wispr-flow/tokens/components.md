# Components

## Primary CTA Button

Lavender Whisper fill, Vast Ink text, 2px Vast Ink border, 12px radius, Figtree 500 at 16px, compact 14px padding or standard 16px 24px padding.

## Outlined Secondary Button

Lumen Cream fill, Vast Ink text, 2px Vast Ink border, 12px radius, Figtree 500 at 16px, 16px 24px padding.

## Ghost Text Button

No fill, no border, Vast Ink text, Figtree 400 at 16px, underline on hover.

## Floating Navigation Pill

Cream pill with 9999px radius and 2px Vast Ink border. Contains Flow logo, nav links, dropdown chevrons, and a lavender CTA.

## Cream Content Card

Lumen Cream fill, 32px radius, 32px padding, no shadow, optional 2px Vast Ink border.

## Dark Feature Card

Vast Ink fill, 40px to 80px radius, 55px to 70px padding, Lumen Cream text, no shadow.

## Teal Status Badge

Forest Ink fill, Lumen Cream text, 1000px radius, 8px 16px padding, Figtree 500 at 14px.

## Platform Pill Badge

Transparent fill on dark surfaces, Lumen Cream text, 1px to 2px Pure White border, full pill radius, 8px 16px padding.

## Dark Square Badge

Vast Ink fill, Lumen Cream text, 8px radius, 8px 16px padding. Use sparingly as a tight geometric counterpoint.

## Waveform Visualizer

Cream pill with 9999px radius and 2px Vast Ink border. Use 5 to 7 Vast Ink bars, 8px to 24px tall, with active animation when recording.

## Phone Mockup

Flat dark device frame with 40px radius, cream chat bubbles, avatar circle, and waveform visualizer. Avoid photorealistic device rendering.

## Section Container

Alternating Lumen Cream and Vast Ink full-bleed bands with 40px to 80px radius and no separators.

## Hand-Drawn Underline Accent

Lavender Whisper SVG squiggle beneath one or two headline words, 3px to 4px stroke, slightly irregular path.
