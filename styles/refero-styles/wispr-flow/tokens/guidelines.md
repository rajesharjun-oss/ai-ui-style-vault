# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Lavender Whisper as the sole primary action color.
- Set display headlines in Eb Garamond weight 400.
- Use 2px Vast Ink borders on interactive elements.
- Alternate cream and dark sections page-wide.
- Use 32px radius on standard cards.
- Use 40px to 80px radius on dark chamber sections.
- Use Figtree 400 for body copy.
- Reserve Figtree 600 to 700 for nav, badges, and buttons.
- Pair Lavender Whisper with Forest Ink.
- Use Ember Glow only for live or active indicators.

## Do Not

- Do not use bold display serif.
- Do not use box shadows for cards.
- Do not add blue or unrelated action colors.
- Do not use radius below 12px on buttons.
- Do not use radius below 32px on cards.
- Do not use gradients.
- Do not center body text.
- Do not place cream content on cream canvas without border or fill contrast.
