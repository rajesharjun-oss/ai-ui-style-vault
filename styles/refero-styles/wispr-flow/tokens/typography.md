# Typography

## Font Roles

| Font | Role | Weights | Sizes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eb Garamond | Display and editorial headings | 400 | 32px, 48px, 64px, 120px |
| Figtree | Body text, UI labels, nav, buttons, badges | 400, 500, 600, 700 | 14px, 16px, 20px, 22px, 24px, 32px |

## Type Scale

| Role | Font | Size | Weight | Line Height | Letter Spacing | Token |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caption | Figtree | 14px | 400 | 1.3 | normal | `--text-caption` |
| Body Small | Figtree | 16px | 400 | 1.3 | normal | `--text-body-sm` |
| Body | Figtree | 20px | 400 | 1.3 | normal | `--text-body` |
| Subheading | Figtree | 24px | 400 | 1.3 | normal | `--text-subheading` |
| Heading Small | Eb Garamond | 32px | 400 | 1.3 | -0.96px | `--text-heading-sm` |
| Heading | Eb Garamond | 48px | 400 | 0.95 | normal | `--text-heading` |
| Heading Large | Eb Garamond | 64px | 400 | 0.95 | -1.92px | `--text-heading-lg` |
| Display | Eb Garamond | 120px | 400 | 0.85 | -3.6px | `--text-display` |

## Typography Rules

- Do not use bold display headings.
- Serif authority comes from scale, not weight.
- Use Figtree 400 as the normal body setting.
- Reserve Figtree 500 to 700 for nav, badges, and buttons.
- Keep body copy left-aligned.
