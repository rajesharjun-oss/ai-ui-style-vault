## Capture Notes

The Refero page describes Wispr Flow as a warm editorial broadsheet translated into software. The page alternates cream paper sections with deep dark chambers. It uses Eb Garamond for large regular-weight display headlines and Figtree for UI and body copy.

The AI-useful pieces to preserve are:

- Lumen Cream `#ffffeb` as the main canvas.
- Vast Ink `#1a1a1a` as text, border, and dark chamber surface.
- Lavender Whisper `#f0d7ff` as the only primary CTA fill.
- Forest Ink `#034f46` as secondary badge and panel color.
- Ember Glow `#ffa946` only for live or active state punctuation.
- 2px Vast Ink borders on interactive controls.
- 32px standard cards and 40px to 80px dark chamber sections.
- No box shadows.
- Cream and dark sections must alternate page-wide.
- Display serif stays weight 400, even at 64px to 120px.
