- Mood: cream broadsheet, dark velvet chambers

## Use This When

Use this style for voice products, AI assistants, productivity software, creator tools, editorial SaaS pages, dictation products, and landing pages that should feel like warm print design rebuilt for software.

## Core Idea

Wispr Flow alternates a warm Lumen Cream canvas with deep Vast Ink chambers. Display authority comes from regular-weight Eb Garamond at huge sizes, while Figtree handles UI, navigation, buttons, and badges. The system is flat and border-driven: 2px ink borders, oversized radii, lavender CTAs, forest teal badges, and no card shadows.
