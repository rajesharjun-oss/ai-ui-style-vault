# Typography Tokens

## Fonts

| Font | Token | Role | Substitute |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arcadia | `--font-arcadia` | Display headings, hero copy, premium editorial moments. | Tiempos, Georgia |
| Sans | `--font-sans` | Body, nav, buttons, labels, dashboard data, tables. | Inter, system sans |

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Line Height | Weight | Token |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| caption | `12px` | `1.33` | `500` | `--text-caption` |
| body-sm | `14px` | `1.43` | `400` | `--text-body-sm` |
| body | `16px` | `1.5` | `400` | `--text-body` |
| body-lg | `18px` | `1.56` | `400` | `--text-body-lg` |
| subheading | `21px` | `1.33` | `500` | `--text-subheading` |
| heading-sm | `32px` | `1.16` | `500` | `--text-heading-sm` |
| heading | `48px` | `1.08` | `500` | `--text-heading` |
| display | `64px` | `1.0` | `500` | `--text-display` |

## Font Rules

- Use Arcadia for hero and major headings only.
- Use sans for all product UI, navigation, body, forms, and data.
- Keep financial data clear, compact, and aligned.
- Do not use decorative or playful display faces.
- Keep letter spacing normal or subtly tight.
