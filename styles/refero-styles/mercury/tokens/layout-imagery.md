# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Grammar

Mercury uses a premium banking layout: dark hero, controlled content width, focused CTA row, product preview cards, and atmospheric full-bleed photography. The system should feel like financial infrastructure with editorial restraint.

## Common Layouts

- Dark hero with mountain or terrain photography, Arcadia display heading, subhead, and CTA row.
- Graphite dashboard preview beneath or beside hero copy.
- Finance metric cards arranged in calm grids.
- Treasury or banking feature cards with one cobalt highlight.
- Transaction rows and account panels in compact product UI.
- Legal, compliance, and footer sections in muted dark surfaces.

## Imagery

- Use atmospheric mountain, terrain, sky, and horizon photography.
- Keep photography dim enough to support text.
- Use product screenshots and dashboard mockups as secondary visuals.
- Avoid lifestyle stock photos, cartoon illustration, and colorful abstract backgrounds.

## Motion

- Use subtle `180ms` to `240ms` UI transitions.
- Product transitions can be slow and calm.
- Avoid bounce, playful hover effects, and aggressive parallax.
