# Guidelines

## Do

- Use onyx and graphite as the main surfaces.
- Reserve cobalt for primary action and active states.
- Use Arcadia for major headings.
- Use sans for all operational UI and financial data.
- Use landscape or terrain imagery for atmospheric depth.
- Keep finance cards calm and legible.
- Use 12px cards and 6px buttons.
- Use subtle hairline borders for structure.

## Don't

- Do not add neon gradients or multiple accent colors.
- Do not use playful illustration.
- Do not make finance dashboards overly colorful.
- Do not use heavy shadows on normal cards.
- Do not use large pill CTAs everywhere.
- Do not use pure white page sections in the dark flow.
- Do not let photography make text unreadable.
