# Mercury Design Notes

Mercury is a premium dark fintech system. It presents banking as calm infrastructure: near-black surfaces, quiet gray cards, soft text hierarchy, one cobalt action color, and large atmospheric landscape imagery. The result feels trustworthy, founder-oriented, and restrained.

## Visual Principles

- Keep the canvas dark and stable.
- Use one blue for action and emphasis.
- Use grayscale surface depth instead of decorative color.
- Let mountain photography carry the hero mood.
- Keep financial UI calm, compact, and legible.
- Use small radii for controls and moderate radii for cards.

## Color System

The palette is onyx, graphite, steel grays, pale text, and cobalt. Cobalt `#546de5` is the only strong color and should be reserved for conversion, active states, and key financial highlights.

## Typography

Arcadia gives the brand its editorial finance tone. Use it for display and major headings. Use a clean sans fallback for body, nav, product UI, labels, and data tables. Body copy stays readable at 16px to 18px, with muted gray for secondary information.

## Layout

Use a 1200px to 1280px content width, large dark hero sections, full-bleed photographic bands, and finance card grids. Product dashboards should appear as calm graphite panels with strong data hierarchy.

## Elevation

Use borders, tonal contrast, and small blur/shadow only where necessary. Avoid glossy or glassy effects. Finance UI should feel solid and exact.
