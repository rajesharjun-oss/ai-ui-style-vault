# Components

## Pill Button - Light

White fill, `#1f1f1f` text, 2px dark border, full pill radius, 10px by 24px padding, and 16px Inter semibold.

## Pill Button - Dark

`#1f1f1f` fill, white text, full pill radius, compact horizontal padding, and 16px Inter semibold.

## Ghost Pill On Photo

Translucent white fill, white text, white 1px border, full pill radius, and 10px by 24px padding.

## Pill Text Link

Transparent, near-black text, 12px radius, and no padding. Use for small inline interactions.

## Promo Gradient Bar

Top strip with cobalt gradient, white centered text, and one underlined white link. Height around 32px to 40px.

## Navigation Header

Dark `#1f1f1f` bar with white wordmark, centered nav, login text, and sign-up pill.

## Location Selector Pill

White country or region selector with full radius, compact Inter text, and a small flag or location mark.

## Phone Preview Card

iPhone-style product frame with 22.5px top radius. Place it overlapping the lower edge of a photo hero.

## Pricing Tier Card

White rounded card on dark section, 22.5px radius, 24px to 40px padding, Aeonik tier name and price, muted Inter description.

## Trust Badge Block

Transparent cell with centered brand mark and muted 12px to 14px caption.

## Muted Section Header

24px to 40px Aeonik weight 500 in Slate `#717173`.

## List Container

Recessed `#f7f7f7` surface, 20px radius, 16px to 24px padding, and border-led row separation if needed.
