# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use full-bleed photo hero sections for high-impact brand moments.
- Overlay white display text directly on image areas with clean negative space.
- Keep constrained content around 1200px max width.
- Use 80px section spacing.
- Flip pricing and conversion sections to dark `#1f1f1f` backgrounds.
- Place white cards on dark bands for contrast.
- Use symmetrical grids for pricing, badges, and plan comparisons.
- Keep layouts conversion-oriented, not dashboard-dense.

## Photography

Photography is the main color source.

Use:

- High-key sky and cloud scenes.
- Pale blues, whites, and warm natural light.
- Candid close-up human subjects.
- Crops with enough open space for white type.

Avoid:

- Moody dark photography.
- Generic stock business imagery.
- Illustrations.
- 3D renders.
- Abstract graphics.
- Loose flat product screenshots.

## Product Imagery

Show product UI inside phone-frame cards with a rounded top edge. Do not place raw rectangular screenshots in the layout.
