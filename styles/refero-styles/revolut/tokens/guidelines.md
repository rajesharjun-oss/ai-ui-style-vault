# Guidelines

## Do

- Use full pill geometry for every major button, tag, and input.
- Keep Aeonik-style display headings at weight 500 or lower.
- Reserve the cobalt gradient for the top promotional strip.
- Use gray hairline borders instead of shadows.
- Let full-bleed photography carry color and atmosphere.
- Keep UI surfaces white, mist, or ink.
- Use a dark pricing/conversion band with white tier cards.
- Tighten display tracking from hero size down to smaller headings.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce additional brand colors.
- Do not add shadows to normal cards or buttons.
- Do not use heading weights above 500.
- Do not reuse the blue gradient on hero, card, or button surfaces.
- Do not use square or lightly rounded primary actions.
- Do not use color as the main state indicator on grayscale UI.
- Do not use decorative graphics, 3D renders, or illustrations.
