# Guidelines

## Do

- Use black as the single filled action color.
- Use `rgba(0,0,0,0.12)` for borders and outlines.
- Use 9999px radius for buttons, tags, chips, and inputs.
- Use 6px radius for cards and images.
- Set body text around 17px with generous line-height.
- Use 400 for body, 500 for UI and most headings, and 600 sparingly.
- Let white space carry the composition.
- Keep image and text blocks editorial, calm, and precise.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce accent colors.
- Do not use gradients.
- Do not use colored background bands.
- Do not add card shadows.
- Do not use square or lightly rounded buttons.
- Do not use heavy display weights.
- Do not fill the UI with icons.
- Do not make dense dashboard-style panels from this system.
