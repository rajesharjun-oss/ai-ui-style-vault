# Typography

Primary family: `"OpenAI Sans", "Inter", "Sohne", system-ui, sans-serif`

OpenType features: `calt`, `liga`

Scale: Major Second, base 17px, ratio 1.125.

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Label | 13px | 500 | 1.51 | 0 | Small labels, compact metadata |
| UI | 14px | 500 | 1.4 | 0 | Nav, buttons, chips |
| Body Small | 16px | 400 | 1.5 | -0.01em | Compact paragraphs |
| Body | 17px | 400 | 1.65 | -0.01em | Main body copy |
| Minor Heading | 18px | 500 | 1.32 | -0.01em | Card titles and small section copy |
| Subhead | 22px | 500 | 1.26 | -0.01em | Section introductions |
| Section Heading | 28px | 600 | 1.21 | 0.011em | Mid-size editorial headings |
| Display | 48px | 500 | 1.16 | -0.03em | Hero and featured article headline |

## Guidance

- Use 400 for body copy.
- Use 500 for nav, buttons, labels, and most headings.
- Use 600 sparingly at the 28px heading tier.
- Avoid weights 700 and 800.
- Avoid tiny body text below 16px.
