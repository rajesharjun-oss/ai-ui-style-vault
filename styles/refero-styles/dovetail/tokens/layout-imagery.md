# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Pattern

The core product structure is:

1. Dark app shell.
2. Top navigation with thin border.
3. Sidebar or filter column.
4. Main research grid.
5. Evidence cards with video, transcript, tags, and metadata.
6. Detail panel for selected research artifact.
7. Dashboard metrics and list rows.

## Grid

- Use compact card grids for insights and evidence.
- Use sidebar widths around 240px to 280px.
- Use 12px to 20px gaps inside app views.
- Use 64px to 96px gaps on product marketing pages.
- Let the content density feel intentional.

## Imagery

Use:

- Video thumbnails.
- Transcript snippets.
- Insight cards.
- Customer quotes.
- Dashboard charts.
- Research media previews.

Avoid:

- Abstract purple blobs.
- Decorative hero illustration.
- Generic stock photos.
- Bright colorful screenshots that break the dark system.
