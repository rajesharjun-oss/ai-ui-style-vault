# Typography

## Families

- Sans: `"Inter", system-ui, sans-serif`
- Mono: `"JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, monospace`

## Roles

| Role | Size | Family | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Use |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Metadata | 12px | JetBrains Mono | 500 | 1.33 | 0 | Timestamps, labels, IDs, technical metadata |
| Caption | 13px | Inter | 500 | 1.38 | 0 | Muted descriptions and small labels |
| UI | 14px | Inter | 500 | 1.43 | 0 | Nav, buttons, inputs |
| Body | 15px to 16px | Inter | 400 | 1.5 to 1.6 | 0 | Research notes and descriptions |
| Card Title | 18px to 20px | Inter | 600 | 1.3 | -0.01em | Evidence and insight card titles |
| Section Heading | 32px | Inter | 600 | 1.15 | -0.02em | Dashboard and marketing sections |
| Hero | 48px to 56px | Inter | 700 | 1.05 | -0.03em | Rare product/marketing hero |

## Guidance

- Use Inter for product UI and content.
- Use JetBrains Mono only for metadata.
- Keep body and UI compact.
- Avoid decorative display fonts.
- Avoid oversized marketing type in app views.
