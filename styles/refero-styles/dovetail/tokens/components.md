# Components

## Primary Indigo Button

- Background: `#8b7cf6`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 8px
- Font: Inter 14px/600
- Use for create, analyze, import, and primary workflow actions.

## Secondary Dark Button

- Background: `#111111` or `#1e1e1e`
- Border: 1px solid `#2d2d2d` or `#3a3a3a`
- Text: `#fafafa`
- Radius: 8px
- Use for toolbar and secondary actions.

## Research Card

- Background: `#111111`
- Border: 1px solid `#1e1e1e`
- Radius: 8px
- Padding: 16px to 20px
- Content: title, metadata row, tags, snippet, optional preview.

## Evidence Video Card

- Background: Obsidian or Charcoal
- Radius: 10px to 12px
- Content: video thumbnail, timestamp, transcript snippet, tag pills
- Use dark overlays and quiet controls.

## Tag Pill

- Background: `#1e1e1e`
- Border: 1px solid `#2d2d2d`
- Text: Silver or Pearl
- Radius: 9999px
- Font: 12px to 13px
- Selected: Soft Indigo fill or border.

## Sidebar Navigation Item

- Background: transparent by default
- Hover: Charcoal
- Active: Slate with Pearl text
- Optional active marker: Soft Indigo
- Radius: 8px
- Font: Inter 14px/500

## Search Or Filter Input

- Background: Obsidian
- Border: 1px solid Charcoal
- Radius: 8px
- Placeholder: Silver
- Text: Pearl
- Compact 12px vertical padding.

## Metric Tile

- Background: Obsidian
- Border: 1px solid Charcoal
- Radius: 8px
- Label: JetBrains Mono 12px
- Value: Pearl, larger, semibold
- Context: Silver text.

## Transcript Panel

- Background: Obsidian or Charcoal
- Rows: compact with dividers
- Timestamp: JetBrains Mono
- Speaker/title: Pearl
- Body: Silver
- Selected quote: Indigo highlight.

## Table Or List Row

- Base: Graphite or Obsidian
- Divider: Charcoal
- Hover: Charcoal
- Active: Slate
- Use for projects, clips, insights, and participants.
