# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Max width: 1200px.
- Full-bleed black and white section alternation.
- Section gaps: 80px to 120px.
- Cards and imagery use large rounded frames.
- Avoid typical small SaaS card grids.

## Imagery

- Use athlete and body-in-motion photography.
- Use swimming, running, recovery, sleeping, and training scenes.
- Use natural light and physical texture.
- Overlay metric stats directly on images.
- Avoid illustrations, 3D renders, and abstract decorative graphics.

## Source

- Refero page: https://styles.refero.design/style/05053a60-1964-4154-9d58-ebdf6352ed3a
- Reference site: https://whoop.com

## Direction

The page should feel like a premium performance lab: dramatic black stage, clean white analytics surfaces, athletic photography, and data-first confidence.
