# Typography

## Font Family

| Role | Family | Fallback |
|------|--------|----------|
| All text | `Proxima Nova` | Montserrat, Nunito Sans, DM Sans |

## Scale

| Token | Size | Weight | Line height | Tracking | Use |
|-------|------|--------|-------------|----------|-----|
| Caption | 14px | 400-500 | 1.59 | 0 or 0.1em uppercase | Labels and captions |
| Body Small | 16px | 400 | 1.33 | -0.48px | Supporting body text |
| Body Large | 20px | 600 | 1.29 | -0.6px | Confident body and metrics |
| Subheading | 24px | 500 | 1.13 | -0.72px | Subheads |
| Heading Small | 32px | 600 | 1.09 | -0.96px | Feature headings |
| Heading | 35px | 600 | 1 | -1.05px | Main headings |
| Heading Large | 50px | 600 | 0.8 | -2px | Large section headings |
| Display | 120px | 400 | 0.71 | -4.8px | Hero display |

## Rules

- Use huge display type as the primary design device.
- Compress tracking aggressively at large sizes.
- Use uppercase 0.1em tracking on nav, buttons, and metric labels.
- Keep body copy at 19px to 20px when possible.
- Do not use small running prose below 16px.
