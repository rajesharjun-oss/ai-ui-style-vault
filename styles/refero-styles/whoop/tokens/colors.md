# Colors

## Brand

| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Pulse Violet | `#4a53ff` | Announcement bar and key brand action moments |

## Neutrals

| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Obsidian | `#000000` | Dark hero, dark sections, text on light, neutral filled buttons |
| Carbon | `#191919` | Secondary dark bands and footer surfaces |
| Fog Gray | `#808080` | Muted body text and secondary descriptions |
| Ash | `#999999` | Tertiary metadata and inactive labels |
| Hairline | `#e5e7eb` | Borders, dividers, outlined buttons, input frames |
| Lab Mist | `#f3f5f9` | Light feature cards and flat elevated panels |
| Paper White | `#ffffff` | Light sections, text on dark, white pill buttons |

## Color Behavior

- Alternate black and white full-bleed sections.
- Use Lab Mist for light cards instead of shadows.
- Use Pulse Violet sparingly.
- Do not add another accent color.
- Keep photography natural and chromatic, but UI chrome nearly achromatic.
