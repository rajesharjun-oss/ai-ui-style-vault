# Guidelines

## Do

- Use 300px border radius for every button.
- Use 120px display type with tight line height and -4.8px tracking.
- Alternate full-bleed black and white sections.
- Use Pulse Violet only for announcement bars and key brand actions.
- Use 24px cards without drop shadows.
- Apply uppercase 0.1em tracking to labels, buttons, and metrics.
- Keep body text confident at 19px to 20px.

## Do Not

- Do not add drop shadows to cards or buttons.
- Do not introduce a second accent color.
- Do not loosen line-height on large display text.
- Do not center long body paragraphs.
- Do not use square or tiny-radius buttons.
- Do not use colored gradients on UI surfaces.
- Do not set running prose below 16px.

## AI Builder Checks

- Does the page alternate effort black and recovery white?
- Are buttons full pills everywhere?
- Is the typography huge, tight, and athletic?
- Is violet rare and intentional?
- Is photography the main visual asset?
