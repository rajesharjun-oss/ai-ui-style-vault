## Use This When

Use this style when a product should feel athletic, premium, data-driven, and dramatically focused. It works well for fitness, health analytics, biometric dashboards, performance coaching, subscription hardware, quantified-self products, and lifestyle products with strong photography.

The key behavior is a strict split between black theatrical sections and white clinical content sections. Typography is oversized and tightly tracked. Buttons are fully pill-shaped. Cards are big, flat, and rounded. Color is almost absent except for a single Pulse Violet accent.
