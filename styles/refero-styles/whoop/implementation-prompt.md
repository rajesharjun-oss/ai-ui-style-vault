# Implementation Prompt

Build a premium performance-lab interface in the WHOOP style.

Use a high-contrast split canvas: full-bleed black sections (`#000000`) alternate with full-bleed white sections (`#ffffff`). Do not blend them with gray transitions. Use Lab Mist (`#f3f5f9`) for light feature cards and Carbon (`#191919`) for secondary dark bands.

Use Proxima Nova for all typography, with Montserrat, Nunito Sans, or DM Sans as fallback. Set the hero display headline at 120px, weight 400, line-height around 0.71, and letter-spacing around -4.8px. Use 32px to 50px weight-600 headings, and keep body copy confident at 19px to 20px with tight tracking.

All buttons are pills with 300px radius. On dark sections, use white pill buttons with black uppercase labels. Use Pulse Violet (`#4a53ff`) only for announcement bars and key brand action moments. Use outlined pill buttons with Hairline borders on light sections.

Cards should be large and flat: 24px radius, 24px to 32px padding, no drop shadow. Use photography as the primary visual asset: athletes, recovery, sleep, motion, and biometric data. Overlays can use white text and small metric labels.

Avoid extra accent colors, decorative SaaS cards, 3D assets, generic gradients, heavy shadows, small body text, and square buttons.
