# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Warm Paper as the dominant canvas.
- Use Charcoal Ink for primary text.
- Use Campfire Ochre for the main booking action.
- Use Forest Green for map markers and nature accents.
- Use rounded cabin cards and booking panels.
- Use topographic map cues and trail-style linework.
- Keep photography natural and product-relevant.
- Keep booking controls calm and easy to scan.

## Do Not

- Do not use generic hotel booking blue.
- Do not introduce neon or saturated extra accents.
- Do not use hard black as the main text color.
- Do not make the map look like default Google Maps styling.
- Do not use glossy hotel marketplace cards.
- Do not use sharp cards or square CTAs.
- Do not crowd the layout with dense tables.
