# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Pattern

The core page structure is:

1. Warm top navigation with simple links and an ochre CTA.
2. Hero or search area with topographic map and booking controls.
3. Map/list split for stay discovery.
4. Rounded cabin location cards.
5. Sticky booking panel for dates, guests, price, and action.
6. Editorial nature sections with cabin photography.
7. Quiet footer on Warm Paper or Linen Wash.

## Grid

- Use max width around 1200px.
- Use card grids for cabins and stays.
- Use split map/list layouts for discovery.
- Keep booking panels to about 360px on desktop.
- Stack map, cards, and booking controls on mobile.

## Imagery

Use:

- Real cabin photography.
- Nature and forest scenes.
- Topographic contour lines.
- Trail-map textures.
- Map pins and route diagrams.
- Booking card UI.

Avoid:

- Generic hotel stock photos.
- Default map tiles.
- City or business travel imagery.
- Neon gradients.
- Glossy SaaS mockups.
