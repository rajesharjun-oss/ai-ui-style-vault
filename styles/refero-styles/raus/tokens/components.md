# Components

## Header Navigation

- Background: Pure White or transparent Warm Paper
- Height: about 72px
- Links: Charcoal Ink or Stone Gray
- CTA: Campfire Ochre pill
- Keep nav simple and outdoorsy.

## Primary CTA Pill

- Background: `#c98a3f`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Radius: 9999px
- Padding: 12px 20px
- Font: 14px/600
- Use for booking, reserve, search, or check availability.

## Outline Pill Filter

- Background: transparent or Pure White
- Border: 1px solid `#e8e0d2`
- Text: `#25231f`
- Radius: 9999px
- Selected: Charcoal Ink fill with Pure White text.

## Location Card

- Background: Warm Paper or Pure White
- Radius: 20px
- Contains cabin image, price pill, metadata, and calm copy
- Shadow: subtle warm card shadow
- Use rounded images and uncluttered metadata rows.

## Booking Panel

- Background: Pure White
- Border: 1px solid Parchment Border
- Radius: 24px
- Padding: 32px
- Width: about 360px on desktop
- Contains date selectors, guest controls, price details, and one ochre CTA.

## Map Marker Button

- Size: 40px
- Shape: circle or compact pill
- Background: Forest Green or Charcoal Ink
- Text/icon: Pure White
- Shadow: marker shadow
- Use for cabins, selected locations, and trail-map pins.

## Topographic Map Panel

- Background: Sand or Linen Wash
- Radius: 24px
- Details: contour lines, muted labels, forest markers
- Avoid default map-tile styling.

## Date Input

- Background: Pure White or Linen Wash
- Border: 1px solid Parchment Border
- Radius: 12px
- Text: 14px
- Focus: subtle Forest Green or Ochre outline.

## Editorial Card

- Background: Warm Paper
- Radius: 20px
- Border: optional Parchment Border
- Content: nature photo, cabin detail, short copy.
