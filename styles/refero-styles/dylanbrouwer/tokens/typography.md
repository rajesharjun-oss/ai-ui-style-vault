# Typography

## Families

- Display: `Tilt Warp`
- Body: `Sohne`
- Supporting: `ABC Gravity`, `Die Grotesk B`
- Fallback: `Inter, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`

Use Tilt Warp for poster-scale display moments. Use Sohne or a clean grotesk for readable copy and metadata.

## Rules

- Make display type enormous on desktop.
- Keep display weight at 400.
- Use tight negative tracking for large text.
- Keep body copy compact and direct.
- Do not use many font families in one view.
- Do not convert the design into a normal SaaS heading hierarchy.

## Responsive Notes

On mobile, reduce mega display to 84-120px and keep the compressed line-height. Preserve the poster feeling with cropping, overlap, or stacked words rather than shrinking everything into polite text.
