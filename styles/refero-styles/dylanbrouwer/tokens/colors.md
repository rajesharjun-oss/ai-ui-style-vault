# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cinder Black | `#161616` | `--color-cinder-black` | Main ink, borders, nav text, inverted surface |
| Bone White | `#f7f7f7` | `--color-bone-white` | Light card surface, footer text, foreground on dark |
| Concrete Mist | `#efefee` | `--color-concrete-mist` | Primary page background and neutral gallery field |
| Ember Orange | `#f7601b` | `--color-ember-orange` | Accent tags, hover fills, special CTA capsules |
| Utility Gray | `#777777` | `--color-utility-gray` | Metadata, counters, inactive links, secondary text |
| Ash Line | `#d5d5d5` | `--color-ash-line` | Subtle dividers on light sections |
| Deep Field | `#000000` | `--color-deep-field` | Full black panels and image-backed overlays |

## Color Behavior

The palette is deliberately narrow. Most screens should be built from concrete, bone, and cinder. Ember Orange should appear as an event, not as a general decoration layer.

Use Ember Orange for:

- Accent capsules.
- Hover fills on key pills.
- Project labels.
- Small attention marks.
- One special CTA treatment.

Use Cinder Black for:

- Primary type.
- Borders.
- Nav pills.
- Dark surface panels.
- Project index rules.

Use Utility Gray for:

- Metadata.
- Dates and counters.
- Inactive states.
- Secondary labels.

## Forbidden Color Moves

- No additional vivid accent color.
- No blue or purple CTA system.
- No soft beige page tint.
- No multicolor tag system.
- No gradients used as decoration.
