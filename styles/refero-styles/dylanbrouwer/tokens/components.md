# Components

## Poster Masthead

- Background: concrete or deep black.
- Display type: Tilt Warp, very large, uppercase.
- Size: 144-288px desktop.
- Tracking: very tight negative tracking.
- Metadata: compact uppercase labels.
- Composition: words may stack, crop, or occupy the full viewport width.

The masthead should feel like a poster, not a marketing hero.

## Pill Navigation

- Shape: 9999px radius.
- Border: 1px solid `#161616`.
- Background: transparent or bone white.
- Text: uppercase 13px.
- Padding: 8px 14px.
- Hover: fill with cinder black or ember orange.
- Active state: cinder black background with bone text, or ember accent for a special state.

## Accent Capsule

- Shape: 9999px radius.
- Background: `#f7601b`.
- Text: cinder black or bone white depending on contrast.
- Typography: uppercase 12-13px.
- Padding: 6px 12px.
- Use sparingly.

## Project Card

- Background: bone white or transparent.
- Radius: 0px.
- Border: optional 1px cinder or ash line.
- Padding: 24px if content is boxed.
- Media: sharp 0px radius image.
- Title: 44px Tilt Warp or display-like grotesk.
- Metadata: 12px uppercase, utility gray.
- Shadow: none.

## Work Index Row

- Layout: horizontal row.
- Border: 1px top or bottom rule.
- Columns: project title, category, year, status.
- Title: 44px or 76px depending on scale.
- Metadata: 12px uppercase.
- Hover: orange capsule or image reveal.

## Media Frame

- Radius: 0px.
- Border: optional 1px cinder.
- Shadow: none.
- Object fit: cover.
- Crop: assertive, not soft.

## Dark Panel

- Background: `#000000` or `#161616`.
- Text: `#f7f7f7`.
- Accent: rare `#f7601b` capsule or link.
- Use for transitions, footer, or dramatic case-study detail.

## Metadata Strip

- Text: 12px uppercase.
- Tracking: 0.24px.
- Color: utility gray on light, bone white on dark.
- Layout: row or wrap with 12px gaps.
