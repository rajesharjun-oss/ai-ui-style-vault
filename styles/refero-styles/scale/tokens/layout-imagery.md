# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Patterns

- Over-hero navigation on a dark photographic background.
- Full-bleed cinematic hero with white headline and one primary CTA.
- Pure-black wireframe or model-graph band after the hero.
- White product UI sections with constrained 1280px content.
- Enterprise dashboard cards in 2-3 column grids.
- Case-study cards and logo strips on white or paper backgrounds.
- Data tables and model evaluation rows with compact vertical rhythm.

## Imagery

- Use high-contrast photography, infrastructure, lab, defense, data center, aerial, or cinematic AI imagery only for campaign moments.
- Use wireframes, node maps, graph lines, and model pathways in black sections.
- Use actual product UI for light sections: dashboards, tables, metrics, charts, and review workflows.
- Avoid stock illustration, 3D mascots, colorful blobs, and consumer lifestyle imagery.

## Responsive Notes

- On mobile, keep the photographic hero dramatic but shorten the headline line length.
- Stack dashboard cards vertically while preserving borders and mono metrics.
- Keep dark wireframe visuals subtle so text stays readable.
- Avoid horizontal tables on mobile unless they can scroll cleanly.
