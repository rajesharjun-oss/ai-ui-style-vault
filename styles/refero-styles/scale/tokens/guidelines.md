# Guidelines

## Do

- Use full-bleed photography only in the hero or major campaign moments.
- Use pure black for the wireframe/data transition band.
- Use white and near-white surfaces for product UI sections.
- Keep accents small and analytical.
- Use mono type for metrics, model IDs, data labels, and code.
- Use 1px borders to define cards, dashboards, and tables.
- Preserve a strong dark-to-light page transition.
- Keep layouts gridded and enterprise-serious.

## Don't

- Do not use colorful gradients as general backgrounds.
- Do not overuse green, blue, or violet outside analytical marks.
- Do not use rounded consumer cards beyond 16px.
- Do not add playful illustration or sticker assets.
- Do not make the whole site dark.
- Do not use heavy shadows on product UI.
- Do not set long body copy in mono.
- Do not make data tables spacious or decorative.
