# Components

## Over-Hero Navigation

- Background: transparent or black at low opacity.
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Border: optional `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`.
- CTA: black or white pill depending on contrast.
- Keep nav compact and serious.

## Black Primary CTA

- Background: `#000000`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Radius: `999px`
- Padding: `12px 20px`
- Font: Suisse Intl `14px`, weight `500`
- Use on light sections.

## White Outline CTA

- Background: transparent.
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`
- Radius: `999px`
- Padding: `12px 20px`
- Use on dark hero and wireframe sections.

## Cinematic Hero

- Full-bleed photographic background.
- Overlay: `--gradient-hero-overlay`.
- Text: white.
- Layout: large left or centered copy with short supporting text.
- Avoid decorative cards over the hero.

## Wireframe Data Band

- Background: `#000000`
- Visual: fine lines, graph nodes, model pathways, or 3D data frame.
- Accents: blue, violet, and green at low volume.
- Text: white and ash.

## White Product Card

- Background: `#ffffff`
- Border: `1px solid #e6e6e6`
- Radius: `16px`
- Padding: `24px`
- Text: black and ash.
- Use for product panels, case cards, analytics cards.

## Analytics Dashboard Panel

- Background: `#ffffff` or `#fafafa`
- Border: `1px solid #e6e6e6`
- Radius: `16px`
- Header: sans 14px to 16px.
- Metrics: mono 13px to 16px.
- Chart accents: green, blue, violet, amber, red only when meaningful.

## Model Evaluation Row

- Background: `#ffffff`
- Border bottom: `1px solid #e6e6e6`
- Height: compact, roughly `48px` to `56px`
- Model ID: mono.
- Status chip: pill with neutral or accent color.

## Metric Chip

- Background: `#f6f6f6`
- Text: `#000000`
- Radius: `999px`
- Padding: `6px 10px`
- Font: Suisse Intl Mono `12px`

## Category Tag

- Background: low-alpha green, blue, violet, amber, or red.
- Text: corresponding full accent color.
- Radius: `999px`
- Padding: `4px 8px`
- Use only for categories/status, not decoration.

## Case Study Card

- Background: `#ffffff`
- Border: `1px solid #e6e6e6`
- Radius: `16px`
- Padding: `24px`
- Logo area: monochrome.
- Body: restrained, text-led.

## Logo Strip

- Logos should be monochrome.
- Use generous horizontal spacing.
- Do not add colored brand lockups unless the source product requires it.
