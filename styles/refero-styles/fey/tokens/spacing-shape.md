# Spacing And Shape

## Spacing

| Purpose | Value | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Page max width | 1200px | Centered content rail |
| Section gap | 64px | Dark-room section rhythm |
| Element gap | 10-16px | Nav, cards, rows, badges |
| Card padding | 18-24px | Product and feature cards |
| Dock icon gap | 24px | Monoline icon dock |

## Radius

| Element | Value | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Cards | 16px | Product cards and mockup surfaces |
| Icons | 6px | Small icon containers |
| Small surfaces | 10px | News recap cards and nested rows |
| Buttons/nav/badges | 99px | Signature pill controls |
| Featured cards | 275px | Distinctive large feature cards |

## Elevation

- Product card halo: `rgba(0,0,0,0.8) 0px 0px 44px 0px`
- Primary action halo: `rgba(0,0,0,0.85) 0px 1px 0px 0px, rgba(255,255,255,0.25) 0px 0px 14px 0px`
- Featured card halo: `rgba(0,0,0,0.5) 0px 0px 35px 0px`
- Avoid stacked shadow recipes.
