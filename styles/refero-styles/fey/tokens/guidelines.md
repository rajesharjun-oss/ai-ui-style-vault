# Guidelines

## Do

- Use 99px radius for interactive controls, nav items, and badges.
- Set display headlines at 48-54px with -0.08em tracking and Calibre 700.
- Use Fey Ink as the primary canvas.
- Layer Fey Charcoal and Fey Obsidian for nested depth.
- Use Ember, Signal, and Growth only as meaning-carrying accents.
- Use 16px radius for content cards and product mockup surfaces.
- Pair every dark surface with a single heavy black halo.
- Default secondary copy to Fey Graphite and reserve white for headings and primary values.

## Don't

- Do not use filled chromatic CTA buttons.
- Do not introduce a secondary typeface.
- Do not apply zero or positive letter spacing on display sizes.
- Do not use pure black as the page background.
- Do not use Signal blue for financial status.
- Do not add more than one chromatic accent per headline or card.
- Do not stack multi-layer drop shadows.
- Do not turn product screenshots into decorative thumbnails; they are the main content.
