# Components

## Pill Navigation Button

- Radius: 99px.
- Background: transparent.
- Text: 12-14px Calibre 500.
- Inactive: Fey Graphite.
- Active: Fey White plus 1px Fey Signal bottom border.
- No fill and no shadow.

## Primary Action Button

- Radius: 99px.
- Background: transparent or Fey Ink.
- Text: Fey White, 12-14px Calibre 500.
- Shadow: subtle white halo.
- No chromatic fill.

## Product Showcase Card

- Background: Fey Charcoal.
- Radius: 16px.
- Shadow: heavy Fey Abyss halo.
- Content: charts, tickers, news feeds, insider transactions.

## Insider Transaction Card

- Surface: Fey Ink or Fey Charcoal.
- Radius: 16px.
- Left: circular avatar.
- Center: white name and Fey Graphite company.
- Right: Buy or Sell pill.

## Feature Section Card

- Outer radius can use 275px for a distinctive pill-card shape.
- Inner content uses Fey Ink.
- Body text: Fey Mist.
- Caption below: 12px Fey Graphite.

## Dock Navigation Bar

- Radius: 99px.
- Background: Fey Charcoal.
- Border: 1px Fey Mist.
- Icons: 7-8 monoline white icons around 20px.
- Include separated search button.
- Float over a dark product/stone image.

## Status Badge

- Radius: 99px.
- Padding: 4px 8px.
- Text: 10-11px Calibre 500 uppercase.
- Buy/positive: Fey Growth.
- Negative: muted warm/red tone.

## Ticker Display

- Large value: 48px Calibre 700 Fey White.
- Positive change: Fey Growth.
- Negative change: muted red.
- Use tabular numerals.
- Use tight display tracking.

## Section Headline

- Size: 48-54px.
- Font: Calibre 700.
- Color: Fey White.
- Tracking: about -0.08em.
- One optional highlighted word in Fey Ember or Fey Signal.

## News Recap Card

- Background: Fey Charcoal.
- Radius: 10px.
- Headline: 14px Calibre 500 Fey White.
- Summary: 12px Fey Mist.
- Timestamp: 10px Fey Graphite.

## Carousel Navigation Arrow

- Diameter: about 40px.
- Background: Fey Ink.
- Border: 1px Fey Mist.
- Icon: centered Fey White chevron.
