# Components

## Primary Action Button - Dark Filled

- **Role:** Main CTA such as book demo, get started, or get match.
- **Style:** Obsidian fill, white text, `1.5px` dark border, subtle inset highlight, `14px` radius.
- **Type:** Cosmica `14px`, weight `400`.
- **Rule:** This is the most important interactive element in the system.

## Ghost Action Button - White

- **Role:** Secondary CTA on dark backgrounds.
- **Style:** White fill, dark text, `1px` Iron border, pill-like radius, generous padding.
- **Rule:** Use mainly for contrast inside dark sections or navigation contexts.

## Neutral Pill Button - Light

- **Role:** Subtle action on light backgrounds.
- **Style:** Very light background, Graphite text, `14px` radius, compact padding, no visible border.

## Category Card - Image Top

- **Role:** Service category showcase.
- **Style:** White card, `36px` radius, `1px #ececee` border, image filling the top half, text and tags below.
- **Content:** Title around `20px` weight `600`, tag pills in the lower area.
- **Rule:** The image should be real work/product imagery, not generic illustration.

## Dark Feature Card

- **Role:** Pain-point or contrast feature block.
- **Style:** Slate or Graphite background, white text, `28px` to `36px` radius, `24px` padding.
- **Content:** Strong heading and arrow-led list items around `20px` weight `500`.

## Tag Pill Badge

- **Role:** Quiet category tag.
- **Style:** Transparent background, `1px #ececee` border, Graphite text, `12px` radius, compact padding.
- **Rule:** Keep tags quiet; they should not dominate cards.

## Filled Tag Badge

- **Role:** Higher-emphasis skill or category label.
- **Style:** Iron fill, near-white text, `12px` radius, compact padding.

## Orange Accent Badge

- **Role:** YC batch tags, startup credential chips, or one-word category highlights.
- **Style:** Ember fill, white text, `12px` radius, compact padding.
- **Rule:** This is the only vivid chromatic UI element.

## Email Input Field

- **Role:** Hero email capture or newsletter signup.
- **Style:** White background, dark text, `14px` radius, compact padding, transparent or very subtle border.
- **Pairing:** Usually sits directly beside a dark CTA button.

## Logo Strip

- **Role:** Social proof.
- **Style:** Grayscale/desaturated partner logos in Fog or muted zinc tones.
- **Rule:** No background container; keep the strip calm.

## Stats Block

- **Role:** Large-number proof point.
- **Style:** Number at `40px` to `56px` weight `600`, descriptor at `14px` in muted text.
- **Layout:** Label can sit adjacent to the number with compact spacing.

## Breakthrough Image Section

- **Role:** Full-bleed visual divider.
- **Style:** Full-width landscape or macro nature image, no overlay text, generous corner radius on top corners.
- **Rule:** Use photography structurally to create breath between dense marketplace sections.

## Navigation Bar

- **Role:** Top-level site navigation.
- **Style:** Sticky white header, logo left, centered nav links, login and dark CTA right.
- **Type:** `14px` Cosmica links.
- **Rule:** No visible border or heavy shadow; it should float on the canvas.
