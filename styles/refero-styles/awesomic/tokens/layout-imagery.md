# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Rhythm

- Use a centered `1200px` max-width container.
- Keep major vertical section gaps around `80px`.
- Hero can be split: large left-aligned headline, supporting copy and email capture on the other side.
- Use horizontal or grid-based category image cards for portfolio/service discovery.
- Alternate light card sections with dark feature blocks.
- Use a three-item stats row for large-number proof.
- Use a full-width image divider to break dense content.
- Keep internal card density compact while preserving generous page rhythm.

## Imagery

- Use real product/work imagery in category cards.
- Use landscape or macro nature photography as structural dividers.
- Keep images raw: no overlays, duotone treatments, masks, or abstract effects.
- Use generous corner radius on images, usually `48px` to `64px` for section dividers.
- Desaturate social-proof logos to grayscale.
- Avoid generic illustrations and 3D renders.

## Surface Strategy

- Canvas: Paper.
- Cards: Snow with Cloud borders.
- Subtle cards: near-white.
- Dark contrast blocks: Graphite or Slate.
- Accent: Ember badges only.

## Geometry Philosophy

- Tags and small controls: `12px`.
- Buttons and inputs: `14px`.
- Cards and large surfaces: `36px`.
- Navigation pills: `10000px`.
- The system should feel generously rounded but still precise.
