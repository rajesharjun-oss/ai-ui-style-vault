# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Obsidian (`#09090b`) for all primary action buttons.
- Set cards to `36px` radius and use `1px #ececee` borders for elevation.
- Keep body text at `14px` to `15px`, Cosmica weight `400`, and Graphite text color.
- Reserve Ember (`#ff5a00`) for YC-style accent badges and credential chips.
- Use `56px` to `64px` Cosmica weight `600` for hero and display headlines.
- Use `28px` card padding and `80px` section rhythm.
- Use `10000px` radius for navigation pill CTAs.
- Use `14px` radius for inline action buttons and inputs.

## Don't

- Do not introduce new accent colors.
- Do not use drop shadows on cards.
- Do not set display headlines below weight `600`.
- Do not use Ember for body text, links, or large fills.
- Do not use container radii below `12px`.
- Do not break the single-font rule.
- Do not use pure black; Obsidian is the deepest permitted ink.
- Do not use illustrations or 3D renders where product/work photography is expected.
