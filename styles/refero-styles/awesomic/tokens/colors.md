## Surface Levels

| Level | Name | Value | Purpose |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 0 | Canvas | `#f4f4f5` | Page background. |
| 1 | Card | `#ffffff` | Elevated content surfaces on the canvas. |
| 2 | Subtle Card | `#fafafa` | Slightly recessed content surfaces. |
| 3 | Dark Surface | `#18181b` | Dark feature blocks and inverted sections. |
