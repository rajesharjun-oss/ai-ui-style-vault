# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Paper Cream as the page canvas.
- Use Ivory Card for product UI and content cards.
- Use Electric Cobalt for primary actions and active states.
- Use Velvet Ink for headings and key text.
- Use Warm Gray for secondary copy.
- Use pill buttons and 24px rounded cards.
- Use product screenshots as the main visual proof.
- Keep sections spacious and calm.

## Do Not

- Do not use pure white as the outer page background.
- Do not introduce a second saturated accent.
- Do not use square CTAs or sharp cards.
- Do not make cobalt a full section background.
- Do not use heavy dark shadows.
- Do not rely on generic stock images.
- Do not make the hero a dense dashboard wall.
