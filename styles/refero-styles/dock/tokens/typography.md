# Typography

## Families

- Brand/body: `"Roobert", "Inter", "Plus Jakarta Sans", system-ui, sans-serif`
- UI/control: `"Product Sans", "Google Sans", "Inter", system-ui, sans-serif`

## Roles

| Role | Size | Family | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Use |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Small UI | 14px to 16px | Product Sans | 500 to 600 | 1.3 to 1.5 | 0 | Nav, buttons, form labels |
| Body | 16px | Roobert | 400 | 1.5 | 0 | Main body copy |
| Body Large | 18px to 20px | Roobert | 400 to 500 | 1.45 to 1.55 | -0.01em | Hero support and intros |
| Card Heading | 22px | Roobert | 600 | 1.3 | -0.01em | Feature cards |
| Section Heading | 36px to 48px | Roobert | 700 to 800 | 1.1 to 1.2 | -0.02em to -0.03em | Product sections |
| Hero | 64px | Roobert | 800 | 1.05 | -0.04em | First-screen headline |

## Guidance

- Use Roobert for the warm brand voice.
- Use Product Sans or Google Sans for crisp controls.
- Keep headings tight and confident.
- Avoid overly condensed or editorial serif type.
- Do not make body text tiny; 16px should be the default.
