# Guidelines

## Do

- Use white Canvas as the main page surface.
- Use Obsidian Ink for text, icons, hairlines, and filled primary buttons.
- Use Iris Pulse as a full-width announcement band above navigation.
- Use the display face for all headings 26px and larger.
- Set display headings at weight 400 with tight tracking.
- Use Inter for body, navigation, buttons, captions, and product UI.
- Use 100px radius for buttons and tags.
- Use 8px radius for cards and images.
- Use 1px hairline borders for separation.
- Use dark full-bleed bands for major section contrast.
- Use product mockups and abstract artwork instead of lifestyle imagery.

## Do Not

- Do not use Iris Pulse for primary CTA fills.
- Do not add additional accent colors.
- Do not use drop shadows for cards or buttons.
- Do not set large display headings in Inter.
- Do not use dark section colors as ordinary light page cards.
- Do not mix radius values between 8px and 100px.
- Do not loosen body tracking beyond the tight system feel.
- Do not make the page feel colorful, playful, or gradient-heavy.
