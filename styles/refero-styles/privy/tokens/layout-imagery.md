# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Max width: 1200px centered.
- Main canvas: white.
- Announcement bar: full-bleed violet above nav.
- Navigation: white with hairline bottom border.
- Hero: two-column split with text and CTAs left, product artwork right.
- Mid-page: logo strip and full-bleed dark feature band.
- Cards: flat, 8px radius, with clean internal structure.
- Footer: white with a subtle dotted pattern and link grid.

## Imagery

Use:

- Product phone mockups.
- Dashboard preview panels.
- Abstract green/teal wash behind mockups.
- Monochrome isometric line art.
- Dotted or halftone decorative patterns.

Avoid:

- Lifestyle photography.
- Saturated multi-color gradients.
- Decorative shadows.
- Playful cartoon illustrations.
- Heavy card frames.

## Dark Band Pattern

When using dark sections, set the section to Carbon and feature cards to Graphite. Keep headings white and supporting copy Fog. This dark treatment should be a distinct band, not a global dark-mode page.
