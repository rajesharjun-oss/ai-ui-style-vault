# Implementation Prompt

Build a polished editorial crypto-fintech interface inspired by the Privy Refero style system.

Use a white canvas, near-black ink, tight custom display typography, flat surfaces, hairline borders, and a single violet announcement accent. The experience should feel like a precise editorial infrastructure page, not a colorful SaaS template.

## Required Style Decisions

- Page background: Canvas `#ffffff`.
- Primary text and CTA fill: Obsidian Ink `#010110`.
- Muted text: Fog `#73737c`.
- Hairlines and subtle patterns: Ash `#d9d9d9`.
- Dark band background: Carbon `#111117`.
- Dark feature cards: Graphite `#22222a`.
- Accent: Iris Pulse `#635bff`, limited to announcement bar, link hover, and small chart strokes.
- Decorative artwork wash only: Deep Teal `#072723`.
- Cards and images: 8px radius.
- Buttons and tags: 100px radius.
- No shadows on buttons, cards, or panels.

## Typography Direction

Use an ABC Favorit-like display face for headings 26px and up. Use weight 400, very tight tracking around -0.03em, and compact line heights between 1.03 and 1.15 for large headlines.

Use Inter for body, UI, navigation, captions, and buttons. Use tight tracking around -0.02em for body and UI text.

## Layout Direction

Create:

- A full-width violet announcement bar above the nav.
- A white navigation bar with hairline border.
- A 1200px centered split hero: editorial headline and CTAs left, abstract wash and product mockups right.
- A partner logo strip.
- A full-bleed dark feature band with three graphite cards.
- Product preview panels on white.
- A white footer with a subtle dotted pattern.

## Avoid

- Violet primary CTA buttons.
- Additional accent colors.
- Drop shadows.
- Rounded values between 8px and 100px.
- Large color fills besides the violet announcement bar.
- Display headings set in Inter.
- Dark section colors used as ordinary light page cards.
