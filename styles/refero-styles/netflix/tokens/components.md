# Component Recipes

## Hero

- Background: `#000000`.
- Backdrop: dimmed, full-bleed poster collage.
- Content: centered.
- Headline: 56px, weight 700, line-height 1.17, `#ffffff`.
- Supporting copy: 16-20px, white or silver.
- Signup row: email input plus red CTA.

## Hero CTA Button

- Background: `#e50914`.
- Text: `#ffffff`.
- Font: Netflix Sans, 700, 24px.
- Radius: 4px.
- Padding: 16px 24px.
- No shadow.

## Header Sign-In Button

- Background: `#e50914`.
- Text: `#ffffff`.
- Font: Netflix Sans, 500, 14px.
- Radius: 4px.
- Padding: 4px 16px.

## Translucent Header Button

- Background: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.
- Text: `#ffffff`.
- Border: `1px solid #808080`.
- Radius: 4px.
- Padding: 6px 16px.

## Hero Email Input

- Background: `rgba(22, 22, 22, 0.7)`.
- Border: `1px solid #5a5a5a`.
- Text: `#ffffff`.
- Placeholder: `#808080`.
- Font size: 16px.

## Trending Poster Card

- Use vertical poster imagery.
- Overlay a ranking number from 1 to 10.
- Number: 100px, weight 900, white or dark-stroked white depending on imagery.
- Keep the card image-first, with minimal chrome.

## Feature Card

- Background: `linear-gradient(149deg, #192247, #210e17)`.
- Radius: 16px.
- Padding: 24px.
- Heading: 24px, weight 900, `#ffffff`.
- Body: 16px, weight 400, `#ffffff`.

## Footer Link

- Text: `#b3b3b3`.
- Font: Netflix Sans, 400, 14px.
- No underline by default.
- Underline on focus or hover if needed for accessibility.

## Promotional Banner

- Background: `#232323` or Graphite.
- Radius: 8px.
- Text: white.
- Include one small icon, brief offer copy, and a compact link-style action.
