# Guidelines

## Do

- Use pure black for immersive page backgrounds.
- Reserve Netflix Red for logo, primary CTAs, and critical active states.
- Use Netflix Sans or a close sans substitute throughout.
- Build discovery with shelves, carousels, poster grids, and feature cards.
- Use deep gradient cards for dimensionality.
- Keep primary text white on dark surfaces.
- Keep buttons and inputs at 4px radius.
- Let media imagery carry density and atmosphere.

## Do Not

- Do not use saturated accent colors besides Netflix Red.
- Do not apply traditional box shadows.
- Do not use light or gray page backgrounds.
- Do not outline primary buttons.
- Do not clutter the header with many navigation links.
- Do not mix several type families.
- Do not introduce complex shapes or playful rounded cards.

## Implementation Heuristic

If the page feels empty, add better content imagery or stronger shelf rhythm. Do not solve emptiness by adding colors, shadows, or decorative UI chrome.
