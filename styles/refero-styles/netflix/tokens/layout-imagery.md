# Layout And Imagery

## Page Composition

- Use a full-bleed pure black canvas.
- Keep the main content rail around 1280px.
- Stack sections vertically with clear shelf rhythm.
- Keep the header minimal and signup-focused.
- Use centered hero content before transitioning into image-heavy shelves.

## Hero

- Use a dimmed collage of poster art or stills.
- Keep the hero headline centered and high contrast.
- Place the email input and CTA as the main conversion path.
- Use black overlays to maintain text contrast.

## Content Shelves

- Use horizontal carousels for trending content.
- Use vertical posters for movies or shows.
- Avoid decorative frames that compete with poster art.
- Use large ranking numerals as an editorial device.

## Feature Cards

- Use the blue-purple gradient for feature cards.
- Pair each card with a short heading, brief body copy, and one simple icon or media cue.
- Avoid using the gradient as a full-page background.

## Footer

- Keep footer links silver and quiet.
- Use dense but organized link groups.
- Keep all copy readable against black.
