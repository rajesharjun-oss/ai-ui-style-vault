# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Token | Usage |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Paper | `#ffffff` | `--color-paper` | Full page background and card surface |
| Ink | `#333333` | `--color-ink` | Primary text, borders, hairlines, checkboxes |
| Carbon | `#000000` | `--color-carbon` | Rare emphasis stroke or strongest text |

## Behavior

- Keep the palette achromatic.
- Use Ink for nearly everything.
- Use Carbon only for emphasis.
- Do not create brand colors or semantic colors.
- Do not tint surfaces; the page is white.
