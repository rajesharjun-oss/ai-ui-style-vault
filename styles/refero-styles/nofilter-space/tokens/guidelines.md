# Guidelines

## Do

- Use a white page background.
- Use `#333333` for text, borders, hairlines, and checkbox controls.
- Use Pragmatica at weight 400 throughout.
- Use a strict 3-column editorial grid.
- Use 30px grid gaps and about 35px gutters.
- Keep all imagery square-cornered.
- Use 1px borders for featured cards.
- Let typography and whitespace carry hierarchy.

## Don't

- Do not add shadows.
- Do not add gradients, blur, or glass effects.
- Do not introduce accent colors.
- Do not round cards, images, or controls beyond square checkbox geometry.
- Do not use heavy borders.
- Do not use SaaS-style buttons or pill CTAs.
- Do not over-style hover states.
- Do not add icons unless the editorial source requires them.
