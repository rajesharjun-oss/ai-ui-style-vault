# Component And Composition Tokens

## Top Centered Nav

- **Role:** Primary navigation.
- **Text:** Arial, `14px / 20px`, weight `400`, uppercase.
- **Color:** `#333333`.
- **Layout:** Single centered row at the top of the yellow viewport.
- **Padding:** `10px 20px` per link.
- **Container:** None. No background, card, pill, or border.

## Nav Link - Active

- **Base:** Same as inactive link.
- **Indicator:** Centered `20px` wide `#333333` border-bottom.
- **Avoid:** No fill, no bold, no color shift, no underline spanning the full link width.

## Full-Bleed Illustrated Hero

- **Background:** `#ffe600`.
- **Height:** At least one viewport.
- **Content:** Flat vector houses, clouds, blue globe, and display type.
- **Avoid:** No photo frame, overlay, card, shadow, or gradient.

## Circular Globe Surface

- **Color:** `#007fff`.
- **Radius:** `1440px`.
- **Placement:** Bottom-center, often partly off-screen.
- **Role:** Hero focal surface and backing for white display text.

## Curved Display Headline

- **Font:** Changa One.
- **Size:** Around `75px`.
- **Line height:** `54px` or `0.72`.
- **Color:** `#ffffff`.
- **Behavior:** Lines can rotate individually to follow the globe arc.
- **Avoid:** No text shadow, stroke, background chip, or card.

## Self-Intro Statement

- **Font:** Changa One.
- **Size:** Around `50px`.
- **Line height:** `40px` or `0.8`.
- **Color:** Often `#ffffff`.
- **Placement:** Floating directly over a full-bleed scene, often rotated.

## Illustration Layers

- **Sky:** `#ffe600`.
- **Globe:** `#007fff`.
- **Clouds/windows/text:** `#ffffff`.
- **Roofs/accent details:** `#ef3b2c`.
- **Linework:** `#333333`.
- **Depth model:** Layer order only, not shadows.
