# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Grammar

Karl uses full-bleed viewport-height scenes. There is no conventional content grid, no card row, and no centered marketing column. The scroll experience should feel like moving through a stack of illustrated poster panels.

## Section Model

- Each section fills the viewport.
- Background remains `#ffe600`.
- Section changes are hard cuts.
- Text floats as signage over the scene.
- No neutral bands or dividers sit between sections.

## Imagery

- Use original flat vector illustration.
- Favor cartoon canal houses, puffy clouds, simple windows, curved world/globe shapes, and chunky outlines.
- Keep illustration edge-to-edge and unframed.
- Avoid photography, screenshots, stock art, icon grids, and product UI mockups.

## Composition Signatures

- A blue globe rises from the bottom of the viewport.
- Display text can follow the globe arc through slight rotation.
- Changa One display lines overlap or compress vertically.
- The nav remains a quiet top-center signpost.
