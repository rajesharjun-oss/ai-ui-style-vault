# Guidelines

## Do

- Use warm paper surfaces instead of sterile white sections.
- Use black ink borders as a core design material.
- Use `#ffda6e` for primary CTAs and emphasis panels.
- Use mint for analytics, success, and privacy status.
- Use DM Sans only.
- Use `3px` card radius and `7px` button radius.
- Keep cards shadow-free.
- Keep dashboard data compact and readable.
- Use uppercase heavy captions for labels.

## Don't

- Do not add box shadows or glossy gradients.
- Do not use large rounded SaaS cards.
- Do not use more than one primary CTA color.
- Do not use muted border-only UI everywhere; keep black structure visible.
- Do not introduce serif, monospace, or decorative fonts.
- Do not make analytics panels look like enterprise glass dashboards.
- Do not use pure gray canvas as the page background.
