# Color Tokens

## Brand

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sunshine | `#ffda6e` | `--color-sunshine` | Primary CTA, emphasis panel, hero action. |
| Amber Ink | `#e5aa30` | `--color-amber-ink` | Warm chart accent and secondary yellow state. |
| Mint | `#3db09c` | `--color-mint` | Positive analytics, privacy status, success marks. |
| Deep Mint | `#063c36` | `--color-deep-mint` | Dark green panel, strong analytics contrast. |

## Neutrals

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | `#fffffb` | `--color-paper` | Main page canvas and light cards. |
| Soft Paper | `#f5f1e6` | `--color-soft-paper` | Alternate sections and muted panels. |
| Ink | `#111111` | `--color-ink` | Primary text, borders, button text. |
| Charcoal | `#1d1d1b` | `--color-charcoal` | Strong text and dark panel variant. |
| Slate | `#6f6f6a` | `--color-slate` | Secondary copy and muted labels. |
| Ash | `#c3c3bd` | `--color-ash` | Light dividers and disabled details. |
| Clay | `#d8d5cc` | `--color-clay` | Soft border or neutral fill. |

## State And Chart

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crimson | `#f94144` | `--color-crimson` | Error, negative chart values, destructive state. |
| Sky | `#268bf7` | `--color-sky` | Rare blue chart series or info state. |

## Rules

- Use Paper and Soft Paper for most surfaces.
- Use Ink as both primary text and primary border color.
- Use Sunshine for CTAs and emphasis, not full-page backgrounds.
- Use Mint for positive analytics and privacy status.
- Keep Crimson and Sky rare and state-driven.
