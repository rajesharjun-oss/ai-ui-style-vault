# Typography Tokens

## Fonts

| Font | Token | Role | Substitute |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DM Sans | `--font-dm-sans` | All UI, body, headings, labels, buttons, analytics text. | Inter |

## Type Scale

| Role | Size | Line Height | Weight | Token |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| caption | `11px` | `1.2` | `800` | `--text-caption` |
| body | `16px` | `22px` | `400` | `--text-body` |
| body-lg | `18px` | `25px` | `400` | `--text-body-lg` |
| subheading | `20px` | `28px` | `700` | `--text-subheading` |
| heading-sm | `26px` | `31.2px` | `800` | `--text-heading-sm` |
| heading | `30px` | `36px` | `800` | `--text-heading` |
| heading-lg | `44px` | `52.8px` | `800` | `--text-heading-lg` |
| display | `58px` | `63.8px` | `800` | `--text-display` |

## Font Rules

- Use DM Sans everywhere.
- Use heavy weights for headings and labels.
- Use uppercase captions for taxonomy and product labels.
- Keep body at 16px with practical line length.
- Do not introduce serif, monospace, or decorative display fonts.
