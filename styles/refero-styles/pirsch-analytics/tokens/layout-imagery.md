# Layout And Imagery

## Layout Grammar

Pirsch Analytics uses compact SaaS composition with a notebook-like surface system. The page should feel like a tidy analytics product explained on paper.

## Common Layouts

- Hero with large bold headline, short privacy-focused subhead, sunshine CTA, and analytics dashboard preview.
- Paper sections alternating between `#fffffb` and `#f5f1e6`.
- Bordered dashboard panels with charts, metrics, and tables.
- Feature cards arranged in a simple grid with ink borders.
- Pricing or plan cards with sunshine highlights.
- Privacy or compliance badges in deep mint.

## Imagery

- Use product dashboards, charts, metric tiles, map panels, referrer tables, and event rows.
- Avoid lifestyle photography, glossy 3D illustrations, glassmorphism, and dark hero renders.
- Keep visuals flat, direct, and readable.

## Motion

- Use minimal hover feedback around `150ms` to `200ms`.
- Buttons can shift slightly or change fill.
- Avoid cinematic scroll effects and heavy animation.
