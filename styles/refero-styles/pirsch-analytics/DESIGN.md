# Pirsch Analytics Design Notes

Pirsch Analytics is a warm, compact analytics system with a hand-built feel. It avoids the usual frosted SaaS vocabulary. Instead, it uses paper colors, strong black borders, square-ish cards, flat fills, and high-confidence DM Sans typography.

## Visual Principles

- Use warm paper instead of sterile white.
- Structure the interface with black ink borders.
- Use yellow for decisive actions and emphasis.
- Use mint and deep mint for analytics, success, and privacy-oriented data.
- Keep everything flat; no shadows.
- Use low-radius cards so the design feels like a tidy product notebook.

## Color System

The palette is grounded in paper, ink, yellow, and mint. `#fffffb` is the canvas. `#111111` is the ink. `#ffda6e` is the optimistic action color. `#3db09c` and `#063c36` are data colors. Crimson, sky, and amber should remain state or chart accents.

## Typography

Use DM Sans only. The voice is compact and clear, with bold labels and headings. Captions can be uppercase and heavy. Body text stays readable at 16px, while analytics UI and labels should remain tight.

## Layout

Use a 1200px max-width container, 96px section rhythm, and card padding around 32px. Product UI should appear as bordered analytics panels, metric cards, tables, and charts rather than glossy mockups.

## Elevation

Do not use shadows. Contrast comes from border weight, fill color, and spacing. If something needs to feel more important, give it a yellow fill, stronger ink border, or larger type.
