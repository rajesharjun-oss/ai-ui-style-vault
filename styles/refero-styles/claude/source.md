## What To Preserve

Claude uses a warm off-white page canvas, ink-like near-black text, soft paper card layers, restrained serif headlines, and a calm sans family for every functional detail. The palette is intentionally narrow: warm monochrome plus clay. Clay is a decorative signature mark, not an action color.

The page relies on:

- Bone Parchment as the default page canvas.
- Paper White as the main card surface.
- Soft Stone as nested paper layers.
- Carbon Ink for headings, body, icons, and buttons.
- Graphite, Ashen, and Pebble for text hierarchy.
- Mist and Chalk for hairline dividers and borders.
- Clay for small ornaments, illustration details, marks, and dots only.
- Anthropic Serif for editorial headings at 24px and 30px.
- Anthropic Sans for body, nav, buttons, cards, links, labels, and forms.
- 8px controls, 16px cards, and 24px elevated cards.
- A single Obsidian footer band as the only true-black surface.
