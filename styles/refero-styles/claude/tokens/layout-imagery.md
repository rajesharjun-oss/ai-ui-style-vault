# Layout And Imagery

## Layout

- Use a centered 1200px max-width rail.
- Place everything on Bone Parchment unless a section is explicitly a card or footer.
- Use centered editorial hero stacks with generous whitespace.
- Alternate between parchment canvas, Paper White cards, and Soft Stone bands.
- Use 3-column pricing grids with one featured or elevated tier.
- Use asymmetric 2-column feature sections for text and imagery.
- Use centered stacks for FAQs and CTAs.
- Keep the nav minimal with logo left and link cluster right or center.

## Imagery

- Use sparse editorial product imagery or calm illustrations.
- Put images inside 16px to 24px rounded containers.
- Let Clay appear in small illustration details only.
- Use monochrome Carbon Ink icons.
- Avoid gradients, glow effects, 3D renders, multicolor icons, and dense dashboard screenshots.
