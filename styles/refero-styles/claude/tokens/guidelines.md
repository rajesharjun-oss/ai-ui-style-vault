# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Bone Parchment as the default page canvas.
- Use Anthropic Serif 24px to 30px at weight 400 for editorial headings.
- Use Anthropic Sans for body, nav, buttons, cards, links, labels, and forms.
- Keep sans weights mostly between 400 and 580.
- Use Clay only for small decorative marks and illustration details.
- Use 24px radius for elevated cards and 16px for secondary cards.
- Use 8px radius for buttons, inputs, and nav controls.
- Pair Carbon Ink text with Paper White cards on Bone Parchment.
- Use warm grays for hierarchy instead of adding chromatic emphasis.

## Don't

- Do not use Clay for button fills, links, or large surfaces.
- Do not use pure black for body text.
- Do not set headlines at 700 or heavier.
- Do not apply heavy shadows to cards.
- Do not use cool blues, greens, or reds as brand accents.
- Do not set large display headings in Anthropic Sans.
- Do not add decorative gradients, glows, or 3D renders.
