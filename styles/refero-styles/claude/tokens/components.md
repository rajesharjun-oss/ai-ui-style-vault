# Components

## Filled Dark Button

Carbon Ink fill, Bone Parchment or Paper White text, 8px radius, 8px vertical and 20px horizontal padding, Anthropic Sans 15px/500. Use for primary CTA on light surfaces.

## Pill Navigation Button

Transparent background, Graphite text, no border, 8px radius, Anthropic Sans 15px/500. Keep the navigation minimal and paper-like.

## Pricing Tier Card

Paper White surface on Bone Parchment, 24px radius, 32px padding. Plan name uses Anthropic Serif 24px to 30px. Price uses Carbon Ink, description uses Ashen. Optional soft warm hover shadow.

## Feature Benefit Card

Soft Stone or Paper White, 16px radius, 24px to 32px padding. Checkmark/icon in Carbon Ink, body text at 14px Graphite, flat by default.

## Editorial Section Header

Anthropic Serif 30px/400, Carbon Ink, line-height 1.2, followed by short Ashen or Graphite body sentence.

## Footer Band

Full-width Obsidian band, muted Pebble links, compact 14px sans link columns, 12px copyright copy.

## Inline Link

Graphite default, Carbon Ink hover, underline for clarity. No blue or Clay link coloring.

## Input Field

Transparent or Paper White fill, 1px Mist border, 8px radius, 14px Anthropic Sans, soft focus ring.

## FAQ Accordion Item

Hairline-separated item, question in Graphite 14px to 16px, body in Carbon Ink 14px, 24px vertical padding, sans only.

## Clay Accent Mark

Small Clay dot, ornament, icon detail, or illustration mark. Decorative and non-interactive.

## Status Badge

11px to 12px sans label, 8px radius, low-contrast warm-gray background, Carbon Ink or Graphite text.
