# Typography

## Families

| Family | Token | Fallback | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anthropic Serif | `--font-anthropic-serif` | Source Serif 4, Charter, Georgia | Display and editorial headings only. |
| Anthropic Sans | `--font-anthropic-sans` | Inter, IBM Plex Sans, system-ui | Body, nav, buttons, cards, links, labels, inputs, FAQs, and captions. |

## Rules

- Use Anthropic Serif only for headings at 24px and 30px.
- Keep serif headings at weight 400.
- Use Anthropic Sans for everything functional.
- Use weight 580 as the practical upper bound.
- Do not use 700+ weights.
- Do not use sans for the primary editorial display voice.
