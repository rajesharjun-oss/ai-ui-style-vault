# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Void Canvas as the dominant page background.
- Use Bone White for primary text, borders, and icon strokes.
- Let oversized Kmr Waldenburg display type define the page.
- Use Editorialnew italic words sparingly inside large display compositions.
- Use Dirtyline as a rare ornamental object, not a readable paragraph font.
- Use Arterial Red for small brand punctuation, featured borders, or rare primary actions.
- Use 8px card radius and 1px hairline borders.
- Use Linen only for the occasional inverted editorial body panel.

## Don't

- Do not add conventional SaaS chrome or bulky button systems.
- Do not use broad red backgrounds.
- Do not introduce additional saturated colors.
- Do not rely on drop shadows.
- Do not wrap the whole page in a centered max-width shell.
- Do not round editorial imagery into contained cards.
- Do not use gradients on content surfaces.
- Do not turn the system into a dashboard.
