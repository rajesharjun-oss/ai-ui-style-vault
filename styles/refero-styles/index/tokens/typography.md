# Typography

## Font Families

| Role | Family | Fallback |
|------|--------|----------|
| Primary | `Raveo Variable` | Inter Variable, Manrope |
| Micro labels | `Geist Mono` | JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono, Space Mono |

## Scale

| Token | Size | Weight | Line height | Tracking | Use |
|-------|------|--------|-------------|----------|-----|
| Micro | 9px | 500 | 1.6 | 0.02em | Eyebrows, version tags, step labels |
| Caption | 12px | 400 | 1.2 | 0 | Small labels |
| Body Small | 14px | 400 | 1.6 | -0.14px | Supporting copy |
| Body | 16px | 400 | 1.6 | -0.16px | Main body copy |
| Subheading | 24px | 1000 | 1.4 | -0.24px | Strong subheads |
| Heading | 32px | 1000 | 1.2 | -0.32px | Section headings |
| Display | 70px | 1000 | 1.1 | -2.8px | Hero and major statements |

## Rules

- Use weight 1000 for all display and section-heading moments.
- Keep the 70px display tight and blocky.
- Use mono only for tiny uppercase technical labels.
- Use 400 body text to keep supporting copy restrained.
- Do not introduce intermediate type sizes outside the scale.
