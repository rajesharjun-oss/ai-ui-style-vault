# Components

## Dashed Section Container

- Wraps major content blocks.
- Border: 1px dashed Void or Carbon depending on contrast.
- Works as the primary visual frame.
- Padding can range from 40px to 80px vertically.

## Hero Spotlight Banner

- Carbon background with a subtle radial light wash.
- Centered content stack.
- Mono eyebrow chip above the headline.
- 70px Raveo 1000 display headline.
- Steel supporting copy.
- One outlined pill button.

## Outlined Pill Button

- Border: 1px solid Paper.
- Background: transparent.
- Radius: 100px.
- Padding: about 8px 18px.
- Label: Raveo 500 around 14px.

## Eyebrow Chip

- Dashed outline.
- Radius: 100px.
- Padding: 4px 12px.
- Geist Mono 9px, uppercase, tracked.

## Step Card

- No fill and no shadow.
- Periwinkle icon at top.
- Raveo label and restrained description below.
- Lives inside a dashed container.

## Feature Split Panel

- Left text column with mono label, weight-1000 heading, body, and checklist.
- Right square illustration module.
- Checklist icons use Periwinkle Annotation.

## Illustration Module

- 1:1 square with 20px radius.
- Carbon background.
- Periwinkle wireframe line art.
- Small white data-dot accents.
- No fills, gradients, or glows.
