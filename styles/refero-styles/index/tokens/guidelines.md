# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Raveo Variable weight 1000 for display headlines at 32px and above.
- Wrap every major section in a dashed 1px container.
- Keep the hero display at 70px with tight tracking.
- Use #7089ba only for annotation accents.
- Use Geist Mono 9px for uppercase micro labels.
- Keep action buttons outlined, pill-shaped, and unfilled.
- Create depth through Carbon on Void, not shadows.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce extra colors beyond neutrals and periwinkle.
- Do not use lower display weights for the main headlines.
- Do not replace dashed borders with solid borders.
- Do not add drop shadows, glows, or blur to cards and buttons.
- Do not center long body copy.
- Do not use periwinkle as a button fill.
- Do not add intermediate type sizes outside the system scale.

## AI Builder Checks

- Does it read as a blueprint or schematic?
- Are major sections visibly dashed-contained?
- Is the headline weight aggressively architectural?
- Is periwinkle rare and annotation-like?
- Are actions wireframe outlines instead of filled buttons?
