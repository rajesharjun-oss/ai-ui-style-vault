# Colors

## Palette

| Name | Value | Token | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas White | `#ffffff` | `--color-canvas-white` | Page background, card surfaces, ghost button fill |
| Graphite Ink | `#3a3a3e` | `--color-graphite-ink` | Primary text, links, icons, hairline borders |
| Deep Carbon | `#090b11` | `--color-deep-carbon` | Strongest contrast text and card borders |
| Ash Veil | `#b5b0b0` | `--color-ash-veil` | Warm gray section bands and soft borders |
| Apricot Wash | `#ffcf9e` | `--color-apricot-wash` | Warm card fills and hairline card borders |
| Iris Mist | `#babfff` | `--color-iris-mist` | Lavender card borders and paired accents |
| Fuchsia Bloom | `#e3a2ef` | `--color-fuchsia-bloom` | Single vivid card fill or highlight block |
| Polar Blue | `#bfe0f7` | `--color-polar-blue` | Cool section field or soft surface tint |

## Usage

Graphite Ink should carry nearly all text, links, icon strokes, and borders. Apricot, Iris, Fuchsia, and Polar Blue are surface colors only. Do not use the chromatic tints for text, icons, or CTA button fills.
