# Guidelines

## Do

- Keep nearly all text, links, icon strokes, and borders in Graphite Ink.
- Use Apricot Wash, Iris Mist, Fuchsia Bloom, and Polar Blue only as card fills, card borders, or section backgrounds.
- Set every radius to 0px.
- Use Instrument Sans for every text element.
- Use weight 400 for display headlines.
- Reserve weight 500 for the wordmark, nav labels, tiny labels, and small emphasis.
- Use negative tracking everywhere.
- Anchor sections with about 90px vertical spacing.
- Let one large 3D artwork dominate the lower half of the hero.
- Use whitespace and hairlines for hierarchy.

## Do Not

- Do not introduce shadows, glows, or elevated surfaces.
- Do not round corners on cards, buttons, nav, tags, or images.
- Do not use chromatic colors as text, icons, or CTA fills.
- Do not add a normal horizontal nav bar.
- Do not use positive letter spacing.
- Do not add decorative gradients to UI components.
- Do not use stock photography.
- Do not frame the hero artwork in a card.
