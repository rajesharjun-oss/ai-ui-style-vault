# Typography

## Fonts

- Primary: `Instrument Sans`
- Fallback: `Inter`, `Sohne`, `General Sans`, `ui-sans-serif`, `system-ui`

## Behavior

Instrument Sans is the only typeface. It handles every UI element, headline, caption, wordmark, and link. Weight 400 is the default for display and most content. Weight 500 is reserved for the wordmark, nav labels, tiny labels, and emphasis.

The system is tightly tracked. Use negative letter spacing throughout and avoid positive tracking entirely.

## Scale

| Role | Size | Line Height | Weight | Tracking |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Micro | 7px | 1 | 500 | tight |
| Tiny Label | 9px | 1.5 | 500 | tight |
| Small Label | 11px | 1 | 500 | tight |
| Caption | 12px | 1.5 | 400 | -0.36px |
| Body | 24px | 1.5 | 500 | -0.24px |
| Heading | 48px | 1.2 | 400 | -0.54px |
| Display | 54px | 1 | 400 | -1.62px |

## Rules

- Use only Instrument Sans or a close fallback.
- Use weight 400 for display headlines.
- Use weight 500 sparingly.
- Apply -1.62px tracking at the 54px display size.
- Do not use positive tracking.
- Do not introduce a serif, mono, or script accent face.
