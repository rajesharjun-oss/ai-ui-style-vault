# Implementation Prompt

Build a polished editorial venture-capital interface inspired by the BlueYard Capital Refero style system.

Use a pure white canvas, warm near-black type, Instrument Sans only, sharp 0px corners, compact card spacing, pale card-only tints, and one large atmospheric 3D artwork. The result should feel like a printed monograph for frontier technology.

## Required Style Decisions

- Background: Canvas White `#ffffff`.
- Primary text, links, borders, icons: Graphite Ink `#3a3a3e`.
- Strong contrast accents: Deep Carbon `#090b11`.
- Warm gray band: Ash Veil `#b5b0b0`.
- Warm card tint and border: Apricot Wash `#ffcf9e`.
- Lavender card border: Iris Mist `#babfff`.
- Single vivid card fill: Fuchsia Bloom `#e3a2ef`.
- Cool section field: Polar Blue `#bfe0f7`.
- Typeface: Instrument Sans only.
- Radius: 0px everywhere.
- Shadows: none.
- Section gap: about 90px.
- Card padding: 12px.
- Element gap: 5px.

## Typography Direction

Set display headlines in Instrument Sans at 54px, weight 400, line-height 1, and about -1.62px tracking. Use 48px for secondary headings and 24px for prominent body text. Use weight 500 only for the wordmark, navigation labels, and small emphasis.

## Layout Direction

Create:

- A tiny stacked wordmark in the top-left.
- A 40px square hamburger button in the top-right.
- A centered hero headline in the upper third.
- A large unframed 3D sphere or planetary artwork in the lower half, bleeding off the viewport.
- Alternating white and soft-tinted full-bleed sections.
- Sharp cards in one-, two-, or three-column grids with 5px to 12px gaps.
- Hairline dividers instead of shadows.

## Avoid

- Rounded corners.
- Drop shadows, glows, or elevated card wells.
- Chromatic text or chromatic CTA fills.
- Additional typefaces.
- Positive letter spacing.
- Standard horizontal nav bars.
- Stock photography or framed product screenshots.
- Decorative gradients on UI components.
