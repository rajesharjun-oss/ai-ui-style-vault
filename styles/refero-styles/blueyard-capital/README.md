- Theme: light
- Mood: sunset editorial observatory on a white printed canvas

## Use This When

Use this style for venture capital firms, research labs, frontier technology funds, editorial investment pages, science-backed portfolios, minimalist studios, and thought-leadership sites that should feel spacious, intellectual, and sharply composed.

## Core Idea

BlueYard Capital is a printed editorial object expressed as a website. A pure white canvas, warm near-black type, compact spacing, sharp 0px geometry, pale accent-card tints, and one large planetary 3D artwork create the mood. The interface earns attention through whitespace, scale, and restraint rather than through chrome.
