# Components

## Enterprise Top Navigation

- Surface: White.
- Logo: left.
- Links: Navy or Slate, compact product sans.
- Dropdowns: structured columns with product names and short descriptions.
- CTA: Atlassian Blue button at right.
- Divider: Cloud or subtle border.

## Blue Primary CTA

- Fill: Atlassian Blue.
- Text: White.
- Radius: pill or `8px`.
- Padding: `12px 20px`.
- Type: `14px` to `16px`, weight `600`.
- Hover: Bright Blue or darker blue.

## Secondary Ghost Button

- Fill: White or transparent.
- Border: Atlassian Blue or Cloud.
- Text: Atlassian Blue or Navy.
- Radius: pill or `8px`.
- Padding: `12px 20px`.
- Use beside the primary CTA.

## Hero Product Screenshot Frame

- Surface: White.
- Radius: `20px`.
- Border: Cloud.
- Shadow: soft and sparing.
- Content: real or believable product screenshot.
- Surrounding accents: a few colored shapes, not a full confetti field.

## Feature Card Grid

- Surface: White on Cloud or pale surface.
- Radius: `16px`.
- Padding: `24px`.
- Icon tile: colored secondary hue.
- Title: Navy, `22px`, weight `600`.
- Body: Slate, `16px`.
- Link: Bright Blue.

## Integration Logo Grid

- Surface: White or Cloud.
- Tiles: small rounded rectangles or simple logo rows.
- Gap: `16px` to `24px`.
- Text: short labels, Slate.
- Keep logos evenly aligned.

## Workflow Diagram

- Nodes: rounded cards with compact text.
- Connectors: Blue or Cloud lines.
- Status chips: Green, Yellow, Red, Purple, or Teal.
- Keep the diagram readable and not overdecorated.

## Testimonial Proof Band

- Surface: Pale Blue, White, or Cloud.
- Content: customer quote, logo, metric blocks, or proof statements.
- Type: Navy heading and Slate copy.
- Radius: `24px` for grouped panels.

## Confetti Accent Shapes

- Shapes: circles, arcs, plus marks, small geometric pieces.
- Colors: Blue, Purple, Teal, Yellow, Green, Red.
- Placement: hero corners, announcement edges, empty illustration space.
- Use sparingly and never behind dense text.

## Product Tab Bar

- Active: Atlassian Blue label and underline.
- Inactive: Slate.
- Surface: White.
- Spacing: compact.
- Use for product screens, feature demos, and plan comparisons.

## Status Chip

- Radius: `999px`.
- Fill: pale version of status color.
- Text: darker matching color.
- Padding: `4px 10px`.
- Type: `12px` to `14px`, weight `600`.
