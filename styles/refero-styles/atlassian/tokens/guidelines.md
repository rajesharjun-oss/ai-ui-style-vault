# Guidelines

## Do

- Use Atlassian Blue for the most important action.
- Keep White and Cloud as the dominant page surfaces.
- Use Navy headings and Slate supporting copy.
- Use rounded cards, product screenshots, tab bars, and integration grids.
- Use secondary colors for status, charts, icon tiles, and small accents.
- Keep layouts clear and enterprise-friendly.
- Use confetti shapes sparingly around brand moments.
- Show product UI or workflow proof instead of abstract claims.

## Don't

- Do not use a dark luxury palette as the main identity.
- Do not use serif editorial typography.
- Do not overuse all secondary colors in one section.
- Do not add heavy glassmorphism, blur blobs, or decorative gradients.
- Do not make the UI childish or toy-like.
- Do not turn marketing pages into dense admin dashboards.
- Do not hide primary actions behind low-contrast gray buttons.
- Do not use generic stock business photos when product proof would work better.
