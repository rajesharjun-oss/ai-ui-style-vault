# Typography

## Font Stacks

- Display: `Charlie Display`, `Inter`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Text/UI: `Charlie Text`, `Inter`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Weights: `400`, `500`, `600`, `700`

## Scale

| Purpose | Size | Line height | Weight | Tracking | Token |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Caption | `12px` | `1.4` | `500` | `0.01em` | `--text-caption` |
| Body small | `14px` | `1.45` | `400` | `0` | `--text-body-sm` |
| Body | `16px` | `1.5` | `400` | `0` | `--text-body` |
| Body large | `18px` | `1.5` | `400` | `0` | `--text-body-lg` |
| Card title | `22px` | `1.25` | `600` | `-0.01em` | `--text-card-title` |
| Section heading | `40px` | `1.12` | `700` | `-0.02em` | `--text-section-heading` |
| Hero heading | `56px` | `1.04` | `700` | `-0.02em` | `--text-hero-heading` |

## Rules

- Use clean product sans typography only.
- Make headings confident and readable, not editorial or fashion-like.
- Keep labels compact and practical.
- Use body copy for clarity and product explanation.
- Avoid serif typefaces, ultra-condensed type, and decorative display fonts.
