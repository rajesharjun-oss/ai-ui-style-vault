# Atlassian Design Reference

## Essence

Atlassian balances enterprise clarity with collaborative warmth. The system is structured, readable, and product-led, but it avoids feeling cold by using bright blue, soft cloud surfaces, rounded cards, friendly spot illustrations, and small confetti-like brand shapes. It should feel capable, trustworthy, and team-oriented.

## Color System

White and Cloud are the primary work surfaces. Navy and Slate carry body text, headings, and navigation. Atlassian Blue is the main action and brand color. Bright Blue is used for active states, links, and high-energy product emphasis. Purple, Teal, Green, Yellow, and Red are secondary system colors for icons, charts, badges, illustrations, and status. Keep the palette crisp and bright rather than muted or luxury-dark.

## Typography

Use Charlie Display and Charlie Text if licensed. If unavailable, use Inter, Arial, or a similar clean product sans. Headings are bold, friendly, and clear. Body copy is pragmatic and readable. Labels, tabs, and nav links are compact, direct, and not overly stylized. Do not use expressive serif type or narrow fashion display fonts.

## Shape And Space

The geometry is rounded but not bubbly. Buttons and tags can be pill-shaped, cards often use 12px to 16px radius, and product screenshots can use 16px to 24px radius. Spacing is comfortable and modular, with 96px section gaps on marketing pages and tighter 16px to 24px rhythms inside product UI.

## Layout Rhythm

Use stacked SaaS sections: top nav, clear hero, logo/integration bands, product screenshot panels, feature card grids, workflow diagrams, customer proof, and a structured footer. Marketing pages may use large white or pale blue bands. Product UI views should use a denser but still calm card and tab system.

## Components

- Enterprise top nav with white surface, logo left, dropdown links, and blue CTA at right.
- Blue primary CTA with rounded pill or 6px to 8px radius, white text, and bold label.
- Secondary ghost button with blue border or navy text.
- Hero product screenshot frame with rounded corners, subtle border, and soft shadow.
- Feature card grid with white fill, cloud border, icon, title, body copy, and optional link.
- Integration logo grid with neutral surface and evenly spaced app tiles.
- Workflow diagram with connected cards, blue lines, and colored status chips.
- Testimonial or customer proof band with soft blue/white surface and clear metric blocks.
- Confetti accent shapes used sparingly near heroes, announcements, or illustration edges.
- Product tab bar with blue active indicator, neutral inactive text, and compact spacing.

## Implementation Direction

Start with a clean white or cloud canvas and a confident blue CTA. Use product screenshots and structured feature grids as the main proof. Add friendly illustration or confetti accents only where they support comprehension or brand energy. Avoid dark luxury styling, harsh black surfaces, excessive gradients, oversized decorative blobs, playful toy-like UI, and dense admin panels on marketing pages.
