# Camera and Scroll Guidance

Camera movement must reveal hierarchy, detail, continuity or inspection. Define named presets, bounds, reset/back and reduced-motion direct states.

Scroll-linked scenes use semantic chapter IDs, direct navigation and normal browser history. Do not hijack scrolling or make meaning depend on wheel speed. Guided nodes are primary in spatial worlds; first-person movement is optional.

Avoid continuous rotation, rapid acceleration, roll, strong parallax and long forced travel. Provide pause, skip and reset.
