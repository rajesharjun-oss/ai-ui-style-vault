# Real Estate Content & Listing Standard

Property decisions depend on facts. Keep location, property/development name, type, availability status, price status, key specifications and enquiry action close to the primary media.

Never invent price, service charge, dimensions, completion date, title status, developer history, amenities, travel times, yield, rental income, availability or financing terms. Distinguish verified fact, owner-supplied fact, provisional marketing statement and unknown.

For off-plan developments, state project/status dates precisely when verified. Do not use “minutes from” claims without a reliable basis. Avoid fake scarcity such as “only 2 left” unless connected to a current source of truth.

Property descriptions should add decision value rather than repeat visible specifications. Lifestyle copy may support positioning but cannot replace location/specification clarity.
