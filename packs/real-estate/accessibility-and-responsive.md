# Real Estate Accessibility & Responsive

Property galleries, maps, floorplans and filters require keyboard/touch alternatives and textual equivalents. Do not rely on map pins, colour or hover alone to communicate availability or location.

On mobile, keep property name, status, price status, essential facts and viewing CTA easy to reach. Large galleries must not trap scroll or hide browser controls. Filters need clear applied-state and reset controls. Floorplan text must be zoomable or available in an accessible alternative.
