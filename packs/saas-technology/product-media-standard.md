# SaaS & Technology Product Media Standard

Prefer real product UI, annotated workflows, real diagrams and verified architecture over decorative 3D. Product screenshots must represent actual or clearly labelled conceptual states.

Do not fabricate dashboards, analytics, customer data, terminal output or integrations to make the product appear mature. Use fictional sample data when needed and label it appropriately.

3D may visualise a real system model, data topology, hardware relationship or spatial concept only when semantically justified. Generic chrome blobs, planets and glass cubes are not product proof.
