# Hospitality Content and Booking Standard

A guest must quickly understand where the property is, what type of stay it offers, which room types exist, who they suit, what amenities are verified, and how booking works.

Never invent room rates, live availability, star rating, awards, walking times, airport transfers, breakfast inclusion, Wi-Fi, pool, gym, parking, accessibility, check-in/out, cancellation or child/pet policies.

When live rates are unavailable, state the rate status clearly and route to the verified booking or enquiry channel. Separate property facts from destination inspiration.
