# Beauty & Wellness Photography and Results Standard

Prefer owner-supplied, consented photographs of real practitioners, spaces, tools, process and work. Label generated or stock media as conceptual and never present generated people as real clients or staff.

Before-and-after content requires provenance, consent and a fair comparison. Do not retouch or crop in a way that materially exaggerates a result. Do not imply typical outcomes from a single example.

Match imagery to claims: hygiene claims need real process/environment evidence; specialist-skill claims need practitioner/work evidence; spa atmosphere claims need actual space imagery. Avoid generic candles, flowers and luxury objects when they do not represent the business.
