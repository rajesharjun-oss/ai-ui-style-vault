# Beauty & Wellness Content and Booking Standard

Lead with the real service category, customer fit and booking path. Keep service names, durations, prices, practitioner details, location, preparation, cancellation/deposit rules and availability explicit when verified.

Never invent treatment outcomes, medical benefits, accreditation, ingredients, equipment, prices, discounts, availability or practitioner qualifications. Medical or quasi-medical services require cautious, evidence-aware language and must not promise diagnosis, cure or guaranteed outcomes.

Use client questions as structure: What is it? Who is it for? What happens? How long? How much? Who performs it? What should I know before booking? What happens if I need to cancel?

Conversion should be concrete: Book appointment, Check availability, WhatsApp the studio, Call reception, or Request consultation. Do not use vague luxury copy as a substitute for service clarity.
