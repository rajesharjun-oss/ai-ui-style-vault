# Fashion & Couture Motion Guidance

Motion must reinforce garment inspection, material quality, tailoring precision, continuity or brand ceremony. It must not turn the site into a generic luxury demo.

## Preferred patterns

- restrained garment-stage parallax;
- fabric or material movement tied to verified product/material context;
- silhouette reveal or crop-to-detail transition;
- editorial image sequencing;
- measurement/pattern-line motion when it explains tailoring;
- scroll handoff from cinematic brand moment into semantic collection browsing;
- subtle hover/focus depth on collection and enquiry controls.

## Limits

Use one dominant motion idea and no more than three supporting patterns on a standard marketing site. Avoid constant floating objects, unrelated chrome/liquid effects, excessive cursor followers, uncontrolled cloth simulations and motion on every text block.

## 3D rule

If 3D is requested, select from the 3D capability pack only after `business-profile.json` is ready and the chosen scene is semantically relevant to garments, tailoring, textile/materials, atelier process or verified brand cues. Fashion is not permission to add arbitrary luxury cars, jewellery, architecture or futuristic objects.

## Accessibility

Every animated sequence must have a static or normal-flow equivalent. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; do not hide content behind animation initialization. Gallery, garment detail and enquiry tasks must work with motion disabled.
