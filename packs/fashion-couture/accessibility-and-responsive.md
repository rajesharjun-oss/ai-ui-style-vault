# Fashion & Couture Accessibility and Responsive Guidance

- Keep collection names, garment facts, price guidance, availability and enquiry actions in semantic DOM.
- Gallery/viewer controls must be keyboard operable, touch friendly and visibly focused.
- Do not rely on colour, texture or animation alone to distinguish categories or selected states.
- Provide meaningful alt text for garment, fabric and process imagery; decorative texture should be empty-alt.
- At mobile widths, transform editorial split layouts into a clear vertical sequence rather than shrinking desktop composition.
- Ensure garment crops still show the decision-relevant area on mobile; do not crop away fit, embroidery, neckline, sleeve or construction detail.
- Sticky enquiry controls must not cover gallery controls, captions, forms or browser UI.
- Support 200% zoom without clipped navigation or hidden CTAs.
- Respect reduced motion and save-data; use still media and normal document flow when appropriate.
