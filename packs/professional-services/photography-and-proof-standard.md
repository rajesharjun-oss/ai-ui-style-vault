# Professional Services Photography & Proof Standard

Prefer approved real team, workplace, process, event, publication, architecture/project or data visuals that demonstrate the firm's work. Stock imagery may establish mood but must never impersonate staff, clients or project evidence.

For each major asset record subject, provenance, purpose, consent/rights, crop, mobile treatment and fallback. Generated boardrooms, executives, project sites or client scenarios must be labelled concept imagery internally and must not be presented as documentary evidence.

Proof hierarchy: verified client/case evidence > verified credentials > approved people/process imagery > owned insight/data graphics > licensed category imagery > generated concept visuals. Decorative “handshake”, skyscraper and generic laptop photography should not substitute for proof.
