# Professional Services Accessibility & Responsive

Long-form service and insight content must remain readable at 200% zoom and narrow widths. Maintain semantic heading order, visible focus, descriptive link text, accessible tables/charts, labelled forms and error summaries.

Desktop editorial layouts must transform intentionally on mobile; do not simply shrink multi-column grids. Keep the primary enquiry action reachable without fixed elements covering content. Profiles, credentials and case studies must remain understandable without imagery or animation.
