# Professional Services Motion Guidance

Motion should clarify hierarchy, data relationships, process or navigation. Default to restrained transitions, subtle reveal, count/data transitions only when sourced, and diagram progression when it improves comprehension.

Avoid continuous ambient motion, gratuitous parallax, floating objects, unrelated 3D luxury props and cinematic intros that delay access to services. All motion must support reduced motion and a no-animation state. For 3D, require the separate 3D semantic relevance contract; relevant examples may include architecture models, engineering assemblies, verified geospatial/data structures or service-process diagrams—not generic spectacle.
