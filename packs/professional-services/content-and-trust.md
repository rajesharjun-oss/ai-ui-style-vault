# Professional Services Content & Trust Standard

Lead with the exact problem solved, service scope, client fit and next action. Avoid generic claims such as “transforming tomorrow” unless substantiated by specific evidence.

## Required hierarchy

1. What the firm does.
2. Who the service is for.
3. What problem or risk it addresses.
4. Why the firm is credible.
5. How an engagement starts.

## Trust rules

- Never invent clients, awards, rankings, years in business, staff counts, credentials, memberships or results.
- Anonymous case studies must say they are anonymised.
- Distinguish firm-wide credentials from individual credentials.
- Keep regulatory, legal, tax and financial claims current and sourceable.
- Thought leadership should support expertise; it must not bury service navigation.
- Use professional terminology where it helps qualified buyers, but explain uncommon terms.
- Do not turn every paragraph into a card.

## Copy budgets

Hero headline: usually 5–12 words. Hero support: 20–40 words. Service summary: 20–60 words. Long technical detail belongs on service/insight pages with clear headings and progressive disclosure.
