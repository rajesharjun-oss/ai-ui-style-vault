# Implement a Professional Services Website

Use only after the Business Research Gate is `ready` and `business-profile.json` validates. Read all files in this pack and complete `PROFESSIONAL_SERVICES_BUILD_CONTRACT.md` before implementation.

Select the service taxonomy from verified business information. Build around client questions, expertise, proof and engagement—not a generic agency template. Record every claim and proof item as verified, owner-supplied, anonymised, provisional or omitted. If the business operates in regulated advice, keep claims precise and current.

When 3D is requested, add the 3D capability pack only after proving relevance. A tax, audit or law firm does not receive random cars, spaceships, metallic sculptures or unrelated luxury scenes.

Render and inspect home, at least one service detail, contact path, mobile navigation, form states, reduced motion and long-content layouts before handoff.
